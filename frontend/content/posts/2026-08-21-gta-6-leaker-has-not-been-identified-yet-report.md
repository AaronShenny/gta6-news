# GTA 6 Leaker Has Not Been Identified Yet – Report

Recent extensive GTA 6 gameplay leaks, reportedly used to promote a memecoin, have prompted an internal response from Rockstar Games. A new report from Bloomberg indicates that Rockstar has not yet identified the individual(s) responsible for the breach or the specific method by which the footage was obtained. Internally, a top Rockstar executive reportedly sent a memo to staff, condemning the leaker and reassuring employees that the incident would not overshadow their hard work. Furthermore, the report clarifies that Rockstar does not intend to alter its marketing strategy for GTA 6 due to these leaks, with the highly anticipated reveal planned for Netflix on August 27 proceeding as scheduled. This decision comes despite a precedent set in December 2023 when Rockstar released the first trailer ahead of schedule after it leaked. The article also touches upon the financial context, noting that Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar's parent company, recently experienced a loss of over $2 billion in market value since the latest leaks began, though the report cautions that this decline could stem from various factors beyond just the leaks. In an effort to track down the culprits, Take-Two has taken legal action by subpoenaing Microsoft and Discord. GTA 6 is currently slated for release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
