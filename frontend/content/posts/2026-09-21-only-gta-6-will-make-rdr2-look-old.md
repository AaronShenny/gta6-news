# Only GTA 6 Will Make RDR2 Look Old

A passionate gamer recently shared their thoughts on the r/GTA6 subreddit, asserting that only the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6 has the potential to make Rockstar's critically acclaimed Red Dead Redemption 2 'look old'. The user, currently aiming for 100% completion in RDR2, expressed amazement at the game's continued ability to surprise them with new random events and interactions even eight years after its release, highlighting its incredibly 'alive' world. This deep appreciation for RDR2's detail serves as a benchmark for their expectations for GTA 6. They envision GTA 6 as a monumental technical leap, boasting a map three times larger, double the animations, and significantly more world events, intricate NPC interactions, hidden secrets, collectibles, and challenges. The sentiment underscores a high level of anticipation for what Rockstar Games will deliver, suggesting that GTA 6 will set a new standard that eventually eclipses even the groundbreaking immersion of RDR2.
