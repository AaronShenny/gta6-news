# GTA 6 Map Theory: Is the Leaked World Incomplete?

A popular theory circulating among fans suggests that the leaked Grand Theft Auto 6 map may not represent the game's full, final world. The core argument posits that since much of the leaked gameplay footage originated from an early development build, the accompanying map could also be an early, restricted version.

The theory draws parallels to Rockstar's previous title, Red Dead Redemption 2, where significant portions of the map were inaccessible until later stages of the storyline, and the world itself evolved after the epilogue's time jump. It's speculated that Rockstar might continue and even expand upon this theme of an evolving map in GTA 6.

Given GTA 6's rumored focus on drug running and evading law enforcement, the theory introduces the possibility of a fictionalized Bahamas or Cuba being added to the map. These locations would serve as ideal plot devices for smuggling operations and as hideouts for protagonists. While a fictional Bahamas could become a permanent, explorable location later in the story (similar to Mexico in RDR2), a fictional Cuba is considered less likely for launch, though it could be a future GTA Online expansion. The overall sentiment is that the current leaked map 'feels smaller than expected,' lending credence to the idea of unrevealed expansions.

## Key Takeaways
- The leaked GTA 6 map might be an early-game version, not the complete world map.
- This theory is supported by the belief that leaked gameplay footage originated from an early development build.
- Rockstar's Red Dead Redemption 2 featured an evolving map with locked areas and post-epilogue changes, suggesting a similar approach for GTA 6.
- A fictional Bahamas or Cuba could be added later in the game, fitting the rumored drug running and law evasion themes.
- The current leaked map 'feels smaller than expected,' indicating potential future expansions beyond what has been seen.
