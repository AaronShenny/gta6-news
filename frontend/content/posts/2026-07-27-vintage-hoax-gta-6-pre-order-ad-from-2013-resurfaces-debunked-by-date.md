# Vintage Hoax? GTA 6 Pre-Order Ad from 2013 Resurfaces, Debunked by Date

An old image depicting a supposed pre-order advertisement for Grand Theft Auto VI has recently resurfaced, causing a stir among eager fans. The image, which shows the 'Grand Theft Auto VI' logo accompanied by 'Pre-Order Now' text, was reportedly sighted in Ontario, Canada. It originated from a Reddit post by user /u/Technical-Manager921. Crucially, the original Reddit submission dates back to August 2013. This timeline immediately raises red flags for any seasoned gaming enthusiast or journalist. In August 2013, Rockstar Games was just weeks away from launching Grand Theft Auto V, making an official pre-order campaign for GTA VI an absolute impossibility. Therefore, this 'ad sighting' is overwhelmingly considered to be a hoax or a fan-made concept that circulated nearly a decade before GTA VI's official announcement. While the image might look convincing to the uninitiated, its historical context firmly establishes it as an unreliable piece of information, serving as a reminder to always verify sources and dates in the fast-paced world of gaming rumors.
