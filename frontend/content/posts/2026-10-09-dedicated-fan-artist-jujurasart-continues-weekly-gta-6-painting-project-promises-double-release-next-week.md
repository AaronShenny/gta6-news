# Dedicated Fan Artist jujurasart Continues Weekly GTA 6 Painting Project, Promises Double Release Next Week

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto fan, known as 'jujurasart', has provided their Week 6 update on an ongoing art project to paint a unique scene inspired by GTA 6 every week until the game's official release. Shared on the r/GTA6 subreddit by u/Goylianos, this week's update reveals that the artist was unable to complete the painting for Week 6 due to time constraints. However, in a show of commitment, jujurasart has pledged to deliver two finished paintings in the upcoming Week 7. The artist also mentioned framing some of their previous works, indicating the growing collection and the tangible progress of this impressive fan endeavor. The artwork is created using acrylics on paper, showcasing the artist's skill and passion for the highly anticipated title.
