# GTA 6's Miami Marketing Blitz Faces Local Official Pushback

Rockstar Games is launching an unprecedented real-life marketing campaign for Grand Theft Auto 6 in Miami, the inspiration for the game's Vice City setting, ahead of its November 19 release. This expansive effort includes an airlifted logo at the Kaseya Center, a Miami Heat crossover event, and real-world activations like GTA-style cars, blimps, and branded beach amenities across South Florida. The marketing push isn't confined to Miami, with ads appearing in major cities like Chicago and New York.

While Miami-Dade County Mayor Daniella Levine Cava's office champions the partnership, anticipating millions in revenue and describing it as a 'love letter' to the city, the initiative has sparked considerable controversy among local officials. Several Miami-Dade County commissioners, including René Garcia and Micky Steinberg, have voiced strong objections, arguing that promoting a game centered on violence and crime unfairly depicts Miami as a 'murder capital' and goes against the city's values. Conversely, Chairman Anthony Rodriguez defends the deals, emphasizing that GTA 6 is a work of fiction and adults understand it's 'just a game.'

Notably, the Mayor has the authority to approve deals under $1 million without commission oversight, which allowed a $975,000 Kaseya Center project to proceed. However, future deals for marketing at city airports and ship ports remain subject to commission approval, highlighting the ongoing debate.
