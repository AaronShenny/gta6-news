# GTA 6 Platinum Trophy: Will Online's Delayed Launch Impact Achievement Hunters?

A discussion has emerged within the Grand Theft Auto 6 community, sparked by a Reddit user's query concerning the release timing of the game's platinum trophy. The core of the speculation revolves around the widely anticipated scenario where GTA Online, the multiplayer component, will launch sometime after the main story mode of GTA 6. The user, u/Arthurmorgan69_420, raises the pertinent question of whether the platinum trophy will be available immediately upon the game's initial release. They suggest that if the platinum trophy were to be live from day one, it might exclusively consist of story-related achievements, potentially making it 'a lot easier' to obtain before any online-centric trophies are introduced with the later release of GTA Online.
