# GTA 6 Owner's CEO, Strauss Zelnick, Shares His Secrets to a Long and Healthy Life

Strauss Zelnick, the CEO of Take-Two Interactive (parent company of Rockstar Games and thus, GTA 6), is renowned for his commitment to health and fitness. Having even published a book titled "Becoming Ageless," Zelnick recently shared his personal philosophy and actionable advice for a healthier, longer life in an interview with Business Insider.

While friends with extreme longevity advocate Bryan Johnson, Zelnick clarified he doesn't subscribe to such intense methods. Instead, he focuses on "healthspan" over "lifespan," a concept he learned from celebrity doctor Peter Attia.

Zelnick's core advice begins with strong prohibitions:
*   **Absolutely no smoking**, citing its correlation with a short, unhealthy life.
*   **Avoid abusing alcohol and drugs**, as these lead to "a bad old age, or no old age."

Beyond these, his recommendations are practical and accessible:
*   **Regular exercise**: Even a modest 30-minute walk four to five days a week is sufficient, though Zelnick himself goes further as it's a hobby.
*   **Responsible diet**: No need for "insanely healthy" eating, just avoid overindulging in unhealthy foods.
*   **Adequate sleep**: Aim for seven hours nightly, as less than six hours consistently leads to poor decision-making.
*   **Emotional and spiritual well-being**: Emphasize having loved ones and a supportive community.

Zelnick also cautioned against taking unregulated peptides, stressing the importance of FDA-approved, double-blind tested substances. For those struggling with weight, he strongly advocated for GLP-1 medications, dismissing any "shame" associated with their use and comparing resistance to them to refusing Tylenol for a migraine.

## Key Takeaways
- Strauss Zelnick, Take-Two CEO, prioritizes "healthspan" over extreme longevity methods.
- Top health advice: Avoid smoking, alcohol, and drug abuse.
- Recommends moderate exercise (e.g., 30-min walks 4-5 times/week) and a responsible diet.
- Stresses the importance of 7 hours of sleep nightly and a strong emotional/spiritual life.
- Cautions against unregulated peptides and advocates for GLP-1s for weight management without shame.
