# GTA 6's Mandy Actor Lexie Shoaibi Addresses Online Reactions and Her Surprise Role

Lexie Shoaibi, the actor portraying the memorable GTA 6 character Mandy, has opened up about her experience with the game and the online community. Mandy, who attempts to seduce Jason early in the game and has been speculated to be a serial killer, quickly became a focal point for players, leading to significant online "thirst."

In an interview with Vulture, Shoaibi shared her perspective on the internet's "weirdos," stating that as a content creator, she's learned to see "more good than bad." She emphasizes the importance of surrounding oneself with supportive communities, both online and offline, to avoid negativity, expressing gratitude for the positive support she receives.

Remarkably, Shoaibi was completely unaware she was auditioning for or would be featured in GTA 6. Due to an "incredibly" strict NDA, her audition for "Project Bubblegum" was presented as a standard commercial role. She was instructed to play a "sexy ex-girlfriend" and "be seductive." It wasn't until GTA 6's launch on November 19 that she discovered her role as Mandy, having recorded her scenes in late 2023 or early 2024.

Shoaibi described the revelation as "very exciting," especially knowing her scenes weren't cut, and feels like she's "living in a dream." She hopes this high-profile role in one of the biggest video games ever will provide "momentum" for her career and lead to more opportunities. GTA 6 itself had a monumental debut, selling 32.5 million copies and generating nearly $4 billion on its first day.
