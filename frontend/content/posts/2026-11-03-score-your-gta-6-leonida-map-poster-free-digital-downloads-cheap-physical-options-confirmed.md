# Score Your GTA 6 Leonida Map & Poster: Free Digital Downloads, Cheap Physical Options Confirmed

Rockstar Games has confirmed exciting details for Grand Theft Auto 6 fans regarding physical memorabilia, despite the game being a digital-only release set for November 19. Players will be able to get their hands on a printable physical GTA 6 cover art poster and a map of the game's new setting, Leonida.

Both the digital poster and Leonida map will be available for free download on the official GTA 6 website starting November 19. For those who prefer a tangible item, a double-sized physical version of the cover art poster will be sold for 99 cents through the Rockstar Store "shortly after launch." Alternatively, this physical poster can be acquired for free with any purchase from the Rockstar Store.

While GTA 6 will not include a physical disc – retail boxes will contain a digital download code – this move by Rockstar harks back to previous GTA titles that famously included fold-out maps and posters. Additionally, some retailers, like Walmart, are reportedly planning to include their own "surprises" with GTA 6 orders.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 is a digital-only release, launching November 19.
- Rockstar will offer free digital downloads of the Leonida map and cover art poster on the official GTA 6 website starting November 19.
- A double-sized physical cover art poster will be available from the Rockstar Store for 99 cents or free with any purchase, shortly after launch.
- Physical retail boxes for GTA 6 will contain a digital download code, not a game disc.
- Some retailers, such as Walmart, are rumored to include additional 'surprises' with GTA 6 pre-orders.

## FAQ
**Is GTA 6 available on a physical disc?**
No, Grand Theft Auto 6 is a digital-only game. Physical retail boxes will contain a code for a digital download, not a disc.

**How can I get a physical map of Leonida for GTA 6?**
Rockstar Games will offer a free digital download of the Leonida map (and cover art poster) on the official GTA 6 website starting November 19. The article does not specify a physical map for sale, only a physical poster.

**Will there be a physical GTA 6 poster available?**
Yes, a double-sized physical version of the GTA 6 cover art poster will be available from the Rockstar Store for 99 cents, or free with any Rockstar Store purchase, 'shortly after launch'.

**When can I download the digital map and poster?**
Both the digital Leonida map and cover art poster will be available for free download on GTA 6's official website starting November 19.
