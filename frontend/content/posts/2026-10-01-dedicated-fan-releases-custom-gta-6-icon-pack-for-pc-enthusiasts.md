# Dedicated Fan Releases Custom GTA 6 Icon Pack for PC Enthusiasts

In a display of fervent anticipation for the upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI, a dedicated fan known as P00TERMAN has unveiled a custom icon pack for the highly anticipated title. The pack, designed with PC users in mind, features six unique icons, ready for enthusiasts to customize their desktops ahead of GTA VI's eventual release on the platform. Available in both 512x512 .PNG and standard .ICO formats, these fan-made assets offer a creative way for the community to visually prepare for the next installment in the iconic series. P00TERMAN shared the creation on Reddit, hoping fellow fans will enjoy personalizing their setups.
