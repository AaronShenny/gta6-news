# Fan Artist Blends GTA 6's Lucia with Classic Vice City Aesthetic

A dedicated fan artist, known as u/tomy_wose on Reddit, has unveiled a captivating piece of artwork that merges the anticipated look of Grand Theft Auto VI's protagonist, Lucia, with the iconic, vintage aesthetic of Vice City. The artwork specifically incorporates a 'wig from the extended trailer,' suggesting a keen eye for detail from the game's promotional material. The artist noted the challenge in accurately depicting Lucia's face when experimenting with different hairstyles, highlighting the intricate process of character interpretation within fan creations. This piece offers a fresh, retro perspective on one of gaming's most anticipated characters.
