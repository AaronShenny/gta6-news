# Dedicated Fan Reimagines GTA 6 Protagonists Lucia & Jason in Stunning Fan Art

Reddit user u/Wooden_Act973 has shared an impressive piece of fan art featuring the widely anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI protagonists, Lucia and Jason. The artwork is described by the artist as a re-draw of their pose from what they believe to be the game's cover art, showcasing the vibrant and engaged fan community already building around Rockstar Games' upcoming title. The artist conveyed their personal excitement to play as these characters upon the game's release. They also highlighted the challenges encountered during the creation process, specifically mentioning the difficulty in accurately depicting intricate details such as Jason's tattoo and an element on a skull's head, largely due to the current scarcity of clear official reference images available to the public. This fan creation serves as a testament to the strong anticipation for GTA 6.
