# GTA 6 Hype Train: Fans Predict Upcoming Rockstar Drops Before Preload

The gaming community is buzzing with speculation as Rockstar Games has established a clear pattern of releasing Grand Theft Auto VI related content almost every Thursday since August 27. With the game's preload date set for Thursday, November 12, fans are meticulously tracking the three remaining Thursdays leading up to it: October 22, October 29, and November 5. Reddit user /u/Dasun_Flint initiated a discussion, sharing their predictions for these crucial dates. The user suggests a special GTA-themed PS5 Pro or bundle on October 22, coinciding with the 25th anniversary of GTA III. Additional music releases are predicted for October 29, followed by a highly anticipated GTA 6 Online Trailer on November 5, which some believe would explain the relatively early release of the main launch trailer. The discussion also probes whether Rockstar would dare to overlook the significant GTA III anniversary.
