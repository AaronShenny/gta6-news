# Australian Player Confirms GTA VI Standard Edition Upgrade to Ultimate Is Possible

A recent post on the r/GTA6 subreddit has provided clarity for Australian players wondering about upgrading their Grand Theft Auto VI pre-orders. A user, who pre-ordered the Standard Edition of GTA VI from JB Hi-Fi using a gift card, initially faced a dilemma as they couldn't use the gift card to purchase a Sony gift card for the Ultimate Edition. The user inquired whether an upgrade from the Standard to the Ultimate Edition would be possible after purchase. In a subsequent edit, the user confirmed that 'indeed it can be done,' indicating that players who initially purchase the Standard Edition can later upgrade to the Ultimate Edition. This is good news for players who might have similar purchase constraints or simply wish to enhance their game experience post-purchase.

## Key Takeaways
- An Australian player confirmed the ability to upgrade GTA VI Standard Edition to Ultimate Edition.
- The initial purchase was the Standard Edition from JB Hi-Fi due to gift card limitations.
- The upgrade path is available for players who may not initially purchase the Ultimate Edition.
