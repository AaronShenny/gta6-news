# Dispelling the 30FPS Fear: A Player's Surprising Take on GTA 6 Performance

The ongoing discussion about Grand Theft Auto 6 potentially launching at 30 frames per second (fps) has sparked considerable debate within the gaming community. One player, curious about the severity of a 30fps experience, decided to test it themselves by playing Grand Theft Auto V on a PlayStation 5 in Fidelity mode, which locks the framerate at 30fps with ray tracing enabled.

Their surprising conclusion? 30fps is "not **THAT** bad." While acknowledging a desire for a smoother 60fps, the player emphasized that the experience was far from "game-breaking" or "unplayable." Even during intense moments like high-speed driving and rapid camera movements, the performance held up better than anticipated. The author drew a distinction between console and PC gaming, suggesting that 30fps on console is "doable" due to controller stick movements, whereas it might be closer to "unplayable" on PC with the more twitchy mouse and keyboard controls.

Despite their findings, the player clarified that they are not a "Rockstar glazer" and would "obviously" prefer 60fps. However, they expressed a willingness to accept 30fps to play the highly anticipated title sooner, rather than waiting potentially for a PlayStation 6 or a PC version. The author also voiced surprise that such a graphically intensive game like GTA 6 is expected to run on current-generation consoles, which they refer to as approximately "6-year" old hardware (referring to the console generation's lifespan, given the rumored 2025 release for GTA 6).
