# GTA 6 Witness System: Players Hope for More Lenient and Realistic Reactions Compared to RDR2

A recent discussion on the r/GTA6 subreddit, initiated by u/Spiritual-Cry7953, highlights player hopes for a more nuanced and lenient witness system in Grand Theft Auto 6. The user points to Red Dead Redemption 2's often-criticized system, where even minor actions like bumping into an NPC or self-defense could instantly trigger reports for 'unarmed assault' or 'disturbing the peace.' The core of the argument is that such immediate and severe reactions feel out of place in a modern setting, unlike the late 1800s backdrop of RDR2.

The submission suggests that in today's world, people witnessing minor altercations (like a slight push or bump) would more likely observe, record, walk away, or attempt to de-escalate, rather than immediately calling the police. Therefore, the user proposes that GTA 6's witness system should only prompt police involvement for more serious infractions, such as sustained physical assaults or prolonged public nuisances. This sentiment reflects a desire for a more immersive and less frustrating gameplay experience, where the consequences better align with real-world social reactions to different levels of transgression.

## Key Takeaways
- Players desire a more lenient witness system in GTA 6.
- The current witness system in Red Dead Redemption 2 is criticized for being overly sensitive to minor actions.
- In RDR2, minor bumps or self-defense often lead to immediate reports and law enforcement pursuit.
- The user argues that modern-day witnesses would react differently to minor offenses (observing, recording, walking away, de-escalating) rather than instantly calling the police.
- It is hoped that GTA 6 will reserve police calls for serious offenses like prolonged assaults or significant public disturbances.

## FAQ
**What is the main concern regarding the witness system in GTA 6?**
The main concern is that the witness system in GTA 6 should be more lenient and realistic, particularly in how NPCs react to minor player actions, avoiding the over-sensitivity seen in Red Dead Redemption 2.

**How did the witness system in Red Dead Redemption 2 behave?**
In RDR2, even minor actions like bumping into an NPC or engaging in self-defense could instantly lead to NPCs reporting the player for offenses like 'unarmed assault' or 'disturbing the peace,' resulting in law enforcement pursuit.

**What kind of NPC reactions are desired for minor offenses in GTA 6?**
Players hope that for minor offenses, NPCs would react more realistically, such as observing, recording, walking away, or trying to calm things down, rather than immediately calling the police.

**When do players believe police should be called in GTA 6?**
Players suggest that police should only be called for serious offenses like straight-up beating someone up or being a nuisance for a prolonged period.
