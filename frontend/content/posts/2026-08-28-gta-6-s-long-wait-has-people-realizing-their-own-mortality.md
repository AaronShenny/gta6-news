# GTA 6’s Long Wait Has People Realizing Their Own Mortality

The recent extended-look trailer for **Grand Theft Auto VI** has elicited a surprising reaction from the gaming community, with top comments focusing less on the game's visuals or action and more on the human condition and the passage of time. Many fans are reflecting on their lives and even their mortality, given the almost 13-year gap since GTA 5's original launch. 

Comments on Rockstar's YouTube channel include poignant statements like "a moment of silence for people that didn't make it here" and personal anecdotes about significant life milestones passed during the wait, such as completing middle school, high school, and college. One commenter even shared overcoming a serious accident to still anticipate the game.

Beyond these profound reactions, the trailer also revealed new gameplay details. Players can expect NPCs to react to openly carried weapons, and car theft mechanics will be more complex, requiring a key cloner for newer models. GTA 6 is slated for release on **November 19** for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with an estimated playthrough time of roughly 80 hours.
