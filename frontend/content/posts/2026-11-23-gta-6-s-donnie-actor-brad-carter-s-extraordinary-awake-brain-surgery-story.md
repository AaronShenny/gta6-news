# GTA 6's Donnie Actor Brad Carter's Extraordinary Awake Brain Surgery Story

Brad Carter, the acclaimed actor bringing the drug-smuggler Donnie to life in the monumental *Grand Theft Auto 6*, has a personal story as captivating as any in-game narrative. Approximately 13 years ago, Carter underwent a groundbreaking awake brain surgery to alleviate severe hand tremors. In a truly extraordinary turn, as a dedicated musician, he played his guitar throughout the procedure, enabling surgeons to precisely position electrodes for optimal results. This remarkable event was extensively documented live on platforms like Twitter and Vine in 2013, quickly becoming an international headline. While the multiple surgeries didn't entirely eradicate his tremors, they successfully restored his ability to play guitar. Beyond his role in *GTA 6*, Carter boasts an impressive acting resume, including appearances in hit series such as *It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia*, *Dexter*, *True Detective*, and *Ozark*. Gamers might also recognize his voice as Jonah from Rockstar's *Red Dead Redemption* (2010). His portrayal of Donnie is poised to become his most iconic role, given *GTA 6*'s unprecedented cultural impact. The game, which launched on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, has already shattered sales records, with over 44.9 million copies sold and more than $4 billion in revenue generated within its first four days alone.
