# GTA 6's $3,000 Varsity Jacket Sells Out Instantly at London Selfridges Event

Rockstar Games continues its extensive marketing push for Grand Theft Auto 6, extending beyond Miami with a high-profile takeover event at the upscale British retailer Selfridges in London. On November 9, the flagship store hosted a GTA 6-themed event featuring a blue Grotti Cheetah Classic car from the game and immersive branding throughout. A standout item among the more than 60 themed products was a "Goodtime State Varsity Jacket" priced at a hefty £2,500 ($3,300 USD), which, despite its premium cost, sold out almost immediately.

The jacket boasts Rockstar and GTA 6 logos, game-inspired imagery on the sleeves, and a distinctive alligator in sunglasses design on the back. Another, slightly less expensive, black 'Vice City' varsity jacket priced at £1,750 ($2,300 USD) also quickly sold out. The collection includes various other items like clothing, bags, shot glasses, and a cigar ashtray, all available on the Selfridges website. This event underscores the immense anticipation and demand for GTA 6, even for high-end, limited-edition merchandise.
