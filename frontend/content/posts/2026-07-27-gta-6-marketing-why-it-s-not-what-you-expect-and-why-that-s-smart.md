# GTA 6 Marketing: Why It's Not What You Expect (And Why That's Smart)

Many fans are comparing the current GTA 6 marketing campaign to the extensive 22-month rollout of GTA V, leading to concerns about its perceived 'silence.' However, an industry marketing professional argues that this comparison is outdated and misses the strategic shift. According to the analysis, Rockstar's approach to GTA 6 reflects modern audience behavior, opting for a shorter, more concentrated 5-month push rather than a prolonged drip-feed campaign.

The campaign has already begun, with preorders open, cover art released, pricing confirmed, and editions announced. The current phase is about funneling existing interest, with the 'creative peak' reserved for closer to launch. Rockstar doesn't need to build initial awareness, as GTA 6 trailers have already garnered record-breaking views, indicating near 100% audience awareness. Consequently, marketing spend will be focused on conversion in the crucial final six weeks, strategically avoiding costly periods like World Cup summers or peak holiday seasons when attention is divided and media prices are inflated.

Interestingly, the article suggests that the ongoing fan discussions and theories online are, in themselves, a form of free marketing, generating significant buzz without direct spending from Rockstar. The author predicts that the major marketing push, including new trailers and 'unhinged real-life stunts,' will likely commence in the first half of August, aligning with a more modern, impactful strategy.
