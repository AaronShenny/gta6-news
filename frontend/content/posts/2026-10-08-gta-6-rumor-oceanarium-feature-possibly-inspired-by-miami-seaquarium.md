# GTA 6 Rumor: Oceanarium Feature Possibly Inspired by Miami Seaquarium

Speculation within the Grand Theft Auto 6 community points to the inclusion of an 'oceanarium' as a new in-game location. This rumored feature is believed to be inspired by the Miami Seaquarium, a prominent real-world marine park that ceased operations in 2025 after 70 years. The Miami Seaquarium gained significant public attention for being the long-term home of Lolita, a famous captive orca. If these rumors prove true, the oceanarium could offer players a unique environment within the game, potentially featuring marine life exhibits and serving as another example of Rockstar Games' satirical take on real-world landmarks and institutions.
