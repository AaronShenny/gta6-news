# GTA 6's Quality Leap Predicted to Eclipse RDR2's Transformation, According to Fan Speculation

A passionate fan on Reddit has sparked discussion by suggesting that the generational leap in quality from Grand Theft Auto V to the upcoming GTA 6 will be at least as substantial, if not more so, than the remarkable evolution observed between Red Dead Redemption and Red Dead Redemption 2. The user, currently replaying RDR2, highlights the game's enduring impressiveness eight years post-release, attributing its advanced state to being Rockstar's most recent title built on the latest iteration of the RAGE engine.

The core of the argument rests on the time difference: an eight-year gap separated RDR1 and RDR2, during which Rockstar achieved a 'transformative' sequel. With a thirteen-year gap between GTA V (2013) and the anticipated GTA 6 (expected 2025), the Reddit user posits that the developers have had even more time to evolve their technology and game design. This extended development cycle, coupled with the RAGE engine's advancements, is expected to deliver an unprecedented leap for the Grand Theft Auto franchise, potentially making GTA 6 one of the most groundbreaking sequels in gaming history. The user encourages others to revisit RDR2 to fully appreciate Rockstar's capacity for innovation and to build excitement for GTA 6.
