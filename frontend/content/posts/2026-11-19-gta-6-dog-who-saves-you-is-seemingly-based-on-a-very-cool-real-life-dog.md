# GTA 6 Dog Who Saves You Is Seemingly Based On A Very Cool Real-Life Dog

One of the charming discoveries players have made in Grand Theft Auto 6 is the presence of a jetski-riding dog that intervenes to coax players back to shore if they swim too far into the ocean.

This in-game canine companion appears to draw inspiration from a real-life golden retriever named Loosy, famous for her impressive stunts in Florida. Loosy gained viral attention alongside her owner, Eddie Inch, who was filmed riding a jet-propelled hoverboard while towing a goggle-wearing Loosy on a jetski in Fort Lauderdale. Rockstar Games seems to have taken a cue from this viral sensation, incorporating a similar jetski-riding dog into GTA 6. While the in-game dog is of a different breed, it notably sports goggles, just like Loosy.

Despite the strong resemblance and apparent homage, neither Loosy nor Eddie Inch are listed in GTA 6's official credits. However, the connection to the real-world viral dog is widely considered obvious.

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched today, November 19, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, garnering rave reviews, including a perfect 10/10 from GameSpot. The game has already achieved immense commercial success, reportedly selling over 19 million copies and generating nearly $2 billion.
