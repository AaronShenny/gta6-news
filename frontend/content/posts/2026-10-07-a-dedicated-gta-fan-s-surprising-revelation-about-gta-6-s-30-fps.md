# A Dedicated GTA Fan's Surprising Revelation About GTA 6's 30 FPS

The article details a dedicated *Grand Theft Auto* player's journey from initial anxiety to eventual acceptance regarding the rumored 30 FPS frame rate for *Grand Theft Auto 6* on consoles. Initially, the author expressed significant concern, fearing that a 30 FPS experience would feel "buggy, clunky, and difficult to play."

However, a personal revelation dramatically shifted their perspective. The author realized that their extensive playtime with *Grand Theft Auto 5*—six playthroughs, including three 100% completions—had primarily been on the Xbox One version, played on an Xbox Series X, which runs at a consistent 30 FPS. This realization struck them as "pretty funny" and profoundly reassuring.

The author now argues that 30 FPS is considerably more playable than many gamers perceive, especially on larger television screens where it can feel "much smoother than you might expect." While acknowledging that PC players accustomed to 60 FPS or higher will find the transition challenging, and that 60 FPS is inherently superior when available, they firmly believe that 30 FPS is "more than enough" for a console title like *GTA 6*. They emphasize that it will allow players to fully immerse themselves in the expansive world of Leonida, explore Vice City, undertake missions, and enjoy the entirety of the game's potentially 80+ hour experience without feeling hindered by the frame rate.

Ultimately, the author's hundreds of hours of enjoyable 30 FPS gameplay in *GTA 5* have alleviated their worries about *GTA 6*, leading them to conclude that many players will be "surprised by how playable 30 FPS actually is."
