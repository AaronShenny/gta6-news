# GTA 6's 1.5GB Update Fuels Speculation of QSSR for PS5 and AMD FSR for Xbox

A recent 1.5GB update for Grand Theft Auto 6 has ignited significant speculation within the gaming community regarding potential graphical enhancements. The timing of this update has particularly caught the attention of enthusiasts, as it closely follows Insomniac's update for *Marvel's Spider-Man 2*, which introduced QSSR (Quality Super Sampling Resolution) support for the PlayStation 5.

Journalists and fans are now theorizing that Rockstar Games might be preparing to implement similar, platform-specific upscaling solutions for GTA 6. This theory is bolstered by previous reports hinting at the presence of AMD FidelityFX-related libraries within GTA 6's Xbox files, suggesting potential AMD FSR (FidelityFX Super Resolution) support for Microsoft's consoles.

Under this speculative scenario, Rockstar could be aiming to leverage every available technological advantage to optimize visual quality and performance across different hardware. This might translate to QSSR support for the base PS5, PSSR (PlayStation Super Sampling Resolution) or further PSSR optimizations for the PS5 Pro, and AMD FSR for the Xbox Series X/S.

However, it's crucial to note that these are currently unconfirmed rumors. While a 1.5GB update is substantial, it could also encompass various other improvements such as bug fixes, general performance optimizations, or preparations for the game's eventual launch. The discussion remains purely speculative, driven by circumstantial evidence and the community's desire for advanced graphical fidelity in the highly anticipated title.
