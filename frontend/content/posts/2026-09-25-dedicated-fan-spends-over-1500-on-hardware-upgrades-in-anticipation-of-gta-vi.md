# Dedicated Fan Spends Over $1500 on Hardware Upgrades in Anticipation of GTA VI

A dedicated fan from the r/GTA6 subreddit, u/ImHeiden, has shared their substantial financial commitment to upgrading their gaming setup in anticipation of Grand Theft Auto VI. The user meticulously detailed their purchases, which include a used PlayStation 5 PRO for $760, a used LG C2 55" 4K 2022 OLED TV (120hz, HDR) for $460, a controller shell for $40, and a used NVIDIA Shield TV PRO for $300. These upgrades amount to a personal expenditure of $1560. Interestingly, the highly anticipated game itself, Grand Theft Auto VI, was a birthday gift from their girlfriend and therefore not included in their self-funded hardware total. This anecdote highlights the immense excitement and willingness of fans to invest in their gaming experience ahead of one of the most anticipated titles in recent memory.
