# GTA 6 Marketing Lacks Circoloco Records Integration, Fans Speculate

A keen-eyed Reddit user, u/Interesting_Life2487, has sparked discussion within the GTA 6 community by highlighting the apparent absence of Rockstar Games' own record label, Circoloco Records, in any marketing materials released for the upcoming title so far. The user posits that given GTA 6's setting in Vice City, a location renowned for its vibrant nightlife, Circoloco Records would be a 'good addition.' This observation has led to speculation among fans regarding whether the label will eventually be integrated into the game's soundtrack or in-game events, or if Rockstar has different plans for the game's musical landscape.

Circoloco Records is an electronic music record label owned by Rockstar Games, known for its association with the Ibiza-based Circoloco club nights and events.

Given GTA 6's Vice City setting, which is famous for its nightlife, a user speculated that Rockstar's own music label, Circoloco Records, would be a natural fit for the game's soundtrack or in-game elements, making its current absence in marketing surprising.
