# NBA 2K's Unusual Marketing Shift Fuels GTA 6 Announcement Speculation

A recent Reddit post on r/GTA6 highlights an unusual shift in NBA 2K's marketing strategy, leading to speculation about an impending Grand Theft Auto 6 announcement.
