# Unveiling GTA 6's Deadly Secret: A "Hard Target Meets Saw" Survival Mission

This article details a thrilling and brutal secret mission in Grand Theft Auto 6, pitting players against a relentless hunter in a deadly game of survival on Hyde Island. The mission, described as a blend of "Hard Target" and "Saw," begins when players land on Hyde Island, located north of Lummox County, and are incapacitated by a dart. Waking up with an explosive collar, players are given ten seconds to find a basic gun with two rounds in a nearby tent before a heavily armored hunter on a quad bike, accompanied by his tracking dog "Caesar," begins the pursuit.Survival hinges on stealth and strategic resource gathering. Players must quickly locate illuminated outposts across the island to acquire more ammo, adrenaline, and a shotgun, all while evading the sniper-wielding hunter. The key to victory lies in a well-planned last stand: prioritize eliminating the hunter's dog (identifiable by its red collar light) to remove its tracking ability, then use trees for cover to land critical shots on the hunter. Once the hunter is stunned, a barrage of shotgun rounds will finish him off.Successfully completing this harrowing ordeal rewards players handsomely with the Island-Poacher FF-50 sniper rifle, additional ammo, and a substantial $15,000 cash prize after retrieving personal items from a boathouse. Failure results in a cash penalty and a respawn near a safehouse, but the mission can be retried by returning to Hyde Island. The article emphasizes the cunning and sharp-shooting skills required to emerge as the deadliest game.

## FAQ
**How do I start the "Deadliest Game" secret mission in GTA 6?**
You can start this mission by traveling to Hyde Island, located north of Lummox County and the Moreau River. Landing on the island will trigger the mission start.

**What are the initial challenges in this mission?**
Upon waking up with an explosive collar, you'll have 10 seconds to reach a tent for a gun with two rounds. A heavily armored hunter on a quad bike, accompanied by his tracking dog Caesar, will then begin hunting you.

**How can I acquire more weapons and supplies during the hunt?**
Search for lit-up outposts scattered across Hyde Island, which contain additional ammo, adrenaline, and a shotgun.

**What is the best strategy to defeat the hunter?**
First, prioritize eliminating the hunter's dog, Caesar, which has a red light on its collar. Then, use trees for cover to land critical shots on the hunter and finish him off with shotgun rounds.

**What rewards do I get for completing the mission?**
Upon defeating the hunter, you'll receive the Island-Poacher FF-50 sniper rifle, ammo, and $15,000 in cash, along with your personal items from the boathouse.

**What happens if I fail the mission?**
If the hunter kills you, you'll lose a portion of your cash and wake up near a safehouse. You can retry the mission by returning to Hyde Island.
