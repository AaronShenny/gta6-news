# Alleged GTA 6 'Game Informer' Details Hint at Massive Map, Dynamic Weather, and Unprecedented NPC Detail

Alleged new information regarding Grand Theft Auto VI, reportedly courtesy of Game Informer, paints a picture of an incredibly ambitious and detailed open world. The leaks highlight several key areas of improvement and innovation:

*   **Dynamic Weather System:** GTA VI is said to feature a significantly upgraded dynamic wind system, capable of generating offshore breezes, convectional updrafts, and wind occlusion from buildings. This system will influence everything from hair and debris swirling on rooftops to ripples across water and bending swamp grass. Tropical storms and hurricanes are also confirmed for the game's setting, Leonida, complete with heavy clouds, powerful winds, torrential rain, dripping environments, and dramatic thunder and lightning.
*   **Expansive Wildlife:** The game's map will reportedly host over 170 animal species, including Leonida-native wildlife never before seen in a Rockstar title. This includes more than 60 bird species and 30 land mammals, alongside numerous fish and reptiles. Confirmed wildlife includes manatees, flamingos, pelicans, and alligators.
*   **Unprecedented NPC Detail:** NPCs are set to receive an unprecedented level of physical variety and detail. Citizens will exhibit different heights and body types, with variations in musculature and localized weight distribution (e.g., chest/bust, stomach, butt). Skin types will be more diverse, featuring details like freckles, moles, and tanning. Rockstar has also reportedly put extensive work into culturally accurate tattoo styles matching Leonida’s various communities and regions, as well as significantly more detailed and ethnically diverse hair and body grooming options.
*   **Massive and Dense Map:** Leonida is described as Rockstar's biggest map ever, roughly twice the size of GTA V’s world. Crucially, it's also said to be considerably denser, more detailed, and more interactive. Rockstar reportedly obsessed over every part of the world, including rooftops, alleys, building interiors, and the spaces between them. The game will feature a huge number of interiors, designed to feel realistic both inside and out, contributing to a cohesive environment rather than disconnected areas. The map aims to combine high content density with handcrafted detail throughout.
