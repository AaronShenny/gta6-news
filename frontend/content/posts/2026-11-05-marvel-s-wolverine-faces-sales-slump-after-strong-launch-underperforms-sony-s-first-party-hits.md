# Marvel's Wolverine Faces Sales Slump After Strong Launch, Underperforms Sony's First-Party Hits

Marvel's Wolverine, which launched to a strong start in September as the month's best-selling game, has seen its sales momentum cool considerably. Sony's latest earnings report reveals the Insomniac title has sold 2.4 million units so far. While this figure sounds impressive on its own, it pales in comparison to other Sony first-party games; Marvel's Spider-Man 2, for instance, sold 2.5 million units on its *first day*, and Ghost of Yotei outperformed Wolverine in just a few days. The game faced an uphill battle due to its release during a heavily congested gaming period, with major titles like Control Resonant, Onimusha: Way of the Sword, Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave, and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 vying for attention, and launching ahead of a hypothetical GTA 6 release on November 19. Lukewarm reviews and negative online chatter further compounded its struggles. Despite Marvel's Wolverine's underperformance, which is noted as a 'black mark,' Sony reported an otherwise solid quarter, with PS5 sales nearing 100 million units and robust overall earnings. The upcoming holiday season and Black Friday discounts are expected to help boost its sales.

## Key Takeaways
- Marvel's Wolverine has sold 2.4 million units since its September launch.
- Despite being September's best-selling game initially, its sales momentum has significantly slowed.
- The game notably underperformed compared to other Sony first-party titles, including Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (2.5 million units on day one) and Ghost of Yotei.
- Factors contributing to its sales struggles include a crowded release schedule, lukewarm critical reviews, and negative online discussions.
- The game launched during a busy period with several major titles and live-service games, also ahead of a hypothetical GTA 6 release on November 19.
- While Wolverine's sales are a setback, Sony reported an otherwise strong overall quarter for its other divisions and console sales.
