# GTA 6's Massive Miami Marketing Campaign Underway, Sparks Controversy

Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive have kicked off an extensive marketing campaign for Grand Theft Auto 6 in Miami, ahead of its November 2026 release. The centerpiece of this campaign is the installation of giant letters spelling out "Welcome to Vice City" at the Kaseya Center, a monumental task requiring aerial support from helicopters.

This advertising initiative is reportedly valued at nearly $1 million for Miami-Dade County, with the funds earmarked for developing a community plot of land behind the stadium. However, the deal has drawn criticism from local authorities, including Miami-Dade County Commissioner Bermudez and Sheriff Rosie Cordero-Stutz. They argue that the funds should instead be used to address budget gaps in the law enforcement division and have also voiced concerns about GTA 6's potential to glorify crime and violence against police officers.

The article also touches on game mechanics, noting that while players can commit crimes in GTA 6, new Wanted and Honor systems are designed to make law enforcement more intelligent and persistent, thus making it more challenging to evade capture compared to previous installments.

In other major news, Rockstar Games has officially announced "Grand Theft Auto 6: The Album," a 34-song collection featuring original music from artists like Keith Richards, Yung Lean, and Morgan Wallen, alongside several licensed tracks. A physical edition of the album is available for preorder and is set to release on November 19, 2026, coinciding with the game's launch.

Further updates include the confirmation of Milton from Office Space as an actor in the game, the rapid sale of limited-edition GTA 6 DualSense controllers, and the widespread trend of players planning to take time off work to immerse themselves in the game upon release.
