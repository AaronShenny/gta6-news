# Activision: GTA 6 Will Boost Gaming, Not Overshadow Modern Warfare 4

Activision's Chief Marketing Officer, Tyle Bahl, has expressed a positive outlook on the upcoming release of Rockstar's highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6*, asserting that it will actually benefit, rather than harm, other major titles like *Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4*. Contrary to concerns that GTA 6 might 'blot out the sun' for the industry, Bahl believes the game will act as a 'rising tide that lifts all ships' by drawing more people back to their consoles and into the broader gaming ecosystem.In an interview with The Game Business, Bahl stated that both GTA and Call of Duty are set to be the 'two biggest games of the year,' and the increased player activity on platforms like Xbox and PlayStation will ultimately lead to more engagement for all games, including MW4. He notably referenced Nintendo's *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* remake, launching around the same time, acknowledging its expected success but positioning GTA and CoD as the dominant forces.Ubisoft CEO Yves Guillemot echoed a similar sentiment previously, stating that major releases like GTA 6 are beneficial for the entire industry. Ubisoft is even planning to debut new content for *Rainbow Six Siege* around GTA 6's launch, indicating a lack of fear regarding competition.Bahl also highlighted 'spending time with friends' as a key differentiator for Call of Duty, confident that this social aspect will ensure *Modern Warfare 4*'s success even with GTA 6 arriving just weeks later. He emphasized that increased discussion and press around gaming, fueled by blockbusters like GTA 6, is 'good for everybody.'*Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4* is scheduled for a full release on October 23, with campaign early access on October 16. *Grand Theft Auto 6* will follow on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
