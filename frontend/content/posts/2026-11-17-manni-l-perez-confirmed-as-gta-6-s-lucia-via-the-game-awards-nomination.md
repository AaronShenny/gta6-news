# Manni L. Perez Confirmed as GTA 6's Lucia via The Game Awards Nomination

The identity of Lucia's voice actor in Grand Theft Auto 6 has been officially revealed, confirming popular fan speculation that Manni L. Perez will portray the character. This confirmation comes not directly from Rockstar Games, but through The Game Awards, where Perez was nominated for 'Best Performance' for her role in the highly anticipated title. Fans had already theorized Perez's involvement as early as 2023.

Grand Theft Auto 6 itself is a frontrunner at this year's Game Awards, securing an impressive eight nominations. These include the prestigious 'Game of the Year' award, which has drawn attention given that the game's reviews are not yet live. Other significant nominations for GTA 6 include Best Game Direction, Best Narrative, Best Art Direction, Best Score and Music, Best Audio Design, and Best Action/Adventure Game.

While Lucia's actor is now known, the identity of Jason's actor remains unconfirmed, though Dylan Rourke is widely believed to be in the role. Veteran actor Stephen Root is officially confirmed to play Brian Heder, and Saturday Night Live actor Bobby Moynihan is rumored to portray Cal Hampton.

GTA 6 is slated for release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a PC version reportedly arriving in early 2027.
