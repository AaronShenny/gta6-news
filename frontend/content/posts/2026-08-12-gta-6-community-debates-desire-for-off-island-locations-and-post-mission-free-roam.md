# GTA 6: Community Debates Desire for Off-Island Locations and Post-Mission Free Roam

The Grand Theft Auto community is actively discussing potential features for the highly anticipated GTA 6. A Reddit thread initiated by u/Jfullr92 sparked a conversation about the inclusion of separate, off-island locations, drawing parallels to areas like North Yankton in GTA V and Guarma in Red Dead Redemption 2. The core of the debate centers on whether these unique locations should allow players to return and free-roam after completing their designated missions. While previous Rockstar titles have introduced temporary, distinct environments, players often express a desire for more expansive and lasting access, specifically the ability to revisit and explore these areas at leisure beyond the story's progression. The sentiment suggests a strong interest in diversifying the game world with unique geographical areas, provided they offer enduring player engagement.

## Key Takeaways
- The GTA 6 community is discussing the potential for separate, non-main island locations in the game.
- Previous Rockstar titles like GTA V (North Yankton) and RDR2 (Guarma) featured such temporary areas.
- Players desire the ability to free-roam in these unique locations even after completing their associated missions.
- In past games, these areas were generally inaccessible post-mission, though RDR2's Guarma offered some free-roam during its missions.

## FAQ
**Will GTA 6 feature separate islands or locations away from the main map?**
The article discusses community speculation and desire for such features, referencing past Rockstar games, but there is no confirmed information from Rockstar Games regarding this.

**What examples of separate locations are mentioned from previous Rockstar games?**
GTA V featured North Yankton, and Red Dead Redemption 2 included the island of Guarma as distinct, temporary locations.

**Can players revisit these separate locations in older Rockstar games after completing missions?**
Generally, no. While Guarma in RDR2 allowed some free-roam during its missions, players could not typically return to these areas after completing the associated quests, which is a point of discussion for GTA 6.
