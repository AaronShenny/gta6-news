# GTA Online Players Ponder Absence of GTA VI Promotional Content

A recent discussion on Reddit's r/GTA6 community has sparked conversation about the noticeable absence of Grand Theft Auto VI or Vice City-themed promotions within Grand Theft Auto Online. User u/Nathanyal expressed surprise, recalling how *Red Dead Redemption 2* received a significant promotional tie-in—the Stone Hatchet—in GTA Online approximately two months before its official release. This historical precedent leads to speculation and questions regarding whether Rockstar Games plans any similar cross-promotional content for GTA VI in its massively popular online counterpart, or if the approach to marketing the upcoming title will differ significantly.
