# GTA 6 Marketing: Could In-Game Social Media Be the Next Big Tease?

A recent fan theory from Reddit's /u/rickymoyer posits an innovative marketing strategy for Grand Theft Auto VI, building on comments from Strauss Zelnick about a 'very different marketing approach.' The theory suggests that Rockstar Games could utilize the in-game social media platforms, which are prominently featured in the GTA 6 trailers, as a direct marketing tool. Instead of traditional ads, the marketing could consist of actual in-game social media posts from characters like Jason, Lucia, Cal, and Real Dimez. These posts could include 'day in the life' videos or clips showcasing 'crazy things in Vice City,' all rendered with in-engine footage.

The proponent argues this method would cleverly mirror real-world social media trends, providing a constant stream of authentic content while subtly teasing game elements. The speculation extends to the possibility of an inevitable mobile companion app, suggesting it could serve as the platform for these in-game social media posts, further blurring the lines between game and reality.
