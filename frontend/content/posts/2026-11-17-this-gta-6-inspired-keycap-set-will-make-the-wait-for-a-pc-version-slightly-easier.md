# This GTA 6-Inspired Keycap Set Will Make The Wait For A PC Version Slightly Easier

Grand Theft Auto 6 is on the cusp of release for Xbox and PlayStation players, though PC enthusiasts face an ongoing wait for their version. While Rockstar Games has yet to confirm a PC release date, a recent report hints at a possible early 2027 arrival. In the interim, high-end keycap manufacturer GMK has introduced a new custom keycap set designed to celebrate the highly anticipated game.

Dubbed "Neon Vice," the set draws clear inspiration from GTA 6 and its Vice City setting, albeit without explicit branding. It features sleek black keycaps complemented by vibrant pink, orange, and purple alphas and numerical keys, mirroring the aesthetic of Rockstar's upcoming title. The collection also includes unique novelty keycaps showcasing iconic Vice City imagery, such as speedboats on choppy water, palm trees, alligators, and the city's distinctive skyline.

Crafted from double-shot ABS plastic, these keycaps promise excellent legibility and durability over time. However, quality comes at a premium: the base kit is priced at $170, with an additional $45 for the novelty keys. Preorders for the Neon Vice set are now open globally. For those not yet immersed in the custom keyboard hobby, the price point might be a significant consideration, especially compared to the game itself. Meanwhile, console players can already pre-load GTA 6 ahead of its imminent launch.
