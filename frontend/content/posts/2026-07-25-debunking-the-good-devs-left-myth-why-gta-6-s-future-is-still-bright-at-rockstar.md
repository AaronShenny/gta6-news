# Debunking the 'Good Devs Left' Myth: Why GTA 6's Future is Still Bright at Rockstar

In a recent Reddit post, a user addresses the persistent misinformation circulating within the gaming community that 'good developers have left' Rockstar Games, implying a negative impact on the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6. The author challenges this simplistic narrative by highlighting that key creative and leadership figures, including Aaron Garbut, Rob Nelson (who led Rockstar North during the development of Red Dead Redemption 2), and Sam Houser, remain integral to the studio. The post also points to the less successful ventures of some former key personnel post-Rockstar. Furthermore, the article emphasizes the immense complexity and difficulty of developing a game on the scale of GTA, noting that it requires hundreds, if not thousands, of voice actors and extensive resources. It suggests that if creating such a game were easy, other companies would have already replicated Rockstar's unique success. The piece urges gamers to delve deeper into understanding the intricacies of game development rather than relying on superficial criticisms and spreading doubt.

## Key Takeaways
- Claims of 'good developers leaving' Rockstar affecting GTA 6 are largely misinformation.
- Key figures like Aaron Garbut, Rob Nelson (R* North head during RDR2), and Sam Houser are still at Rockstar.
- Former key developers have not necessarily replicated their past success independently.
- Developing a game like GTA is an incredibly complex undertaking, requiring vast resources and talent.
- Gamers should educate themselves on game development processes before spreading doubt.
