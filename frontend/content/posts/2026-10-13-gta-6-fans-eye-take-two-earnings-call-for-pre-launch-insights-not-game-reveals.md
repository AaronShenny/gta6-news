# GTA 6 Fans Eye Take-Two Earnings Call for Pre-Launch Insights, Not Game Reveals

The gaming world is buzzing, not just for the highly anticipated November 19 launch of Grand Theft Auto VI, but also for Take-Two Interactive's upcoming quarterly earnings briefing on November 9. This call, scheduled for 1:30 PM PT / 4:30 PM ET, will see management discuss fiscal results and address questions, particularly concerning the imminent release of GTA 6.

While analysts and investors are expected to probe for insights into the game's commercial prospects and pre-launch performance, fans should temper expectations for new gameplay details or reveals. Historically, such information is disseminated directly by developer Rockstar Games, not during investor calls.

Early indicators suggest GTA 6 is poised for monumental success. Sensor Tower data reportedly shows over 6 million pre-purchases, generating nearly $600 million in revenue already. Projections indicate preorders could hit 25 million, potentially amassing over $2 billion before the game even launches, dwarfing GTA V's impressive initial sales figures of $800 million in 24 hours and $1 billion in three days.

Investors are also looking ahead to the eventual "next evolution of GTA Online" within the GTA 6 ecosystem, despite Rockstar having yet to announce details for an online component, initially describing GTA 6 as a single-player experience. Given the massive profitability of the current GTA Online, an eventual online mode for GTA 6 is considered a certainty.

Beyond GTA 6, the earnings report will cover Take-Two's diverse portfolio, though GTA 6's launch is expected to significantly boost the franchise's contribution to the company's overall revenue, which typically hovers under 15%. GTA 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
