# KFC Spain Offers Disc-Shaped Chicken for GTA 6 Box Owners Amidst Digital-Only Release

In a quirky response to the digital-only nature of Grand Theft Auto 6's physical release, KFC Spain has announced a "Replace the Disc" campaign. Starting December 19 and 20, from 4 PM to 8 PM, customers in Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, and other Spanish cities can bring their GTA 6 game box to participating KFC locations and receive a free, disc-shaped piece of fried chicken. This promotion addresses player disappointment over the lack of a physical game disc, as GTA 6 boxes only contain a digital download code.

Take-Two, the owner of GTA 6, recently reported that 98% of its game sales are digital, indicating a significant shift in the industry, though they acknowledge a small physical footprint will likely remain. This isn't KFC's only GTA 6-related stunt; KFC Australia previously held a fake funeral to give players a "legitimate" reason to take a day off for the game's launch. Numerous other brands, including McDonald's with its delivery service and regional breweries, are also leveraging the immense popularity of GTA 6 for their own marketing campaigns. GTA 6 officially launched on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
