# GTA 6 Anticipation Fuels PS5 Dominance as Xbox Sales Suffer Record September Low

September witnessed a significant surge in overall video game spending, yet Xbox faced a challenging month. According to new Circana data, Xbox Series hardware sales plummeted by 27% year-over-year, marking an all-time low for September. In stark contrast, PlayStation 5 sales soared, growing 22% in units and 43% in dollar sales. This surge was notably influenced by the rapid sell-out of PS5 Pro stock, presumably ahead of the highly anticipated November 19 launch of Grand Theft Auto 6, a title Sony has been heavily promoting.

Nintendo's Switch 2 also saw a 12% year-on-year sales decline, though it continues to be Nintendo's fastest-selling system, comfortably outpacing the original Switch.

Xbox's sales decline is attributed to its multiplatform strategy, strong focus on cloud gaming, and the 'This Is An Xbox' campaign, which collectively de-emphasized the necessity of owning an Xbox console. However, Microsoft is now actively working to reverse this trend under new Xbox CEO Asha Sharma. The company is refocusing on console exclusives, quietly discontinuing the 'This Is An Xbox' campaign, planning upgrades for the Series X|S consoles, and developing the ambitious 'Project Helix' hardware. This strategic pivot aims to recapture its former market glory by returning to a more console-centric approach.

## Key Takeaways
- Xbox Series hardware sales fell 27% year-over-year in September, reaching an all-time low for the month.
- PlayStation 5 sales surged 22% in units and 43% in dollar sales, driven partly by strong GTA 6 anticipation and PS5 Pro stock selling out.
- Nintendo Switch 2 sales declined 12% year-on-year but remains Nintendo's fastest-selling system.
- Xbox's previous multiplatform and cloud-focused strategy contributed to its console sales decline.
- Microsoft is now shifting its strategy to prioritize console exclusives, ending the 'This Is An Xbox' campaign, and planning new hardware (Project Helix) to revive console sales.
- Sony's consistent console marketing and heavy promotion of Grand Theft Auto 6 are significant factors in its current market dominance.
