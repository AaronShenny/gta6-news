# GTA 6: Will Story Mode Suffer the Same Fate as GTA 5's Single-Player DLC?

A concerned Grand Theft Auto fan has voiced a common sentiment within the gaming community, expressing a strong hope that Rockstar Games will not repeat its perceived abandonment of single-player story content for *Grand Theft Auto 6*. The user, writing on Reddit, highlights the disappointment surrounding the canceled single-player DLC for *GTA 5* once *GTA Online* gained prominence.

The core of the complaint centers on the differing experiences of single-player versus online gaming. The user prefers the 'chill' and explorative nature of story modes, allowing them to enjoy the game world at their own pace. In contrast, they describe online games, specifically *GTA Online*, as feeling like a 'second job' that often pushes players towards microtransactions to bypass extensive grinding.

Drawing a parallel to the beloved *GTA 4* DLCs, the user questions whether Rockstar will heed player feedback this time around and provide substantial future story content or expansions for *GTA 6*, or if the online component will once again overshadow and consume all development resources for post-launch content.

## Key Takeaways
- A fan expresses concern that Rockstar will neglect GTA 6's story mode content, similar to GTA 5.
- The user prefers the 'chill' and explorative nature of single-player experiences.
- Online games like GTA Online are criticized for feeling like a 'second job' and pushing microtransactions.
- The cancellation of GTA 5's single-player DLC after GTA Online's success is a major point of contention.
- The user hopes Rockstar will prioritize future story content for GTA 6, referencing the quality of GTA 4's DLCs.
