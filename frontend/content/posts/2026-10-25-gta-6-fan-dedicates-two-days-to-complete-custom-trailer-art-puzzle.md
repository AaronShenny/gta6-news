# GTA 6 Fan Dedicates Two Days to Complete Custom Trailer Art Puzzle

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto VI enthusiast, known on Reddit as u/noahvhang, has successfully completed a custom-made puzzle featuring artwork from the highly anticipated game.
