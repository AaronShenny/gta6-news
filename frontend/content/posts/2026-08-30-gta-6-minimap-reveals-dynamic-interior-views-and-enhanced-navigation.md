# GTA 6 Minimap Reveals Dynamic Interior Views and Enhanced Navigation

Recent observations from what appears to be GTA 6 footage reveal an intriguing new minimap functionality that could significantly change how players navigate Vice City's sprawling environments. This innovative design provides a practical use for the 3D building models on the map, allowing players to plan their movements within complex structures more effectively without needing to pause or enter a separate map screen. Furthermore, buildings with accessible interiors are reportedly highlighted with a distinct 'blue-ish' tint on the minimap, differentiating them from the standard light grey of non-interactive structures. This subtle visual cue makes it easier for players to identify and locate areas of interest, suggesting a more intuitive and immersive navigation experience in the upcoming title.
