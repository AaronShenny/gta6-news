# GTA 6 File Size Revealed, And It’s Massive

Reports from PlayStation Game Size indicate that the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6 will demand a colossal 210GB of storage space for its standard edition on PlayStation 5. While Xbox Series X|S and Ultimate Edition sizes are yet to be confirmed, they are expected to be similar. This massive requirement, double that of Red Dead Redemption II, necessitates preloading, which is set to begin on November 12. The enormous file size is attributed to a map reportedly three times larger than RDR2's and a high level of detail, akin to RDR2's dynamic elements. GTA 6 is slated for release on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with expectations of becoming the biggest game ever, potentially generating $2 billion in revenue from 25 million preorders. Initially a strictly single-player experience, a new GTA Online is anticipated, and a PC version is rumored for early 2027.

## Key Takeaways
