# Hunger Games Director Jests About GTA 6 Clash: 'Play GTA, Go See Our Movie, Play More GTA'

The highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to launch on November 19, creating a massive ripple effect across the entertainment industry. Just one day later, on November 20, the new Hunger Games movie, *Sunrise on the Reaping*, will hit theaters. Director Francis Lawrence, addressing the direct clash, humorously remarked to IGN, "Hopefully, people find time for both. Play GTA, go see our movie, play more GTA."Other movies releasing on November 20 include Paper Tiger and I Play Rocky. This scheduling conflict highlights a broader trend: publishers are intentionally avoiding GTA 6's launch window, with no other major game releases planned five days on either side. Circana's Mat Piscatella noted that "everyone's just scared" of releasing near GTA 6, though he personally believes this fear is "completely overblown" and more irrational than rational. The immense anticipation for GTA 6 is backed by data, as nearly 1 in 3 US console and PC gamers plan to purchase the game, marking the highest purchase intent ever recorded by Circana. In a related note, Lawrence also shared his favorite video games, including Metal Gear Solid, BioShock, Call of Duty, and the original GTA, confirming his attachment to direct the upcoming Netflix BioShock movie.
