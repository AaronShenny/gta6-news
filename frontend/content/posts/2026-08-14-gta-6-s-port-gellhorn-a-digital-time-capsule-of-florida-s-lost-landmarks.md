# GTA 6's Port Gellhorn: A Digital Time Capsule of Florida's Lost Landmarks

Grand Theft Auto 6's highly anticipated setting, Port Gellhorn, is poised to offer players a unique glimpse into a bygone era of the Florida Panhandle. Recent analysis reveals that many of the real-world structures scouted by Rockstar Games in Panama City, Florida, back in 2015, no longer exist in their original form.The devastating impact of Hurricane Michael in 2018 led to the destruction of over 45,000 structures in Panama City, causing immense losses. Consequently, locations meticulously recreated for GTA 6 will inadvertently serve as a digital time capsule. A prime example is the Crossroad Park Minimall, prominently featured in Trailer 2. Its real-life counterpart, the Callaway Village Square mall near Panama City, was severely damaged by Hurricane Michael and subsequently demolished, replaced by a CEFCO gas station years later.This means that as players explore Port Gellhorn, they will be traversing a meticulously rendered representation of mid-2010s Florida, preserving architectural and environmental details that have since been altered or erased from the real-world landscape. This adds an unexpected layer of historical preservation to the game's immersive world-building.

## Key Takeaways
