# GTA 6 Leaks Hint at More Realistic In-Game Economy and Pricing

Recent leaks concerning Grand Theft Auto 6 have provided a glimpse into its potential in-game economy, suggesting a significant shift towards more realistic pricing compared to its predecessor. Unlike GTA 5's often exaggerated costs, the leaked figures indicate a more grounded approach to everyday expenses and item purchases. Examples from the leaks include gas priced at $32, vehicle repairs at $2,429, earbuds for $119, and headphones for $199. Interestingly, payouts from gas station robberies were also noted, ranging from $1,321 to $1,442. This newfound realism has led to speculation among fans about the possibility of a more complex financial system, such as a live stock market or a prediction market, similar to a mission feature seen in GTA 5. The community is abuzz with the potential implications this could have on gameplay and immersion.
