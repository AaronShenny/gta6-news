# GTA 6 Will Not Stream To PC Through Xbox, Despite Earlier Report

PC players hoping to get their hands on Grand Theft Auto 6 at launch will be disappointed, as Xbox chief strategy officer Matthew Ball has clarified that the highly anticipated game will **not** be available to stream to PC via Xbox Cloud Gaming. This statement directly refutes an earlier report from The Verge, which claimed Xbox had secured exclusive game-streaming rights for GTA 6.

Ball's full statement confirmed that while there's great excitement for GTA 6 and Xbox, previous reports about exclusive PC streaming were inaccurate. This means that on November 19, the official launch date for consoles, PC players will not be able to access Vice City through Microsoft's cloud gaming service.

The initial confusion stemmed from comments by Xbox CEO Asha Sharma, who reportedly told employees that Microsoft was preparing something unique with GTA 6. The Verge's report suggested this unique arrangement could have been a significant workaround for PC players, as Rockstar has yet to announce a PC version of GTA 6, which is officially confirmed only for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. This clarification comes amidst significant changes to Xbox Cloud Gaming, including new payment options and tier adjustments.
