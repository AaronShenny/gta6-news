# GTA 6: The Next Three Months Could Unleash an 'Insane' Marketing Blitz, Says Insider

Following Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick's intriguing remark describing the August 27th extended look for Grand Theft Auto 6 as merely 'one of the hors d’oeuvres,' the gaming community is buzzing with predictions of an 'insane' marketing period. Zelnick's additional comments about 'appetizers, main course and dessert to follow' suggest Rockstar Games is preparing a multi-stage marketing onslaught, potentially spanning September, October, and November.

Drawing parallels to the marketing strategies for GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2, fans anticipate a structured rollout:
*   **August 27:** An 'Extended Look' revealing more gameplay, mechanics, world details, and characters.
*   **September:** A ramp-up phase with new screenshots, character artwork, website updates, social media posts, and initial previews.
*   **October:** A 'big marketing push,' possibly featuring another substantial gameplay trailer or a deep dive into the world, activities, vehicles, heists, businesses, and interiors.
*   **November:** The 'final push,' which could include a final trailer, launch trailer, TV commercials, billboards, interviews, extensive previews, and even special hardware or collectibles.

Further speculation includes the possibility of a lavish GTA 6 Collector’s Edition, potentially featuring a physical map, collectible figures, Vice City-themed items, a steelbook, art book, and exclusive in-game bonuses. There's also excitement around a potential PlayStation partnership, envisioning a GTA 6-themed PS5 or PS5 Pro bundle with custom faceplates and a matching DualSense controller. Zelnick's deliberate wording has shifted perspectives, indicating that the August 27th reveal is just the beginning of what could be several months of significant GTA 6 news and reveals.
