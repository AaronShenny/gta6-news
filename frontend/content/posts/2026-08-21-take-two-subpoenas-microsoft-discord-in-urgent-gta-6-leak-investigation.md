# Take-Two Subpoenas Microsoft & Discord in Urgent GTA 6 Leak Investigation

The gaming world was recently rocked by extensive leaks of Grand Theft Auto 6 gameplay, showcasing everything from combat mechanics to in-game activities like basketball. In response, Rockstar's parent company, Take-Two Interactive, has launched a rigorous investigation into the source of these unauthorized disclosures.Take-Two has **subpoenaed both Microsoft and Discord**, seeking to unmask the individual(s) responsible for sharing this "copyrighted material." This legal action effectively **confirms the legitimacy of the leaked footage**, dispelling any notions of it being an elaborate hoax. The leaker, known online as **CyberLeek**, not only distributed gameplay clips but also reportedly advertised cryptocurrency, and some evidence suggests they may even possess a playable build of the highly anticipated game.The urgency of Take-Two's investigation is clear, with Microsoft given a deadline of September 4 to provide crucial information. The subpoena demands extensive user data, including **account IDs, email addresses, IP addresses, phone numbers, and linked Xbox and Google profiles** for Discord users communicating on specific servers related to the leaks.These significant leaks occurred just prior to Rockstar's planned "GTA 6 Extended Look" reveal, which is set to air exclusively on Netflix on August 27. Grand Theft Auto 6 is slated for release on **November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S**.
