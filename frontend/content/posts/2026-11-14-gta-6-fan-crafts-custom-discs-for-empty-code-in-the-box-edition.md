# GTA 6 Fan Crafts Custom Discs for Empty "Code in the Box" Edition

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto 6 enthusiast, known as /u/Harperrino on Reddit, took creativity into their own hands to enhance their "Code in the Box" edition of the highly anticipated game. Feeling the physical case felt "a little empty" without actual discs, they designed and crafted custom GTA VI discs. These fan-made discs are purely for display and collection purposes, not functional game discs, but the creator expressed satisfaction with the final result, adding a unique touch to their GTA 6 memorabilia. The project highlights the passion and anticipation within the GTA community.
