# GTA 6 Fan Questions Pistol Grip Visibility: Will Customizations Be Hidden?

A passionate Grand Theft Auto 6 fan has voiced a unique concern regarding the visibility of custom pistol grips, fearing that a feature prominent in Red Dead Redemption 2 and hinted at in GTA 6's promotional material might be absent from regular gameplay. The author highlights three main points of contention:

1.  **RDR2 Precedent & GTA 6 Artwork:** Custom pistol grips were a significant visual and reward element in RDR2, with characters like Micah having iconic designs. The artwork for GTA 6's Ultimate Edition suggests an even more elaborate return of this design philosophy, with gorgeous grips seemingly designed for visibility.
2.  **Promo Art Inconsistency:** Jason is conspicuously shown with a waist-holstered sidearm in promo art, a detail Rockstar typically doesn't showcase if it's not present in actual gameplay.
3.  **Lack of In-Game Evidence:** Despite the above, official trailers, previews, and screenshots offer almost no evidence of visible sidearms or their custom grips on protagonists during gameplay. One NPC is seen with a visible waist-tucked sidearm, but the author notes the complexities of implementing this for player characters, especially Lucia with her smaller frame and extensive clothing options. The author speculates a 'whatever Jason can do, so must Lucia' rule might complicate the feature's inclusion for both.

While expressing immense excitement for GTA 6, the fan describes this as the 'one and only thing' that has been disappointing so far, considering the strong indications from RDR2 and GTA 6's own promotional imagery.

## Key Takeaways
- Custom pistol grips were a significant visual feature in RDR2, and GTA 6 Ultimate Edition artwork suggests a similar design philosophy.
- Jason is prominently displayed with a waist-holstered sidearm in GTA 6 promo art, which usually indicates a gameplay feature.
- Current GTA 6 trailers, previews, and screenshots provide almost no evidence of visible sidearms or custom grips on protagonists during gameplay.
- The technical challenges of implementing visible sidearms for both Jason and Lucia, particularly Lucia's smaller body size and diverse clothing options, are a potential factor.
- The author expresses disappointment over this potential omission, despite being incredibly excited for the game overall.

## FAQ
**What is the main concern about pistol grips in GTA 6?**
The main concern is that custom pistol grips, a detailed visual feature, might not be visibly displayed on protagonists during regular gameplay, despite being prominent in RDR2 and hinted at in GTA 6 promo art.

**Why does the author believe custom grips should be visible?**
The author points to Red Dead Redemption 2's detailed and visible custom grip system and the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition artwork, which heavily features elaborate grip designs. Additionally, Jason is shown with a visible waist-holstered sidearm in promo art, suggesting it's a feature.

**What evidence suggests visible grips might be absent from gameplay?**
Trailers, previews, and screenshots released so far show very little to no evidence of protagonists having visibly holstered sidearms or custom grips during gameplay, except for a single NPC.

**What are the potential challenges for implementing visible grips?**
Implementing visible sidearms and custom grips for two protagonists with diverse body types and extensive clothing options, especially Lucia's smaller frame, could pose significant technical and design challenges for Rockstar.
