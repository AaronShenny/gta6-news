# Hacker Group 'Cyberleek' Claims Responsibility for GTA 6 Leak Over Digital-Only Release Concerns

A new report circulating within the gaming community suggests that a hacker group identifying themselves as 'Cyberleek' has taken responsibility for the significant Grand Theft Auto 6 leaks that have recently surfaced. The group's alleged motive for this breach is a protest against Rockstar Games' supposed decision to release GTA 6 exclusively as a digital title, foregoing traditional physical disc releases. This claim, originating from a Reddit post on the r/GTA6 subreddit, has sparked considerable discussion among fans regarding the future of physical media in gaming and the security surrounding highly anticipated titles like GTA 6.
