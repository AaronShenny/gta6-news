# Rockstar Unveils GTA 6 Collector's Edition: $400 for Swag, No Game Included

Rockstar Games has officially announced a premium $400 collector's edition for the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6, dubbed 'The Goodtime State: Vice City Collection'. Crucially, this elaborate bundle *does not* include the game itself, a detail that has already sparked significant discussion among fans. Inspired by Leonida’s fictional hit TV show, Macca the Gator, the collection is available for preorder now and is set to launch on November 19, coinciding with GTA 6's release.

The collection boasts an impressive array of 11 physical items, meticulously crafted to immerse fans in the world of Vice City. Highlights include a six-inch Macca the Gator figure with a stash compartment, matte black GTA 6 Oakley Frogskins sunglasses with Prizm Sapphire lenses, a New Era 9FORTY A-Frame snapback hat, and a variety of themed collectibles such as a Vice City swizzle spoon, razor blade keychain, and a Leonida Keys crossbody bag. A double-sided souvenir poster showcasing the full Leonida landscape also features.

Initial online reactions to the $400 price tag have been largely critical. However, industry observers and fans alike anticipate a rapid sell-out, driven in part by a robust scalper market, much like the recent GTA 6 DualSense controllers. This trend of selling collector's editions without the accompanying game is not unprecedented, with titles like Marathon, Diablo 4, and Borderlands 4 having adopted similar strategies. For publishers such as Take-Two Interactive, this approach helps mitigate the risk of preorder cancellations for the main game, especially given that GTA 6 has already reportedly generated over $400 million in preorders alone.
