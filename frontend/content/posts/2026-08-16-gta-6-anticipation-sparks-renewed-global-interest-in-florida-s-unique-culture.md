# GTA 6 Anticipation Sparks Renewed Global Interest in Florida's Unique Culture

A Reddit user, OldSchoolGaming_YT from Sweden, shares how their excitement for the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6 has sparked a significant new interest in the culture, history, and landscape of Florida and Miami. Despite having limited prior exposure to the region beyond iconic titles like 'Miami Vice' and 'Scarface', the user has actively sought out and consumed various TV series and movies set in the area, including 'Florida Man', 'R.J. Decker', 'Bad Monkey', and 'Ballers'. The user expresses a curiosity about whether other fans are similarly immersing themselves in Florida-centric media in anticipation of GTA 6's setting. They also solicit recommendations for further streaming content. While acknowledging the common perception of Florida as a retirement haven, the author personally finds the area appealing, citing its climate, Cuban-influenced food, abundant water, boats, and beaches. This positive outlook is partly attributed to Sweden's long winters, making the vibrant, sunny portrayal of Florida in media particularly enchanting.
