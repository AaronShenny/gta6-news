# Take-Two CEO: Physical Games Won't Vanish, But GTA 6 Leads the Digital-Only Charge

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick asserts that while the physical video game market is shrinking, it will never entirely disappear, comparing its future to the niche but growing vinyl music business. He anticipates physical media will remain a "very small part of the business," even with industry shifts like Sony discontinuing PlayStation discs by 2028. For Take-Two, digital sales already constitute 98% of their business, driven by the increased profitability for publishers.

Notably, *Grand Theft Auto 6*, one of the most anticipated game releases, will embrace this digital-first approach. Rockstar Games will not offer *GTA 6* on a physical disc. Instead, it will be available in stores as a "code-in-a-box" version, requiring a digital download. Even the $400 collector's edition of *GTA 6* includes numerous physical collectibles but *not* the game itself, which remains a digital-only experience. However, the game's original score will be released on CD and vinyl.

*GTA 6* is scheduled to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a PC edition and online multiplayer rumored for a 2027 release.
