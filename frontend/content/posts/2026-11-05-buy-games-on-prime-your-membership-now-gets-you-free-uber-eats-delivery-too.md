# Buy Games On Prime? Your Membership Now Gets You Free Uber Eats Delivery, Too

As an extra incentive, Uber Eats is offering $20 off your first two qualifying orders until November 18. Detailed instructions are provided for Prime members to effortlessly link their accounts and start enjoying this delicious new benefit.
