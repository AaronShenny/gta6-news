# GTA 6 Mapping Project Joins Forces with GTA Wiki, Final Release Nears

The **GTA 6 Mapping Project** has announced a significant development: it is now officially **co-owned by GTA Wiki**. This partnership marks a new chapter for the community-driven initiative, which aims to meticulously map out the world of the upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI.

According to the announcement, the **final version of the Mapping Project is slated for release sometime in November**, preceding the highly anticipated launch of GTA 6 itself. Following this initial release, the project's focus will transition to a crucial phase: identifying and documenting all real-life counterparts of the locations featured within GTA 6. This extensive work will then be used to update and enrich GTA Wiki and its associated databases, including [stateofleonida.net](http://stateofleonida.net) and [gtadb.org](https://map.gtadb.org/#VI), providing fans with comprehensive geographical information.

In a move to foster community engagement and fairness, the announcement also includes an appeal process for individuals who were previously banned from the GTA VI Mapping Discord server prior to the change in ownership. Those seeking an appeal are encouraged to reach out to any of the GTA Wiki's official social media accounts.

## Key Takeaways
- The GTA 6 Mapping Project is now co-owned by GTA Wiki.
- The final version of the Mapping Project will be released in November, before GTA 6's launch.
- Post-release, the project will focus on identifying real-life GTA 6 locations.
- Location data will be used to update GTA Wiki, Stateofleonida.net, and GTADB.org.
- An appeal process is available for users banned from the Discord server before the ownership change.
