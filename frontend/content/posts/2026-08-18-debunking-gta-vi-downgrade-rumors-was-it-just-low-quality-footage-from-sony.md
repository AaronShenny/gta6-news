# Debunking GTA VI Downgrade Rumors: Was It Just Low-Quality Footage from Sony?

Recent discussions regarding a potential 'downgrade' in Grand Theft Auto VI's visual quality have been challenged by a Reddit user, chud-69. The user posits that the seemingly 'washed out' and lower quality appearance of the GTA VI footage, along with that of the 007 game shown, was not indicative of a game downgrade but rather a result of Sony uploading the footage in a lower quality format. The user provided an adjusted image, claiming that after increasing saturation and sharpness, the footage looked 'exactly the same' as expected high-quality visuals, implying the original upload was simply sub-optimal. The only game that appeared to maintain normal quality in the same presentation was Wolverine.
