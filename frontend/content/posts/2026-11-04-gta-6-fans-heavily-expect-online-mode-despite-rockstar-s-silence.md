# GTA 6 Fans Heavily Expect Online Mode Despite Rockstar's Silence

A recent survey conducted by Piper Sandler among 9,000 teenagers reveals a strong expectation for an online mode in Grand Theft Auto 6, with 90% expressing intent to play 'GTA Online.' This comes despite Rockstar Games' current stance, as product listings explicitly state GTA 6 will be a 'single-player experience,' with no official word yet on an online component.

The survey also highlighted significant purchase intent, with 70% of PS5 and Xbox Series X|S owners planning to buy GTA 6, and 55% of those intending to purchase it immediately upon release. An additional 40% of respondents anticipated making in-game purchases. These findings align with Circana's report of the highest-ever measured purchase intent for a game, with nearly 1 in 3 console and PC gamers in the US planning to acquire GTA 6.

GTA 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preloading beginning November 10. The game's release has also sparked discussions among analysts, with Take-Two's stock receiving a $290 price target from James Callahan. The future of the existing GTA Online and how Rockstar will address the demand for an online mode in GTA 6 remains a key point of speculation and potential contention among the fanbase.
