# Fan Hopes GTA 6 Embraces Experimental Radio Stations for Unique Music Discovery

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto fan has shared their hopes for the upcoming GTA 6, specifically regarding its in-game radio stations. Drawing inspiration from the critically acclaimed 'The Journey' station in GTA 4, the user, u/Ivaylo_87, expressed how such unconventional stations introduced them to artists like Philip Glass and Tangerine Dream, broadening their musical horizons. The sentiment is that GTA 6 should continue this tradition by featuring more unique and experimental music channels, allowing a wider audience to discover diverse sounds from around the world. This would enhance the game's atmosphere and provide a valuable platform for musical exploration beyond mainstream genres.
