# Fan Recreates Iconic GTA Vice City Hotel in Blender Amidst GTA 6 Hype

In a remarkable display of fan dedication and anticipation for the upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI, Reddit user /u/realthomascat has meticulously recreated the iconic Ocean View Hotel from the beloved Grand Theft Auto: Vice City using Blender. This elaborate fan project was directly inspired by the widespread expectation and rumors that GTA 6 will bring players back to a modern rendition of Vice City, a setting heavily reminiscent of Miami. The creator stated they thought it would be fun to revisit one of the most memorable locations from Vice City, given the strong possibility of its return. This artistic endeavor serves as a nostalgic tribute to one of the most recognizable landmarks in the GTA series, showcasing the community's excitement and creativity as they eagerly await official details about the highly anticipated sequel.
