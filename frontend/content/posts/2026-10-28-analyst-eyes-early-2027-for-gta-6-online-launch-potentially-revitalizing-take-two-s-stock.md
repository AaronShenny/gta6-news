# Analyst Eyes Early 2027 for GTA 6 Online Launch, Potentially Revitalizing Take-Two's Stock

Despite the highly anticipated launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 in November, Take-Two Interactive's share price has seen a significant 21% decline this year. However, a major financial institution, Bank of America, sees substantial upside, largely due to the expected release of a new iteration of GTA Online.

According to a memo seen by Business Insider, Bank of America recommends investors remain 'long' on Take-Two stock, believing it 'remains cheap.' The bank specifically highlights GTA Online 2.0 as a potential game-changer, forecasting its launch in the first calendar quarter of 2027 (January-March). They argue that investors are currently underestimating the immense revenue-generating potential of this new online component, drawing parallels to the original GTA Online's monumental success with microtransactions.

Rockstar Games has stated GTA 6 is a single-player experience, but a multiplayer component is widely expected to follow, consistent with how GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption II initially launched without their online counterparts. Furthermore, Bank of America suggests that a PC version of GTA 6 could also arrive around the same early 2027 timeframe, further boosting revenue. The bank maintains a 'buy' rating for Take-Two with a price target of $330, indicating over 60% upside from current levels.

GTA 6 is set to release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with its file size already revealed to be massive.
