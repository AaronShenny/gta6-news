# Take-Two Boss Says Ocarina Of Time Is “Terrific,” But Is It Competing With GTA 6?

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has lauded Nintendo's recent Switch 2 remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, calling it "terrific" and commending Nintendo. When questioned about its potential competition with Rockstar Games' highly anticipated GTA 6, Zelnick offered an insightful philosophy: in the entertainment business, "You compete against everything and nothing." He elaborated that "everything" refers to the multitude of choices consumers have for their time and money, while "nothing" highlights that entertainment is not a "must-have" commodity like essentials, requiring genuine appeal to compel purchase. Zelnick affirmed Take-Two's mission to "make the best entertainment on Earth" to attract audiences.

The article notes that both the Ocarina of Time remake and GTA 6 are significant releases in November 2026. Activision's marketing chief, Tyler Bahl, previously stated that GTA 6 and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 would be 2026's biggest titles over Zelda. The Ocarina of Time remake has garnered critical acclaim, currently holding the title of the highest-rated game of 2026, and its release has positively impacted Switch 2 hardware sales. GTA 6 is scheduled to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with Xbox One users able to stream the game as part of Microsoft's 10-year deal with Take-Two.
