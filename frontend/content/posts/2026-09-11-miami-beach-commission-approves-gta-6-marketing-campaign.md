# Miami Beach Commission Approves GTA 6 Marketing Campaign

In a significant step for the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6, the Miami Beach commission has officially approved the game's marketing campaign with a narrow 4-3 vote. This approval is specifically for the Miami Beach area, with plans for a separate vote concerning the downtown district at a later date. Negotiations between the city and Rockstar Games are reportedly still in progress, as Miami Beach officials are pushing for a financial agreement exceeding the initial $4 million dollar price tag. This development marks a positive forward momentum for the game's promotional efforts in key locations.

## Key Takeaways
- The Miami Beach commission has approved the GTA 6 marketing campaign.
- The vote passed with a narrow 4-3 margin.
- This approval applies specifically to Miami Beach, with a separate vote for the downtown area expected later.
- Negotiations are ongoing between the city and Rockstar Games, with Miami Beach seeking more than the initial $4 million for the deal.
