# Fan Speculates on 'Astonishing' GTA 6 Marketing Blitz Targeting Miami in October

The user, u/thatdudepabloescobar, suggests that an 'astonishing' marketing blitz could commence around mid-to-late October, aligning with the start of the NBA season. This timing, according to the post, could be linked to previous comments by Strauss Zelnick. The core of the speculation centers around leveraging Miami, the presumed setting for GTA 6. The proposed strategy includes prominent advertising at the Miami Heat Kaseya Center during NBA games, utilizing its large exterior screens and digital billboards to reach a massive audience. Furthermore, iconic Miami locations like South Beach and Ocean Drive are highlighted as prime spots for advertising. The user also suggests innovative marketing in Wynwood, Miami's vibrant street-art district, through large murals, featuring GTA 6 logos, or collaborations with local artists to generate authentic local buzz.
