# GTA 6 Leaks: Unpacking CyberLeek's Timeline of Unprecedented Revelations

The gaming world has been rocked by an unprecedented series of Grand Theft Auto 6 leaks, spearheaded by an anonymous entity known as CyberLeek. Beginning in mid-August 2026, CyberLeek unleashed a torrent of unreleased gameplay videos and apparent map images, sending shockwaves through Rockstar Games and its parent company, Take-Two Interactive. The leaks commenced around August 18 with footage of protagonist Jason Duval playing basketball, revealing a 'Focus' mechanic, and a confrontation where Jason used various items as weapons. Accompanying these initial drops was CyberLeek's 'manifesto,' a set of 'commandments' railing against digital preorders, fake single-player DLC, and the absence of physical discs for games like GTA 6.

The following day, August 19, saw further revelations, including a video showcasing Jason using a taser, a potential new weapon, and demonstrating abilities like key cloning and window smashing. Another clip, titled 'Junkies,' hinted at a return of the six-star wanted level and introduced a substance called 'Zombix,' alongside a new mechanic where bystanders could report crimes. Amidst these revelations, consumer advocacy group Stop Killing Games publicly distanced itself from CyberLeek's illegal tactics and warned against purchasing the group's associated $CYBERLEEK memecoin, which was being used to fund a 'secret project' and allow users to poll for the next leak.

By August 20, an extended gameplay video, chosen via the memecoin poll, showed Jason flying a plane over a dense city environment, scrolling through radio stations, and even using gunfire to spell 'Leek' on a wall. CyberLeek escalated its threats, vowing to release story and ending spoilers unless publishers altered their approach to digital games. The saga took a legal turn on August 21, when Take-Two filed multiple subpoenas, requesting Microsoft and Discord to assist in identifying CyberLeek by providing user information, marking a significant step in the ongoing battle to control the narrative around one of the most anticipated games in history.

## Key Takeaways
- An anonymous group called CyberLeek began leaking GTA 6 gameplay videos and map images in mid-August 2026.

- The group launched a $CYBERLEEK memecoin, using it to fund a 'secret project' and allow users to vote on which leaked content would be released next.

- Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar's parent company, responded by filing subpoenas against Microsoft and Discord to identify the leakers.

## FAQ
