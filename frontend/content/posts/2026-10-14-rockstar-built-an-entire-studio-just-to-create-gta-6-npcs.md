# Rockstar Built An Entire Studio Just To Create GTA 6 NPCs

Rockstar Games reportedly established an entire studio in Los Angeles dedicated exclusively to creating Non-Player Characters (NPCs) for *Grand Theft Auto 6*. This unprecedented move was necessitated by the game's ambitious scale and the realization that its highly interactive world would require significantly more production resources for NPCs than initially anticipated.

According to Rockstar development lead Rob Nelson, speaking to Bloomberg, the team faced "horrifying" projections for the number of NPCs needed, which were a major factor in the game's initial delay. These NPCs are designed to be far more interactive than in previous titles, with the ability to notice and react to player actions, such as carrying a weapon in public.

The new LA studio is staffed with writers, producers, and directors focused solely on NPC development, reflecting Rockstar's commitment to making Leonida's residents feel more convincing and detailed. This extensive effort, alongside other challenges like pandemic disruptions and leadership changes, has contributed to *GTA 6*'s lengthy development cycle, spanning over eight years and leading to two release date delays, now targeting November 19, 2026. Despite these delays, Rockstar maintains that the foundational work for *GTA 6* means players won't have to wait another eight years for their next title.
