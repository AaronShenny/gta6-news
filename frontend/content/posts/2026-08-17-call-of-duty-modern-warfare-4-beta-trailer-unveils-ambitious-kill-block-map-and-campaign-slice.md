# Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 Beta Trailer Unveils Ambitious 'Kill Block' Map and Campaign Slice

Activision has released a teaser trailer for the upcoming Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4's first beta weekend, showcasing a slice of the game's single-player campaign for the first time in the franchise's beta history. Players can try the campaign mission 'Entrenched' along with the highly ambitious modular multiplayer map 'Kill Block.' Set at the fictional West Bridge Advanced Military Training Facility, Kill Block dynamically reconfigures before every match, creating hundreds of potential map combinations from fan-favorite elements.

The first beta runs April 21-25 for those who pre-order on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC. While MW4 is confirmed for Nintendo Switch 2, its beta will not be available on that platform during the initial period, with pre-orders beginning later in August. A second, open beta period is scheduled for August 28-31, requiring no pre-order, and will include different multiplayer maps, the 'Ground War' mode, and a first look at Warzone's new 'Resurgence Zodiac' map.

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is set to launch on October 23 for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC, and Switch 2. This marks its return to Nintendo platforms since 2013 and the first time it skips PS4 and Xbox One. Notably, MW4 will not be a day-one release on Xbox Game Pass, a departure from previous titles. The article also mentions that GTA 6 is slated to arrive on November 19, approximately a month after MW4's release.
