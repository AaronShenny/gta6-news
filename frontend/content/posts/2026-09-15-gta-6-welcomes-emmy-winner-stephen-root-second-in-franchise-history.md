# GTA 6 Welcomes Emmy Winner Stephen Root, Second in Franchise History

In a significant development for the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6*, veteran actor Stephen Root has been officially confirmed to voice the character Brian Heder, a drug-runner in the game. This confirmation came on September 13, shortly before Root secured his first-ever Emmy Award on September 14 for his performance in the Apple TV series *Widow's Bay*.

Root's Emmy win for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Comedy Series makes him a notable addition to the GTA franchise, becoming only the second Emmy-winning actor to feature in a *Grand Theft Auto* title. The first was James Woods, known for his role as Mike Toreno in *GTA: San Andreas* (2004), who boasts multiple Emmy wins.

While Rockstar Games has yet to make official casting announcements for *GTA 6*, Root's involvement was identified by observant fans who recognized his voice as Brian Heder, a character featured in promotional material. Root subsequently confirmed his role. The identities of the game's lead characters, Lucia and Jason, and other main cast members, remain undisclosed.

The article also briefly notes that Prime Video's *Fallout* TV series secured two Emmy awards for Season 2, specifically for Outstanding Fantasy/Sci-Fi Costumes and Outstanding Production Design.

## Key Takeaways
- Stephen Root is confirmed to voice Brian Heder in GTA 6.
- Root won his first Emmy for "Widow's Bay" on September 14, making him an Emmy-winning actor.
- He is the second Emmy-winning actor to appear in the GTA franchise, following James Woods (Mike Toreno in GTA: San Andreas).
- Root's role in GTA 6 was confirmed by himself after fan recognition, preceding official Rockstar announcements.
