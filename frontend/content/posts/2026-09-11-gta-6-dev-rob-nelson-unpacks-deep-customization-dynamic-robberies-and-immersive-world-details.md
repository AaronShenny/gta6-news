# GTA 6 Dev Rob Nelson Unpacks Deep Customization, Dynamic Robberies, and Immersive World Details

In a recent Famitsu interview, translated by gtavice.net, Rockstar's Rob Nelson shed new light on various highly anticipated features of Grand Theft Auto VI, offering confirmed details that promise to elevate the player experience. Nelson confirmed an unprecedented level of character customization, stating there will be 'thousands of outfits' and more options than ever before, obtainable through missions or purchases.

While the universes are separate, Nelson playfully hinted at 'slightly clever ways' of looking back at previous titles and confirmed with a laugh that Easter eggs or connections to past GTA games, including GTA: Vice City, 'might be' present. This follows a mention of Tommy Vercetti's name appearing in the 'Vintage Vice City Pack' pre-order bonus description.

The protagonists, Lucia and Jason, are designed for deep player empathy, each carrying 'emotional baggage' but offering 'room for the player to empathize' and form a 'deeper attachment.'

Gameplay mechanics receive significant upgrades. Store robberies will be dynamic, allowing players to choose between a loud, door-kicking approach that triggers alarms, or a stealthy infiltration using tools like a snap gun. Destroying security cameras and disabling security systems will enable silent cash theft, potentially leaving no trace. A new combat feature, 'suppressive fire,' allows players to deliberately miss targets to frighten or confuse them, providing a non-lethal option to escape without harming police or civilians.

Weapon handling and customization are also expanded, with Ammu-Nation offering 'special guns exclusive to particular stores' and 'more weapon customization options than ever.' Players will have flexible weapon storage, not only in their personal vehicle's trunk but also in the trunk of 'any car,' and discarded weapons won't be permanently lost as they're always stored in the personal vehicle or Ammu-Nation armory.

Carjacking interactions will be more nuanced; players might simply shout at an NPC to exit a vehicle, but sometimes this won't work, and the NPC might even fight back. The world itself promises unparalleled immersion, 'far surpass[ing] our previous titles' in scale and depth, with varied places, interactions, and approaches deepening the player's connection. This immersion extends to NPCs, who will exhibit a 'huge variety in character height and body shape,' making the world feel more individual.

Finally, a physical training system will be integrated, requiring players to manage stamina during workouts. Completing daily sets and training for three consecutive days will level up the protagonist's health and strength, with activities like pull-ups and post-workout protein shakes contributing to character progression.

## FAQ
**How extensive is character customization in GTA 6?**
There will be thousands of outfits and more customization options than ever before, obtainable through missions or purchases.

**Will there be Easter eggs or connections to previous GTA games?**
Rob Nelson hinted there 'might be (laughs)' and that Rockstar likes 'looking back at previous titles in slightly clever ways,' suggesting subtle nods to past games, including Vice City.

**What's new about store robberies?**
Players can choose between loud or stealthy approaches. Stealth involves using tools like a snap gun, destroying security cameras, and disabling alarms to quietly steal cash without triggering alerts.

**Is there a new combat mechanic to avoid killing?**
Yes, 'suppressive fire' allows players to deliberately miss enemies to frighten or confuse them, buying time to escape without lethal force against police or civilians.

**How does weapon storage work in GTA 6?**
Guns can be stored in your personal vehicle's trunk, the trunk of any car, or the Ammu-Nation armory. Discarding a weapon on the spot will not result in its permanent loss.

**Can I train my character in GTA 6?**
Yes, a physical training system involves daily exercises where stamina decreases. Completing sets and training for three consecutive days will level up the protagonist's health and strength, with activities like pull-ups and protein consumption.
