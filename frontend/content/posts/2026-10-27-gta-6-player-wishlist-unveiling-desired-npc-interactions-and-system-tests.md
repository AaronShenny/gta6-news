# GTA 6 Player Wishlist: Unveiling Desired NPC Interactions and System Tests

A recent Reddit thread sparked a discussion among GTA 6 enthusiasts, highlighting various scenarios players are eager to test regarding NPC behavior and game mechanics. The user /u/d0cv compiled a list of intriguing experiments they plan to conduct upon the game's release, showcasing a desire for deep, dynamic, and realistic world interactions.

Key areas of interest include:

*   **NPC Car Theft Reactions**: Testing how NPCs react to their car being stolen, especially if the thief remains visible or switches characters.
*   **Relationship Impact**: Investigating how in-game actions, like picking up a prostitute, might affect character relationships, specifically with Lucia.
*   **Environmental Interactions**: Exploring the possibility of easier store robberies when establishments are closed.
*   **Crime and Witness Systems**: Assessing the efficiency of witness reporting and police response time after a rooftop sniping incident.
*   **Gang Warfare Dynamics**: Attempting to instigate large-scale shootouts by luring rival gangs into each other's territory.
*   **Open-World Exploration**: Determining if it's possible to break into mansions in affluent neighborhoods, suggesting a desire for more interactive and permeable environments.

These proposed tests underscore the community's high expectations for GTA 6's realism, complex AI, and emergent gameplay possibilities, pushing the boundaries of what's been seen in previous titles.
