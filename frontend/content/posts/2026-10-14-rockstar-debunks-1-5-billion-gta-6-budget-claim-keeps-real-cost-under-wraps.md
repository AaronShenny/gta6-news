# Rockstar Debunks $1.5 Billion GTA 6 Budget Claim, Keeps Real Cost Under Wraps

Rumors have swirled that Grand Theft Auto VI is the most expensive video game ever made, with some reports citing a staggering $1.5 billion development cost. However, Rockstar Games has directly addressed these claims in a statement to Bloomberg, denying the $1.5 billion figure. Despite debunking this specific number, the developer has opted not to reveal the true investment required to bring GTA 6 to its November release.

Given the years of development, involvement of multiple studios (including one dedicated solely to NPCs), and a vast team of employees, the final price tag is undoubtedly substantial. Nevertheless, industry experts anticipate GTA 6 will quickly recoup its expenses and become a massive profit generator for Rockstar and parent company Take-Two Interactive. The game has already seen impressive financial success, reportedly securing over 5 million pre-orders and generating more than half a billion dollars. Projections suggest this could escalate to 25 million pre-orders and $2 billion in revenue by launch, though Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick cautions that pre-order figures aren't guaranteed sales.

Further bolstering its revenue streams, the $400 GTA 6 collector's edition, a merchandise-only package, has also proven popular. Looking ahead, an online multiplayer component for GTA 6 is rumored for a 2027 launch. While GTA Online consistently brings in hundreds of millions annually, some, like Valve veteran Chet Faliszek, express concern that pulling players away from their established investments in the original GTA Online might be a challenge. Rockstar faces the task of finding a balance to satisfy its diverse player base with the new online experience.

## Key Takeaways
- Rockstar Games has officially denied that GTA 6 cost $1.5 billion to develop.
- The actual development budget for GTA 6 has not been disclosed by Rockstar.
- Despite the high expected cost, GTA 6 is projected to be highly profitable, with pre-orders already generating over $500 million from 5+ million copies.
- The $400 merchandise-only collector's edition has also been a strong seller.
- GTA 6's online multiplayer component is rumored for a 2027 release, facing potential challenges in migrating players from the highly successful GTA Online.
