# GTA 6: Lots Of New Images Have Been Released

Rockstar Games has officially released a comprehensive "launch gallery" of new Grand Theft Auto 6 screenshots, offering fans fresh glimpses into the highly anticipated title. These brand-new images highlight the game's main characters, Jason and Lucia, in a variety of dynamic settings, including a vibrant nightclub, the gritty dive bar known as The Rusty Anchor, and a gym where Jason is seen working out.

The gallery also features intense action sequences, such as an airboat chase involving alligator hunting at night and a dramatic scene where police are closing in on a superyacht, presumably in pursuit of the protagonists. Another close-up shot shows Jason leaning out of a car window, wielding an automatic rifle while being chased by police helicopters.

These new images were released to celebrate GTA 6 being less than two weeks away from its official launch date of November 19. The game has reportedly already achieved significant commercial success, with nearly 8 million units sold and close to $1 billion in revenue generated. Players should prepare for a substantial download, as the game's file size is approximately 212GB. Thankfully, preloading will commence more than a week before the launch.

The worldwide release is set for November 19 at midnight local time, meaning players in New Zealand will be the first to access the game, while those in the US will be among the last. All the newly released images can be viewed on Rockstar's official GTA 6 website.
