# PSN Experiences Temporary Glitch on Eve of GTA 6 Launch, Network Strain Anticipated

On November 19, the eve of **Grand Theft Auto 6's** official global launch, Sony's PlayStation Network reportedly faced temporary issues for some users. Despite the [PlayStation Status website](https://status.playstation.com/en-US/) indicating normal operations, various reports suggested a glitch. The specific problem identified was a temporary outage affecting the launching of games, which has since been confirmed as fixed.The gaming community anticipates immense strain on both PSN and the Xbox Network as millions are expected to log in for GTA 6. While the game is already live in regions like New Zealand and Australia, the majority of players will gain access on November 19. GameSpot encourages readers to stay updated on network performance as the highly anticipated title rolls out globally for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Key Takeaways
- The PlayStation Network (PSN) experienced temporary issues for some users on November 19, the eve of GTA 6's global launch.
- Despite user reports, the official PlayStation Status website initially indicated no problems.
- A specific, temporary outage preventing the launching of games was identified and has since been resolved.
- The global launch of GTA 6 is expected to place significant strain on both PSN and Xbox Network.
- GTA 6 officially launches for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19 worldwide, although it's already live in some regions.
