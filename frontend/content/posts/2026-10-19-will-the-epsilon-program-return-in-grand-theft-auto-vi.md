# Will the Epsilon Program Return in Grand Theft Auto VI?

The Grand Theft Auto community is abuzz with speculation regarding the potential return of the Epsilon Program, the satirical cult featured prominently in GTA V, for the upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI. The discussion, originating from a Reddit post, highlights the real-world connection between Clearwater, Florida – a major hub for Scientology – and GTA VI's widely rumored Vice City-inspired Florida setting. Given that the Epsilon Program in GTA V was a clear parody of Scientology, fans are questioning whether Rockstar Games will leverage this geographical alignment to reintroduce the cult. The debate centers on whether its inclusion would be a minor Easter egg or a substantial part of the game's narrative and side content, offering players another memorable series of missions.

## Key Takeaways
- Fans are speculating about the Epsilon Program's return in GTA VI.
- The theory is based on GTA VI's rumored Florida setting and Scientology's significant presence in real-world Clearwater, Florida.
- The Epsilon Program in GTA V was a satirical take on Scientology and provided extensive side missions.
- The community is debating if the cult's return would be a minor detail or a major narrative component.

## FAQ
**What is the Epsilon Program?**
The Epsilon Program is a fictional satirical cult within the Grand Theft Auto universe, heavily inspired by Scientology, which featured prominently in GTA V with a series of side missions for the character Michael.

**Why do fans believe the Epsilon Program might return in GTA VI?**
The speculation stems from GTA VI's rumored setting in a Florida-inspired location, as Clearwater, Florida, is a real-world hub for Scientology, the organization that inspired the in-game cult.

**Will the Epsilon Program play a significant role if it returns?**
It is currently a point of fan discussion and speculation. Some hope for a major narrative or side-mission arc akin to GTA V, while others suggest it might be a smaller Easter egg or reference.
