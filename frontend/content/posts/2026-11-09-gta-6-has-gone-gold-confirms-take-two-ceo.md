# GTA 6 Has Gone Gold, Confirms Take-Two CEO

In a monumental announcement that will undoubtedly excite fans worldwide, Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick has officially confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) has "gone gold". This significant milestone indicates that primary development on Rockstar Games' highly anticipated title is complete. During an earnings call, Zelnick lauded Rockstar's efforts, stating, "They’ve done their very best work." Furthermore, in a separate interview with IGN, Zelnick explicitly addressed concerns about potential delays, assuring the public for the first time that the game will not be delayed again. This confirmation from the highest levels of Take-Two Interactive solidifies the game's progress towards its expected release.
