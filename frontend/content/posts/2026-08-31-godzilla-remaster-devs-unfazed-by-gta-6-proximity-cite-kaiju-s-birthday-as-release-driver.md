# Godzilla Remaster Devs Unfazed by GTA 6 Proximity, Cite Kaiju's Birthday as Release Driver

While many game developers are strategically avoiding a release near the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6, Pipeworks, the studio behind the upcoming *Godzilla Destroy All Monsters Melee: Remastered*, is notably unconcerned. Senior designer Simon Strange confirmed in an interview with IGN that **GTA 6's November 16 release date was not a factor** in their decision to launch the Godzilla remaster on November 3.

Instead, the chosen date holds significant meaning for kaiju fans: **November 3 marks Godzilla's official birthday**, commemorating the original 1954 *Gojira* film's debut. Strange emphasized that this was a 'respectful' choice, confident that dedicated Godzilla fans would purchase the game regardless of other major releases. He also noted that if publisher Atari had any concerns regarding *GTA 6*'s potential impact on sales, they would have initiated a discussion, but no such concerns were raised.

Interestingly, *Godzilla Destroy All Monsters Melee: Remastered* isn't the only title seemingly brave enough to launch near Rockstar's juggernaut. *Barbie Rewind* is also set for a close release, and Devolver Digital's *Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed* is even scheduled to release on the exact same day as *GTA 6*.

## Key Takeaways
- The developers of *Godzilla Destroy All Monsters Melee: Remastered* are not concerned about its release proximity to *Grand Theft Auto 6*.
- Senior designer Simon Strange stated that *GTA 6* was 'not even in our heads' when choosing the remaster's November 3 release date.
- The November 3 release date was specifically chosen to coincide with Godzilla's official birthday, marking the 1954 release of the original *Gojira* film.
- Pipeworks believes dedicated Godzilla fans will purchase the game regardless of other major titles releasing around the same time.
- *Barbie Rewind* and *Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed* are also releasing near or on the same day as *GTA 6*, demonstrating other companies' varying approaches to the competitive landscape.
