# GameStop Pulls Mysterious GTA 6 Collector's Map Posters After Viral Buzz

A Reddit user initially reported finding handwritten posters at their local GameStop advertising a 'Physical Collector’s Map' along with a 'Vintage Vice City Pack,' 'Ultimate Edition DLC,' and 'early pickup' for Grand Theft Auto 6 preorders. This information, notably absent from official Rockstar or GameStop online sources, quickly went viral across various gaming outlets. Upon a follow-up visit to preorder, the user discovered all the handwritten GTA 6 signs had been removed and replaced with unrelated promotions. The same GameStop employee who previously confirmed the map now offered a less confident 'should' come with the map, suggesting the signs were taken down 'after a meeting yesterday.' The user speculates this might have been an employee's independent attempt to generate preorder hype, as there remains no official confirmation regarding the physical collector's map or the other rumored bonuses.

## Key Takeaways
- A Reddit user initially found handwritten GameStop posters advertising a 'Physical Collector’s Map' and other unannounced bonuses for GTA 6 preorders.
- The information on these posters was not officially available online from Rockstar Games or GameStop.
- The Reddit post detailing these findings gained significant traction and was covered by multiple gaming news outlets.
- Upon a subsequent visit, all the handwritten GTA 6 preorder signs at the GameStop store had been removed.
- The GameStop employee's confirmation of the physical map became less confident, and they mentioned the signs were removed 'after a meeting.'
- There is still no official confirmation from Rockstar or GameStop regarding the inclusion of a physical collector's map with GTA 6 preorders.

## FAQ
**What did GameStop posters initially promise for GTA 6 preorders?**
The initial handwritten GameStop posters promised a 'Physical Collector’s Map,' a 'Vintage Vice City Pack,' 'Ultimate Edition DLC available on pickup,' and 'early pickup one week before release' for GTA 6 preorders.

No, the details on the posters were not found online from either GameStop or Rockstar's official channels.

An employee suggested the signs were taken down 'after a meeting yesterday,' but the exact reason for their removal has not been officially stated.

**Is a physical collector's map confirmed for GTA 6 preorders?**
As of now, there is still no official confirmation that a physical collector's map will be included with GTA 6 preorders.
