# Surprise TGA Nomination for Defunct Fighter 2XKO While GTA 6 Dominates Nods

The Game Awards 2026 nominations, announced by Geoff Keighley, brought a surprising inclusion: **2XKO** by Riot Games was nominated for Best Fighting Game. This comes despite Riot Games announcing earlier this year that active development for the *League of Legends*-themed fighter would cease in December 2026 due to a lack of player retention for sustainability.

2XKO is joined in its category by *Invincible Vs.*, *Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls*, *Avatar Legends*, and *My Hero Academia: All's Justice*. Notably, *Invincible Vs.* also faces an uncertain future following significant developer layoffs post-launch.

Meanwhile, **Grand Theft Auto VI** emerged as the most nominated game, securing an impressive eight nominations, including the prestigious Game of the Year award. *Control Resonant* and *Resident Evil Requiem* followed with six nominations each, and *Phantom Blade Zero* received five. Nintendo was recognized as the leading publisher with 12 nominations across all categories.

The Game Awards 2026 ceremony is scheduled for December 10, live from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, California.
