# Rockstar's Unprecedented GTA 6 Marketing Campaign Transforms Reality into Vice City

Rockstar Games is deploying an extraordinary and immersive marketing campaign for Grand Theft Auto 6, pushing beyond traditional advertising to physically transform real-world locations, most notably Miami, into the game's fictional setting, Vice City. This groundbreaking strategy includes a giant "Welcome to Vice City" sign, extensive GTA branding across Miami Beach, and even real-life GTA-style supercars, including a Vice City Police Department vehicle, cruising the city streets.

The campaign extends to unique collaborations and global activations. The Miami Heat NBA team is planning a special Vice City-themed game against the Milwaukee Bucks on November 18th, coinciding with the game's anticipated release. Globally, "We Love Vice City" murals and GTA VI artwork featuring protagonists Jason and Lucia have appeared in major US cities and across Europe, with reports of extensive activation in France. The marketing blitz also encompasses audio experiences, with Spotify promotions, artists sharing GTA VI artwork, and the reveal of in-game radio stations building pre-release hype. Further enhancing the immersive experience are a Vice City-themed blimp, downtown Miami being covered in GTA 6 brands, a reported Netflix extended look collaboration, and a partnership with Wynwood Walls offering free entry. This extensive and ambitious campaign underscores that Rockstar's CEO, Strauss Zelnick, was indeed serious about the scale of GTA 6's marketing.
