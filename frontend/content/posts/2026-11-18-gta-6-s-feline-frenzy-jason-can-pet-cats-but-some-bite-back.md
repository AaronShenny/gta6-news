# GTA 6's Feline Frenzy: Jason Can Pet Cats, But Some Bite Back!

Grand Theft Auto 6's co-protagonist, Jason, demonstrates a varied relationship with the feline population of Leonida. Players can experience heartwarming moments where Jason pets friendly cats, an action that not only prompts him to speak to them but also provides a small 'focus boost.' This level of detail highlights the game's immersive open world, as confirmed by social media clips. However, not all cat encounters are serene. Other clips reveal more perilous interactions, such as a carjacking incident where an aggressive cat in the vehicle attacks Jason, requiring a quicktime event to subdue it before he can let it out. This showcases the dynamic and sometimes unpredictable nature of the wildlife in GTA 6's expansive world, reminding players that while some animals are friendly, others can pose an unexpected challenge.
