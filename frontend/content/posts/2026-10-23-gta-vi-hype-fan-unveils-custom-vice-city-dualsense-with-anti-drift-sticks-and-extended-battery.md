# GTA VI Hype: Fan Unveils Custom Vice City DualSense with Anti-Drift Sticks and Extended Battery

A passionate Grand Theft Auto fan, known as /u/JS_Custom_Controller on Reddit, has showcased an impressively customized PlayStation DualSense controller, designed specifically to celebrate the upcoming release of *Grand Theft Auto VI*. The controller, sporting a vibrant Vice City aesthetic, goes beyond mere cosmetics with significant functional upgrades. The modifications include the integration of advanced **Hall-Effect/TMR sticks**, a popular solution to combat the infamous 'stick drift' issue prevalent in many modern controllers, promising enhanced durability and precision for intense gaming sessions. Furthermore, the controller boasts a **larger battery**, addressing a common complaint about the DualSense's battery life and ensuring longer uninterrupted gameplay. This custom creation highlights the immense excitement within the gaming community for *GTA VI*, with fans already preparing for its arrival in unique and innovative ways. The post, shared on the r/GTA6 subreddit, quickly garnered attention, demonstrating the community's appreciation for such dedicated craftsmanship.
