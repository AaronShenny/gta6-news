# GTA 6 Prologue Unveiled? Fan Theory Connects Trailer Clues

This act allegedly triggers a high-speed police chase, forcing the duo to abandon their car and flee by boat. Key evidence for this sequence includes a scene from Trailer 2 showing Lucia engaging a police helicopter from a bullet-riddled boat, a visual echoed in the game's promotional artwork.The escape continues as Lucia and Jason reportedly reach the docks and take refuge in a nearby motel. However, their respite is short-lived, as another Trailer 2 snippet depicts Lucia, still in her robbery attire, escaping a motel window, implying they are once again cornered by law enforcement. The theory further speculates this mission occurs shortly after Lucia's release from prison or house arrest, with the robbery possibly serving as a means to settle debts, forge a new beginning, or simply drive the plot forward. While presented as a prologue, the theorist also acknowledges it could be a significant early-game mission.
