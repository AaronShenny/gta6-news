# GTA 6's Real-Life Burger Shot & Cluckin' Bell: Taste Vice City, But Be Ready to Pay!

Rockstar Games is intensifying its marketing campaign for Grand Theft Auto 6 by introducing real-life versions of its in-game fast-food chains, Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell, at Miami's Kaseya Center. This partnership with the home of the NBA's Miami Heat now allows fans to purchase themed burgers and fried chicken, though they come with a hefty price tag.

The Burger Shot offerings are not new culinary creations but rather existing Skinny Louie burgers previously sold at the Kaseya Center, now adorned with GTA 6 branding. Staff at these pop-up locations further enhance the immersion by wearing Burger Shot shirts and hats. A basic cheeseburger combo, including fries and a drink, will set fans back $28.50 before tax, with an applewood bacon burger combo reaching $31. Cluckin' Bell also offers fried chicken, likely at similar premium prices, consistent with food costs at major sporting events.

These real-life eateries appear to be exclusive to the Kaseya Center for now, forming part of a broader, aggressive marketing blitz. The arena also features GTA 6 advertisements on its elevators, referencing in-game elements like Vice City FM and the TV show Macca & Friends. This follows Rockstar's earlier stunt of airlifting the Vice City logo onto the Kaseya Center. The campaign is set to culminate on November 18 with a special NBA Heat game where the team will wear GTA 6-themed jerseys and play on a branded court, just one day before the game's highly anticipated release on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
