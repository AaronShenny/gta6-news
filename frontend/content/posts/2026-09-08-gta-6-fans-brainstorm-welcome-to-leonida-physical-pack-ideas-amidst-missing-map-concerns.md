# GTA 6 Fans Brainstorm 'Welcome to Leonida' Physical Pack Ideas Amidst Missing Map Concerns

Dedicated Grand Theft Auto 6 enthusiasts are expressing a strong desire for a standalone physical 'Welcome to Leonida' pack, anticipating that a traditional map will not be included with the game's standard edition. The Reddit discussion, initiated by user /u/Lukeyy19, highlights a nostalgic demand for physical collectibles that have historically accompanied GTA titles. Ideas for the pack's contents include a large poster map of Leonida, collectible postcards, an immersive tourist guide, and unique in-game themed trinkets such as an Ocean View Hotel keychain, a Vice City Manatees bumper sticker, and a Metro card/map. The sentiment suggests a significant portion of the fanbase values these tangible items as part of the complete GTA experience, hoping Rockstar Games will offer such a pack closer to the game's launch.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 fans are keen on a physical 'Welcome to Leonida' collector's pack.

- Fans hope Rockstar will release such a pack separately, possibly closer to the game's launch.
