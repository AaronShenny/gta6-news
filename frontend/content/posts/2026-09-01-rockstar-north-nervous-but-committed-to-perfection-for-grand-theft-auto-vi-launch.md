# Rockstar North "Nervous" But Committed to Perfection for Grand Theft Auto VI Launch

Rockstar North's boss, Rob Nelson, has openly expressed the studio's "nervousness" regarding the highly anticipated launch of Grand Theft Auto VI. With 13 years of immense hype and expectation built up since GTA V, Nelson acknowledges the external pressure but states that the internal drive for perfection within the team far outweighs it.Nelson emphasized the studio's commitment to innovation while retaining familiarity, describing GTA VI as a significant "step forward in all respects." He highlighted advancements in world size, depth, traffic density, population, and the fidelity of NPC responses, promising players a "shock" upon experiencing the game's immersive environment.Adding to this, Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick reiterated Rockstar's philosophy of prioritizing polish and "creating perfection" over rushing a release. Zelnick affirmed that the company "errs on the side of perfection" to ensure the highest creative quality.Grand Theft Auto VI is slated for release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Industry experts have predicted that preorders alone could generate an estimated $2 billion by launch, potentially covering the game's entire production budget.
