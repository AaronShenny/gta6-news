# PS5 Nears 100 Million Sales Milestone, Fueled by Strong Ecosystem and GTA 6 Projections

Sony's PlayStation 5 console has achieved a significant milestone, with lifetime sales reaching 97.9 million units as of September 30, following the sale of 2.6 million consoles in its most recent quarter. While trailing the PS4's pace at the same point in its lifecycle, Sony's gaming ecosystem is currently performing exceptionally well, primarily due to soaring digital revenue and a record-high active user base of 127 million in September.

Despite the PS5's price increasing since launch due to global component shortages, Sony remains confident in its hardware sales profitability for the upcoming year, having secured memory supply. The company has also raised its financial forecast, anticipating increased sales from both first- and third-party titles, notably including Grand Theft Auto 6.

The highly anticipated GTA 6 is projected to have already accumulated nearly 8 million copies sold ahead of its November 19 launch, with the PS5 reportedly dominating sales figures over Xbox Series X|S. This strong performance is set to significantly benefit PlayStation's financials, as Sony retains a 30% share of game revenue through platform fees.
