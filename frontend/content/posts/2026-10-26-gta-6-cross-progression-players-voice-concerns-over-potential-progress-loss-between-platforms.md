# GTA 6 Cross-Progression: Players Voice Concerns Over Potential Progress Loss Between Platforms

A recent discussion on the r/GTA6 subreddit highlights a significant concern among potential Grand Theft Auto VI players: the lack of official information regarding cross-progression. The original poster, u/SeteMan1235, voiced frustration over the possibility of losing preorder bonuses and accumulated progress if they initially purchase the game on PlayStation 5 and later decide to switch to a PC version. The user specifically emphasized the importance of cross-progression for GTA 6 Online, but also noted that transferring story mode progress would be a welcome feature. As of now, Rockstar Games has made no official statements regarding cross-progression or cross-save functionality for GTA 6, leaving players to speculate and express their hopes for platform flexibility.

## Key Takeaways
- Players are expressing significant concern regarding the potential for cross-progression in GTA 6.
- A primary worry is the loss of preorder bonuses and accumulated in-game progress when switching between gaming platforms (e.g., from PS5 to PC).
- This concern extends to both GTA 6 Online progress and the transfer of story mode saves.
- Rockstar Games has not yet provided any official statements or confirmations regarding cross-progression features for GTA 6.

No, Rockstar Games has not made any official announcements or confirmations regarding cross-progression or cross-save functionality for GTA 6 at this time.
