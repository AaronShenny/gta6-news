# GTA VI better set the tone.

A dedicated fan on Reddit, known as u/omega617, has expressed a mixed but ultimately excited outlook on the upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI. The user describes a 'rollercoaster' of anticipation, oscillating between eager excitement and temporary disinterest, but recent images published by DAZED have reignited their enthusiasm. The author was particularly impressed by the visual quality of the characters, specifically mentioning Lucia and Jason, whose in-game transformations are expected to be a significant narrative element. Furthermore, the user speculates on the potential roles of other characters: 'Boobie Ike' is envisioned as a character similar to Gerald from previous titles, while 'Roxy' is humorously compared to the 'pros' players might encounter in the game world. In an edit, the author clarified that their initial statement 'better set the tone' refers to the game's graphical fidelity, gameplay development, and physics, indicating high expectations for technical advancements in GTA VI. The post concludes with the user eagerly awaiting the game's release to experience new friendships and adventures within its world.
