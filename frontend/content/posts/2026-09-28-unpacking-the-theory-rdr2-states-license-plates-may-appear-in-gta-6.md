# Unpacking the Theory: RDR2 States' License Plates May Appear in GTA 6

A compelling new fan theory circulating among the Grand Theft Auto 6 community posits an intriguing connection between Rockstar's two major franchises: Red Dead Redemption 2 and the highly anticipated GTA 6. The theory suggests that the fictional states introduced in RDR2 will not only have a presence in GTA 6 but that their distinct, real-world inspired license plates have already been hinted at.

According to the theory, each RDR2 state is a direct parody or representation of real-world American states, and their corresponding license plate designs reflect this. For instance, **New Hanover** is believed to represent Arkansas, Oklahoma, and Nebraska, and its license plates are described as Oklahoma-esque. Similarly, **New Austin**, which draws inspiration from New Mexico, Arizona, and Texas, is theorized to have Arizona-style plates.

**Lemoyne**, a clear analogue for Louisiana, is expected to feature Louisiana-style plates. Perhaps the most direct link is **Ambarino**, whose name is a Spanish word for 'amber coloured,' paralleling 'Colorado' which means 'coloured red.' This linguistic connection supports the idea that Ambarino's plates will be Colorado-like.

This theory, initially shared by Reddit user /u/DartBoardEater, sparks excitement about the potential for deeper world-building and subtle nods to Rockstar's expansive universe within GTA 6, hinting that the developers might be reusing or expanding upon these regional identities.
