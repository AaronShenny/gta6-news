# GTA 6 Backflip Brouhaha: New Trailer Shows Smoother Animation, Fueling Fan Speculation

The highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 has once again captured the internet's attention, this time over a seemingly minor detail: a backflip animation. A new trailer, released on November 10, showcased a race car driver performing a remarkably smooth backflip, a stark contrast to the 'janky' animation of protagonist Jason seen in the 'Extended Look' trailer from August.

This noticeable improvement has sparked immediate discussion among fans. Some are quick to credit Rockstar Games for actively listening to community feedback and refining animations. However, the narrative might be more complex. It's known that GTA 6 features player-controlled backflips that aren't restricted to a single animation. A key question arises: who is performing the backflip in the latest trailer? If, as some Reddit users speculate, the character is an NPC, then the animation could be a unique, motion-captured sequence, explaining its polished look without necessarily indicating a universal overhaul of player-controlled backflips.

Regardless of the underlying reason, this 'backflip controversy' underscores the immense anticipation surrounding GTA 6. Fans are meticulously scrutinizing every frame, highlighting the game's extraordinary hype.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preloading now live. Players in the US will be among the last globally to experience the game as it unlocks at midnight local time.
