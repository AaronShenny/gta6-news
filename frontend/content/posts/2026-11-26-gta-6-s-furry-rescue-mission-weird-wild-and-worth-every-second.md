# GTA 6’s Furry Rescue Mission: Weird, Wild, and Worth Every Second

Grand Theft Auto 6 introduces a surprisingly quirky yet rewarding side mission centered around saving a 'Furry' character named Chase the Fox from the hostile authorities of Leonida. This mission highlights the state's animosity towards Furries, presenting players with a chance to intervene.

To initiate this encounter, players must be cruising around Miramar Drive in Crosstown, Vice City, specifically between midnight and 4 AM, after completing Chapter 3. The mission triggers randomly, with police cornering Chase the Fox.

Should you choose to assist Chase, prepare for a thrilling chase sequence as you'll immediately acquire a three-star Wanted level. Your goal is to deliver Chase to a nearby parking garage where his Furry allies await. Following a brief standoff and a warm welcome into their ranks, one of your new allies will use your car as a decoy to distract the police.

Players will then take a van to transport the remaining Furries to their opulent hideout on Starfish Island, witnessing some 'interactions' along the way.

Upon successful completion, you're presented with a choice of rewards: either a hefty $15,000 in cash or the unique Furry DW-9 submachine gun. The article strongly recommends opting for the DW-9, citing its exclusivity and the relative ease of earning money elsewhere in GTA 6. Additionally, players gain safehouse access to the Furry hideout, providing a convenient spot to resupply on weapons and body armor.
