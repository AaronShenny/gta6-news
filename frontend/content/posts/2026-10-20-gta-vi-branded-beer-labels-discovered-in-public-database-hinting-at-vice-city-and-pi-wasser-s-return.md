# GTA VI-Branded Beer Labels Discovered in Public Database, Hinting at Vice City and Pißwasser's Return

Gaming enthusiasts have uncovered intriguing registrations in the TBB public COLA database, revealing several beer labels branded with 'GTA VI.' These labels, registered by Tripping Animals Brewing, include designs for kegs, cans, and bottles.

Most notably, the registered names include "No Mames Vice City," which strongly suggests a direct connection to the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI's rumored Vice City setting. Furthermore, the iconic in-game beer brand "Pißwasser" from previous GTA titles also appears among the registered labels, alongside "Patriot."

The discovery, initially reported by IGrandTheftAuto and submitted by Reddit user /u/Roel_driesvink, provides specific TBB-IDs for verification. While Rockstar Games has not officially commented on these registrations, the presence of a real-world brewing company registering labels with such explicit GTA VI references and established in-game branding points towards a potential marketing collaboration, merchandise tie-in, or even a subtle hint at the game's immersive world-building.
