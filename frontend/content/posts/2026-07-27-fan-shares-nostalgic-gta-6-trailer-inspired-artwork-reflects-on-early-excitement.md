# Fan Shares Nostalgic GTA 6 Trailer-Inspired Artwork, Reflects on Early Excitement

A Reddit user, u/Human_Gur6982, recently posted a colorful drawing they created 'a few days after the first GTA 6 trailer was released.' The artwork, shared on the r/GTA6 subreddit, serves as a personal time capsule from the initial wave of excitement surrounding the game's reveal.
