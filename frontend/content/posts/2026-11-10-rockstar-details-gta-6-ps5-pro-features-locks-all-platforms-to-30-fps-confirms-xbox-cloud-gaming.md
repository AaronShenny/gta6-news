# Rockstar Details GTA 6 PS5 Pro Features, Locks All Platforms to 30 FPS, Confirms Xbox Cloud Gaming

Rockstar Games has finally unveiled crucial details regarding Grand Theft Auto 6's performance and features across various platforms, including specific enhancements for PlayStation 5 Pro owners. The highly anticipated title will leverage Sony's PSSR upscaling technology on the PS5 Pro, promising a cleaner visual output compared to the base PS5. Additionally, ray-traced shadows will be an exclusive graphical fidelity option for PS5 Pro players, potentially adding a noticeable layer of realism to Leonida's environments.

Crucially, Rockstar confirmed that GTA 6 will run at a locked 30 frames per second across *all* platforms at launch, including the PS5, PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X|S. This statement directly contradicts earlier datamine speculation suggesting multiple performance modes, as no such options were mentioned in the official announcement.

Beyond console specifics, Rockstar also announced that GTA 6 will be available on Xbox Cloud Gaming from day one. This broadens accessibility, allowing players to stream the game without needing a dedicated Xbox Series X|S or PlayStation 5 console, with Xbox One also supported exclusively via cloud streaming. While PlayStation consoles won't support cloud streaming for GTA 6, remote play (streaming from one's own console) will be available for both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Pre-loading for GTA 6 is now live across all release platforms. Reports of GTA 6's story appearing as a separate download during pre-load have fueled speculation about an imminent announcement for a new online mode. Furthermore, recent unconfirmed reports continue to suggest that a PC version of GTA 6 might launch significantly earlier than expected, potentially in early 2027, marking a quicker turnaround than previous Rockstar PC ports.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 on PS5 Pro will feature PSSR support for a cleaner image.

- All console platforms, including PS5, PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X|S, will run GTA 6 at a locked 30 frames per second at launch.
