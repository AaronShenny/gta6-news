# Justice Duo Crafts Futuristic GTA 6 Soundtrack, Keeps Hype at Bay

French electronic music duo Justice, comprised of Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay, are the creative force behind the highly anticipated soundtrack for *Grand Theft Auto 6*. Aware of the game's unprecedented hype, the duo consciously maintains a professional distance to ensure their creative process remains untainted by external pressures, viewing the 'colossus approaching' from an outside perspective. The soundtrack is described as having a unique 'flavor' built from various instruments. While a subtle '80s influence is present, Justice clarifies that the score is far from solely retro, incorporating 'futuristic' sounds, such as a synth that mimics a 'phasey' guitar sound beyond what a real guitar could produce. The first single, 'Flower in a Hurricane,' featuring Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, has already garnered significant attention with 2.8 million YouTube plays in under a week. De Rosnay expressed delight at collaborating with such a 'dream team' of performers. Beyond Justice's original score, *GTA 6* promises the 'next evolution of in-game radio,' offering multiple radio stations and podcasts accessible even on foot. The official *GTA 6* album will feature 34 original songs, including new tracks from iconic artists like Keith Richards and Travis Scott, and will be available on physical media.
