# Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick Defends GTA 6's $80 Price Tag as a 'Bargain' Amid Economic Headwinds

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has robustly defended the $80 price tag for the upcoming *Grand Theft Auto 6*, labeling it a 'bargain' despite prevailing economic challenges such as rising gas and grocery prices. In an interview with CNBC, Zelnick acknowledged the financial pressures consumers face but asserted that 'interactive entertainment is actually a real bargain,' particularly for a title like GTA.

He emphasized the immense hours of engagement games like GTA offer, citing *Grand Theft Auto 5*'s hundreds of hours of playability as an example. Zelnick anticipates *GTA 6* will provide similarly extensive playtime, thereby justifying its cost in terms of entertainment value per hour. He further underscored this value by noting that *GTA 5*'s 2013 launch price of $60 would equate to approximately $85 today when adjusted for inflation, making *GTA 6*'s $80 base price comparatively lower in real terms.

While the base edition is priced at $80, a $100 Ultimate Edition featuring additional in-game content will also be available. Zelnick previously indicated that Rockstar could have charged even more but opted for the current pricing to ensure broader accessibility.

The article also touches upon the higher cost of current-generation console hardware (PS5, Xbox Series X|S) due to component shortages. However, it highlights a recent 10-year deal between Microsoft and Take-Two, which will bring *GTA 6* to Xbox Cloud Gaming, enabling older Xbox One consoles to stream the game.

*Grand Theft Auto 6* is set to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preloading already underway.
