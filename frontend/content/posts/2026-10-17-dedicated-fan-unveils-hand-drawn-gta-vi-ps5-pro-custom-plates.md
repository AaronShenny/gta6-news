# Dedicated Fan Unveils Hand-Drawn GTA VI PS5 Pro Custom Plates

Reddit user u/Additional_Town_9896 has shared a personal project, revealing custom hand-drawn plates for a PS5 Pro console, adorned with a Grand Theft Auto VI theme. Using markers and a white pencil, the artist created unique artwork for the console accessories. The creator openly acknowledged that they are not a professional artist and that the painting has some "rough edges" and imperfect palm trees, stating the project was simply an attempt to try something new.
