# Fan Expresses Unwavering Confidence in Rockstar Games, Expects GTA VI to Exceed All Hopes

In a heartfelt post on Reddit, user /u/GoodfellaCLE reflects on Rockstar Games' remarkable track record of consistently exceeding player expectations, regardless of how high they might be. The author recounts their journey through the Grand Theft Auto series, starting with the unexpected brilliance of GTA III, which was later surpassed by Vice City and San Andreas. They note that even with "massive expectations" for GTA IV, it still "blew me away," and its DLCs were "just about perfect." While acknowledging GTA V as their "least favorite in the series," they admit it still "completely blew me away in sheer size, scale, missions, characters." Beyond the GTA franchise, the author highlights Red Dead Redemption 2 as a game for which they had the "highest expectations" in their life, only to be "shocked" by how much they still underestimated its phenomenal quality. This consistent history of over-delivery instills "all the faith in the world" that GTA VI will deliver "one of the best games of all time." The post concludes with the sentiment that in an era of tempering gaming expectations, Rockstar stands out as a giant where players can "let our imaginations run wild and still be surprised" by getting everything they hoped for and more.
