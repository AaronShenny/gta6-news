# GTA 6 Rumor: Vice City May Feature Its Own Baseball Team

A recent screenshot, reportedly from an 'extended look' of Grand Theft Auto 6, has ignited discussions among fans about the potential introduction of a dedicated baseball team to the game's Vice City setting. The image, captured at the 26:06 mark and shared by Reddit user /u/Chi-Town22, reveals a distinctive logo featuring an alcoholic drink design with a baseball backdrop and a team name prominently displayed. This discovery is particularly noteworthy as previous GTA titles haven't featured dedicated baseball teams. Fans are now speculating on several aspects, including the team's specific affiliation (whether from Vice City or another in-game location), its official name, home stadium (with 'Fleeca Field' being a suggested possibility), and its overall role or influence within the game's narrative and world. The community is also debating if this team will serve as a parody of a major league like MLB, similar to how the 'Manatees' parody the NFL, or if it will represent a smaller, fictional league.
