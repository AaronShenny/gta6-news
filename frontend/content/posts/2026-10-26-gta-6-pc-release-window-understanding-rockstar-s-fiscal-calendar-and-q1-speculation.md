# GTA 6 PC Release Window: Understanding Rockstar's Fiscal Calendar and Q1 Speculation

Speculation surrounding the PC release of Grand Theft Auto 6 often references 'Q1,' leading to confusion due to varying fiscal year definitions. This article highlights that Rockstar Games, like its parent company Take-Two Interactive, operates on a fiscal year that commences on April 1st and concludes on March 31st. Consequently, 'Q1' for Rockstar Games spans from April to June, rather than the calendar year's January to March. This distinction is crucial for interpreting any rumors or reports about a 'Q1' release for GTA 6 on PC, suggesting that such a timeframe would point towards a late June launch, which is presented as a more plausible scenario than a late March release.

## Key Takeaways
- Companies can adopt fiscal years that differ from the standard calendar year.
- Rockstar Games follows Take-Two Interactive's fiscal year, which runs from April 1st to March 31st.
- For Rockstar, 'Q1' refers to the period between April and June.
- Reports of a GTA 6 PC release in 'Q1' would imply a late June launch, not late March.

## FAQ
**What is Rockstar Games' fiscal year?**
Rockstar Games follows the fiscal year of its parent company, Take-Two Interactive, which begins on April 1st and ends on March 31st.

**What does 'Q1' mean for Rockstar Games' release schedule?**
For Rockstar Games, 'Q1' refers to the first fiscal quarter, which spans from April 1st to June 30th.

**If GTA 6 PC is rumored for 'Q1,' when could it actually be released?**
A 'Q1' rumor for GTA 6 PC would suggest a release window between April and June, potentially as late as the end of June, rather than the calendar year's first quarter (January-March).
