## FAQ
**Is Take-Two Interactive considering selling itself?**
No, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has explicitly stated the company is not interested in being acquired, emphasizing its ability to thrive independently.

**Why is Take-Two not interested in selling?**
Zelnick cites Take-Two's strong financial health, significant operational scale, a robust balance sheet, and projected annual revenues of $8 billion to $8.2 billion as reasons for its independent strength.

**Has Take-Two been the target of acquisition attempts in the past?**
Yes, Electronic Arts publicly pursued a buyout of Take-Two in 2008, which was ultimately abandoned. Additionally, there were unconfirmed rumors of Sony's interest in 2019.

**What are Take-Two's current financial projections?**
Take-Two's guidance for the current fiscal year is between $8 billion and $8.2 billion in annual revenue.
