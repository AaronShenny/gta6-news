# Flying High: How Long to Traverse GTA 6's Massive Leonida Map?

The highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 features an "absolutely massive" map, codenamed Leonida, whose sheer scale is truly breathtaking. To gauge its immense size, journalists conducted a flight test, piloting a single-propeller plane across the entire expanse. The journey from one end to the other took a significant **four minutes and 10 seconds**, offering a tangible sense of its vastness.

Previous reports suggested the GTA 6 map would be larger than GTA 4, GTA 5, and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined, a claim that feels increasingly accurate after this direct traversal experience. The article also explores the consequences of attempting to escape Leonida's invisible boundaries; players trying to venture too far on a jet ski will inevitably run out of fuel and be left stranded. While there are anecdotal mentions of a "dog on a wave runner" coming to the rescue, the author could not replicate this phenomenon.

Within Leonida's expansive borders, players are promised an abundance of activities, from engaging missions and developing relationships to simply immersing themselves in the scenery of its reactive open world. The game has already garnered widespread critical acclaim, with GameSpot awarding it a rare perfect 10/10 score, a sentiment echoed by numerous other reviewers globally, underscoring Rockstar Games' monumental achievement.
