# GTA 6 In-Game TV Show Macca & Friends Looks Ridiculous In The Best Way

Rockstar Games has unveiled the first footage of 'Macca & Friends,' an upcoming in-game TV show for **Grand Theft Auto 6**. This unique program, airing on GTA's fictional 'Weazel' channel (a parody of Fox), centers around a sunglass-wearing pink alligator named Macca and his peculiar group of wetland companions in Leonida, the game's Florida-inspired setting.The show's cast includes Pinchie the crab, who serves as Macca's landlord, alongside a hat-wearing manatee and a flamingo. The teaser trailer hints at a storyline where Macca needs to secure funds to pay his rent, leading the group to seemingly impersonate police officers and infiltrate a police station, possibly with the aim of acquiring cash from an evidence locker.Given Rockstar's renowned commitment to intricate detail and immersive gameplay, it's speculated that 'Macca & Friends' could evolve into a full-fledged, multi-episode series viewable within the game world. This reveal further highlights GTA 6's impressive attention to detail, which extends to elements many players might even overlook.**GTA 6** is set to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preloading already available for the massive 212GB title.
