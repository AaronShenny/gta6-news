# GTA 6's Leonida Woodlands: Fans Anticipate Immersive Environmental Detail

The article, originating from a Reddit post by user JamaicanX, expresses considerable anticipation for the woodlands of Leonida in the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6. The user highlights their expectation for "incredibly dense and detailed" forests, eager to explore the environmental intricacies Rockstar Games will implement. A particular point of interest is the desire for immersive, passive interactions, such as being able to "sit down on a log, take in the scenery, and watch the wildlife do it's thing."

Furthermore, JamaicanX speculates on how protagonists Jason and Lucia will physically interact with the vegetation. Drawing parallels to Arthur Morgan's environmental interactions in Red Dead Redemption 2, they hope for expanded mechanics where characters might "push aside bushes, tree branches, and tall grass." The user also ponders whether different environments will influence character movement and animations, suggesting that navigating "thick bushes could look and feel noticeably different from walking along a clear trail." The "Grassrivers" area is specifically mentioned as a priority destination for early exploration.
