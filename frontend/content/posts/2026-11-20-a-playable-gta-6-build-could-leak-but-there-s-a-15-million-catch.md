# A Playable GTA 6 Build Could Leak, But There’s A $15 Million Catch

Notorious hacker Cyberleek, responsible for previous Grand Theft Auto 6 leaks, has resurfaced with a new threat: to release the executable file for a working Xbox build of the game. The condition for this release is that the public helps raise their associated meme coin to a $15 million market cap. Cyberleek claims to possess a "full November 2026 build" for Xbox, stating it will be released via torrent, with only the main executable held back until demands are met. The alleged files are over 200GB, consistent with an Xbox game, though their authenticity as actual GTA 6 data or potential malware remains unconfirmed.

This isn't Cyberleek's first attempt at leveraging GTA 6 leaks for crypto gain; a previous $30 million demand for a playable build failed, though several GTA 6 video clips were subsequently released. Evidence suggests Cyberleek's motives are rooted in a pump-and-dump crypto scheme rather than acting in gamers' interests. Rockstar Games has previously described the leaks as "heartbreaking," and parent company Take-Two has issued subpoenas to identify the hacker, who remains anonymous despite these efforts.
