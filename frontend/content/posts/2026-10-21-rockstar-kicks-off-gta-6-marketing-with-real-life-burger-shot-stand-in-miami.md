# Rockstar Kicks Off GTA 6 Marketing with Real-Life Burger Shot Stand in Miami

Rockstar Games appears to have initiated real-world marketing efforts for the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6. Reports indicate the presence of a real-life Burger Shot food stand, a beloved in-game fast-food chain from the GTA universe, at the Kaseya Center in Miami. This move strongly suggests that Rockstar is leveraging iconic elements of the franchise and the game's implied Vice City setting (Miami) to build hype. While official announcements regarding specific marketing campaigns are pending, this physical presence marks a tangible step towards the game's eventual release, exciting fans who recognize the subtle yet significant nod to the series' lore and location.

## FAQ
**What is Burger Shot?**
Burger Shot is a fictional fast-food chain featured prominently within the Grand Theft Auto video game series, known for its satirical take on real-world fast-food establishments.

**Where was the Burger Shot stand spotted?**
The real-life Burger Shot food stand, part of Rockstar's GTA 6 marketing, was reportedly seen at the Kaseya Center in Miami, Florida.
