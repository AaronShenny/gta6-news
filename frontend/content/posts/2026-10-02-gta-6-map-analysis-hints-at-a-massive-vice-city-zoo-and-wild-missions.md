# GTA 6 Map Analysis Hints at a Massive Vice City Zoo and Wild Missions

A recent community deep-dive into available GTA 6 map information indicates that the in-game zoo in Vice City could be far larger than initial assumptions. This comparison suggests the virtual zoo will be "totally enormous," possibly rivaling the size of the game's large lake.Further bolstering this theory is the observation that the Miami Seaquarium appears to have a near one-to-one recreation within GTA 6. The analysis also re-evaluates previously identified smaller map clusters, suggesting they might be botanical gardens rather than the main zoo.The significant scale of these animal-centric locations has sparked speculation regarding potential gameplay. One prediction involves a "Free Willy"-esque mission, stemming from an "aquarium van" seen in an "Extended Look" that referenced freeing an orca, a clear parody of the real-life Lolita orca at Seaquarium. Another exciting possibility, given the zoo's size and the game's development timeframe, is a "Tiger King" inspired mission, perhaps involving the theft of a tiger.
