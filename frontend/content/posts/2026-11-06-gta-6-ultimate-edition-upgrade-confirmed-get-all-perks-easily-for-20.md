# GTA 6 Ultimate Edition Upgrade Confirmed: Get All Perks Easily for $20

Good news for Grand Theft Auto 6 fans! Rockstar Games has confirmed that players who purchase the standard $80 edition of GTA 6 will have an easy and affordable option to upgrade to the $100 Ultimate Edition. This 'Ultimate Edition Upgrade' will be available for just $20, making it simple for players to access all the premium content without having to buy the full $100 version outright. This upgrade path applies to both digital purchases from console storefronts and physical 'code-in-a-box' editions.

The Ultimate Edition, which is not sold physically in stores, boasts a wealth of exclusive in-game content. This includes unique vehicles, weapons, and clothing items for protagonists Jason and Lucia, which will be integrated throughout their story progression. Crucially, the $100 edition also grants access to exclusive in-game businesses like a hair salon, tattoo parlor, and mod shop, unavailable in the standard version.

All GTA 6 preorders, regardless of edition, will also receive 'The Vintage Vice City Pack' and a one-month subscription to GTA+. In related news, preloading for the massive 212GB game begins on November 10, ahead of its November 19 launch. Players eager to jump in first can consider the 'New Zealand trick' to play at midnight local time.

## Key Takeaways
- Players can upgrade from the $80 standard edition to the $100 Ultimate Edition for an additional $20.
