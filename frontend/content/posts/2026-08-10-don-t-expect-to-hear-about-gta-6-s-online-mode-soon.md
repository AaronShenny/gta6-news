# Don’t Expect To Hear About GTA 6’s Online Mode Soon

Rockstar Games is intentionally keeping details about Grand Theft Auto 6's online mode under wraps, despite the monumental success of GTA Online for Grand Theft Auto V. According to a report from Bloomberg's Jason Schreier, parent company Take-Two is currently only discussing the single-player aspect of GTA 6, which is not expected to feature microtransactions or recurring revenue. Schreier emphasized that there are no immediate plans to reveal what the new GTA Online will look like or how its recurring spend model will function.This silence has surprised many, especially considering GTA Online's enduring popularity and its reported annual revenue exceeding $500 million even 13 years post-release. When pre-order details for GTA 6 were first announced, the game was explicitly described as a "single-player experience," with no mention of any online support. Industry analysts, like GameSpot's Mark Delaney, suggest that Rockstar faces a challenging transition from the current GTA Online to its successor, with any decision potentially alienating a segment of its dedicated fanbase.Fans can anticipate an "extended look" at GTA 6 on August 27, airing exclusively on Netflix at 3 PM ET. This decision to place the trailer behind a streaming service paywall has already sparked frustration among some players, though it's seen by some as a reflection of current media trends. Grand Theft Auto 6 is slated for release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
