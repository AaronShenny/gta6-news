# Rockstar's Proven Track Record: Why Early GTA 6 Glitches Shouldn't Alarm Fans

Recent 'Extended Look' footage of Grand Theft Auto 6 has sparked criticism among some fans, who are pointing out minor animation, NPC behavior, physics, and graphical glitches. However, an article by /u/Aware-Screen-8982 argues that such concerns are premature, urging players to consider Rockstar Games' well-documented history of fixing these exact types of issues.

The piece highlights that Rockstar's own patch notes for Grand Theft Auto V demonstrate a consistent commitment to polish. Examples cited include fixes for incorrect NPC and character animations, flickering animations, erroneous NPC behavior, graphical glitches and stretched textures, camera problems, physics issues, and even animals falling through the world. Crucially, the article notes that Rockstar was still issuing fixes for GTA V, a game released in 2013, as recently as 2026 (in the context of the article's publication), addressing graphical issues, animation problems, cutscene bugs, and NPC/animal behavior.

This extensive and long-term support for GTA V serves as a strong indicator that Rockstar is highly unlikely to overlook minor imperfections in GTA 6, their 'biggest and most ambitious game yet.' The author emphasizes that fans should not assume that any visual glitch or odd animation seen in early footage will persist into the final product. Many of these issues may already be known to the developers or could be resolved well before the game's launch. The overarching message is a plea for patience, encouraging the community to allow Rockstar to complete the game before passing final judgment.

## Key Takeaways
- Early GTA 6 'Extended Look' footage has drawn criticism for minor animation, NPC, physics, and graphical issues.
- Rockstar Games has a documented history of extensively fixing these exact types of bugs in their titles.
- Grand Theft Auto V's patch notes show numerous fixes for similar problems, with support extending years after its 2013 release, even into 2026 (as per the article's context).
- Rockstar's long-term commitment to polishing even older games suggests they will address any minor issues in GTA 6 before launch.
- Fans are urged not to assume that early glitches will be present in the final game and to reserve judgment until completion.
