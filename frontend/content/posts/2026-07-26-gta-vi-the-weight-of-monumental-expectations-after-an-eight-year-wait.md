# GTA VI: The Weight of Monumental Expectations After an Eight-Year Wait

A Reddit user, u/Roox4, expresses their high expectations for Grand Theft Auto VI, arguing that after an unprecedented eight-year gap since Rockstar's last major release (Red Dead Redemption 2), the upcoming title simply 'has to be a masterpiece.' The author highlights the significant development time, noting that GTA VI has been in production for three years longer than the critically acclaimed RDR2, which they consider Rockstar's best game to date. This extended development period leads to the belief that GTA VI will be an exceptionally large and improved game compared to its predecessors. While acknowledging advice to temper expectations, the author finds it difficult, feeling both excited and nervous about the monumental potential of the game.
