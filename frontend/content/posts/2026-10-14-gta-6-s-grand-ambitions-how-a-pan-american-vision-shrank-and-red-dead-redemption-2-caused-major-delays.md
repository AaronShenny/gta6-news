# GTA 6's Grand Ambitions: How a Pan-American Vision Shrank and Red Dead Redemption 2 Caused Major Delays

A recent comprehensive report has unveiled captivating details about the development of Rockstar's highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6*. The game, initially codenamed "Project Americas," was conceived with an incredibly ambitious scope, envisioning a vast playable world encompassing "big swaths of land across both North and South America."

However, this grand vision was eventually scaled back. The current iteration of *GTA 6* focuses on a fictionalized version of Miami, known as Vice City, and its surrounding areas in the greater Leonida region (a stand-in for Florida). Despite the reduction from its initial pan-American scope, the game's map is still colossal, reportedly three times larger than that of Rockstar's previous title, *Red Dead Redemption 2*. Confirmed major locations include Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and Mount Kalaga.

The report also sheds light on the game's prolonged development timeline. *GTA 6* was originally planned for concurrent development with *Red Dead Redemption 2*. However, as the scope of *RDR2* expanded, Rockstar's leadership made the strategic decision to reallocate staff across its network of studios to ensure *RDR2*'s 2018 release. This pivotal decision resulted in *GTA 6* being delayed by "several years." Rockstar's development philosophy has since shifted, with teams now focusing on one major project collaboratively before moving to the next.

Regarding financial specifics, Rockstar has denied rumors that *GTA 6* cost an exorbitant $1.5 billion to develop, though they did not disclose the actual budget. Despite this, the game has already reportedly generated $600 million in pre-sales, with projections suggesting it could reach $2 billion before launch.

The game has faced multiple release date adjustments. Originally slated for 2025, it was first pushed to May 2026, and then again to November 2026. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick's reaction to the second delay was a telling, "Please tell me you're kidding." *GTA 6* is currently set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, though Rockstar has not entirely ruled out the possibility of further delays.

## Key Takeaways

- GTA 6 was delayed "several years" because Rockstar prioritized and expanded Red Dead Redemption 2's development.
