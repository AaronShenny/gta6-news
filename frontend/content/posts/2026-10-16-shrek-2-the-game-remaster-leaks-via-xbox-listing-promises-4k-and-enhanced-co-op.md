# Shrek 2: The Game Remaster Leaks via Xbox Listing, Promises 4K and Enhanced Co-op

A remaster of the beloved 2004 title, Shrek 2: The Game, appears to be on its way, following a premature listing on Xbox.com. The page, which has since been removed, revealed that the upcoming remaster will boast significant graphical upgrades, including better textures, an enhanced user interface, and full 4K resolution. Staying true to the original PlayStation 2, Xbox, and GameCube versions, players will be able to enjoy the game solo or team up with up to three friends in co-op mode, both locally and online. Fan-favorite characters like Shrek, Fiona, and Donkey are confirmed to be playable.

Beyond the visual enhancements, the Shrek 2 remaster is set to introduce a new Photo Mode and a Cheat Mode. Aspyr Media, known for its work on recent The Lord of the Rings re-releases, is developing the game, taking over from original publisher Activision. While the leaked Xbox page did not specify a release date or price, it's anticipated that the game will also launch on PlayStation, with a Nintendo Switch version also a strong possibility. The article humorously suggests a November 19 release to compete with GTA 6.

The leak aligns with recent activity from the official Shrek social media account, which posted a compilation of Shrek video game footage with the caption, "Only real gamers played Shrek," leading many to believe it's a teaser for the remaster. This news comes amidst a busy year for the Shrek franchise in gaming, with Shrek recently appearing in Ubisoft's Brawlhalla and FIFA Heroes. The original Shrek movie also returned to theaters for its 25th anniversary, and Shrek 5 is slated for 2027, followed by a Donkey movie in 2028. The Shrek universe continues to thrive with theme park attractions and a strong meme culture, keeping the ogre relevant for a new generation of players.
