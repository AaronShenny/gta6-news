# UK Tyre Store Shuts Down for 4-Day GTA 6 Gaming Break for Staff

In a testament to the immense anticipation surrounding Grand Theft Auto 6, a regional tyre store located in the North East of England has announced it will close its doors for three days. This unprecedented move is specifically to allow its employees ample time to dive into the upcoming game. Including their usual Sunday closure, staff will enjoy a four-day break dedicated to gaming.

Beyond just giving their team time off, the store is also engaging with the community by running a social media competition, offering fans the chance to win a copy of GTA 6. This initiative highlights the cultural impact and excitement that Rockstar Games' next installment is generating, even among businesses outside the gaming industry.
