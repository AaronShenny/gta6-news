# GTA 6 Extended Look: Exclusive Netflix Premiere Details and How to Watch

Rockstar Games is gearing up to unveil an "Extended Look" at Grand Theft Auto 6 on August 27, offering fans a deeper dive into the highly anticipated title. However, unlike traditional reveals, this preview will first debut exclusively on Netflix for a six-hour window before becoming publicly available. The Netflix premiere is scheduled for August 27 at 3 PM ET / 12 PM PT, among other international times. Following this exclusive period, the "Extended Look" will then be released on YouTube and the official GTA 6 website starting August 27 at 9 PM ET / 6 PM PT.

While a Netflix subscription will grant early access, it is not mandatory, as the preview will be free to watch on YouTube a few hours later. Rockstar has remained tight-lipped about the exact content or duration of the "Extended Look." Still, fans are widely anticipating brand-new gameplay footage, a closer inspection of the game's setting, Vice City, and core mechanics like driving and shooting, building on the excitement generated by previous leaks and official teasers.
