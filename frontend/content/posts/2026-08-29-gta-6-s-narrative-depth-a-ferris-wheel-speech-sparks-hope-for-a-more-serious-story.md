# GTA 6's Narrative Depth: A Ferris Wheel Speech Sparks Hope for a More Serious Story

A recent discussion on Reddit's r/GTA6 subreddit highlights a specific moment from what appears to be a leaked or rumored segment of Grand Theft Auto 6: a 'Ferris Wheel speech' delivered by Brian to Jason. The original poster, u/ILikesStuff, suggests this small detail, combined with an 'ending montage narrated by Cal,' could indicate a significantly more serious and introspective narrative for GTA 6, potentially surpassing previous installments.

The author draws parallels to moments of introspection found in GTA IV, which they believe didn't fully realize its potential, unlike Red Dead Redemption 2. While acknowledging Michael's 'You can't run no more' speech in GTA V, the poster argues that GTA V's narrative ultimately failed to deliver on its serious undertones. The primary concern for the author regarding GTA 6 has always been the story's depth, not the world or gameplay. This brief speech, however, offers a glimmer of hope that GTA 6 could deliver an impactful, emotionally resonant story rather than merely an entertaining one.
