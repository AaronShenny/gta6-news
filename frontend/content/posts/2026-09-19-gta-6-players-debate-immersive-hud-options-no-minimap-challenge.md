# GTA 6 Players Debate Immersive HUD Options: No Minimap Challenge?

A recent Reddit post by /u/ChrisCaliburGaming has ignited a lively discussion among Grand Theft Auto VI enthusiasts regarding their preferred Heads-Up Display (HUD) settings for their initial playthrough. The original poster expressed a strong desire to strip back the HUD as much as possible, specifically advocating for the removal of the minimap to enhance immersion. The rationale behind this is to encourage players to learn the game's environment, recognize landmarks, and actively pay attention to their surroundings rather than relying solely on the corner-screen map.

However, the user also shared a cautionary tale from their experience with Red Dead Redemption 2, where attempting a minimap-less playthrough led to significant disorientation, forcing them to reinstate the map. This highlights the inherent challenge of navigating vast open worlds without traditional guidance. The post speculates whether the in-game indicators seen in early GTA VI glimpses might serve as a viable alternative for navigation in a minimal HUD setup, though acknowledges that reaching entirely new locations without a map remains a hurdle.

The discussion invites other players to weigh in on their strategies: will they opt for a full HUD, go completely without, or find a middle ground? For those choosing a limited HUD, the question is posed: which elements are essential enough to keep? The sentiment across the post is one of high anticipation for the upcoming title.
