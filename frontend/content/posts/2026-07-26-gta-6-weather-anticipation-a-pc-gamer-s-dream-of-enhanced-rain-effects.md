# GTA 6 Weather Anticipation: A PC Gamer's Dream of Enhanced Rain Effects

A passionate Grand Theft Auto fan has taken to Reddit to share their keen anticipation for the advanced weather effects expected in the upcoming *Grand Theft Auto 6* story mode. The user, a self-proclaimed PC gamer, acknowledges the likely longer wait for the PC version but remains eager. Their excitement primarily revolves around the potential for the improved RAGE engine to deliver more realistic and immersive rain effects, specifically mentioning how rain will interact with car windows. This desire stems from their current gaming habits, where titles like *GTA V* and *Red Dead Redemption 2* serve as "cozy stuff" for decompression. They describe a routine of turning on in-game rain or thunderstorms, listening to talk radio, or background YouTube shows, highlighting the atmospheric importance of weather in their gaming experience. The user has explored existing YouTube videos for glimpses of the new weather system and expresses a strong hope for more official information to be released as development progresses.
