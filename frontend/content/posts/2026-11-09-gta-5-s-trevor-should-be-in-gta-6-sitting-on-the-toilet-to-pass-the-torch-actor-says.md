# GTA 5’s Trevor Should Be In GTA 6 Sitting On The Toilet To “Pass The Torch,” Actor Says

Steven Ogg, the actor behind Grand Theft Auto V's iconic wildcard Trevor Philips, has expressed his desire for a brief cameo appearance in the upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI. Speaking with Principle Magazine, Ogg suggested a 'pass the torch' moment, envisioning Trevor in a mundane or peculiar situation, like 'sitting on the toilet,' to signify the end of his story arc and introduce GTA 6's new protagonists, Jason and Lucia. He drew a parallel to Trevor's dramatic introduction in GTA V, where he brutally dispatched Johnny Klebitz from GTA IV. While acknowledging he's 'not a fan of all the reboots and sequels,' Ogg did express curiosity about how GTA V characters might appear with GTA 6's advanced technical capabilities. Furthermore, Ogg took the opportunity to debunk persistent online theories suggesting he dislikes GTA or the Trevor character. He affirmed his pride in Trevor and Rockstar's creation, clarifying that his previous comments about not liking being called 'Trevor' in real life were misinterpreted, as he simply found it odd for people to use a character's name instead of his own. The article also touches upon Ogg's successful acting career post-GTA V and the reportedly canceled Trevor-centric DLC for GTA V, which was a James Bond-like story, scrapped in favor of focusing on GTA Online.
