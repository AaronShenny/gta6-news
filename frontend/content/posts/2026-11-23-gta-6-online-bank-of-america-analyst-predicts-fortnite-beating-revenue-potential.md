# GTA 6 Online: Bank of America Analyst Predicts Fortnite-Beating Revenue Potential

The financial success of **Grand Theft Auto 6** is already significant, with 45 million copies sold and $4 billion in revenue. However, Bank of America stock analyst Omar Dessouky believes this is merely the prelude to an even greater financial boom, specifically from the next iteration of GTA Online.

Dessouky is highly optimistic, stating that GTA Online alone "can become a multibillion-dollar franchise, potentially larger than Fortnite," which currently generates up to $5 billion annually. This bullish forecast has led him to raise his price target for Take-Two Interactive's stock to $350, a substantial increase from its current trading price of around $210/share.

Despite Take-Two's stock facing a challenging year in 2026, long-term investors who bought shares when GTA 5 launched in 2013 would have seen a $10,000 investment grow to over $120,000 today. While Rockstar Games has not officially detailed the next GTA Online, it is widely anticipated. Reports suggest a new version, potentially alongside a PC edition of GTA 6, could launch in early 2027. The current GTA Online for GTA 5 remains a major revenue driver, and new monetization strategies, possibly including user-generated content marketplaces, are expected for the upcoming online experience.

GTA 6 launched on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, achieving impressive initial sales figures, including 32.5 million pre-purchases and $3.79 billion in revenue on day one, quickly reaching 44.9 million copies sold and over $4 billion in revenue within four days.
