# GTA 6's Sahara Arena Confirmed to Be Modeled After Miami's Kaseya Center

The latest 'Article of the Day' from the GTA Wiki confirms a significant detail regarding the upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI: the 'Sahara Arena' is directly modeled after Miami's Kaseya Center. This real-life landmark holds particular importance as it was one of the very first locations in Miami to be utilized in official GTA VI marketing campaigns. This revelation further solidifies the game's setting in a Vice City-inspired Miami and highlights Rockstar's attention to detail in replicating real-world environments for an immersive experience.

## Key Takeaways
- The 'Sahara Arena' in GTA VI is based on the Kaseya Center in Miami.
- The Kaseya Center was one of the first real-life Miami locations used for GTA VI marketing.
- This detail reinforces GTA VI's Vice City setting and Rockstar's commitment to real-world replication.

The Sahara Arena in Grand Theft Auto VI is based on the Kaseya Center, a prominent real-life sports and entertainment arena located in Miami, Florida.

The Kaseya Center is significant because it was one of the very first real-life locations in Miami that Rockstar Games utilized for GTA VI marketing, indicating its early importance in the game's development and promotion.
