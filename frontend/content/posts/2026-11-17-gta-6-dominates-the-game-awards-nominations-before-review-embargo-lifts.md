# GTA 6 Dominates The Game Awards Nominations Before Review Embargo Lifts

Grand Theft Auto 6 has made an unprecedented splash at The Game Awards, securing a staggering **eight nominations**—the most of any title this year—even before official reviews have gone live. With the ceremony scheduled for December 10th, Rockstar Games' highly anticipated action-adventure title is already a frontrunner for major accolades.

Its nominations include:
*   Game of the Year
*   Best Art Direction
*   Best Audio Design
*   Best Game Direction
*   Best Action/Adventure
*   Best Narrative
*   Best Performance (for Manni L. Perez as Lucia)
*   Best Score

This unusual situation is attributed to The Game Awards' eligibility cutoff date of November 20th, one day after the game's release. A jury comprising over 100 global media and influencer outlets, many of whom likely had extensive hands-on access to GTA 6, selected the nominees. While some find it awkward for a game to receive so many nominations without public critical reception, the article notes that GTA 6 has already sold approximately 16 million copies and generated nearly $2 billion in revenue pre-launch, indicating immense public anticipation and potential critical acclaim.
