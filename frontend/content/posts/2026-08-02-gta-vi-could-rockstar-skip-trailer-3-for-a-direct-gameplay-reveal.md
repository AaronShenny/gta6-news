# GTA VI: Could Rockstar Skip Trailer 3 for a Direct Gameplay Reveal?

A recent discussion on Reddit's r/GTA6 community, initiated by user u/Ambitious-Kitchen696, explores the possibility of Rockstar Games foregoing a traditional third cinematic trailer for Grand Theft Auto VI. With the game reportedly just '3 and half months away' from a major reveal (as per the original poster's context), the prolonged 'radio silence' since the last trailer, released over a year ago, has sparked debate. The speculation posits that Rockstar might jump directly to showcasing gameplay footage. This theory is supported by previous statements from Take-Two Interactive, which indicated a 'shorter marketing window' for the highly anticipated title. The Reddit user suggests that this approach could explain the lack of new trailers and predicts that a significant gameplay reveal might occur as late as September, rather than releasing another cinematic preview this month.
