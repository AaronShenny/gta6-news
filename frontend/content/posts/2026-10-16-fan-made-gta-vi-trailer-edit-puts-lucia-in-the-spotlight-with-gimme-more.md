# Fan-Made GTA VI Trailer Edit Puts Lucia in the Spotlight with 'Gimme More'

A creative fan on Reddit, u/Sk8d3r, has produced a unique edit of the Grand Theft Auto VI launch trailer. This version specifically highlights the character Lucia, playing Britney Spears' hit 'Gimme More' whenever she is on screen. The creator noted that this edit features fewer scenes of Lucia compared to a previously made version focusing on the character Jason, due to Lucia's less frequent appearances in the original trailer. Despite the difference in screen time, the fan hopes the community enjoys this character-focused rendition of the highly anticipated trailer.
