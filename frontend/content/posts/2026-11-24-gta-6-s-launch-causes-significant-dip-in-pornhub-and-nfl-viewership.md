# GTA 6's Launch Causes Significant Dip in Pornhub and NFL Viewership

Grand Theft Auto 6 has not only achieved a record-breaking launch with 32.5 million pre-orders and becoming the "biggest launch" in PlayStation history, but its release has also profoundly impacted other entertainment industries. Players on PS5 averaged an impressive 17.4 hours of gameplay in the first few days, leaving little time for other activities.

One of the most notable effects was on Pornhub, where US traffic saw a significant dip of 15.5% an hour after GTA 6's launch. This comes after a paradoxical 16.5% increase in traffic prior to the game's release, suggesting pre-launch anticipation. The game's success also triggered a massive 'Rule 34' effect, with GTA-related searches on Pornhub spiking by an astonishing 16,196%, accumulating up to 15 million queries on November 18 alone. Searches frequently targeted 'GTA 6,' 'GTA 6 strip club,' and specific characters like co-protagonist Lucia and the fan-favorite Mandy.

Beyond adult entertainment, Thursday Night Football also experienced its lowest viewership of the season on GTA 6's launch day, indicating a potential impact from the game's release. However, not all entertainment suffered; 'The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping' defied expectations, opening to a strong $120 million in the US, unaffected by the gaming phenomenon.
