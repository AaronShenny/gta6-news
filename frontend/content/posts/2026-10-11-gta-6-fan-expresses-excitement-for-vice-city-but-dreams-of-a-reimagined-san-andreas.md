# GTA 6: Fan Expresses Excitement for Vice City, But Dreams of a Reimagined San Andreas

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto fan on Reddit, u/Yung_booger, has shared a relatable sentiment regarding the upcoming GTA 6. While expressing genuine excitement for the game, particularly for its confirmed setting in a Florida-inspired Vice City, the user also admitted to a 'whisper' of a thought: a hypothetical scenario where GTA 5 was set in Vice City, and GTA 6 could have offered a fully reimagined and expanded San Andreas map, complete with updated versions of Las Venturas and San Fiero.
