# Dedicated Fan Crafts Custom GTA VI Puzzle from Popular 'LOVE Magazine' Image

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto VI enthusiast on Reddit, known as /u/noahvhang, has taken their fandom to a new level by commissioning a custom Ravensburger puzzle. The puzzle's design is based on a widely circulated image, often referred to as the 'LOVE magazine cover,' which has become a popular unofficial visual associated with GTA VI. The user meticulously edited out the original text at the bottom of the image to create a clean, puzzle-ready design. Their ambitious plan is to complete, glue, and frame this unique piece of memorabilia before the highly anticipated release of GTA VI, expressing eagerness to begin the project.
