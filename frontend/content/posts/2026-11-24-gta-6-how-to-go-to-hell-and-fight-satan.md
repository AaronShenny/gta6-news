# GTA 6: How To Go To Hell And Fight Satan

Grand Theft Auto 6 features a bizarre secret mission that allows players to descend into Hell and confront Satan, known as Beezleboss. This unique side quest, accessible in Chapter 2, offers exclusive cosmetics, a powerful shotgun, and a permanent health increase upon completion. Players can initiate this Doom-inspired encounter in **Port Gellhorn, Kelly County**, by following a specific set of steps at night.

To begin, players must head to the northern section of Port Gellhorn, specifically to a house dubbed the 'Circles of Hell' with an open garage. Inside, two NPCs will be rocking out. Interacting with them by selecting the dialogue options "Having fun?" and "It's good" will lead to a van appearance. Entering the van transports the player to a camping ground, where staring into a campfire triggers the descent into Hell.

Once in Hell, players are armed with a powerful shotgun to combat an army of demons and eventually face Satan. The Prince of Darkness summons reinforcements, but a few well-placed shots can overcome them. After defeating the devilish hordes and Satan, players awaken at the campsite, rewarded with the 'Circles of Hell' top, a new tattoo design, the 'Hellspawn Comshot 12' shotgun (found near Satan's throne), and a permanent health boost.

## FAQ
**How do I start the 'Go to Hell' mission in GTA 6?**
To start the mission, go to the northern section of Port Gellhorn, Kelly County, at night. Look for the 'Circles of Hell' house with an open garage, interact with the two NPCs inside, select specific dialogue options, enter the appearing van, and then stare into the campfire at the camping grounds.

**Where is the 'Circles of Hell' house located?**
The 'Circles of Hell' house is located in the northern section of Port Gellhorn, Kelly County, within GTA 6.

**What rewards do I get for completing the 'Go to Hell' mission?**
Upon completing the mission, you will receive the 'Circles of Hell' top, a new tattoo design, the 'Hellspawn Comshot 12' shotgun, and a permanent health boost.

**Do I need a specific character to play this mission?**
No, you can access and complete this mission with either Jason or Lucia, the two main protagonists of GTA 6.

**When is the best time to attempt this mission?**
It is recommended to tackle this mission once you've gotten your bearings in Chapter 2 of GTA 6, and specifically to start the mission at night, preferably around midnight.
