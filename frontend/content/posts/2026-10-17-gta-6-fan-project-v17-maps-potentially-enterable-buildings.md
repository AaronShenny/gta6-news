# GTA 6 Fan Project V17 Maps Potentially Enterable Buildings

A dedicated fan of Grand Theft Auto 6, Reddit user u/juicewrldfan4life999, has unveiled the 17th version of their 'GTA 6 mapping project'. This ambitious community effort aims to visually categorize buildings within the rumored GTA 6 map based on their potential enterability. According to the project's legend, any building marked with blue is considered '100% enterable'. The creator also clarified that buildings depicted in gray are of unknown status regarding enterability; while not confirmed as accessible, they are also not definitively marked as inaccessible. This fan-made map provides an interesting speculative look at the potential interactive elements of the upcoming highly anticipated title.
