# GTA 6 Update Imminent? Insider Points to August Reveal and On-Track Launch

According to 'NateTheHate', a highly credible insider known for accurate leaks, significant new details regarding Rockstar's highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6* are expected to drop in August. The insider specifically stated he anticipates seeing 'a fair bit' of GTA 6 content next month, reassuring fans that despite minimal marketing so far, the game is not delayed and remains on schedule for its November 19 release. He emphasized that Rockstar is adhering to its marketing plan, and there's no reason for concern.

This August timeline aligns strategically with Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar's parent company, which is set to release its next earnings report on August 7. Notably, this report will break tradition by being released on a Friday and before market open, fueling speculation for a major announcement. Furthermore, Take-Two had previously indicated that GTA 6's marketing campaign would commence in the summer, making August an opportune moment.

Preorders for *GTA 6* have been available since June 25, with the standard edition priced at $80 and a premium version offering extras for $100. While a new trailer hasn't been released in over a year, Rockstar has shared numerous new screenshots. The game is confirmed for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with reports also suggesting testing for a potential Switch 2 version.
