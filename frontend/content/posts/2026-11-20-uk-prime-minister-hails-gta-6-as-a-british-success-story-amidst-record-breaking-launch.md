# UK Prime Minister Hails GTA 6 as a 'British Success Story' Amidst Record-Breaking Launch

UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham has officially lauded Rockstar Games as a 'British success story' in the wake of *Grand Theft Auto 6*'s phenomenal launch. A spokesperson from his office highlighted Rockstar North's Edinburgh base as the reason for this designation, also noting the original 1997 *Grand Theft Auto* was developed in Edinburgh by DMA Design. Despite the global effort involving thousands of developers, the UK connection remains a point of national pride.

However, when asked if the Prime Minister himself would delve into the highly anticipated title, his office stated that Burnham is too busy to play *GTA 6*.

The game, which launched on November 19, has already shattered records, reportedly selling nearly 25 million copies and generating an astounding $2 billion in revenue by its release day. Critically, *GTA 6* has achieved a remarkable 99 on Metacritic, placing it alongside the legendary *Ocarina of Time* for the highest aggregate review score ever recorded for a video game.

Currently a strictly single-player experience, industry observers anticipate Rockstar will eventually introduce an online component. Reports suggest a new *GTA Online* for *GTA 6* and a PC edition of the game could both arrive in 2027.
