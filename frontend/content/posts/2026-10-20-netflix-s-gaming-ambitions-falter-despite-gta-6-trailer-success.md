# Netflix's Gaming Ambitions Falter Despite GTA 6 Trailer Success

Netflix's long-standing efforts to establish a strong foothold in the video game industry are reportedly falling significantly short of internal targets, despite some notable successes like hosting the **GTA 6 Extended Look trailer**. According to a Netflix memo seen by The Wall Street Journal, only 3.5% of Netflix's global subscribers engage with its gaming offerings monthly, a figure 10% below the company's 2026 engagement goals. This represents a modest increase from the less than 1% reported in 2022.

Initially, Netflix had grand ambitions for AAA game development, even hiring prominent industry talent. However, the company has since scaled back, now prioritizing mobile, social, and tie-in games based on its popular franchises like *Stranger Things* and *Love is Blind*, alongside commissioning new titles such as *Unhinged* and *The Conjuring: Unspoken*. Co-CEO Ted Sarandos has affirmed Netflix's commitment to gaming, viewing it as a crucial tool for subscriber engagement and brand retention after viewers finish a show.

A significant bright spot amidst these struggles was Netflix's decision to host the **Grand Theft Auto VI Extended Look trailer**. This move reportedly drove an impressive 100,000 new Netflix sign-ups in the U.S. alone within a single day and garnered massive viewership, demonstrating the immense draw of high-profile gaming content. Netflix has a prior relationship with Take-Two, Rockstar's parent company, having previously brought *Grand Theft Auto* titles to its platform, and *Red Dead Redemption* remains available.

These revelations about Netflix's gaming performance come at a turbulent time for the company, marked by recent executive departures, over 1,000 layoffs (the largest in its history), and a substantial drop in stock value, as investors push for strategic changes.
