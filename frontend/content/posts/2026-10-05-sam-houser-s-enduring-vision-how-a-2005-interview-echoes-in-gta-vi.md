# Sam Houser's Enduring Vision: How a 2005 Interview Echoes in GTA VI

A recent Reddit post by u/Educational-Hope-495 has sparked discussion by drawing parallels between a 2005 interview with Rockstar Games co-founder Sam Houser and the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI. In the original interview, Houser articulated Rockstar's ambitious goal of crafting deeply realistic, immersive, and 'fully living and breathing' open worlds, seamlessly interwoven with compelling narratives and storytelling. The user observes that, almost two decades later, the development of GTA VI seems to be the culmination and full realization of this long-standing vision. The post also features a custom-made 'dope edit' by the user, combining snippets from the interview with Raf's 'Self Control' – a track whose Laura Brannigan cover famously featured in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City – further enhancing the nostalgic and forward-looking sentiment.

## Key Takeaways
- A 2005 interview with Sam Houser outlined Rockstar's vision for creating realistic, immersive, and narrative-rich open worlds.
