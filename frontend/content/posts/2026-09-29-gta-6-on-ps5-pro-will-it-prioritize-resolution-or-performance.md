# GTA 6 on PS5 Pro: Will it Prioritize Resolution or Performance?

A recent community discussion highlights the fervent anticipation surrounding how Grand Theft Auto 6 will leverage the rumored capabilities of the PlayStation 5 Pro. According to various leaks and rumors circulating, the PS5 Pro-enhanced version of GTA 6 is primarily expected to deliver an increased resolution, potentially running at native 4K. This would be an upgrade from the base PS5, which is rumored to render the game at 1440p, then upscale it to 4K. While a native 4K experience is certainly appealing, the original poster of the discussion expresses a strong preference for performance enhancements, such as a stable 60 or even 40 frames per second, over a mere resolution bump. The sentiment suggests that a significant portion of the player base might find a resolution-only upgrade insufficient to justify investing in a PS5 Pro, potentially leading to disappointment. The article concludes with a plea for Rockstar Games to officially confirm the specific enhancements for GTA 6 on the PS5 Pro, allowing players to make informed decisions about potential console upgrades.

## Key Takeaways
- Rumors suggest GTA 6 on PS5 Pro will primarily offer native 4K resolution.
- The base PS5 is rumored to run GTA 6 at 1440p, upscaled to 4K.
- Many players, including the article's author, prefer performance upgrades (higher FPS) over just a resolution increase.
- There's concern that a resolution-only enhancement might disappoint players who upgrade to a PS5 Pro.
- Players are urging Rockstar Games to officially confirm the PS5 Pro enhancements for GTA 6.

## FAQ
**What are the rumored PS5 Pro enhancements for GTA 6?**
Leaks suggest that GTA 6 on the PS5 Pro will likely increase resolution, potentially running at native 4K, compared to the base PS5's rumored 1440p upscaled to 4K.

**Will GTA 6 run at a higher frame rate on the PS5 Pro?**
Current rumors indicate a focus on resolution rather than significant frame rate increases (like 60fps or 40fps), though many players hope for better performance.

**Why are players asking Rockstar for official confirmation?**
Players want Rockstar to confirm the exact enhancements to understand if upgrading to a PS5 Pro will be worthwhile, especially if they prioritize performance over resolution.
