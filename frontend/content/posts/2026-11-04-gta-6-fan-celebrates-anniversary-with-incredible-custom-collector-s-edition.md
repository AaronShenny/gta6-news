# GTA 6 Fan Celebrates Anniversary with Incredible Custom Collector's Edition

In a heartwarming display of dedication and craftsmanship, a Grand Theft Auto 6 enthusiast, u/andeluiven, was recently surprised by their girlfriend with a custom-made collector's edition to celebrate their 7th anniversary. The unique gift, shared on Reddit, showcases the girlfriend's impressive artistic skills and passion for the upcoming title.The centerpiece of this special edition is a meticulously crafted 3D model of the 'Macca alligator,' a nod to the vibrant world of Vice City. While initially stating she created the model from scratch, the girlfriend later clarified that it involved significant editing of an existing 3D model. She completely re-envisioned parts like the base, arms, and split the glass elements, before hand-coloring the entire piece. This personal touch highlights the deep connection between the couple and their shared excitement for GTA 6.The post quickly garnered attention within the GTA 6 community, with many praising the thoughtful and creative gesture. It serves as a testament to the passionate fanbase eagerly awaiting the game's release and the imaginative ways fans are celebrating their anticipation.
