# Blackpink's Lisa Unveils Unique PS5 DualSense Controller and Exclusive Merchandise

Sony has officially unveiled a new limited-edition PS5 DualSense controller, born from its ongoing partnership with Blackpink member and actor Lisa. This unique controller, part of a broader merchandise collection, stands out from previous game-themed designs with its highly personal aesthetic.The Lisa Limited Edition DualSense incorporates elements deeply meaningful to the artist. Its touchpad features an illustration of bangs and eyes, referencing Lisa's iconic look, complemented by stars and "LL" lettering set against her preferred Chroma Pearl DualSense colorway. The design, while distinct, has been humorously likened to a 'Live, Laugh, Love' aesthetic for controllers.Priced at $85, preorders for the controller will commence on October 2 via the PlayStation Direct store, with a wider retail rollout slated for October 30. Consumers should be aware that scalpers may target this release, potentially driving up prices.Beyond the controller, fans can also acquire a range of PlayStation merchandise, including T-shirts, hoodies, caps, and keychains, all adorned with Lisa's custom illustrations.This collaboration is part of Sony's 'PlayStation Playmakers' program, which has previously featured celebrities like LeBron James. However, the announcement arrives amidst a backdrop of significant public backlash Sony has faced throughout 2026, stemming from PS5 price hikes, the controversial decision to cease disc-based game production by 2028, the closure of Bluepoint studio, and the cancellation of funding for Hideo Kojima's Physint project.

## Key Takeaways
- Sony and Blackpink's Lisa have collaborated on a new limited-edition PS5 DualSense controller.
- The controller's design features Lisa's signature bangs and eyes on the touchpad, stars, 'LL' lettering, and a Chroma Pearl colorway.
- It will be available for $85, with preorders starting October 2 on PlayStation Direct and a general retail release on October 30.
- A collection of Lisa-designed PlayStation merchandise, including T-shirts, hoodies, caps, and keychains, will also be released.
- This partnership is part of Sony's 'PlayStation Playmakers' program, which includes other celebrity collaborations.
- The announcement comes amid significant public backlash against Sony in 2026 regarding PS5 price hikes, disc game cessation, studio closures, and project cancellations.
