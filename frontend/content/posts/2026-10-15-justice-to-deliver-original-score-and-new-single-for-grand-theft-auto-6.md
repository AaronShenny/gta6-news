# Justice to Deliver Original Score and New Single for Grand Theft Auto 6

Rockstar Games has announced that acclaimed French electronic music duo Justice will be crafting the original score for the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6*. The game, set for a November 19 release on PS5 and Xbox Series X, promises an immersive audio experience.

Justice, comprising Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay, will also contribute a new single, "Flower in a Hurricane," to *Grand Theft Auto 6: The Album*. This track will feature collaborations with other notable artists including Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. The duo is known for their Grammy-nominated work and chart-topping hits that blend rock and disco themes, promising a dynamic backdrop for Vice City.

Beyond Justice's original compositions, *GTA 6* will continue the series' tradition of diverse in-game radio stations and podcasts, which Rockstar describes as the "next evolution of in-game radio." Players will be able to enjoy these audio experiences both while driving and on foot. The official game album will boast 34 original songs, with additional confirmed contributions from music legends like Keith Richards and Travis Scott.

While the initial launch is exclusive to consoles, a PC port is reportedly expected sooner than previous installments.
