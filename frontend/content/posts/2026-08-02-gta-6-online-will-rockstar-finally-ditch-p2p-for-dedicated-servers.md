# GTA 6 Online: Will Rockstar Finally Ditch P2P for Dedicated Servers?

A concerned Reddit user, u/Annyxd, has sparked a discussion regarding the potential networking infrastructure of the rumored Grand Theft Auto 6 Online. Drawing from frustrating experiences with Grand Theft Auto V Online, where frequent package loss on their end leads to complete session disconnections, u/Annyxd postulates that GTA V Online's peer-to-peer (P2P) networking model is a significant contributing factor. They highlight that similar internet issues in other games result in minor lag rather than full disconnections, suggesting GTA V's P2P system is less forgiving. The user expresses a strong desire for Grand Theft Auto 6 Online to feature improved connectivity, specifically advocating for a shift to dedicated servers. They also note that Red Dead Online, another Rockstar title, appears to utilize a P2P model, reinforcing their concerns about the company's approach to online networking. The sentiment reflects a common hope among players for a more stable and robust online experience in future Rockstar titles.

## Key Takeaways
- A Reddit user is frustrated with GTA 5 Online's P2P connectivity issues.
- Frequent package loss leads to full disconnections in GTA 5 Online, unlike other games.
- The user hopes GTA 6 Online will adopt dedicated servers for better stability.
- Red Dead Online is also cited as having P2P-based networking.
