# Spectrum Offers Free Gig Speed Boost to Tackle GTA 6's Massive 212GB Download

Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, bringing with it a colossal 212GB file size. To help its customers cope with this massive download, internet provider Spectrum is rolling out a "Free Gig Speed Lift" promotion.

Available from November 9 to November 30, this offer promises a temporary, no-cost internet speed upgrade. Qualifying Spectrum customers with compatible equipment can opt-in via the My Spectrum app to boost their download speeds to 1Gbps. Those already subscribed to a 1Gbps plan will see their speeds temporarily increased to an impressive 2Gbps. Spectrum claims this boost will allow users to download GTA 6 in less than 30 minutes.

Beyond the game's launch, the promotion also conveniently covers the Thanksgiving holiday, as well as the Black Friday and Cyber Monday shopping events. After November 30, the speed increase will automatically revert to the customer's original plan without any required action. Spectrum's strategy behind this free upgrade is to showcase the benefits of higher speeds, potentially enticing customers to subscribe to a permanent, faster internet plan.
