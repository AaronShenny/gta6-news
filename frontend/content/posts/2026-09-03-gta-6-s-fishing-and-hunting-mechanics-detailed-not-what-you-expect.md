# GTA 6's Fishing and Hunting Mechanics Detailed: Not What You Expect

Rockstar Games has officially revealed new details regarding fishing and hunting mechanics in the upcoming *Grand Theft Auto 6*, confirming they will operate differently than anticipated. According to Rob Nelson of Rockstar North, speaking with YouTuber TGG, fishing in *GTA 6* will be strictly **catch-and-release**. Players will not be able to keep fish, as Nelson stated this mechanic 'didn't fit this game's' modern setting, unlike *Red Dead Redemption 2*'s 1899 context where cooking and eating fish was relevant. Instead, fishing in *GTA 6* is designed for enjoyment, challenges, and collecting.

Conversely, hunting in *GTA 6* serves as a method for players to **earn money**. This system is unlocked through an in-game friend of protagonist Jason, though this character has not yet been seen in any *GTA 6* footage. Both hunting and fishing will allow players to 'inspect' creatures, a feature borrowed from *Red Dead Redemption 2*. The game will also include interactable dogs that players can pet, scold, and inspect. *GTA 6* is set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a *GTA 6*-themed DualSense controller also announced.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 fishing is strictly catch-and-release; players cannot keep fish.
- Fishing in GTA 6 is primarily for fun, challenges, and collectibles.
- Hunting in GTA 6 is a confirmed method for players to make money.
- The hunting system is unlocked via a friend of Jason, not yet revealed.
- Players can 'inspect' fish, animals, and even dogs, similar to Red Dead Redemption 2.
- GTA 6 launches November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## FAQ
**Can you keep fish you catch in GTA 6?**
No, fishing in GTA 6 is strictly catch-and-release. You cannot keep any fish you catch.

**What is the purpose of fishing in GTA 6 if you can't keep the fish?**
Fishing is intended for fun, completing challenges, and collecting items. Rockstar noted it didn't fit the modern game's setting to keep and consume fish.

**How does hunting work in GTA 6?**
Hunting in GTA 6 is a way for players to earn money. The system is unlocked through a friend of the character Jason.

**Can players interact with other animals in GTA 6?**
Yes, players can inspect creatures, including fish and hunted animals. The game also features dogs that players can pet, scold, and inspect.

**When is GTA 6 being released?**
GTA 6 is scheduled to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
