# Is GTA 6 Losing Its Edge? A Player Expresses Discontent Over Rumored Artist Collaborations

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto fan has voiced strong concerns about the perceived direction of GTA 6, leading them to believe they are no longer the game's target audience. The core of their apprehension stems from rumored collaborations with contemporary artists, particularly those labeled as 'TikTok artists' and mainstream figures like Travis Scott. The user criticizes these artists for producing 'slop en masse' and fears they will be integrated into the game in a non-satirical capacity, primarily to promote their music and personal brand, rather than playing exaggerated, satirical versions of themselves as seen in previous GTA titles. They cite the Dr. Dre integration in GTA Online as an example of this 'out of place' trend. While acknowledging that GTA 6 aims to depict modern times, the player finds the current artistic landscape 'bleak' and expresses disappointment if the game foregoes its signature satire for straightforward endorsements. The post suggests that the excitement for these collaborations primarily comes from younger audiences, likening the anticipated 'Travis concert event' to something found in Fortnite, signaling a potential shift in the game's identity. Despite admitting that the game 'looks great,' the user finds the numerous real-world brand and celebrity collaborations 'off' for a Grand Theft Auto experience.
