# GTA 6 Online: Players Demand More Passive Business Management

A recent post on the r/GTA6 subreddit by user /u/Polacat highlights a major frustration with GTA 5 Online's business mechanics, expressing a fervent wish that these issues do not carry over to GTA 6 Online. The core complaint revolves around the tedious nature of businesses, which often require constant player intervention despite hiring expensive staff. 

The user cites several examples: the Nightclub's security guard, Marcel, who frequently asks the player to handle minor disturbances; the need for players to personally deliver cars from their car wash; and the inability of hired Salvage Yard staff to tow vehicles. The post emphasizes that if players invest 'half a million or more' in employees, those employees should provide tangible value by handling tasks independently. 

Ultimately, the community sentiment, as echoed by the post, is a strong call for GTA 6 Online's businesses to be 'MUCH, MUCH more passive,' allowing owners to delegate responsibilities and not be tethered to managing every minor detail.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 5 Online businesses are criticized for being overly tedious and requiring constant player presence.
- Hired staff in GTA 5 Online are often perceived as ineffective, failing to manage tasks independently.
- Examples include Nightclub security, car wash deliveries, and Salvage Yard towing.
- Players desire GTA 6 Online businesses to be significantly more passive, with employees handling more responsibilities.
- The community expects hired staff to provide actual value for their substantial cost.
