## Key Takeaways

## FAQ
**What is the core theme of GTA 6?**
GTA 6 aims to reflect and amplify the current state of the world, offering an 'over the top,' more insane, and in-your-face experience, according to Senior VP of Narrative Rupert Humphries.

**Who are the protagonists in GTA 6?**
The protagonists are Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, who are described as polar opposites in a Bonnie and Clyde-style narrative. Lucia is an ambitious go-getter, while Jason is bound by life's circumstances.

**How has the 'wanted' system changed in GTA 6?**
Getting out of trouble in GTA 6 will be more challenging, as cops are smarter and more persistent. Players will need to be more cunning, and actions will have more significant consequences.

**When will GTA 6 be released and on what platforms?**
GTA 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

**Is there a Collector's Edition for GTA 6?**
Yes, a $400 GTA 6 Collector's Edition has been announced, but it notably does not include the game itself, instead offering various trinkets.
