# GTA VI Leak: Car Side Mirrors Feature Dynamic Damage and Can Fall Off

A recent Reddit post circulating within the GTA 6 community has highlighted a seemingly minor yet significant detail about the upcoming game's vehicle damage system: car side mirrors will be able to get damaged and even fall off. The post, which includes a visual reference, points to an 'obviously more dynamic damage system than simpler GTA V'.

This detail, while small, implies a much deeper and more realistic physics and damage model for vehicles in Grand Theft Auto VI. In previous titles, vehicle damage was often more generalized, with parts deforming but rarely detaching with such granularity. The ability for side mirrors to break off suggests that Rockstar Games is pushing for a higher level of environmental and vehicular interaction, enhancing immersion and realism during chases, collisions, and general gameplay. This could extend to other vehicle components, promising a more visceral and unpredictable driving experience.
