# GTA 6's Missing Mission Replay: Rockstar Explains Why It Felt "Alien and Disconnected"

Grand Theft Auto 6, despite its massive launch success with 32.5 million prepurchases and nearly $4 billion in day-one revenue, has notably forgone a straightforward mission replay feature, a staple in previous titles like GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption II. According to Senior Design Director Scott Butchard, this decision was a deliberate one, made after considering the feature "early on in development."

Butchard explained to Kotaku that as the game's narrative took shape, replaying missions began to feel "at odds with the story we were trying to tell and felt a bit alien and disconnected." He further elaborated that the ability to replay missions "really diminished the choices you had made," suggesting a focus on maintaining the weight and consequence of player decisions within the unfolding narrative.

While a dedicated replay option is absent, players are not entirely without recourse. Missions can still be replayed by loading an earlier save file. Additionally, if a mission isn't going as planned, players can access the in-game menu to select "retry, quit, or restart" during active gameplay.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 does not feature a direct, straightforward mission replay option, unlike GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption II.
- Rockstar's Senior Design Director Scott Butchard confirmed the feature was intentionally omitted.
- The decision was made because mission replay felt "alien and disconnected" and "at odds with the story" GTA 6 aims to tell.
- Butchard stated that replaying missions "really diminished the choices" players had made.
- Players can still replay missions by loading earlier save files or using in-mission retry/restart options.
- GTA 6 achieved immense commercial success with 32.5 million pre-purchases and approximately $4 billion in day-one revenue.

## FAQ
**Can you replay missions in GTA 6?**
No, Grand Theft Auto 6 does not include a direct, straightforward mission replay feature from a menu, unlike previous Rockstar titles.

**Why did Rockstar remove the mission replay feature from GTA 6?**
Senior Design Director Scott Butchard stated that the feature was removed because it felt "alien and disconnected" from the story and diminished the impact of player choices within the narrative.

**How can I replay a mission in GTA 6 if I want to?**
You can replay missions by loading an earlier save file from before the mission. Additionally, if you're currently in a mission, you can use the in-game menu to select "retry, quit, or restart."

**Did previous Grand Theft Auto games have mission replay?**
Yes, GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption II both offered a dedicated mission replay option.
