# Dan Houser Explains Why He's Steering Clear of GTA 6 and Other Open-World Games

Rockstar Games co-founder Dan Houser, who departed the company in 2020 to establish Absurd Ventures, has shared his unique approach to creative inspiration, particularly regarding Grand Theft Auto 6 and other open-world games. In a recent interview with Variety, Houser stated he deliberately avoids consuming content like the GTA 6 'Extended Look' trailer and generally refrains from playing open-world titles.

His reasoning stems from a desire to prevent creative disruption. Houser explained that as someone dedicated to developing open-world games and innovative marketing strategies, he doesn't need to observe competitors' work, preferring to focus on generating new ideas rather than comparing his efforts. He finds playing such games distracting, often deconstructing their strengths and weaknesses in real-time, which interferes with his creative process as a 'creative director' rather than a 'game designer.'

This method isn't new for Houser; he previously avoided Western films while developing Red Dead Redemption and HBO's 'The Sopranos' during the formulation of the GTA series, fearing they would overly influence his work. While he personally avoids direct consumption, his team at Absurd Ventures is tasked with examining industry trends. Houser is currently developing multiple projects at Absurd Ventures, including an upcoming open-world comedy game and a dystopian sci-fi title about rogue AI in game development.

Despite his distance from GTA 6, Houser considers being forever associated with the franchise a high compliment, though he hopes his new ventures will achieve independent success. Notably, his brother and Rockstar co-founder, Sam Houser, remains at Rockstar and was involved in GTA 6's creation. GTA 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
