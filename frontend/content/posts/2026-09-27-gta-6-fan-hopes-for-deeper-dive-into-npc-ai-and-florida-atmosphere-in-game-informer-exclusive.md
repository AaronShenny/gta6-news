# GTA 6 Fan Hopes for Deeper Dive into NPC AI and Florida Atmosphere in Game Informer Exclusive

A Reddit user, 'Sea_Philosopher1080', has shared their 'unpopular opinion' regarding a potential Game Informer cover story on Grand Theft Auto VI. While expressing excitement for the traditional GTA Game Informer feature, the user hopes the interview will move beyond discussions solely focused on the weather system, wildlife, and general 'Florida feel'—aspects reportedly hinted at by Marcus Stewart. Instead, the fan is particularly keen on a deeper exploration of the game's Non-Player Characters (NPCs). They express a 'hyper-fixation' on understanding the density of NPCs, their unique characteristics, and whether they will possess complex day-and-night routines similar to those seen in Red Dead Redemption 2. The user specifically desires insights into the technology powering these advanced NPC behaviors and the methods Rockstar plans to use to authentically capture the broader atmosphere of Florida within the game world. The post concludes by inviting other community members to share their own hopes for what a Game Informer exclusive on GTA VI should reveal.
