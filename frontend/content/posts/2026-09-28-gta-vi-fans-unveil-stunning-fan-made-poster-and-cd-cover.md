# GTA VI Fans Unveil Stunning Fan-Made Poster and CD Cover

The Grand Theft Auto VI community is buzzing with excitement over a high-quality, fan-made poster and CD cover for the highly anticipated game. Shared on Reddit by user /u/Acrobatic-Onion-779, the artwork showcases a creative vision for GTA VI's aesthetic.

The creator explicitly stated that the artwork was made without the use of artificial intelligence, though they noted that upscaling might have been involved. They generously offered the community permission to use the designs, requesting only that proper attribution be given and that users refrain from claiming the work as their own. This fan creation highlights the immense anticipation and creativity within the GTA community as they await official announcements and assets for Grand Theft Auto VI.
