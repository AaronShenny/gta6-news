# GTA 5 Set to Exit PlayStation Plus Days Before GTA 6's Highly Anticipated Launch

Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 5, a staple on PlayStation Plus, is reportedly scheduled for removal from the service for Extra and Premium subscribers on November 17. This timing, just two days ahead of Grand Theft Auto 6's launch on November 19, strongly suggests a strategic move by Sony and Rockstar to encourage players to transition to the new title. Users on Reddit have reported receiving notifications confirming the removal date, with some notices even including a direct link to the GTA 6 preorder page. The message from Sony explicitly encourages players to either experience or revisit GTA 5's story before the new Vice City adventure begins. This move mirrors past instances where GTA 5 has rotated in and out of Xbox Game Pass.
