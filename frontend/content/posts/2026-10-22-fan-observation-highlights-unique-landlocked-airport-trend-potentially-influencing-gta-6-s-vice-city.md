# Fan Observation Highlights Unique Landlocked Airport Trend Potentially Influencing GTA 6's Vice City

A keen-eyed Grand Theft Auto enthusiast on Reddit has brought to light an intriguing design pattern within the series regarding international airports. The observation, shared by user u/darkwoods625, points out that the airports in Vice City (from *Grand Theft Auto: Vice City*) and Las Venturas (from *Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas*) are uniquely landlocked, making them the only two international airports in the entire franchise not directly bordered by an ocean. This contrasts sharply with more recent iterations like Los Santos International Airport (LSIA) in *Grand Theft Auto V* and Francis International Airport in *Grand Theft Auto IV*, both of which feature prominent oceanic borders. While the original post humorously acknowledges the author's 'too much free time,' this detail offers a fascinating look into Rockstar's environmental design choices across different eras of the series. The discussion, originating from the r/GTA6 subreddit, prompts contemplation on whether this 'landlocked' design characteristic might return or be reinterpreted in the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto VI*, especially given its rumored return to a Vice City-inspired setting. This subtle observation highlights the depth of detail fans often uncover and the intricate world-building present in the Grand Theft Auto universe.
