# Ocarina Of Time Is 2026’s Best-Selling Physical Game In The US, But GTA 6 Is Coming

Nintendo's _The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time_ remake for the Switch 2 has emerged as the best-selling physical game of 2026 in the US, according to Circana. The title achieved over 1 million physical units sold in its launch week, specifically within just two days (November 5-7), marking the first game since 2023's _Tears of the Kingdom_ to reach this milestone. The physical edition, which includes a cartridge, retails for $70, while its digital counterpart is $60. Globally, the _Ocarina of Time_ remake sold over 5 million copies in its first four days.
