# GTA 6: Fan Debate Ignites Over Potential Removal of Controversial Feature

A recent Reddit thread initiated by user /u/LukeD1992 has sparked a lively debate among the Grand Theft Auto community regarding the potential removal of a long-standing, albeit controversial, feature in the upcoming GTA 6: the ability to hire prostitutes.The core of the discussion centers on the evolving narrative structure and character dynamics expected in GTA 6. Previous installments often featured single protagonists or those in highly dysfunctional relationships, making the inclusion of sex workers a less jarring element within the game's world. However, with the rumored strong bond between GTA 6's dual protagonists, Jason and Lucia, the user posits that allowing both characters to engage in such activities would feel narratively 'weird,' unless Rockstar Games explicitly establishes a very open relationship between them.While the original poster personally expressed indifference to the feature's potential removal, having rarely utilized it themselves, they acknowledge that this change could significant backlash from 'purists' who value the series' traditional elements. The discussion draws parallels to previous heated debates within the gaming community, such as the discourse surrounding the inclusion or exclusion of sex minigames in the God of War remakes.

## Key Takeaways
- A fan-initiated discussion questions the future of the prostitution feature in GTA 6.
- The debate stems from the rumored strong relationship between protagonists Jason and Lucia, suggesting a narrative conflict.
- The feature's removal might be considered to align with updated character dynamics, deviating from past GTA titles.
- While some players may not miss the feature, its removal could face opposition from 'purists' within the community.
