# GTA 6's Minimalist UI and Map Approach: A Refreshing Change for Some Players

A dedicated Reddit user, u/jd_junior057, has voiced a strong preference for what they perceive as a 'minimal style of UI' and a 'minimalism' in the map design for the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6. The user, who spent five years playing GTA V, highlights how this simplified approach aligns well with a limited item-carrying system, similar to what's found in Red Dead Redemption 2. When discussing the map, the user draws a parallel to Elden Ring, appreciating a less cluttered interface. They contrast this with their experience in GTA V, where they often relied solely on waypoints, leading to a lack of familiarity with the game's locations and city designs. For this player, the potential shift in GTA 6 to emphasize the city's visual experience over a detailed map interface is a welcome change.

## Key Takeaways
- A Reddit user prefers a minimalist UI and map design for GTA 6.
- The simplified UI is seen as complementary to a limited item-carrying system, akin to Red Dead Redemption 2.
- The minimalist map design is compared favorably to Elden Ring, promoting exploration and memorization.
- The user found GTA V's map experience forgettable, relying heavily on waypoints.
- The preference is for GTA 6 to prioritize the visual immersion of the city over an extensive map interface.
