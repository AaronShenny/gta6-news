# GTA 6 Review Embargo: When to Expect First Impressions Before Launch

Anticipation is building for Grand Theft Auto 6, set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Gamers eager for critical impressions will be pleased to learn that reviews are expected to be released *before* the official launch date, according to a report by Insider Gaming. While the exact timing remains unconfirmed, insider NateTheHate suggests that review copies will be sent to journalists by the end of October.

However, it's important to note that this trend doesn't guarantee the same approach for GTA 6.
