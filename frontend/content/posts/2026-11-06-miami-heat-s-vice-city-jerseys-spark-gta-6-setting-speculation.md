# Miami Heat's 'Vice City' Jerseys Spark GTA 6 Setting Speculation

The Miami Heat basketball team has revealed striking new "Vice City" themed jerseys, immediately capturing the attention of the Grand Theft Auto community and fueling speculation about the setting for the highly anticipated GTA 6. The vibrant, neon-infused designs prominently feature the "Vice City" aesthetic, a clear nod to the Miami-inspired fictional metropolis popularized by Rockstar Games' Grand Theft Auto: Vice City.

Two distinct versions of the jersey have been unveiled: the "Heat-branded" on-court jerseys, reserved for player use during games, and the "Vice City swingman" jerseys, which will be commercially available for fans to purchase. While these are official NBA and Miami Heat merchandise, the prominent use of the "Vice City" moniker and its distinctive visual style has intensified long-standing rumors that GTA 6 will indeed return to a modern-day Vice City, building excitement among players eager for details on the game's location.
