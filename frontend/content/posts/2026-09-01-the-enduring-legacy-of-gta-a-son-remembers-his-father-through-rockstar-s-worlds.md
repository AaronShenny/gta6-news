# The Enduring Legacy of GTA: A Son Remembers His Father Through Rockstar's Worlds

The article, titled "Dad Would've Loved This Game," is a deeply personal and poignant reflection by Reddit user /u/BisfoBama, who shares how the Grand Theft Auto series created an indelible bond with his late father. Having just turned 29, the user recounts a childhood steeped in Rockstar Games, an introduction facilitated by his dad who passed away a year prior.

His earliest memories include playing *Grand Theft Auto: Vice City*, where his father would print out cheat codes for unlimited weapons, allowing him to enjoy the game's chaotic freedom. The bond deepened with *Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas*, a game they frequently played together in two-player mode. His father particularly loved the music, with "Hollywood Swinging" by Kool and the Gang being a favorite. The in-game radio humor also provided shared laughter.

As the user progressed to *Grand Theft Auto IV* and *V* on Xbox 360 with friends, his father continued to enjoy *San Andreas* on PS2. The post expresses a profound sense of loss, as the user now faces the release of another GTA installment—presumably *Grand Theft Auto VI*—without his father. He articulates a heartfelt wish for the return of a retro radio station, a nod to the nostalgic sounds of their shared past, and a hope for a future reunion with his dad. The piece concludes with a moving thank you to both Rockstar Games for the memories and his father for the shared experiences.
