# GTA 6 Secures Major Nominations at The Game Awards 2026 Amidst Snub Debates

The nominations for The Game Awards 2026 have officially been announced, featuring a lineup of highly anticipated and critically acclaimed titles. Among the prominent nominees is **Grand Theft Auto 6**, which has garnered recognition in two significant categories:

*   **Game of the Year**: GTA 6 stands alongside an impressive roster including *007 First Light*, *Control Resonant*, *Phantom Blade Zero*, *Resident Evil Requiem*, and *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (2026)*.
*   **Best Performance**: Manni L. Perez receives a nomination for her compelling portrayal of Lucia Caminos in GTA 6.

While celebrating these well-deserved nods, the article also spotlights several perceived 'snubs' across various categories. For instance, *House House's co-op adventure Big Walk* and *Rebel Wolves' vampire RPG The Blood of Dawnwalker* were notably absent from the Game of the Year contention, despite their appearances in other categories. In the Best Performance category, the journalist argues that Nick Apostolides (Leon Kennedy in *Resident Evil Requiem*), Sean Durrie (Dylan Faden in *Control Resonant*), and Will de Renzy-Martin (Coen in *The Blood of Dawnwalker*) were deserving of nominations.

Further omissions highlighted include:
*   ***Marathon***: Believed to be a strong candidate for Best Art Direction and Best Action Game.
*   ***Overwatch*** and ***WoW/WoW: Forever***: Considered strong contenders for Best Ongoing Game.
*   ***Denshattack***: Its exceptional soundtrack was deemed worthy of a Best Score and Music nomination.
*   ***Castlevania: Belmont's Curse***: Potentially overlooked in categories such as Best Action/Adventure Game, Best Art Direction, and Best Narrative.

The Game Awards 2026 ceremony is scheduled to air live on December 10 at 4:30 PM PT / 7:30 PM ET, where the winners will finally be revealed.
