# Reggie Fils-Aimé, Former Nintendo President, Gets Caught Up In GTA 6's Mandy Frenzy

The gaming community is buzzing after former Nintendo of America President, Reggie Fils-Aimé, chimed in on the phenomenon surrounding the controversial Grand Theft Auto 6 character, Mandy. The discussion began when Krysta Yang, a former Nintendo marketing manager, humorously likened GTA 6's early game busywork to "adult Animal Crossing." Fils-Aimé's response, "If so, where's Mandy?", quickly drew attention.

Mandy is introduced early in GTA 6 during a mission where protagonist Jason collects rent from her. She's depicted as a flirtatious and aggressive character who attempts to romance Jason, despite his clear disinterest. Players cannot romance Mandy. Adding to her enigmatic nature, a dark secret is hinted at when Jason discovers a severed finger while fixing her sink.

Fils-Aimé's comment is particularly noteworthy given his past role at family-friendly Nintendo. While his exact thoughts on Mandy remain ambiguous—he merely inquired about her Animal Crossing equivalent—his engagement suggests he's either playing GTA 6 or is deeply attuned to the game's significant cultural impact. This interaction highlights how deeply GTA 6 has permeated popular culture, even reaching figures from seemingly contrasting gaming spheres.
