# Devolver Digital Challenges Gamers: Delete Your GTA 6 Pre-Load to Win 'Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed'

Devolver Digital is employing an unconventional marketing tactic for its upcoming game, *Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed*, by directly challenging the impending release of Rockstar's highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6*. With GTA 6's massive 212GB file size and guaranteed dominance of the gaming spotlight, Devolver aims for "counter-programming."Players can enter a contest to win a Steam key for *Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed* by reposting a Devolver message alongside a video of themselves deleting their *GTA 6* pre-load. Those who weren't planning to purchase *GTA 6* can still participate by submitting a video of themselves biting into a raw onion. It's noted that while deleting the pre-load is temporary, re-downloading 212GB could be time-consuming.*Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed*, a retro-style 2D platformer originally prototyped for Game Boy Color by Doinksoft, is priced at $5 (or $3.74 pre-launch) and coincidentally releases on November 19, the same day as *GTA 6*. Devolver's strategy targets players not interested in *GTA 6* or PC users, given *GTA 6*'s initial absence from Steam. This campaign comes as *GTA 6* reportedly boasts nearly 15 million pre-orders and $1.5 billion in revenue.

- Devolver Digital is running a promotional contest for its game, Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed, leveraging the release of GTA 6.
