# GTA 6: Dive Deep into Jason and Lucia's Dynamic Relationship, Including Physical Intimacy and Player Choice

Recent revelations from Rockstar North's Rob Nelson and official ratings shed light on the intricate relationship between GTA 6's protagonists, Jason and Lucia. Confirmed as both partners in crime and potential lovers, their dynamic will feature "physical intimacy" as a "huge part" of their bond, according to Nelson in an interview with IGN. While the exact mechanics remain undisclosed, Nelson emphasized the importance of getting it right through iteration. 

The game's ESRB and international ratings corroborate this, explicitly mentioning "nudity," "strong sexual content," and "sex scenes." Notably, a character switch could reportedly show Jason's POV while naked in the shower. However, there's no indication that any sex scenes will be interactive, a point that echoes the infamous 'Hot Coffee' controversy from GTA: San Andreas.

Players will have the unique opportunity to "role-play" Jason and Lucia's relationship. While they can pursue a romantic connection, the narrative also allows them to remain strictly "partners in crime" without romantic involvement. Nelson stressed that this choice will not provide a "particular gameplay benefit" in terms of mechanics, but it will significantly influence how their story unfolds and evolves. The game features a dedicated "relationship system" separate from the criminal tracking system, allowing players to nurture their bond through various activities, from kayaking and mini-golf to joint robberies. Regardless of their romantic status, they will always have each other's back. This narrative approach, likened to Bonnie and Clyde, promises a compelling and emotionally resonant experience that is entirely new for the GTA series, with Rockstar aiming to provide mechanisms for players to opt out of the romantic aspect if they prefer.
