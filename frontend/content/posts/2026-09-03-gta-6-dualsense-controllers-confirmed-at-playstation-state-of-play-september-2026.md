# GTA 6 DualSense Controllers Confirmed at PlayStation State of Play September 2026

The PlayStation State of Play September 2026 event, while packed with numerous game announcements and trailers, offered very little in the way of Grand Theft Auto 6 news. Despite the article's title potentially implying significant GTA 6 content, the only mention related to the highly anticipated title was the confirmation of **limited-edition Grand Theft Auto 6 DualSense controllers**. These controllers, showcased with a brief video, will be available in a choice of white or other unspecified colors. Fans eager for gameplay reveals or release date updates for GTA 6 will have to continue waiting, as the State of Play primarily focused on titles like Final Fantasy 7 Revelation, Marvel's Wolverine, Ghost of Yotei, Monster Hunter Wilds: Ascendance, Metro 2039, and the surprising sequel Until Dawn 2, among many others.

## Key Takeaways
- The PlayStation State of Play September 2026 event did not feature any new game trailers or announcements for Grand Theft Auto 6.
- Limited-edition Grand Theft Auto 6 DualSense controllers were officially confirmed.
- The controllers will be available in white and potentially other colors, as shown in a brief video.
- Other major announcements at the State of Play included Final Fantasy 7 Revelation, Marvel's Wolverine, Ghost of Yotei, Monster Hunter Wilds: Ascendance, and Until Dawn 2.
