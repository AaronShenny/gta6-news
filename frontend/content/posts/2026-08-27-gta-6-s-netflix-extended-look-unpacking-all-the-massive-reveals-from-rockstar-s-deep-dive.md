# GTA 6's Netflix Extended Look: Unpacking All The Massive Reveals From Rockstar's Deep Dive

Rockstar Games has finally given fans a comprehensive look at Grand Theft Auto 6 gameplay through a Netflix-exclusive 'Extended Look' titled Grand Theft Auto 6: An Extended Look. This deep dive showcases the open-world activities, combat, story missions, characters, and vehicles, intensifying the anticipation for the game's November 19 release.Key revelations from the extended preview include the return of the fan-favorite **six-star Wanted level system**, which was absent in GTA 5, now featuring visible crime icons. Players will also have the unique ability to **interact with dogs**, choosing to either pet or scold them.The sheer scale of GTA 6 has been highlighted, with Rockstar North's Rob Nelson estimating a **main narrative playthrough of approximately 80 hours**. Complementing this extensive gameplay is an **enormous map, reportedly three times larger than that of Red Dead Redemption 2**.At its heart, the game's story revolves around the relationship between **Lucia and Jason**. While their partnership is central to the narrative, players can choose whether it's romantic or platonic. A new **Criminal Profile system**, akin to Red Dead Redemption 2's Honor system, will track players' long-term actions and violence, unlike the transient Wanted stars.Other interesting tidbits include Jason's voice, which has been compared to Tom Hardy's Bane, and Rockstar's commitment to **avoiding direct real-world political figures or events** (like Trump or COVID-19), though social satire will remain. Crucially for many fans, Rockstar has confirmed that the single-player GTA 6 will **not feature any microtransactions** and that **no generative AI was used** in its development.

## Key Takeaways
- A Netflix-exclusive 'Extended Look' provided a comprehensive reveal of GTA 6 gameplay.
- The game will bring back the six-star Wanted level system, absent since GTA 5.
- Players will be able to interact with dogs, with options to pet or scold them.
- Rockstar North estimates the main narrative, including story-based optional content, will last roughly 80 hours.
- GTA 6 features a massive map, reportedly three times larger than Red Dead Redemption 2's.
- The core story centers on the relationship between protagonists Lucia and Jason, which can be romantic or platonic.
- A new 'Criminal Profile' system will track long-term player violence and actions, similar to RDR2's Honor system.
- Rockstar confirmed that the single-player game will not contain any microtransactions.
- No generative AI was utilized in the development of GTA 6.
- The game will include political and social satire but will avoid directly addressing real-world political figures or events.
