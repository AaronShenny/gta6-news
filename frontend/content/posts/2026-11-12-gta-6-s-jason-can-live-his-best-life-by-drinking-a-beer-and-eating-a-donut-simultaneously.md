# GTA 6’s Jason Can Live His Best Life By Drinking A Beer And Eating A Donut Simultaneously

Rockstar Games is pushing the boundaries of realism in Grand Theft Auto 6, as evidenced by an impressive level of detail, including the ability for one of the main characters, Jason, to consume a beer and a donut at the same time. Rockstar boss Rob Nelson shared this insight with The Guardian, highlighting how Jason can "take bites of a donut with one hand while sipping a beer with the other." The report also noted meticulous attention to how Jason eats a hot dog.

Beyond these simultaneous actions, GTA 6 features a dynamic body-shaping system. Characters like Jason and Lucia will gain weight if they frequently indulge in high-calorie items like beer, hot dogs, and donuts. Conversely, this weight can be converted into muscle through gym workouts.

Another example of Rockstar's commitment to detail is visible when Jason buys a six-pack of beer and jogs; the game renders the liquid visibly sloshing within the bottles. While some of these intricate details might go unnoticed by players, they collectively contribute to an immersive world. GTA 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preloading currently available.
