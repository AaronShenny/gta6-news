# GTA 6 Marketing Is Taking Place On “Virtually Every Social Media Outlet On Earth”

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has revealed that the marketing campaign for Grand Theft Auto 6 is exceptionally broad, spanning "virtually every social media outlet on Earth." This announcement follows the news of an upcoming extended look at GTA 6, which will debut exclusively on Netflix for a six-hour window on August 27.

During an August 7 earnings call, Zelnick lauded Netflix as a "great marketing partner" and described the collaboration as a "groundbreaking partnership" orchestrated by Rockstar. While expressing excitement for the initiative, he declined to elaborate on the specific terms of the arrangement.

The unprecedented Netflix exclusivity has, however, sparked debate among fans and industry observers. Concerns have been raised regarding Netflix's server capacity to handle the anticipated demand, the potential for immediate re-uploads of the trailer, and the principle of 'paywalling' a game trailer behind a subscription service.
