# My gear shifter runs GTA VI's boot animation, made from a recycled smartwatch and tablet

In a remarkable display of DIY ingenuity, Reddit user u/Desmontei has shared a unique car modification that brings a touch of Grand Theft Auto VI to their Audi A4 (2001). The project involves repurposing an old smartwatch, embedding it into a custom-built gear shifter. Originally used to display gear position, this innovative shifter now proudly runs the highly anticipated GTA VI boot animation. Complementing this, an old tablet was rescued and mounted into the car's original multimedia slot, serving as an additional dash display. What makes this project particularly noteworthy is that no components were purchased specifically for this build; all parts were salvaged and repurposed from existing items, highlighting a commitment to recycling and creative problem-solving. The user stated they were inspired to create this crossover after seeing the GTA VI trailer play on their car's dashboard, leading to this impressive and timely showcase of fan dedication.

## Key Takeaways
