# GTA 6 Glitch Turns Iconic Crime Spree into Hilarious Underwear Heist

Chapter 2 of Grand Theft Auto 6 features a pivotal mission called "Breakfast Run," where protagonists Jason and Lucia embark on an iconic Bonnie-and-Clyde-like crime spree, marking Jason's recovery from injuries. This montage, set to Dire Straits' "Money or Nothing," showcases the duo robbing various establishments and is intended as a serious statement of the game's narrative.

However, one player experienced a hilarious technical mishap: after sprinting toward a motel room door, the game placed them back outside. Upon re-entering, Jason was inexplicably up and about, but only in his underwear, bandages, and a buzz cut. This appearance persisted throughout the entire "Breakfast Run" mission, turning the intense crime spree into an absurd comedy. The author found themselves cackling at "Underwear Jason" terrorizing locals and slamming the gas pedal with a bare foot.

The glitch, which likely bypassed a clothing check, transformed the serious tone into something reminiscent of a Saints Row game. While GTA 6 does feature nudity, players cannot typically dress characters in their underwear outside of their home. The author notes that other players have reported similar "underwear glitches." Despite the unexpected humor, the bug didn't ruin the mission; instead, it provided an unforgettable, albeit unintended, experience. Rockstar Games is actively releasing post-launch patches to address various glitches in the game.
