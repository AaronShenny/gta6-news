# Final Frame Friday Pre-Release Concludes as GTA 6 Launch Nears

The 127th and final installment of the 'Final Frame Friday Pre-Release' series has been posted by Reddit user Denso95, signaling the imminent launch of Grand Theft Auto VI. The series, which began nearly three years ago following the release of the first trailer, was initially expected to last only a few weeks but grew into a consistent weekly feature, filling the information void for anticipating fans.

In a deeply personal reflection, Denso95 shared significant life events that transpired during the series' run, including a breakup, discovering new gaming genres, experiencing a house fire, overcoming financial struggles, finding love in Peru, and getting engaged. The author acknowledged the privilege of being able to play such a highly anticipated game and encouraged community members to stay safe and appreciate their circumstances.

Looking ahead to release day, Denso95 has planned an extended break from work to fully immerse in the game, opting for a full day's play from morning to evening rather than a midnight launch. The 'Frame Fridays' series is not entirely over, as the author intends to continue it post-release, utilizing GTA VI's anticipated photo mode to share spoiler-free cinematic shots, funny moments, and discovered easter eggs. Denso95 also noted the satisfaction of revisiting past leak analyses and finding many details confirmed by subsequent official content, reinforcing the game's massive scope. The post concludes with a heartfelt thank you to the community and moderators for their support throughout the journey, eagerly anticipating the arrival in Vice City.
