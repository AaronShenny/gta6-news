# Rockstar President Sam Houser Makes Surprise Silent Cameo in GTA 6

Grand Theft Auto 6 is packed with an array of characters voiced by famous personalities, but one particular cameo stands out as a delightful surprise: Rockstar Games president, Sam Houser.

In a later mission, players will encounter Houser's digital likeness during an errand with the character Boobie. Houser appears as a silent "little Santa" sweeping the floor of a club, even sporting a white t-shirt adorned with the iconic Rockstar Games logo. This brief, wordless appearance is a nod to his past involvement in the series, as he previously voiced an Ammu-Nation store clerk in GTA 3 and had a statue dedicated to that character in GTA: San Andreas.

Despite generally maintaining a low profile, Sam Houser recently issued a rare statement celebrating GTA 6's immense critical and commercial success. Meanwhile, his brother, Dan Houser, who departed Rockstar in 2020, is currently developing an open-world comedy game at his new studio, Absurd Ventures.
