# Tripping Animals Brewing Launches GTA-Themed Beers in Miami

Tripping Animals Brewing Co., a popular Miami-based brewery, has officially launched a new line of beers inspired by the Grand Theft Auto universe. Announced via their official Instagram, the collection features three distinct brews: "Patriot Beer" (4.2% ABV), "Pißwasser Pils" (4.2% ABV), and "No Mames Vice City Lager" (6% ABV). These beers are currently available in cans at the brewery's Doral taproom and at select locations across Miami, catering to local enthusiasts. The initiative taps into the iconic imagery and humor of the GTA series, particularly resonating with the upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI, which is heavily rumored to be set in a Vice City-inspired location.
