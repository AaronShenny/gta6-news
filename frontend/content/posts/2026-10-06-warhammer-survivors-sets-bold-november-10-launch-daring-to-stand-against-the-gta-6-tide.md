# Warhammer Survivors Sets Bold November 10 Launch, Daring to Stand Against the GTA 6 Tide

In a display of impressive confidence, the new 'Survivaton' game, Warhammer Survivors, is slated to launch on November 10. This release date places it mere days before the industry-shaking arrival of Grand Theft Auto 6, a window most developers are actively avoiding. Available on PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, Switch, and mobile devices, the game will also be accessible via Xbox Game Pass.

Fans eager to dive into the grimdark 41st millennium can try a new PC demo on Steam right now, featuring heroes Lyssa Revenya and the Legend of Catachan. True to the 'Vampire Survivors playbook,' players will battle endless waves of Tyranid enemies, evolve their arsenal of 70 weapons, and master over 40 playable characters, pushing their builds to the limit against Extremis bosses. This audacious launch strategy is shared by only a few other titles, including a Godzilla-themed game, while another anticipated 'Survivaton,' Jujutsu Kaisen Rumble, has shifted its release to 2027.

## Key Takeaways
- Warhammer Survivors launches on November 10 for PC, consoles (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, Switch), and mobile.
- A PC demo is currently available on Steam, featuring Lyssa Revenya and the Legend of Catachan.
- The game is a 'Survivaton' title, similar to Vampire Survivors, set in the Warhammer 40,000 universe, focusing on battling Tyranids.
- It will feature over 40 playable characters and an arsenal of 70 weapons.
- Warhammer Survivors will be available on Xbox Game Pass.
- Its release date is notably close to the highly anticipated GTA 6, a bold move that few other games are attempting.
