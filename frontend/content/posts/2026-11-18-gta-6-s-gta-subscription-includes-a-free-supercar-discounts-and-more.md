# GTA 6’s GTA+ Subscription Includes A Free Supercar, Discounts, And More

Rockstar Games has announced that its GTA+ subscription service is expanding to cover Grand Theft Auto 6, offering a range of exclusive in-game benefits for players in Vice City. Priced at $8 per month, the service provides perks that are only accessible while actively subscribed. Players who pre-order GTA 6 will also receive a complimentary month of GTA+.

Key advantages for GTA 6 subscribers include:

*   **Free Supercar and Garage:** After completing Chapter 3, subscribers will unlock a '90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy supercar and a dedicated single-car garage in Crosstown. This garage also features a weapon locker and the ability to fence stolen items directly.
*   **Transit Service Bonuses:** Enhanced public transportation options, such as Scooter Bros Premium, premium membership on the RydeMe rideshare app, free rentals for scooters, bikes, and Seasharks, and free rides on Leonida public transportation.
*   **Store Discounts:** A 10% discount at various in-game businesses, including weapon and clothing shops, hairdressers, tattooists, and vehicle modification shops.
*   **Increased Sell Value:** An additional 10% cash bonus when selling eligible vehicles.

The article also briefly mentions ongoing GTA+ benefits for GTA Online players and highlights GTA 6's critical acclaim, noting its rare perfect score from GameSpot.

## Key Takeaways
- Rockstar's GTA+ subscription is expanding to include Grand Theft Auto 6, costing $8 per month.
- GTA+ subscribers will receive a free '90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy supercar and a dedicated garage after completing Chapter 3 in GTA 6.
- Additional benefits include enhanced public transit options, 10% discounts at various in-game businesses, and a 10% increase in vehicle sell value.
- A free month of GTA+ is included with GTA 6 pre-orders, but all perks are only available while the subscription is active.
- GTA 6 has received widespread critical acclaim, earning a rare perfect score from GameSpot.

## FAQ
**What is GTA+ and how much does it cost for GTA 6?**
GTA+ is Rockstar Games' subscription service, now covering GTA 6, and it costs $8 per month. Pre-ordering GTA 6 grants a free month of the service.

**What free vehicle do GTA+ subscribers get in GTA 6?**
GTA+ subscribers will receive a free '90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy supercar and a single-car garage in Crosstown after completing Chapter 3 in GTA 6.

**What public transit benefits are included with GTA+ in GTA 6?**
GTA+ members get Scooter Bros Premium, premium membership on the RydeMe app, free scooter, bike, and Seashark rentals, and free rides on Leonida public transportation.

**Are there any discounts for GTA+ members in GTA 6?**
Yes, subscribers receive a 10% discount at select in-game businesses, including weapon shops, clothing stores, hairdressers, tattooists, and vehicle mod shops.

**Are the GTA+ benefits for GTA 6 permanent?**
No, all GTA+ benefits are only available while your membership is active; they cease if your subscription expires or is canceled.
