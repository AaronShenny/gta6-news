# Rockstar Unveils Official GTA 6 Merchandise Collection, Featuring Macca the Gator

Rockstar Games has officially announced an exclusive merchandise collection for the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6*, set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The collection, available through the Rockstar Store, features a variety of items for fans.

A standout piece in the collection is a unique t-shirt featuring "Macca the Gator," an intriguing in-universe character depicted wearing sunglasses and sipping a cocktail. Macca the Gator is teased as the star of his own fictional TV show, adding a layer of mystery and excitement for players.

Beyond the gator-themed apparel, the merchandise lineup includes a range of other items such as t-shirts sporting the GTA 6 logo, branded hats in black, pink, and blue, a GTA 6-branded Zippo lighter, canvas tote bags, and various vinyl stickers. Rockstar has also provided specific care instructions for the t-shirts, advising machine wash cold and hang drying only.

In addition to the main collection, Rockstar has collaborated with the NBA's Miami Heat to release a special "Vice City Collection" of gear, which is also available for purchase now. This merchandise reveal builds anticipation for the game, which is also confirmed to feature an extensive selection of music, podcasts, and famous radio hosts.
