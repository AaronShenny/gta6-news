# GTA 6: Will Rockstar Replicate Red Dead Redemption 2's Dynamic World Progression?

A recent Reddit discussion, initiated by u/SalamanderCommander3, delves into highly anticipated features for Grand Theft Auto 6, specifically speculating on whether it will adopt the dynamic environmental and NPC behaviors famously seen in Red Dead Redemption 2. The user highlighted RDR2's intricate details, such as buildings visibly progressing through construction phases as the game unfolds, and expressed hope that similar real-time evolution might be a core feature in GTA 6. The discussion extends to other potential dynamic elements, including the updating of in-game advertisements and flyers, and whether non-player characters (NPCs) will exhibit varied appearances and activities based on the time of day or changing seasons. The rationale behind this speculation is the expectation that Rockstar Games, leveraging lessons learned from RDR2 and positioning GTA 6 as potentially the most expensive game ever, would incorporate such immersive, evolving world mechanics to enhance realism and player engagement.

## Key Takeaways
- Fan speculation suggests GTA 6 may feature dynamic world progression similar to Red Dead Redemption 2.

- Hopes for GTA 6 include evolving advertisements, flyers, and NPC behavior/appearance changes based on time or season.
- The expectation is driven by Rockstar's previous work on RDR2 and GTA 6's anticipated scale.
