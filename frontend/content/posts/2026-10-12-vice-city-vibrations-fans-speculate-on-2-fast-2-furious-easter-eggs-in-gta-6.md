# Vice City Vibrations: Fans Speculate on '2 Fast 2 Furious' Easter Eggs in GTA 6

A passionate Grand Theft Auto fan has ignited discussion on Reddit, speculating on the exciting possibility of "2 Fast 2 Furious" Easter eggs making their way into the highly anticipated GTA 6. Given Vice City's undeniable resemblance to Miami, the film's primary setting, the community believes Rockstar Games, known for its intricate references, will undoubtedly include nods to the iconic movie.

The user outlined several compelling ideas for integration:
*   **Iconic Vehicles**: The appearance of GTA equivalents to the movie's hero cars, like the Karin Sultan RS and Maibatsu Penumbra FF, engaged in street races.
*   **The Scramble Event**: A dynamic in-game event where players encounter multiple JDM cars emerging from a warehouse, leading to a chaotic police standoff, offering players the choice to join the racers or assist law enforcement.
*   **Carter's Boatyard**: A detailed recreation of this key movie location, potentially featuring the impounded Ferrari for a special weapon pickup or a humorous cigar Easter egg.
*   **"Ejecto Seato Cuz"**: A subtle yet memorable reference with a car seat, possibly accompanied by a skeleton, submerged in a pond or lake near a highway.
*   **Drawbridge Jump**: With drawbridges confirmed for GTA 6, a random night-time encounter depicting NPCs recreating the film's famous bridge jump.
*   **Tej's Garage**: A vibrant social hub akin to the movie's garage, offering vehicle customization, lively parties, and opportunities for high-stakes jet ski and boat races.
*   **Yacht Jump Aftermath**: Instead of a live event, a damaged yacht visibly showing the impact of the film's daring jump, serving as a static environmental detail.

While acknowledging that not all suggestions may be implemented, the user expresses excitement for even one or two of these iconic references to appear, fueling the anticipation for GTA 6's release.
