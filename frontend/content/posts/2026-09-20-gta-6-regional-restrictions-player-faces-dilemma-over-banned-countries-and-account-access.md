# GTA 6 Regional Restrictions: Player Faces Dilemma Over Banned Countries and Account Access

A Reddit user, /u/SaneTrevorPhilips, recently shared a pressing concern regarding the anticipated release of *Grand Theft Auto 6*. In a post titled 'GTA 6 UE in countries where it's banned,' the user highlighted their predicament: residing in a country where the highly anticipated title is expected to be regionally banned.

The core of the issue stems from the user's extensive investment in their current PlayStation account, boasting over 2,000 hours logged in *GTA Online*. They are vehemently against creating a new account, fearing the loss of their accumulated progress, in-game friends, groups, and crew affiliations.

Attempts to seek clarity from official channels have yielded little success. PlayStation support reportedly offered only vague, automated responses, hinting at possibilities like game sharing or the Ultimate Edition *potentially* working. More surprisingly, Rockstar Games support, which the user usually praises for its responsiveness, has been uncharacteristically unhelpful. Despite submitting approximately five tickets, each inquiry was met with generic replies directing to the official *GTA 6* FAQs page, failing to address the specific nuances of regional access.

Feeling at a 'dead end,' the user has turned to the gaming community for suggestions and expresses a strong hope for the availability of physical copies, which could potentially offer a workaround for the digital regional restrictions. It's worth noting that the original post referenced the game's release as 'less than 2 months' away, a timeline that does not align with current official announcements regarding *Grand Theft Auto 6*'s release window.
