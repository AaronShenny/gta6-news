# NBA 2K27 Dominates August Sales, But All Eyes Are on GTA 6's November Arrival

Circana's August 2026 report reveals that **NBA 2K27** once again secured the best-selling game title for the month, a feat it has achieved for three consecutive Augusts, consistently outperforming EA's Madden NFL series. While NBA 2K27 took the lead, **Madden NFL 27** still performed strongly, claiming both second and fourth place with its standard and Kickoff Bundle editions, respectively. The month saw a robust performance from new releases, with five new titles entering the top 10. Notable debuts include **Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls** at third, **Star Wars Zero Company** at fifth, and **Big Walk** at eighth. Other games rounding out the top 10 were Halo: Campaign Evolved, the Switch 2 port of Elden Ring, Mario Kart World, and Splatoon Raiders. On the year-to-date charts, **Resident Evil Requiem** maintains its top position, while NBA 2K27 entered the YTD ranking at third, displacing Crimson Desert. The report concludes by noting that while current rankings are relatively stable, significant changes are anticipated with the bumper releases in September and October, with **GTA 6** specifically highlighted as an expected 'sales juggernaut' upon its November launch.

## Key Takeaways
- NBA 2K27 was the best-selling game in August 2026, marking its third consecutive August at the top.
- Madden NFL 27 performed strongly, securing both second and fourth positions with its standard and bundle editions.
- Five new releases, including Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls and Star Wars Zero Company, debuted in the August top 10.
- Resident Evil Requiem remains the year-to-date best-seller, with NBA 2K27 entering the YTD top three.
- GTA 6 is highly anticipated and projected to be a 'sales juggernaut' upon its November release.
