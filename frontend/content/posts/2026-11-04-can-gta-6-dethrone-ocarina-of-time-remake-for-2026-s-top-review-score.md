# Can GTA 6 Dethrone Ocarina of Time Remake for 2026's Top Review Score?

The highly anticipated remake of *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* has already garnered critical acclaim, securing a formidable 96 Metascore from 81 reviews, making it the highest-rated game of 2026 so far. While other strong contenders like *Sektori* and the *Resident Evil 2 remake (Switch 2 Deluxe Edition)* sit at 93, *Ocarina of Time*'s lead seems stable for now.However, the gaming world is buzzing with anticipation for *Grand Theft Auto 6*, slated for release on November 19. Historically, Rockstar's mainline *Grand Theft Auto* titles have consistently achieved stellar review scores, often rivaling or surpassing *The Legend of Zelda* series. Previous entries like *GTA 3* (97), *GTA San Andreas* (95), *GTA 4* (98), and *GTA 5* (97) demonstrate a strong track record of high 90s Metascores.Given Rockstar's reputation for delivering polished experiences, *GTA 6* is widely expected to continue this trend, potentially challenging *Ocarina of Time*'s current reign. The article acknowledges that new immersive gameplay mechanics in *GTA 6* might introduce some friction for players accustomed to previous titles' crime sprees without heavy consequences, but this is unlikely to deter its critical success. The ultimate victor for 2026's review crown will be decided after *GTA 6*'s launch.
