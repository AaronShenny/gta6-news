# The real life GTA 6 cars are a Rockstar and West Coast Customs collaboration

A recent report from a dedicated fan community indicates that Rockstar Games might be once again teaming up with the renowned automotive customizer West Coast Customs to bring Grand Theft Auto 6 vehicles to life. This potential collaboration builds on a successful partnership established in 2013, when West Coast Customs famously crafted a real-life rendition of the iconic Bravado Banshee for Grand Theft Auto 5, which garnered significant attention. While specific details regarding which GTA 6 vehicles might be involved or the overall scope of this new project remain unconfirmed, the prospect of seeing next-generation in-game cars translated into tangible, physical models is already generating considerable excitement among the fanbase, echoing the anticipation from the highly popular GTA 5 promotional vehicle.
