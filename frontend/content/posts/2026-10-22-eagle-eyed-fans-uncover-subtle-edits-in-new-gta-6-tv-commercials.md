# Eagle-Eyed Fans Uncover Subtle Edits in New GTA 6 TV Commercials

The marketing campaign for Grand Theft Auto 6 is significantly intensifying, now extending to television commercials. Dedicated fans have meticulously analyzed these new TV spots, uncovering subtle yet notable differences compared to the game's initial launch trailer.

A 30-second version of the TV spot omits the "steamy shower scenes" featuring protagonists Jason and Lucia, which were present in the original launch trailer. Despite these omissions, this shorter commercial includes slightly extended sequences: one showcasing an airplane and another depicting Jason and Lucia standing next to their car, where Jason playfully nudges Lucia, causing her to almost lose balance. Interestingly, the Xbox logo has also been removed from this 30-second edit, a change presumed to be linked to Rockstar's reported promotional agreement with Sony.

Conversely, a 90-second version of the TV spot reintroduces the shower scenes while retaining the slightly longer airplane and car sequences found in the 30-second version. These subtle alterations might easily go unnoticed by a casual viewer, but the highly dedicated GTA 6 fan community has been quick to spot and highlight every minute detail.

GTA 6 is slated for release on November 19 for both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, despite the absence of the Xbox logo in certain TV advertisements. Beyond traditional commercials, Rockstar's extensive marketing efforts also include real-life Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell pop-up restaurants, offering GTA-themed food items.

In other significant news surrounding the game, recent reports indicate that GTA 6 pre-orders are vastly outperforming any other major AAA title from the past five years. This comes as little surprise, given the widespread expectation that GTA 6 will be the largest game launch in history, with projections suggesting it could generate an astounding $2 billion in revenue even before its official release.
