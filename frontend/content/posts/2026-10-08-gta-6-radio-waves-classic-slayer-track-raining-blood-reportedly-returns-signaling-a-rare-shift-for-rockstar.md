# GTA 6 Radio Waves: Classic Slayer Track "Raining Blood" Reportedly Returns, Signaling a Rare Shift for Rockstar

A recent Reddit post by /u/RuneWarhammer has ignited excitement within the Grand Theft Auto community, suggesting that Rockstar Games is making a rare move by bringing back classic music tracks for GTA 6. According to the user, a 'music preview' indicates that Slayer's iconic metal anthem 'Raining Blood' will be featured on a metal station in the upcoming title.

This revelation is particularly noteworthy because 'Raining Blood' previously graced the airwaves of V-Rock in 2002's *Grand Theft Auto: Vice City*. The article highlights that this would only be the second known instance of Rockstar re-introducing old music from prior GTA iterations. The precedent cited is Joe Jackson's 'Steppin' Out,' which was a Vice City classic and later appeared in *GTA Online* as part of The Black Madonna's set on LSUR.

The user expresses optimism that this trend might extend to other beloved 80s classics heavily associated with *Vice City*, such as tracks from Hall & Oates or songs reminiscent of Flashback FM and The Wave. This move could signal a departure from the perceived 'we used them already' philosophy, potentially allowing iconic songs to reappear and enhance the nostalgic experience for players in GTA 6.

## Key Takeaways

- The return of old tracks sparks hope for more classic 80s songs from *Vice City* to appear in GTA 6.
