# Development of Grand Theft Auto (GTA) VI Started in 2015; NOT 2013 nor 2020!

The highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI officially began its development in **2015**, as confirmed by Rob Nelson, Head of Rockstar North. This revelation debunks widespread rumors suggesting earlier start dates (like 2013 or 2012) or a much later one (2020). While the initial conceptualization and pre-production activities, such as writing and location scouting in Miami, commenced in 2015, the "majority of the team" truly began full-scale development after the release of *Red Dead Redemption 2* in 2018.Rockstar Games is known for its "very long cycles" of development, meticulously choosing ideas that will "stand the test of time" for its satirical narratives. The article highlights that the casting of lead actors Manni L. Perez (Lucia) and Dylan Rourke (Jason) occurred in mid-2019, with motion capture sessions starting in late 2019/early 2020, pausing briefly due to the March 2020 pandemic shutdowns before resuming in September 2020.The game's extensive development, now spanning an estimated 11-12 years, is notably longer than previous Rockstar behemoths like *Red Dead Redemption 2* (7 years, 8 months) and *Grand Theft Auto V* (nearly 5 years). The article also speculates on various factors contributing to this prolonged timeline, including the demanding "development hell" of *Red Dead Redemption 2*, internal cultural shifts, the alleged "collapse and reboot" of 'Project Americas' in 2018-2019, the loss of key personnel, pandemic-related delays, and the impact of the September 2022 leaks. Further speculation touches on potential staff revolts and a possible mini-reboot or quality concerns from Sam Houser, potentially pushing the release to 2026.This information provides a clearer picture of GTA VI's ambitious and challenging journey through production, underscoring Rockstar's commitment to its lengthy and meticulous creative process.

## Key Takeaways
