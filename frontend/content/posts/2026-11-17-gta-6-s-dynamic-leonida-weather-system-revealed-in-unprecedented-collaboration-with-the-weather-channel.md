# GTA 6's Dynamic Leonida Weather System Revealed in Unprecedented Collaboration with The Weather Channel

Rockstar Games has forged an unexpected yet fitting partnership with The Weather Channel to unveil one of *Grand Theft Auto 6*'s most impressive features: its highly dynamic weather system in the fictional state of Leonida. Inspired by Florida, Leonida's climate will feature everything from intense sunshine to torrential rain, thunderstorms, and tropical storms, making weather a crucial element of the game world.

The Weather Channel announced the collaboration on X, providing an exclusive look at six new screenshots accompanied by realistic, localized weather forecasts for various areas across Leonida. These reports detail conditions such as heavy rain reducing visibility in the Keys, thunderstorms over Pelican Valley swamps, sea fog at South Ocean Beach, and a tropical storm making waters choppy near the Rusty Anchor.
