# GTA 6 Fans Hope for Red Dead Redemption 2-Level Parkour and Verticality

A passionate Grand Theft Auto 6 fan has taken to Reddit to share their hopes for the game's traversal mechanics, drawing a direct comparison to the acclaimed Red Dead Redemption 2. Specifically, the user highlighted the city of Saint Denis in RDR2 as a 'parkour playground,' praising its intricate design, verticality, and the freedom of movement it afforded players. The sentiment expressed is a strong desire for GTA 6's smaller towns and neighborhoods to offer similar levels of environmental interaction, allowing for dynamic free-running, climbing, and exploring rooftops or elevated pathways. This suggests a community yearning for more nuanced and engaging urban environments that go beyond simple street-level driving and offer opportunities for vertical exploration and fluid character movement, echoing the beloved mechanics seen in other open-world titles.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 fans desire enhanced parkour and free-running mechanics.
- Red Dead Redemption 2's Saint Denis is cited as a benchmark for environmental verticality and freedom of movement.
- Hopes for GTA 6 include more dynamic and interactive urban environments in smaller towns and neighborhoods.
- The community seeks opportunities for vertical exploration and fluid character movement in the next installment.

## FAQ
**What aspect of RDR2 is being praised for GTA 6?**
The parkour-like freedom of movement and verticality found in Saint Denis, allowing for dynamic exploration.

**What do fans hope for in GTA 6's environments?**
They hope for similar dynamic traversal opportunities and vertical exploration in small towns and neighborhoods, moving beyond simple street-level interactions.

**Why is Saint Denis considered a good example?**
Its design allowed for extensive free-running, climbing, and engaging with the environment in a highly vertical and interactive manner, making it feel like a 'parkour playground'.
