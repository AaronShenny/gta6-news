# GTA 6 Shatters Records with Unprecedented Pre-Release Demand and Purchase Intent

Rockstar Games' highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 is poised for an enormous launch, with new data from Circana confirming unprecedented levels of purchase intent. The game, set to release on November 19 for consoles, holds a staggering 29% purchase intent among US console and PC gamers – the highest ever measured for any game by Circana.Mat Piscatella of Circana describes GTA 6 as a "rare cultural and commercial moment," driven largely by the 13-year gap since GTA 5. This demand is expected to extend beyond software, potentially boosting sales of consoles (PS5, Xbox Series X|S), accessories, and gaming subscriptions, especially with its launch strategically placed before Black Friday and the holiday shopping season.Further solidifying its pre-release success, Sensor Tower estimates GTA 6 has already sold approximately 7 million units, generating $675 million in revenue. Projections suggest it could reach 25 million preorders and $2 billion in revenue before its official launch. While the game is confirmed for consoles, a PC edition is anticipated in early 2027.Despite its immense commercial potential and ability to uplift the industry, experts caution that GTA 6 alone will not resolve the gaming industry's deeper, systemic issues, and new console buyers may face high sticker shock. In related news, the game's file size has been revealed as massive, with preloading scheduled to begin on November 12.
