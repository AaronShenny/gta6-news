# GTA 6 Collectibles: Community Speculates on What's Next for 100% Completion

A recent Reddit discussion on the r/GTA6 subreddit, initiated by user RDR2Enjoyerr, has sparked conversation among fans regarding the potential collectibles for Grand Theft Auto 6's 100% completion. Drawing parallels to past GTA titles that featured unique collectibles like hidden packages, oysters, graffiti tags, and pigeons, the community is now theorizing what would be fitting for a game world based on Miami.

Suggestions from the discussion include thematic items such as art pieces or Cuban cigars, which would align with a vibrant, Miami-inspired setting. The idea of incorporating car collectibles, similar to those found in *L.A. Noire*, was also put forth. Additionally, some fans are hoping for a return to classic 'hidden packages,' a staple from early titles like *Grand Theft Auto: Vice City* and *Grand Theft Auto III*, to challenge players seeking full game completion.
