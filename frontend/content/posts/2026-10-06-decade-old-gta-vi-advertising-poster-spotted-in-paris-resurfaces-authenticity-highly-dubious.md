# Decade-Old 'GTA VI' Advertising Poster Spotted in Paris Resurfaces, Authenticity Highly Dubious

A Reddit post from 2014 has recently drawn attention, showcasing an image of what was presented as an advertising poster for 'Grand Theft Auto VI' in Paris, France. The user, /u/CollegeFlat399, claimed the poster was being installed on one of the two Mercuriales Towers near Porte de Bagnolet. While the image sparked discussion a decade ago, its authenticity is now considered extremely doubtful. Given that Grand Theft Auto VI was only officially revealed by Rockstar Games much later, this poster from 2014 is widely believed to be either a fan-made concept, a speculative placeholder, or an outright fake, long predating any genuine marketing efforts for the highly anticipated title.

## Key Takeaways
