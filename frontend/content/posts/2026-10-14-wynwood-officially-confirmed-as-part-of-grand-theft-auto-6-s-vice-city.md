Wynwood, known for its colorful street art and trendy atmosphere, is a real-world Miami neighborhood, and its appearance in this promotional art strongly suggests its in-game representation will mirror its real-life counterpart, enhancing the immersive experience of Vice City. This art piece serves as a significant confirmation for the game's setting details, building anticipation for the full release.
