# Bigfoot Confirmed to Return in GTA 6, Reportedly Stinks!

Bigfoot, a long-standing mythical creature in the Grand Theft Auto and Red Dead Redemption universes, has been confirmed to return in Grand Theft Auto 6. Early access players, some digitally immigrating to New Zealand to play early, are encountering the cryptid in Leonida's woods. Unlike previous iterations, this Bigfoot appears to be a genuine creature, not a man in a suit, and is tied to a mission. Streamers have shared clips showing Bigfoot emitting noxious fumes, a detail that likely references traditional stories of the creature's potent stench. One encounter involves the player interacting with an effigy, leading to them being knocked out by fumes, with Bigfoot's large feet seen approaching. This marks another chapter in Bigfoot's storied history with Rockstar games, which includes rumors in GTA: San Andreas, a questline in Red Dead Redemption's Undead Nightmare, a secret appearance (and a man in a costume) in GTA 5, and an implied presence in Red Dead Redemption 2.
