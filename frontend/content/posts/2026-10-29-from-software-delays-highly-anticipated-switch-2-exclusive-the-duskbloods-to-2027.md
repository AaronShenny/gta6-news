# From Software Delays Highly Anticipated Switch 2 Exclusive, The Duskbloods, to 2027

From Software has officially announced a significant delay for its highly anticipated Switch 2 exclusive, The Duskbloods, pushing its release from the current year to an unspecified date in 2027. The studio issued an apology to eager fans, thanking them for their patience and support. The primary reason cited for the postponement is to "further enhance" the game's core concepts, including combat, strategic depth, and player expression, along with making "final adjustments to game rules and balancing."This delay follows concerns among fans after The Duskbloods was notably absent from the September Nintendo Direct. Interestingly, the move also places the game's release outside the potential "blast zone" of GTA 6, though the article notes it's unclear if this was a factor for From Software or Nintendo. Despite the delay, excitement for The Duskbloods remains exceptionally high. A From Software employee recently revealed that the game's playtest garnered more interest than even Elden Ring Nightreign’s. The Duskbloods is a multiplayer PvPvE title directed by the acclaimed Hidetaka Miyazaki. Early impressions are overwhelmingly positive, with GameSpot's Jake Dekker praising his hands-on preview, stating he had "never played anything like it" and "couldn’t wait to play more."
