# GTA 6 Brings Burger Shot to Life Across Florida in Partnership with Skinny Louie

Rockstar Games has officially announced a partnership with the burger company Skinny Louie to promote **GTA 6**. As part of this exciting collaboration, all Skinny Louie locations across Florida will convert into in-game Burger Shot restaurants, a well-known parody of Burger King within the Grand Theft Auto universe.

The campaign kicks off in early November and will run through December 31. Fans in South Florida will be able to purchase **GTA 6**-themed burgers at the Kaseya Center, as well as every Skinny Louie outlet statewide. The menu is promised to be "straight out of Vice City," featuring classic smash burgers, fries, and various drinks, available for both in-store purchase and delivery.

In addition to Burger Shot, **GTA**'s infamous Cluckin' Bell fried chicken store is also available in real life at the Kaseya Center, though its wider availability beyond the arena remains unconfirmed. This marketing push extends beyond food, with **GTA 6** also taking over Miami through marketing efforts at local beaches and hotels.

However, this widespread promotion has not been without controversy. Some local lawmakers are expressing concerns, arguing that by accepting Rockstar's money, the city is endorsing a depiction of Miami as a "murder capital." Conversely, other officials maintain that it is merely a video game and such concerns are overblown.
