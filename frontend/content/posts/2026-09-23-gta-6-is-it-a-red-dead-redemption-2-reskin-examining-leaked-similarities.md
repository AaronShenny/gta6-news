# GTA 6: Is It a Red Dead Redemption 2 Reskin? Examining Leaked Similarities

A dedicated gaming journalist raises concerns that Grand Theft Auto 6 might significantly borrow gameplay and UI design elements from the critically acclaimed Red Dead Redemption 2. While acknowledging RDR2 as one of the greatest games ever and expressing high hopes for GTA 6, the author points to specific similarities observed in an 'extended look' (likely referring to leaked footage or early previews) and confirmed features.

The core argument is that RDR2's groundbreaking advancements in NPC behavior, environmental storytelling, and world-building likely served as a foundational blueprint for GTA 6, contributing to the long development cycle. The article highlights several striking resemblances, including an identical interaction tab (e.g., 'Pet/Scold/Study') that appears when approaching characters or animals, mirroring RDR2's system for horses and NPCs. Furthermore, the UI design and font are noted as being 'exactly the same,' suggesting a direct port. A 'sense/tracker ability,' confirmed for GTA 6, is also identified as being akin to Arthur Morgan's tracking mechanic in RDR2. The author speculates that other elements like the honor system, looting mechanics, vitals (such as needing to eat), and enterable buildings with safes will also follow RDR2's lead.

Despite these observations and the underlying fear of a 'reskin,' the author remains optimistic, stating they will 'play the hell out of it' and still anticipate an amazing experience from GTA 6.
