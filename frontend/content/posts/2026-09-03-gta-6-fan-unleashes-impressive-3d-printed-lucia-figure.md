# GTA 6 Fan Unleashes Impressive 3D Printed Lucia Figure

A passionate Grand Theft Auto 6 enthusiast, known on Reddit as u/Independent-Boot-117, has garnered significant attention by unveiling their meticulously crafted custom figure of Lucia, one of the highly anticipated protagonists from the upcoming game. The creator detailed the impressive process, stating that the figure was produced using a 3D printer with PLA material and then entirely hand-painted with acrylics. The post, aptly titled 'Ready for GTA 6! Just finished 3D printing and painting Lucia 💥 What do you think?', serves as a testament to the vibrant creativity and immense anticipation surrounding Grand Theft Auto 6, inviting fellow fans to admire the artistry and share their thoughts.
