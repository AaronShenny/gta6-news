# Metroid Ravenous Logo Generator Unleashes Viral Meme Frenzy Across the Internet

Hype surrounding the reveal of *Metroid Ravenous* has manifested into a massive meme phenomenon, thanks to a circulating logo generator. The tool, created by Pixelframe Design (known for similar generators for *GTA 6*, *The Duskbloods*, and *Pokopia*), allows users to input any text and style it to mimic the game's grainy, jagged logo, complete with four background options featuring Samus. The core hook of *Metroid Ravenous*, where Samus must consume enemies to survive, naturally led to a surge of food-related memes, including riffs on 'girl dinner' and the '55 burgers' scene from *I Think You Should Leave*. Beyond food, the generator has been used for vulgar jokes, absurd phrases like 'I ASKED FOR NO PICKLES,' and even applying the aggressive aesthetic to other game titles like *Hello Kitty Island Adventure*. The sheer volume of these memes briefly overwhelmed the *Metroid* subreddit, prompting moderators to create a megathread—which was promptly filled with memes dunking on the mods themselves. *Metroid Ravenous*, a 2D follow-up to 2021's *Metroid Dread*, was revealed during the September 2026 Nintendo Direct and is slated for release on January 28, 2027. While not officially confirmed, *Dread* developer MercurySteam has hinted at their involvement in the upcoming title.
