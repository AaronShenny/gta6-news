# GTA 6 Fans Hope for More Dynamic NPC Interactions with Buildings

A recent Reddit post by user /u/duckDuckBro has ignited a discussion among the Grand Theft Auto community, highlighting a long-standing observation: the lack of non-player characters (NPCs) entering buildings or houses in previous GTA titles. The user points out the 'super weird' phenomenon of NPCs perpetually remaining on the streets, never seen interacting with the interior spaces of the game world. The post proposes several intriguing possibilities for GTA 6, suggesting how more dynamic NPC behavior could enhance immersion and and gameplay. Imagine NPCs seeking refuge in nearby houses or stores during a chaotic shootout, or the ability to follow an NPC all the way to their workplace or home, observing their daily routines. The idea of NPCs stopping at stores for snacks on their way somewhere is also floated, adding a layer of realism. Furthermore, the user envisions potential gameplay mechanics, such as distracting an NPC to lure them out of their house, creating an opportunity for the player to break in while they are gone. This concept hints at new stealth and exploration possibilities within the game. Ultimately, the sentiment expressed is one of excitement for GTA 6, coupled with a strong desire to see NPCs interact more meaningfully with the game's architecture, moving beyond their street-bound existence to create a more believable and interactive open world.

## Key Takeaways
- NPCs in previous GTA games are observed never entering buildings or houses, remaining exclusively on the streets.
- A fan proposes dynamic NPC interactions with buildings for GTA 6 to enhance realism and immersion.
- Suggested scenarios include NPCs seeking cover in buildings during conflict, going home/to work, or stopping at shops.
- New gameplay opportunities, like distracting NPCs to break into their houses, are envisioned.
- The community expresses excitement for GTA 6 and hopes for more meaningful NPC interaction with the environment.
