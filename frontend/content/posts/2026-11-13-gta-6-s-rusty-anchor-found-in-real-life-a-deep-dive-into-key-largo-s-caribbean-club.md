# GTA 6's "Rusty Anchor" Found in Real Life: A Deep Dive into Key Largo's Caribbean Club

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto fan embarked on a "GTA VI trip" to Miami, leading them to what they believed might be the real-life inspiration for the game's "Rusty Anchor" bar. Located at mile marker 104 on the Overseas Highway in Key Largo, about an hour and a half south of Miami, "The Caribbean Club" proved to be much more than a mere lookalike. The fan discovered numerous in-game references, including a sign identical to the one seen in GTA 6 screenshots featuring the old sailor and the slogan "Sink Your Troubles at The Rusty Anchor". Inside, they spotted Patriot Beer and Pißwasser signs, Leonida license plates adorning the walls, and gig posters for shows "at The Rusty Anchor," along with a banner stating "Key Lento." Upon inquiring, the bartender claimed a direct partnership with Rockstar. The fan also received official-looking Rusty Anchor stickers and koozies. While Rockstar has not officially announced this partnership, the overwhelming evidence and merchandise suggest a strong connection. The user highly recommends the drive for any GTA fans in South Florida before the game's launch, despite the real-life bar not serving Patriot or Pißwasser beer. The fan also shared that more clips and frames of the location are available on their YouTube channel, @LeonidaManYT.
