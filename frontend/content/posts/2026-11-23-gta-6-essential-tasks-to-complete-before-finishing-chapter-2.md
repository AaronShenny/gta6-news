# GTA 6: Essential Tasks to Complete Before Finishing Chapter 2

GTA 6's Chapter 2 marks the true beginning of Jason and Lucia's Bonnie and Clyde journey, unfolding at a faster pace than the prologue. Before reaching this point of no return, players are advised to complete a series of essential and optional tasks to maximize their experience, acquire valuable gear, unlockables, and cash, and ensure 100% game completion.

Key priorities include swiftly paying off the motel manager Linda to avoid escalating fees and reclaim Lucia's sentimental necklace. Engaging with Wyman is crucial as it unlocks thrilling off-roading missions like "Thrillbillies" and "Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab." Players should also tackle the "Gellhorn’s Missing Tourist" challenge early in Chapter 2, as it becomes significantly more difficult in Chapter 3 due to spoiler-specific reasons. Additionally, taking time to play pool before the "Frankie's Delights" robbery offers a chance to hone skills. Finally, a variety of missable collectibles, including specific collectible cards (Bryson Powers, Vicki, Victor Maravella) and magazines (Help Me Help You Help Me, Wizard Wang), are located within the motel and should be gathered before the move to Vice City.
