# Take-Two Issues Takedowns for GTA 6 AI Videos on Twitter

In a decisive move to protect its intellectual property, Take-Two Interactive, the parent company of Rockstar Games, has reportedly initiated Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) takedown notices against users on Twitter. These actions specifically target individuals who have been sharing AI-generated videos falsely purporting to be content from the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6*. This aggressive stance underscores Take-Two's commitment to controlling the narrative around its upcoming titles and preventing the spread of unofficial or fabricated material. The company's swift response to fan-created, AI-generated content suggests a heightened level of vigilance as anticipation for *GTA 6* continues to build, potentially hinting at future official announcements where Take-Two aims to maintain clarity and authenticity.

## Key Takeaways
- Take-Two Interactive is issuing DMCA takedown notices on Twitter.
