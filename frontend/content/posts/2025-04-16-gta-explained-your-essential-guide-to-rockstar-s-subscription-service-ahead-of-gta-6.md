# GTA+ Explained: Your Essential Guide to Rockstar's Subscription Service Ahead of GTA 6

Rockstar Games introduced GTA+ in March 2022 as a premium subscription service for **Grand Theft Auto Online**, initially for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, later expanding to PC in March 2025. This service aims to enhance the GTA Online experience and offers a rotating library of classic Rockstar titles.

### Key Perks and Benefits:

*   **Monthly GTA$**: Subscribers receive GTA$500,000 automatically deposited into their in-game accounts each month.
*   **Shark Card Bonuses**: A 15% bonus is applied to all Shark Card purchases.
*   **In-Game Multipliers & Discounts**: Members benefit from GTA$ multipliers on specific missions and modes, alongside monthly rotating discounts on various in-game items.
*   **Vehicle Access**: Exclusive access to The Vinewood Car Club, offering free vehicles, and a massive 100-car garage spread across five floors.
*   **Monthly Special Bonuses**: Each month brings unique benefits, such as free vehicles (e.g., Grotti Itali RSX in November 2026), new paints, and mansion items.
*   **GTA+ Games Library**: A significant draw is the access to a rotating selection of classic Rockstar titles. As of November 2026, this includes games like *GTA 5*, *Red Dead Redemption*, *GTA Vice City - The Definitive Edition*, *Bully*, and *LA Noire*, playable across PC, console, and mobile.

### Pricing and Sign-Up:

GTA+ launched at $6/month but saw a price increase to **$8/month in 2024**. The subscription can be canceled at any time, with no multi-month or annual options available. To sign up, players need a copy of *GTA V* or *GTA Online* and can subscribe via their platform's store (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC) or directly within the game.

### The GTA 6 Connection:

While Rockstar has not yet confirmed if GTA+ will directly support *GTA 6*, it's anticipated that *GTA Online* for *GTA 5* may continue to operate even after the new title's release. Notably, all preorders for *GTA 6* will include a free month of GTA+, which auto-renews, requiring manual cancellation if not desired.

## FAQ
**What is GTA+?**
GTA+ is a monthly subscription service for Grand Theft Auto Online, launched in March 2022 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and in March 2025 for PC, offering exclusive in-game perks and access to a library of classic Rockstar games.

**What benefits do GTA+ members receive?**
Members get a monthly GTA$500,000 deposit, a 15% bonus on Shark Card purchases, GTA$ multipliers for certain missions, monthly discounts on in-game items, free vehicles through The Vinewood Car Club, a 100-car garage, and access to the GTA+ Games Library.

**How much does GTA+ cost?**
GTA+ costs $8 per month. The price increased from its initial $6/month in 2024. The subscription can be canceled at any time, as there are no multi-month or annual plans.

**Will GTA+ be supported with GTA 6?**
Rockstar Games has not yet confirmed direct GTA+ support for GTA 6. However, all GTA 6 preorders include a free month of GTA+, which auto-renews. It's also possible GTA Online for GTA 5 will continue to be supported after GTA 6's launch.

**What classic games are included in the GTA+ Games Library?**
The GTA+ Games Library offers a rotating selection of classic Rockstar titles. As of November 2026, examples include GTA 5, Red Dead Redemption, GTA Vice City - The Definitive Edition, Bully, GTA: The Trilogy - The Definitive Edition, LA Noire, and more.
