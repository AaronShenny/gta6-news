# GTA 6 Map Leaks Spark Confusion: Which Version is Real?

The ongoing debate surrounding the Grand Theft Auto 6 map has intensified, with fans grappling with conflicting information from various leaks. Two prominent versions are currently circulating: an older '2015 map' and a more recent 'patched-up map from CL'.

The core issue lies in the significant discrepancies between these leaks, particularly regarding the inclusion of additional large islands and a panhandle. This has led to a major question: which map, if any, accurately represents the final vision for GTA 6's open world?

The author highlights concerns that the CL leak, which only showcased the southern portion of the map, might not represent a complete build of the game. This raises doubts about whether rumored additional islands and attractions have truly been scrapped or were simply not present in the partial leak.

Ultimately, these conflicting leaks have, for many, created more confusion than clarity, leaving fans uncertain about the true scope and layout of Vice City and its surrounding areas. A potential '1-D' map without the rumored islands could also render large in-game airports less purposeful, according to the discussion.
