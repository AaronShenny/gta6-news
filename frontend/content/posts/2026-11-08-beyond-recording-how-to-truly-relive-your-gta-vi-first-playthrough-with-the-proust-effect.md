## FAQ
**What is the Proust Effect?**
The Proust Effect is a phenomenon where sensory cues, such as a particular smell, taste, or sound, trigger powerful and vivid involuntary memories.

**How can I apply the Proust Effect to my gaming experience?**
You can deliberately apply it by creating a unique and unusual sensory environment during your first playthrough of a game, making the experience distinct from your everyday routine.

**What kind of sensory changes should I make?**
Focus on changing elements like taste (unusual snacks/drinks), smell (scented candles), sight (different lighting), and touch (changing your gaming posture). The more unusual, the better.

**Is it important to return to my normal routine afterward?**
Yes, it is crucial. By reverting to your normal routine, you ensure that the unique sensory experiences remain specifically associated with that first playthrough, strengthening the memory link.

**How long do these sensory memories last?**
These deliberate sensory memories can last for many years, potentially triggering vivid flashbacks of your first playthrough even a decade later when you re-encounter those specific sensory cues.
