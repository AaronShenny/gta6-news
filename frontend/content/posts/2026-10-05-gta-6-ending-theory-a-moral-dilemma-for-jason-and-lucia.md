# GTA 6 Ending Theory: A Moral Dilemma for Jason and Lucia

A thought-provoking theory circulating within the Grand Theft Auto 6 community suggests a dramatic and morally complex conclusion for the highly anticipated game. Drawing inspiration from a perceived 'loan repayment' gameplay loop, which the theory's author attributes to a 'Love Magazine' article, it posits that protagonist Jason will face a crucial decision regarding Lucia. The core of the theory presents a stark choice: players might be forced to keep Lucia alive, thereby sacrificing all accumulated in-game wealth, cars, and properties and essentially 'starting from zero' in terms of economic progress, or allow Lucia to die to retain all their hard-earned assets. This proposed dilemma aims to leverage the evolving relationship between the two main characters, offering a true moral crossroads that significantly impacts both the narrative and the player's long-term progression within the game world.

## FAQ
**What is the main theory about GTA 6's ending?**
The theory suggests that protagonist Jason will face a moral dilemma: choose to keep Lucia alive but lose all in-game progress (wealth, cars, houses), or let Lucia die to retain all accumulated assets.

The theory is a fan speculation, partly inspired by a Reddit user's interpretation of a 'Love Magazine' article, which they believe hints at a 'loan repayment' gameplay loop impacting story progression.

According to the theory, saving Lucia would mean a significant reset of the player's economic progress, while her death would allow the player to keep their wealth and properties.
