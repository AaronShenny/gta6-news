# Doja Cat Blasts Rockstar Over GTA 6 Music Exclusion: 'So F**kin Bitter'

Grand Theft Auto 6 has launched, featuring a diverse soundtrack from numerous real-world artists. However, superstar Doja Cat is conspicuously absent from the game's music lineup, a fact she has vocally and explicitly lamented online.

Taking to social media, Doja Cat expressed her profound disappointment, stating she was "so f**kin bitter" about being left out of the game. Her frustration escalated as she directly addressed Rockstar, telling the developer to "eat my d**k." Despite her strong language, she affirmed her love for GTA 6, explaining that the exclusion "breaks her heart."

Neither Doja Cat's songs are featured on any of GTA 6's in-game radio stations, nor do they appear on the 34-song "GTA 6: The Album." The album boasts a lineup including Travis Scott, Dua Lipa, Morgan Wallen, Young Thug, Cardi B, Keith Richards, and Lana Del Rey. Meanwhile, the game's radio stations feature a wide array of artists such as Tom Petty, George Strait, Bad Bunny, Drake, Lil Wayne, Slayer, and System of a Down.

Given her public and aggressive remarks, it is highly improbable that Rockstar will consider adding her music to Grand Theft Auto 6 in the future.
