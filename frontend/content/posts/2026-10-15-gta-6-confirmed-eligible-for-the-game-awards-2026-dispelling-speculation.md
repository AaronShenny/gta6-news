# GTA 6 Confirmed Eligible for The Game Awards 2026, Dispelling Speculation

Despite recent speculation, *Grand Theft Auto VI* is indeed eligible for The Game Awards 2026. The highly anticipated title, set to launch on November 19, 2026, falls just one day before the official cutoff date of November 20, 2026, at 9 PM PT. This proximity sparked discussions among some, including reports from Insider Gaming, questioning whether the cutoff date was adjusted to accommodate Rockstar's epic.

However, the article clarifies that this is not the case. Evidence suggests that the Friday before Thanksgiving has consistently served as the cutoff date for The Game Awards nominations in previous years, indicating that Rockstar likely scheduled *GTA VI*'s release to align with Geoff Keighley's established timeline, rather than the awards show making an exception for the game. Previous years' cutoff dates, such as November 21 for 2025 and November 22 for 2024, support this pattern.

The last Rockstar project to be eligible for The Game Awards was *Red Dead Redemption II* in 2018, which garnered 10 nominations and secured four wins, though it did not take home the coveted Game of the Year award. With *Grand Theft Auto VI*'s release now seemingly locked in, its presence at TGA 2026 is all but confirmed, setting the stage for what could be a significant awards season for the title.
