# GTA 5 Switch Mod Project Shuts Down After Take-Two Cease-and-Desist

The ambitious fan-made project, 'Paralympics Productions,' aiming to bring Grand Theft Auto 5 to the Nintendo Switch, has officially announced its termination. The shutdown, effective October 3, comes directly after rights owner Take-Two Interactive issued a cease-and-desist notice to the developer, M0jso. This action aligns with Rockstar Games' recently updated 'Mod Guidelines,' which explicitly prohibit modifying their games for unsupported consoles.

M0jso publicly announced the project's definitive end, expressing gratitude for Take-Two's 'mercy,' hinting at the potential for more severe legal repercussions. The developer confirmed receiving the cease-and-desist and stated intent to comply fully, recognizing the strong legal grounds Take-Two holds. Despite requests from the community, M0jso has firmly refused to release the project's code, fearing it would escalate the situation into a formal lawsuit. The mod was reportedly mere days away from its planned release. While GTA 5 was never officially released on Switch, and GTA 6 is not expected on Switch 2, past reports did suggest Rockstar was exploring the possibility for the next-gen console.
