# Fan Predictions for GTA 6's Story and Features Surface One Week Before Launch

As the gaming community eagerly anticipates the supposed launch of Grand Theft Auto 6, one Reddit user, /u/Every-Examination720, has compiled a comprehensive list of their 'final predictions' for the game's story and features. Shared just a week before what they believe to be the launch date, these predictions offer a glimpse into fan hopes and theories.

The core of the predictions revolves around the game's protagonists, Jason and Lucia. The user foresees **three distinct story endings**: one where Jason dies, another where Lucia dies, and a 'happy ending' where both survive. A period of estrangement between the two main characters is also predicted. Lucia's character arc is highlighted, with a strong emphasis on family, suggesting either a betrayal or death involving a family member.

Specific story beats include Lucia's eventual return to prison, prompting Jason to orchestrate a breakout that would lead into the game's climactic final hours. The user also predicts the demise of a character named Drequan and Lucia's direct involvement in killing Brian.

Beyond the main narrative, the predictions extend to potential easter eggs and gameplay elements. A notable prediction is a **Michael (from GTA 5) easter egg**, appearing as a movie he directed called 'Paradise,' based on GTA 5's story. Other predicted easter eggs include nods to Dexter, a cameo by Roman, a time traveler from Red Dead Redemption 2, and even an Arthur Morgan (RDR2) reference, perhaps on a t-shirt. On the gameplay front, it's suggested that Lucia's personal vehicle will be customizable by the player. Furthermore, the user anticipates **Story Mode DLC** and the potential inclusion of **Liberty City/Los Santos** in some capacity, possibly as part of future expansions.
