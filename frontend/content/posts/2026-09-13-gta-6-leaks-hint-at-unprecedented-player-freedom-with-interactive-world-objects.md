# GTA 6 Leaks Hint at Unprecedented Player Freedom with Interactive World Objects

Observations from leaked Grand Theft Auto VI gameplay footage indicate a significant enhancement in player interaction with the game world. According to a Reddit user analyzing an "extended look," unique object icons disappear upon being thrown, suggesting a dynamic system for interactive items. A prime example highlighted is the basketball, which appears to function not only as a minigame prop but also as a "unique pick up object." This implies players can utilize such items outside their intended minigame contexts, offering an "extra layer of total freedom." The theory suggests players could pick up a basketball, potentially store it in a vehicle's trunk (with an American football seen in a screenshot supporting the idea of trunk storage for various items), play basketball anywhere in Leonida, or even throw it at NPCs.Further supporting this theory, YouTuber TGG reportedly confirmed that even dog poop can be picked up and used to throw at pedestrians. This strengthens the "educated guess" that GTA VI will feature "a ton of unique objects" with potential unique in-game icons, allowing for extensive creativity and roleplaying. The article posits that Rockstar has significantly expanded upon similar, albeit simpler, mechanics seen in GTA IV (like throwing bricks or beer bottles), evolving it into a more "fleshed out system" for GTA VI. This suggests a highly interactive environment designed for player experimentation and chaotic fun.
