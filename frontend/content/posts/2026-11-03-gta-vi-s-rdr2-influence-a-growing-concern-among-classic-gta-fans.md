# GTA VI's RDR2 Influence: A Growing Concern Among Classic GTA Fans?

A recent Reddit post has sparked a lively debate among the Grand Theft Auto community, posing a critical question to fans who struggled to connect with Red Dead Redemption 2 (RDR2): How do they feel about the prospect of Grand Theft Auto VI being heavily influenced by RDR2's gameplay philosophy?

The discussion originates from the observation that many long-time GTA enthusiasts found RDR2 to be overly slow, intensely cinematic, or even clunky in its mechanics, a stark contrast to the rapid, arcade-like chaos that defined Grand Theft Auto V. This stylistic divergence has led to concerns that if GTA VI adopts similar characteristics from RDR2, it might not resonate with the segment of the fanbase that cherishes the high-octane, immediate action synonymous with the classic GTA experience. The post underscores a potential divide in player expectations for Rockstar's next blockbuster title.
