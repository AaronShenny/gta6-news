# GTA 6’s Astonishing $4 Billion Day One Includes 32.5 Million Copies Sold

Grand Theft Auto VI has officially launched to unprecedented success, with publisher Take-Two announcing staggering day-one figures. The game sold an astonishing **32.5 million copies before its official release** and generated a monumental **$3.79 billion in revenue** by the end of its first day on sale, November 19. This makes GTA 6 the biggest entertainment launch across any medium in history, eclipsing even the all-time highest-grossing movie, *Avengers: Endgame*, which made $2.94 billion in its entire run.

Compared to its predecessor, GTA 6 is pacing significantly ahead; GTA 5 earned $800 million in its first 24 hours. The new title's higher price point ($80 for the cheapest version vs. GTA 5's $60) likely contributed to the increased revenue.

Rockstar president and co-founder Sam Houser expressed immense gratitude, stating, "We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world."

These official figures from Take-Two surpass earlier unofficial predictions from Sensor Tower, which had reported 25 million sales at launch. The game's sales trajectory is expected to continue upwards, with a **PC release anticipated in 2027** and a dedicated **online mode also slated for 2027**, both of which are expected to open new revenue streams through additional sales cycles and potential microtransactions.

## Key Takeaways
