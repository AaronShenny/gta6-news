# GTA VI Marketing Material Reportedly Leaks Across Melbourne, Australia

A Reddit user, /u/lightjunior, has reported spotting extensive marketing material for Grand Theft Auto VI across various locations in Melbourne, Australia. The sightings include full-wall advertisements at the Commercial Road/St Kilda Road tram stop and a 'massive wraparound billboard' at St Kilda Junction. The user also noted marketing at the Young and Jackson pub near Flinders St Station and the corner of Collins St & Elizabeth St. Notably, the user claims to have seen previously unrevealed official artwork on one of the billboards. Additionally, there was a mention of a Route 11 tram fully adorned with GTA VI artwork, though the original poster was unable to locate it themselves. These sightings suggest an early rollout or leak of Rockstar's marketing campaign for the highly anticipated title.
