# GTA 6's World, Characters, and Deep Systems Revealed in EDGE Interview

An exclusive interview with Rockstar Games in EDGE magazine has unveiled a wealth of new details about Grand Theft Auto VI, highlighting its ambitious open world, intricate character dynamics, and groundbreaking gameplay systems. Players will be able to explore Leonida's vast map relatively early in the game, a design choice accommodating those who prioritize discovery over linear story progression. Co-Studio Head Aaron Garbut even admits to still finding new locations after years of development, underscoring the map's immense density.

Environmental immersion is being pushed further with a new system for character reactions to unpleasant smells, exemplified by Jason grimacing in a dog-fouled house. The day-night cycle is significantly extended, offering more atmospheric opportunities, and cloud systems now move independently, reacting to wind, humidity, fog, time of day, and season. Transportation is also enhanced with a "fully functioning bus and train system," as confirmed by Head of Development Phil Hooker.

Protagonists Jason and Lucia were meticulously cast and are central to the narrative. Their relationship is a key focus, bound by both emotional and professional history, and measured on a four-point scale. Players will have agency in defining their closeness, with even the possibility of marriage hinted at. Gameplay mechanics include quick weapon access from a car boot, with "Scooter Brothers" available for car delivery. Police encounters promise increased intensity, featuring spike strips and K-9 units that can quickly apprehend players. The world also feels more alive with dynamic NPC interactions, such as groups challenging players to races.

Rockstar is reportedly moving beyond the traditional "rags to riches" narrative, instead exploring themes of "what kind of life you are trying to build inside of it." The development process operates on six-to-eight-week milestones, with developers expressing immense pride in their work. Garbut likens the achievement to "building a pyramid on Everest," while Rob Nelson confidently declares, "It's ready," signaling the game's imminent transition from developer creation to player experience.

## FAQ
**Can players explore the map freely from the start?**
Yes, Leonida's map opens up relatively early, allowing players to head north and explore without strictly following the story.

**How immersive is the game's environment?**
The game features dynamic character reactions to environmental stimuli like unpleasant smells, an extended day-night cycle, and independently moving cloud systems influenced by weather and season.

**What is the nature of Jason and Lucia's relationship?**
They are bound by emotional and professional history, with their closeness measured on a four-point scale. Players can define their intimacy, and even their legal status (like marriage) is hinted at as a possibility.

**Are there new public transportation systems?**
Yes, the game will feature a fully functioning bus and train system that players can utilize.

**How intense are police chases in GTA 6?**
Police units communicate via radio, deploy spike strips that are nearly invisible at high speeds, and K-9 units can join pursuits, quickly pinning down players.
