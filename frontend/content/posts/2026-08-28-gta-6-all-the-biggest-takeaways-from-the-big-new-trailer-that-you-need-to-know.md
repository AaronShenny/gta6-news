# GTA 6: All The Biggest Takeaways From The Big New Trailer That You Need To Know

Rockstar Games recently showcased a substantial 30-minute trailer for Grand Theft Auto 6 at a Netflix event on August 27. The reveal offered an in-depth look at the game, featuring its dual protagonists, Jason and Lucia, in various mission snippets, cutscenes, and character interactions.

The trailer also highlighted several updated gameplay systems. Notably, NPCs will now react if players openly carry weapons in public, the car-stealing mechanics have been revamped, and the game will incorporate a `Red Dead Redemption 2`-style honor system that tracks player violence.

While the article references a GameSpot video by Jake Dekker detailing 29 observations from the trailer, the core confirmed details are the game's launch date: November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. A PC version is anticipated but not expected at launch. Rockstar has yet to disclose any information regarding an online multiplayer mode, despite the immense success of `GTA Online` for `GTA 5`.

## Key Takeaways
- A new, nearly 30-minute GTA 6 trailer was revealed at a Netflix event on August 27.
- Main characters Jason and Lucia were shown in action, including missions, cutscenes, and interactions.
- New gameplay systems include NPCs reacting to visible weapons, updated car theft mechanics, and a `Red Dead Redemption 2`-style honor system.
- GTA 6 is scheduled to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- A PC version is expected but will not be available at launch.
- Rockstar has not yet revealed any details about a multiplayer mode for GTA 6.
