# GTA 6 Leak Reveals Lucia's Prison Prologue and Bonnie & Clyde Dynamic

The highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6* has been hit with another significant gameplay leak, this time revealing crucial story spoilers. The leak, reportedly from the game's prologue, showcases playable character Lucia in prison, a detail previously hinted at in official trailers. This new footage addresses concerns from previous leaks that primarily focused on Jason, giving players a glimpse into Lucia's initial predicament.

The leaked prologue also teases elements of Lucia's backstory, sparking fan speculation that she might strike a deal with federal authorities to secure her release by implicating her partner, Jason. While this narrative trope is common, fans are hopeful Rockstar Games will deliver a more innovative take.

Further insights into the game's narrative come from Rockstar's Senior VP of Narrative, Rupert Humphries, who confirmed to Dazed that *GTA 6* is structured as a 'Bonnie and Clyde-type narrative.' This suggests a potentially dramatic and intertwined fate for Jason and Lucia.

Players will have the flexibility to control either Jason or Lucia, or even play 'as a couple,' leveraging a fluid system that allows seamless character switching for actions like passenger-seat combat or joint jetski rides. The official plot description reinforces their reliance on each other after a botched 'easy score' lands them in a criminal conspiracy across Leonida.

*GTA 6* is slated for launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with Rockstar planning to reveal more details via a Netflix stream on August 27.
