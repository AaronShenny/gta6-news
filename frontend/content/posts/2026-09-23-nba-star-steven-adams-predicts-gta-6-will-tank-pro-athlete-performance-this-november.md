# NBA Star Steven Adams Predicts GTA 6 Will Tank Pro Athlete Performance This November

Houston Rockets center Steven Adams has made a lighthearted, yet attention-grabbing, prediction: the release of Grand Theft Auto 6 this November will cause a significant drop in performance across major professional sports leagues. Speaking to the press, Adams suggested that top athletes would be too preoccupied with the game, leading to a temporary decline in play quality throughout November. He specifically mentioned the NFL and other leagues, stating, "Look at the NFL, look at all the leagues, and watch them plummet. Just for the month of November, performance is gonna go down." Adams did, however, add a caveat, expecting performance to rebound once athletes have completed the game, hoping they "finish the game." Beyond player performance, Adams also joked that coaching and scouting efforts would be "horrendous" during the same period.

While Adams' comments are clearly not entirely serious, they highlight the immense anticipation surrounding GTA 6, which is expected to be one of the biggest game releases ever. The article notes that many people, including professional athletes, will undoubtedly be playing it. The piece also recalls past instances of athletes linking video games to their on-field struggles, such as an NFL player blaming his performance on a video game and Kyler Murray's known affinity for Call of Duty. GTA 6 is set to release on November 19, a day when the Rockets do not play, but four NBA games and a Week 11 Thursday Night Football matchup are scheduled.
