# GTA 6 Removes The Series’ Worst Feature

In a significant departure from its predecessors, Grand Theft Auto 6 is reportedly ditching the series' traditional face button sprinting mechanic. According to Italian YouTuber MikeShowSha, players will now sprint by clicking the left analog stick, a welcome change that aligns with modern gaming control schemes. This update addresses a long-standing criticism from fans who found the old method less intuitive and a relic of past gaming eras. While this control scheme adjustment is highlighted, the article also humorously notes other 'less-important changes' for GTA 6, including a map three times larger than Red Dead Redemption 2's, a new Criminal Profile system, throwable dog poop, and a physical-fitness system. Grand Theft Auto 6 is slated for release on November 19 for Xbox Series X|S and PS5, poised to be one of the biggest entertainment launches ever.
