# Ocarina of Time Remake Rumored to Clash with GTA 6 in November

Gaming enthusiasts are speculating that a remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time could launch in mid-November 2026, placing it in direct competition with the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6, which is expected on November 19. This theory originates from observations of Nintendo's Amiibo release patterns. Specifically, fans pointed to now-removed Nintendo UK store listings for two Kirby Air Riders Amiibo figures, which reportedly showed a November 11 or 12 release date. Nintendo has a history of launching Amiibo figures on the same day as its first-party game releases, even if the Amiibos are not directly tied to the new game, a pattern supported by several recent examples like the Mineru's Construct Amiibo aligning with Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave. Given Nintendo's confirmed 2026 release window for the Ocarina of Time remake, a November slot appears plausible. While most developers are actively avoiding GTA 6's launch window, a simultaneous release of Ocarina of Time would represent a unique 'counter-programming' strategy. The article suggests both titles could thrive despite the proximity, as they cater to distinct audiences, and GTA 6 will not be available on Switch 2 at launch, giving Ocarina of Time a clear path to Switch 2 players.
