# PlayStation Ad Unveils Uncropped GTA 6 Trailer 2 Scene, Revealing New Visual Details

Fans have discovered that a specific scene from the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 2, originally presented with cinematic letterboxing, has now appeared uncropped in PlayStation's latest promotional video, 'It Happens On PS5.' This uncropped version provides a wider field of view, allowing viewers to see previously obscured details, specifically making 'the tip of that framework on the left side of the scene much clearer.' The observation, made after a significant period of fan anticipation, also notes a decreased contrast level in the PlayStation ad's rendition of the scene. Interestingly, a known visual anomaly referred to as the 'floating light pole phenomenon' is still present and appears to be unaddressed in this newer footage.
