# GTA 6: Is October 15th the Date for New Content?

Speculation is mounting among the Grand Theft Auto community that **October 15th** could mark a significant date for new GTA 6 content. This theory is primarily fueled by reports suggesting that the game's marketing campaign, including billboards and advertising spaces in Miami, is slated to commence on this very day.Adding weight to this rumor is the fact that October 15th falls on a **Thursday**, a day that has consistently aligned with Rockstar Games' recent release patterns for GTA VI updates. Fans are particularly hopeful for a more structured **gameplay trailer**, moving beyond the previously released 'Extended Look' and echoing the detailed gameplay showcases seen in past Rockstar titles.However, a note of caution is also present. The article raises the possibility that Rockstar might follow a marketing strategy similar to Red Dead Redemption 2, where a gap occurred between major content drops. RDR2's first gameplay trailer dropped on August 9, 2018, followed by the second on October 1, with no new content in September. Fans are hoping this pattern isn't replicated for GTA VI, eager for continuous updates. The source for this speculation is attributed to 'The Miamian'.

## Key Takeaways
- Rumors suggest new GTA 6 content could be released on October 15th.
- This date reportedly marks the start of the GTA VI marketing campaign in Miami, featuring billboards and advertising.
- October 15th is a Thursday, consistent with Rockstar's recent content release schedule for GTA VI.
- Fans are hoping for a more comprehensive gameplay trailer.
- There's concern Rockstar might skip September for new content, similar to the Red Dead Redemption 2 marketing timeline.
