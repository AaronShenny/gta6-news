# Capcom Unfazed as Digital Dominance Rises, GTA 6 Confirmed Digital-Only

The gaming industry is rapidly transitioning towards an all-digital future, a trend underscored by Sony's decision to cease physical game disc production for PlayStation by 2028. While this move has sparked discussions about game preservation and consumer ownership, major publisher Capcom remains largely unaffected. The company stated that approximately 90% of its unit sales are already digital, mitigating any significant impact from the shrinking physical market.

This digital shift is not unique to Capcom; it's a widespread industry phenomenon. Nintendo recently reported that digital game sales constituted 61.5% of its total sales, an increase from the previous year. Similarly, Electronic Arts' latest report indicated that 81% of its game sales were digital. Sony attributes its decision to 'consumer trends,' though experts also suggest that the move to an all-digital ecosystem grants publishers greater power, control, and profitability.

A significant indicator of this future is the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6*, which is confirmed to be a digital exclusive. Even when purchased from retailers, players will receive a box containing only a code to download the game, solidifying the industry's pivot away from physical media.

## Key Takeaways
- Capcom is largely unaffected by the decline of physical game sales, with 90% of its unit sales already being digital.
- Sony plans to end support for physical game discs on PlayStation by 2028, citing 'consumer trends.'
- The shift to digital sales is a widespread industry trend, with Nintendo (61.5% digital) and Electronic Arts (81% digital) also reporting high digital percentages.
- Experts believe the move to all-digital is driven by increased profitability and control for game publishers.
- Grand Theft Auto 6 is confirmed to be a digital-exclusive title, with retail purchases providing only a download code.

## FAQ
**Why is Sony ending physical game disc support?**
Sony attributes its decision to 'consumer trends' and the growing preference for digital games. Industry experts also suggest it's driven by factors like profitability and increased control for publishers.

**How will the decline of physical games impact Capcom?**
Capcom anticipates minimal impact, as approximately 90% of its current unit sales are already digital. The company states it does not expect a significant effect at this time.

**Will Grand Theft Auto 6 be available on disc?**
No, Grand Theft Auto 6 will be a digital exclusive. Even if purchased from a retailer, the physical box will only contain a download code for the game.

**Are other major publishers also seeing a shift to digital sales?**
Yes, other major publishers like Nintendo and Electronic Arts have reported high percentages of digital sales. Nintendo's digital sales made up 61.5% of total sales, and EA reported 81% digital sales in their latest earnings.
