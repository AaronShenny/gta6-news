# GTA 6 Community Voice: Fan Calls for Dedicated Metalcore and Hard Rock Radio Station

A recent post on the r/GTA6 subreddit has ignited discussions among fans regarding the potential radio station lineup for the upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI. User /u/Limp-Mood3836 articulated a fervent wish for a dedicated metalcore and hard rock station, acknowledging the widespread popularity of genres like hip hop, rap, pop, and country, but highlighting a significant segment of the player base that doesn't resonate with them. The user expressed dissatisfaction with past rock stations in previous GTA titles, specifically citing GTA 4's offerings as 'garbage' and not representative of their preferred sound. To illustrate their point, /u/Limp-Mood3836 provided examples of artists and bands they would like to hear, including **Slipknot**, **Motionless in White**, **Chevelle**, and **Spirit Box**, emphasizing the need for at least one station catering to modern metalcore and hard rock enthusiasts.
