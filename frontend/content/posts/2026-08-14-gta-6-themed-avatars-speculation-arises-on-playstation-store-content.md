# GTA 6 Themed Avatars: Speculation Arises on PlayStation Store Content

A recent post on the r/GTA6 subreddit has sparked discussion among fans regarding the potential release of **Grand Theft Auto VI** themed PlayStation Avatars. User /u/RehanEU initiated the conversation, expressing keen interest in seeing what these avatars might look like. The speculation heavily draws parallels to Rockstar Games' previous title, *Red Dead Redemption 2*. The user notes that RDR2's avatars were released on the PlayStation Store concurrently with the official launch of *Red Dead Online* out of its beta phase on May 14, 2019. This historical precedent leads to the hypothesis that GTA 6 themed avatars could similarly drop when GTA 6's highly anticipated Online Mode eventually goes live. While there's no official confirmation from Rockstar Games, the community is already envisioning a range of character and thematic avatars, mirroring the style and characters expected from the upcoming blockbuster title. This minor detail highlights the immense anticipation surrounding all aspects of GTA 6, from major gameplay reveals to subtle cosmetic additions.
