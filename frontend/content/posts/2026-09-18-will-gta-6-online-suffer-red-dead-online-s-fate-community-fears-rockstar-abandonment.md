# Will GTA 6 Online Suffer Red Dead Online's Fate? Community Fears Rockstar Abandonment

A recent discussion on the r/GTA6 subreddit has ignited a debate among fans regarding the potential future of *Grand Theft Auto 6 Online*. User /u/DingdongIwearathong posted a thread expressing significant worries that Rockstar Games might prematurely abandon *GTA 6 Online* if it fails to achieve the same monumental profitability as its predecessor, *Grand Theft Auto V Online*.

The primary concern stems from the belief that *GTA 5 Online* benefits from an established, massive player base, particularly a younger demographic, who consistently spend on in-game purchases like Shark Cards. The user speculates that *GTA 6 Online* might struggle to replicate this level of success or engagement, leading Rockstar to deprioritize its development.

This apprehension is further fueled by Rockstar's past actions, specifically citing the perceived abandonment of *Red Dead Online*. The user highlights that despite initial expectations for *Red Dead Online* to become a new 'cash cow,' its development slowed significantly, leaving many players feeling neglected. The question posed is, 'What's stopping them from doing the same with GTA 6 Online?' This sentiment resonates with a segment of the community, prompting discussions on Rockstar's long-term commitment to its online titles beyond initial launch success.

## Key Takeaways
- A Reddit user expressed concern about the future of GTA 6 Online's long-term support.
- The main worry is that Rockstar might abandon GTA 6 Online if it isn't as profitable as GTA 5 Online.
- GTA 5 Online's profitability is attributed to its large, established, and often younger player base spending on Shark Cards.
- The perceived abandonment of Red Dead Online is cited as a precedent for Rockstar potentially shifting focus.
- The community is debating Rockstar's commitment to new online titles versus maintaining highly profitable existing ones.
