# GTA 6 Branding Spotted on Golf Carts in Downtown Miami

Recent sightings in downtown Miami have set the gaming community abuzz, as golf carts have been spotted sporting official Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) branding. The images, originally shared on Reddit by user /u/Jichiya, show the distinctive GTA 6 logo and wrapping on the vehicles. While not an official announcement, the appearance of GTA 6 branding in a city widely believed to be the real-world inspiration for Vice City in the upcoming game has been interpreted by many as a strong hint towards the game's setting or an early marketing initiative. The original post includes a link to a video showcasing the golf carts.
