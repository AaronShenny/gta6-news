# GTA 6 Fans Demand Fresh Soundscapes and Interiors: No More Reused Assets

A recent post on the r/GTA6 subreddit highlights a significant sentiment among fans: the fervent wish for Grand Theft Auto 6 to feature entirely new audio assets and vehicle interiors. The user, /u/WeGotThisBeforeGTA7, explicitly states a desire to avoid any reused vehicle sounds, gun sounds, ambient sounds, and vehicle interior designs from previous GTA titles. The core argument is that for GTA 6 to truly 'feel completely different' and offer a fresh experience, Rockstar Games should invest in creating a brand-new auditory and visual identity, rather than relying on recycled elements. This sentiment underscores the community's high expectations for innovation and immersion in the upcoming installment.

## Key Takeaways
- Fans are strongly requesting all-new vehicle sounds for GTA 6.
- A desire for unique, non-reused vehicle interiors has been expressed.
- The community wants fresh gun sounds to enhance the game's feel.
- Players hope for entirely new ambient sounds to create a distinct atmosphere.
- The overarching goal is for GTA 6 to feel 'completely different' from its predecessors through new assets.
