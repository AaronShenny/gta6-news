# Rockstar Just Launched GTA 6 Marketing in Miami with a Nearly $1M 'Welcome to Vice City' Sign

Rockstar Games has officially kicked off its real-world marketing campaign for Grand Theft Auto 6, bringing the iconic Vice City to life in Miami. A colossal 'Welcome to Vice City' sign, prominently featuring the 'VI' logo, has been erected on the roof of the Kaseya Center, home to the Miami Heat. The arena's screens are also reportedly displaying 'VICE'.

According to an agreement shared by SourceVI, Rockstar paid Miami-Dade County a substantial **$975,000** for this high-profile installation. This payment was structured in three milestones: $243,750 upon agreement signing, another $243,750 when materials were delivered, and the final $487,500 once the sign became 'Substantially Functional'.

The complex installation process involved lifting the sign onto the roof by helicopter over several hours, a spectacle captured and shared by @officialjoelfranco on Instagram. Initial reports noted a minor flaw where the 'i' in 'Vice' was incorrectly installed, with its wrap missing and folded over the 'c'. However, this issue has reportedly been rectified, though official photos of the fully completed sign are yet to be released. This move marks a significant step in the GTA 6 promotional cycle, leaving fans eager to see what further real-world activations Rockstar has planned for Miami and beyond.
