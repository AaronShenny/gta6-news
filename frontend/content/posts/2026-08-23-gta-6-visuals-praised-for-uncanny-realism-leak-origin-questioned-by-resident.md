# GTA 6 Visuals Praised for Uncanny Realism, Leak Origin Questioned by Resident

A Reddit user, claiming to reside in the areas featured in the recent *Grand Theft Auto 6* leaks, has provided a compelling firsthand account of the game's visual fidelity, asserting that the leaked footage offers an 'uncanny' and 'hyper-realistic' depiction of their home environment. The user expressed a profound sense of familiarity, stating that watching the game's visuals evoked a feeling of 'home' and even a 'physical unease' due to its accuracy. Addressing criticisms regarding the game's color grading, the user, a Florida resident, defended the visual style as highly authentic to the state's climate. They noted that Florida's intense heat can cause a perceptible shift in colors, making the game's palette entirely believable. The overall sentiment was that the game 'absolutely knocks it out of the park' in terms of visuals. The post also touched upon common complaints about the leaked build, such as rudimentary animations and minor glitches like money disappearing from registers. The user emphasized that the leaked footage represents an older development build, likely 'at least a few months old,' and not indicative of the current state of the game. A positive note was made about the realistic car damage, suggesting a welcome return to realism. Finally, the user voiced significant concern over the cybersecurity lapse that led to the leaks. Drawing a parallel to recent incidents involving ChatGPT and Hugging Face, they contrasted modern cloud-hosted development with older practices, where a friend's father at EA ensured test builds were 'air-locked' and never connected to the internet. The user expressed astonishment that a project of *GTA 6*'s magnitude and investment was hosted online, leading to such a significant security breach.
