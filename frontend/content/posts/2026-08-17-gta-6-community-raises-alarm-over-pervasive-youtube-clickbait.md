# GTA 6 Community Raises Alarm Over Pervasive YouTube Clickbait

A concerned Reddit user, /u/Zantox12, has voiced strong criticism regarding the current state of GTA 6 content on YouTube, highlighting what they describe as a significant problem with clickbait. The user points out that many content creators employ deceptive tactics, such as placing the word 'concept' at the very end of titles to avoid immediate visibility, thereby misleading viewers into believing they are watching genuine leaks or official news. 

The post laments that these channels often disseminate 'complete bullshit words' and expresses a fear that such 'garbage content' could ultimately 'ruin the integrity of GTA 6' once the game is officially released. Furthermore, the Redditor criticizes YouTube's algorithm for actively promoting these questionable channels and videos when users search for 'GTA 6' content, stating that attempts to report such misleading videos have proven ineffective. This sentiment reflects a growing frustration within the dedicated GTA 6 community over the quality and veracity of unofficial content surrounding the highly anticipated title.
