# GTA 6 Car Deformation: What Trailers and Leaks Reveal So Far

The highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 has fans closely scrutinizing every detail, and vehicle deformation is a prominent point of discussion. Based on observations from official trailers and leaked gameplay, there's a noticeable concern among the community regarding the fidelity of car damage. While major crashes in trailers often culminate in spectacular explosions, specific instances of 'minor' crash deformation, reminiscent of the detailed damage models seen in GTA IV, appear to be largely absent. However, Trailer 2 did offer a glimpse of deformation with a police car door visibly bent during an explosion. Unofficial leaks, while showing elements like popping hoods and detached parts, haven't yet showcased the deep, realistic crumpling many players hope for. The overall sentiment suggests that while some deformation exists, the extent and variety of damage are still largely under wraps, leaving players to wonder about the final implementation.

## Key Takeaways
- Community concern exists regarding the extent of car deformation in GTA 6.
- Trailers predominantly show vehicles exploding in crashes, with limited examples of minor deformation.
- Detailed 'real deformation,' akin to GTA IV, appears absent in observed minor incidents.
- Trailer 2 did feature a bent police car door during an explosion, confirming some level of deformation.
- Leaked footage shows cosmetic damage like hoods popping open and parts falling off, but not extensive structural crumpling.
- The full scope and fidelity of the vehicle damage system in GTA 6 remain largely unknown.
