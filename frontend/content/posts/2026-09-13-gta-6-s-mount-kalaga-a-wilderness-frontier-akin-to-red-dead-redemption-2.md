# GTA 6's Mount Kalaga: A Wilderness Frontier Akin to Red Dead Redemption 2?

A recent discussion on Reddit's r/GTA6 subreddit, initiated by user /u/MillienumDuckFighter, has sparked speculation regarding the purpose of Mount Kalaga and the northern areas of the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6 map. The user observed a notable lack of towns and settlements in this region, prompting questions about Rockstar Games' design intent.

The primary theory suggests that Rockstar might be aiming to create a more 'wildlife-centred' area, similar to the expansive, nature-rich environments found in Red Dead Redemption 2. This design choice could provide players with significant opportunities for exploration and immersion in natural landscapes, offering a contrasting experience to the urban sprawl typically associated with GTA titles. The discussion invites community members to share their thoughts on whether this less-civilized northern expanse is indeed intended as a wilderness playground.

## Key Takeaways
- Observation of fewer towns and settlements around Mount Kalaga and the northern GTA 6 map.
