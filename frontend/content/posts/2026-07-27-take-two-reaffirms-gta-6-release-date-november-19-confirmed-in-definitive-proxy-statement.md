# Take-Two Reaffirms GTA 6 Release Date: November 19 Confirmed in Definitive Proxy Statement

Take-Two Interactive has officially filed the definitive version of its proxy statement, a crucial document that provides transparency to shareholders. Within this filing, the letter from CEO Strauss Zelnick to shareholders remains identical to the preliminary version released approximately ten days prior. Most notably for the gaming community, the statement unequivocally reaffirms that the highly anticipated title, Grand Theft Auto 6 (GTA 6), is still slated for a release on November 19. This consistent messaging from Take-Two's leadership through official corporate filings solidifies the previously communicated release window, offering a clear confirmation for fans eagerly awaiting the next installment in the iconic series.

## Key Takeaways
- Take-Two Interactive has submitted the definitive version of its proxy statement.
- CEO Strauss Zelnick's letter to shareholders remains unchanged from the preliminary filing.
- The statement explicitly reaffirms GTA 6's planned release date.
- Grand Theft Auto 6 is confirmed to be targeting a November 19 release.
