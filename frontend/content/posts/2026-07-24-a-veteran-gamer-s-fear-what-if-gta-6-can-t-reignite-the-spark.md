# A Veteran Gamer's Fear: What If GTA 6 Can't Reignite the Spark?

In a poignant reflection, Reddit user /u/Krolevets shares a unique gaming journey, spanning from a Sega Mega Drive 2 three decades ago to a brand-new PS5 Pro. Having largely missed out on cutting-edge gaming experiences throughout his life—only playing GTA IV and V years after their release and experiencing modern titles like RDR2 and Cyberpunk 2077 via cloud gaming—the author is now, for the first time, equipped with a top-tier platform for an anticipated blockbuster: GTA VI.

Despite owning the "best console" for its launch (even without a premium 4K monitor or immersive sound system), the author reveals a growing anxiety. As the speculative release date of November 19 approaches, a profound question looms: "what if I don't even feel like playing?" This concern stems from a broader observation that with age, his interest in video games, and many other previously cherished hobbies, has gradually waned. The article captures the bittersweet anticipation of a gamer finally having the means to experience a major title at launch, only to confront the possibility that the passion itself might have faded.

## Key Takeaways
- The author, a long-time gamer, recently purchased a PS5 Pro, marking his return to modern console gaming after 30 years.
- He has historically missed out on playing games with the best available graphics at launch, experiencing titles like GTA IV, V, Red Dead Redemption 2, and Cyberpunk 2077 much later or through cloud services.
- For the first time, he possesses the "best console" to play GTA VI at its anticipated launch.
- Despite this, he expresses a significant worry that he might find GTA VI boring or simply not want to play it, attributing this fear to a general decline in interest in hobbies as he has gotten older.
