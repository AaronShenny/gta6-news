# Welcome to Vice City: Iconic Sign Appears on Miami's Kaseya Center, Fueling GTA 6 Hype

Grand Theft Auto fans are buzzing with excitement as a massive 'WELCOME TO VICE CITY' sign is reportedly being installed on the Kaseya Center in Miami. The Kaseya Center, formerly known as the FTX Arena, is a prominent landmark in downtown Miami, which is widely recognized as the real-world inspiration for the fictional Vice City in the Grand Theft Auto universe. Images and videos circulating online show the sign being erected, creating a significant stir within the gaming community. While Rockstar Games has yet to officially comment on this installation, the timing and location strongly suggest a direct connection to the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6.
