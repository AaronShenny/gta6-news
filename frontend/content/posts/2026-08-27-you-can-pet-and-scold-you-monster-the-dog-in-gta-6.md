# You Can Pet (And Scold, You Monster) The Dog In GTA 6

Grand Theft Auto VI made its highly anticipated debut on Netflix, revealing a charming detail: the presence of dogs and multiple ways for players to interact with them. During an in-game sequence, protagonists Jason and Lucia encounter a dog, presenting interaction options beyond the typical 'pet'. Players will be able to 'pet' the dog, an expected feature in modern games, but also 'scold' and 'study' them.

The 'scold' option suggests a potential behavioral mechanic for dogs, though its exact function remains to be seen. The 'study' option, reminiscent of Red Dead Redemption 2's wildlife mechanics, hints at the possibility of studying a broader range of animals within GTA 6. Further details from a New York Times report confirm that players can also purchase and give treats to dogs found in the game world, adding another layer to the animal interaction system.
