# GTA 6's Console Sales Boost May Be Muted by High PS5 and Xbox Prices

Rockstar's highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6* is slated for a November 19 launch on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it's widely expected to drive a significant surge in console sales. However, industry experts caution that this boost might be less dramatic than anticipated.

According to Circana's Mat Piscatella, the 'astronomical' current prices of new consoles could temper enthusiasm. A historical comparison reveals a stark difference: when *GTA 5* launched in 2013, the inflation-adjusted prices for a new PS3 and Xbox 360 were $363 and $334 respectively. Today, the average PS5 costs $646 and an Xbox Series X|S averages $663, nearly double the cost.

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick, whose company oversees Rockstar, acknowledged that 'the rising cost of hardware is not a good thing,' as lower prices would mean more consoles in consumers' hands. Despite this, Zelnick expressed confidence that *GTA 6*'s quality will compel players to acquire a new console, irrespective of the price point. The game will first be available to players in New Zealand.
