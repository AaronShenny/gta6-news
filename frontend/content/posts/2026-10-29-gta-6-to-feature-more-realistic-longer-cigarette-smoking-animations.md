# GTA 6 to Feature More Realistic, Longer Cigarette Smoking Animations

Gaming enthusiasts can rejoice as new information indicates that Grand Theft Auto 6 will include significantly more detailed and longer cigarette smoking animations. This comes as a welcome change for players who previously expressed dissatisfaction with the brief, single-drag animations seen in titles like Red Dead Redemption 2, where characters like Arthur Morgan would quickly discard cigarettes. The improved animations are expected to offer a heightened sense of realism and immersion, a detail that was even a subject of community modifications in past Rockstar games to extend these very actions. This small yet impactful detail underscores Rockstar's commitment to delivering a deeply immersive experience in their highly anticipated title.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 will feature extended and more realistic cigarette smoking animations.
- This improvement addresses player feedback from previous Rockstar titles, such as Red Dead Redemption 2, where smoking animations were brief.
