# GTA 6's Morgan Revolver Easter Egg Nods to Vice City Legacy

The Morgan revolvers, a notable inclusion in the Grand Theft Auto VI Ultimate Edition, contain a fascinating Easter egg for long-time fans of the series. As initially revealed in June, the serial numbers etched into these in-game firearms directly reference the original release dates of two iconic titles: *Grand Theft Auto: Vice City* (October 29, 2002) and *Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories* (October 31, 2006). This subtle, nostalgic detail connects the upcoming installment to its revered Vice City predecessors, a setting GTA VI is confirmed to revisit, providing a clever nod to the franchise's rich history.

## Key Takeaways
- The Morgan revolvers in GTA VI's Ultimate Edition feature a hidden Easter egg.
- Their serial numbers contain direct references to the release dates of GTA Vice City and GTA Vice City Stories.
- The specific dates referenced are October 29, 2002, and October 31, 2006.
- This nostalgic detail was first revealed in June.
- It serves as a tribute to the Vice City era, a setting GTA VI is returning to.
