# GTA 6: Players Hope for Dynamic Robbery Failures, Not Predictable Shootouts

A recent Reddit post from u/Strong-Telephone-474 has sparked a discussion among the GTA 6 community regarding the outcomes of failed missions, particularly robberies. The user points out that in previous Grand Theft Auto titles, once a mission has been replayed a few times, players often know exactly how a failed attempt will unfold, typically culminating in a predictable shootout regardless of the initial plan's shortcomings.

The core of the discussion revolves around the desire for more dynamic and emergent gameplay when things go awry. The original poster illustrates this with a hypothetical scenario: a planned robbery where the back entrance is unexpectedly locked, and the intended escape route is simultaneously blocked by responding police. Instead of the situation inevitably devolving into a standard gunfight, the user expresses a strong preference for having to "find a way out on the fly," suggesting a desire for scenarios that demand improvisation and adaptive problem-solving from the player.

## Key Takeaways
- Older GTA games often feature predictable outcomes for failed missions, particularly robberies.
- Players desire more dynamic and unpredictable scenarios when mission plans go wrong in GTA 6.
- A hypothetical scenario includes unexpected obstacles like locked exits and blocked escape routes.
- The community hopes for emergent gameplay that encourages on-the-fly problem-solving over repetitive shootouts.
- The discussion emphasizes a shift from scripted failure states to more adaptive and player-driven consequences.
