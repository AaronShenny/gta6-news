# GTA 6: Community Hopes for Revolutionary Seamless World, Smarter NPCs, and Next-Gen Animations

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto fan has outlined their ambitious hopes for the most revolutionary aspects of GTA 6's gameplay. Central to their vision is the desire for a completely blurred line between main missions, side activities, and free-roam, aiming for an organic and seamless experience where all aspects offer consistent quality. This concept builds upon the foundation laid by Red Dead Redemption 2's random encounters, suggesting an even deeper integration for GTA 6.

Another significant area of anticipation revolves around NPC reactivity. Drawing from the advanced AI seen in RDR2, the fan expects an unprecedented level of sophistication and dynamic interaction from non-player characters in GTA 6. Finally, speculation extends to animations, with the fan recalling information about patents for highly dynamic and realistic animations. Given Rockstar Games' historical excellence in animation, this prospect generates considerable excitement within the community, with hopes that these advancements will be showcased soon.
