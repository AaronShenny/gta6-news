# Jason Schreier Reveals New GTA 6 Details: Decade-Long Dev, Massive Scale, and Intricate Details

Renowned gaming journalist Jason Schreier has unveiled fresh insights into the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6, shedding light on its immense scale and protracted development cycle. The revelations, which didn't make it into his original Bloomberg cover story, paint a picture of a game crafted with an almost obsessive level of detail and a truly colossal team.Key takeaways include:*   **Decade-Long Development**: Some artists have been dedicated to GTA 6 for an astounding 11 years, with map development specifically beginning in 2015. This commitment was so profound that some team members bypassed Red Dead Redemption 2's entire development to remain focused on GTA 6.*   **Evolved Weapon Mechanics**: Rob Nelson confirmed that GTA 6's weapon system was initially designed to mimic Red Dead Redemption 2's more realistic, limited arsenal. While developers explored numerous alternative UI interfaces for months, they ultimately decided to retain the classic weapon wheel.*   **Massive Art Production**: The art team for GTA 6 has expanded dramatically, boasting 65 artists compared to GTA 5's modest 5 or 6. This significant increase underscores the ambition for visual fidelity and environmental richness.*   **Unprecedented Scope**: According to Aaron Garbut, the sheer magnitude of GTA 6 is such that 'no one person at the studio has this whole thing in their head.' He suggests that even top developers don't grasp every single aspect of the game, highlighting its sprawling complexity.*   **Micro-Detail Philosophy**: Expect a wealth of intricate, player-delighting details akin to Red Dead Redemption 2's famous 'horse's balls shrinking in the cold.' These aren't top-down mandates but rather organic implementations by individual developers given the time and resources to integrate their creative ideas.

## Key Takeaways
- Some artists have been working on GTA 6 for up to 11 years, with map development starting in 2015.
- The weapon system initially aimed for a limited arsenal like RDR2 but ultimately kept the weapon wheel after extensive UI experimentation.
- GTA 6's art production team is significantly larger with 65 people, compared to GTA 5's 5-6 artists.
- The game's scale is so immense that no single person, including top developers, fully comprehends its entirety.
- Expect numerous small, immersive details, similar to RDR2, driven by individual developer creativity rather than top-down mandates.
