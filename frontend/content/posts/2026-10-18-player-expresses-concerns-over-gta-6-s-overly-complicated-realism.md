# Player Expresses Concerns Over GTA 6's "Overly Complicated" Realism

A concerned Reddit user, IDrxpezyclpsYT, has voiced apprehension regarding the direction of Grand Theft Auto 6, specifically its rumored embrace of hyper-realism. Drawing insights from various YouTube videos detailing known aspects of the upcoming title, the user argues that features such as an intricate reputation system that penalizes excessive violence, highly detailed vehicle tracking, and an "over-the-top" police recognition system could make the game "way too complicated." The sentiment is that these elements diverge significantly from the free-wheeling, chaotic gameplay synonymous with titles like GTA 5, where spontaneous car theft and combat were central. While acknowledging that GTA 6 "looks like a great game," the user expresses a fear that its perceived complexity might make it feel less like a traditional Rockstar game, leading to frustration, as evidenced by a later edit addressing critical Reddit comments.
