# Finger Out How To Prep For GTA 6 With These Exercises

As the highly anticipated launch of Grand Theft Auto VI approaches, fitness company PureGym, in collaboration with personal trainer David Hope, has unveiled a series of thumb exercises to help gamers prepare for extended play. Recognizing that thumbs and hands bear the brunt of the work during gaming, these warm-up drills are recommended before and after long sessions.

The exercises include:
*   **Thumb-to-finger tapping**: Tapping your thumb with each finger on the same hand.
*   **Fist-to-spread**: Spreading your hand into a 'five' shape, then forming a fist with your thumb on the outside.
*   **Finger pushups**: Pressing the fingertips of both hands on the floor and applying pressure.

PureGym advises performing each exercise 10 times. The article also humorously suggests an alternative warm-up: playing with a minifinger skateboard.
