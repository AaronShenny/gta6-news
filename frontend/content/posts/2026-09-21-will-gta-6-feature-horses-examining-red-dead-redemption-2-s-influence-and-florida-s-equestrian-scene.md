# Will GTA 6 Feature Horses? Examining Red Dead Redemption 2's Influence and Florida's Equestrian Scene

The discussion around Grand Theft Auto 6 frequently speculates on potential features, and a recent article delves into whether horses could make an appearance. The primary argument hinges on Rockstar Games' highly acclaimed horse mechanics in *Red Dead Redemption* (2010) and, more significantly, *Red Dead Redemption 2* (2018), which is widely considered to have the best horse mechanics in gaming to date. The article notes that early versions of *GTA V* did include scrapped horse elements, suggesting a past interest.

A key point is the observation that *GTA 6* appears to be built upon the same foundational mechanics as *RDR 2*. Evidence for this includes past findings of Rockstar testing vehicles and automatic weapons within *RDR 2*'s engine, as well as visible similarities in *GTA 6*'s 'Extended Look' gameplay, such as character interactions with NPCs and pets. This shared technological base implies that if Rockstar chooses to include horses, the technical complications that might have plagued *GTA V*'s early attempts could now be overcome.

Furthermore, the article argues that horses would not be out of place in *GTA 6*'s Southern Florida-inspired setting, citing the presence of horse farms and racing tracks in the region. If implemented with *RDR 2*-level quality, horses in *GTA 6* could set a new benchmark, potentially influencing future horse mechanics in other games for years to come. The central question remains: will Rockstar choose to leverage their established expertise and the thematic appropriateness to bring horses into the modern urban chaos of *Grand Theft Auto 6*?
