# GTA 6’s Landmass Is Nearly As Big As GTA 4, GTA 5, And Red Dead Redemption 2 Combined

Rockstar Games has revealed astonishing details about the sheer scale of the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6. According to statements made to Edge magazine, GTA 6's map is roughly twice the size of GTA 5's and three times the size of Red Dead Redemption 2's. Even more impressively, the total landmass of GTA 6 is nearly as vast as the combined playable areas of GTA 4, GTA 5, and Red Dead Redemption 2.

Beyond its immense size, Rockstar emphasizes that the map will also be incredibly dense. Co-studio head Aaron Garbut highlighted that the game will feature an "order of magnitude more interiors" than any previous Rockstar title. He noted that every back alley is meticulously detailed and engaging, to the point where even he, having worked on the game for years, has yet to personally explore every location.

Garbut also touched upon the immense pressure to deliver an exceptional experience after 13 years of anticipation. He asserted that Rockstar has not compromised on the game's scale, detail, interactivity, story, or open world, likening their accomplishment to "built a pyramid on Everest." GTA 6 is scheduled to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and its massive file size is a testament to its ambitious scope. Speculation also suggests that GTA 6 Online might see a release in early 2027.
