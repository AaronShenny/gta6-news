# GTA 6 Won’t Spoof Disney World, And That’s Too Bad

Grand Theft Auto 6, set in the fictional Vice City (a stand-in for Miami), will notably *not* include a satirical version of Walt Disney World, Universal Studios, SeaWorld, or the iconic 'Space Coast' featuring Cape Canaveral. This news comes directly from Rockstar's Jack McPherson, who, when asked by Game Informer, clarified that while these specific central Florida locations are excluded, the game aims to deliver a 'full Florida experience' through a carefully curated diversity of landscapes, towns, and landmarks.The article expresses disappointment from the author, who appreciates Rockstar's satirical style and sees ample material for parody in Disney World's increasing prices and the 'Disney Adult' phenomenon. However, the exclusion isn't entirely surprising given Vice City's Miami-based setting, which is a significant distance from Orlando. Despite GTA 6 boasting Rockstar's largest map ever, it's not expected to stretch as far north as Orlando.While a physical park spoof won't appear, the article speculates that references to Disney World could still emerge through in-game radio chatter or other subtle nods, similar to how GTA 5, set near Disneyland, included 'Fred's Pictures' as a clear Disney analog without a visitable theme park. The game is slated for release on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 will not feature a spoof of Walt Disney World or Universal Studios.
- SeaWorld and Florida's 'Space Coast' (Cape Canaveral) will also be absent.
- Rockstar aims for a 'full Florida experience' through diverse landscapes, rather than specific Orlando tourist spots.
- The game's setting in Vice City (Miami) explains the exclusion, as Orlando is a 4-hour drive away.
- The GTA 6 map is confirmed to be Rockstar's biggest ever, but not expansive enough to reach Orlando.
- The author expresses disappointment but notes potential for references or radio chatter spoofs.
- GTA 6 launches November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
