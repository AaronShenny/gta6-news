# GTA 6: Are NPCs Too Easy? A Journalist Explores Community Concerns Ahead of Release

A recent Reddit post from a dedicated GTA 6 fan has sparked discussion within the community, raising concerns about the perceived difficulty of Non-Player Characters (NPCs) in the highly anticipated title. While acknowledging the breathtaking visuals and immersive world showcased in trailers and the 'Extended Look' video, the author points out a significant 'nitpick': the NPCs appear remarkably easy to defeat.

Drawing parallels to critically acclaimed games like *The Last of Us Part II* and *Red Dead Redemption 2*, which offered varying and engaging difficulty levels, the author expresses a strong desire for a similar challenge in GTA 6. The post criticizes the 'bot-like' NPCs of GTA V, which often allowed players to clear missions with ease, and hopes Rockstar avoids this pitfall in their next title.

While understanding that the preview footage might be from curated builds and controlled environments, the author emphasizes the importance of a balanced difficulty. They appreciate announced features like limited weapon loadouts, 'cores' (resource management), and the promise of armed random NPCs, which are expected to contribute to an unprecedented open-world experience. The overarching hope is that Rockstar will fine-tune NPC AI to provide a challenging yet enjoyable gameplay experience, ensuring players like Jason and Lucia aren't simply 'gods walking amongst men' through Leonida.
