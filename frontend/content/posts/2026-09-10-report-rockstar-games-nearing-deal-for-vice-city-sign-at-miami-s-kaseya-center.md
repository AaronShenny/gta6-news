# Report: Rockstar Games Nearing Deal for 'Vice City' Sign at Miami's Kaseya Center

According to a report by journalist Francisco Alvarado (@thefrankness), Rockstar Games is reportedly in the final stages of a deal with the Miami HEAT's affiliate, Basketball Properties, and Miami-Dade County. The agreement would permit the installation of a massive 'Vice City' sign on the roof of the Kaseya Center, a prominent basketball arena in Miami. This development is being widely interpreted as a significant hint towards marketing preparations for Grand Theft Auto 6, which is heavily rumored to be set in a modern-day rendition of Vice City, Rockstar's fictionalized version of Miami. The report, initially shared on Reddit by /u/Visara57, points to 'amazing reporting and evidence' regarding these marketing efforts around the Kaseya Center, fueling further speculation among fans about the highly anticipated game.
