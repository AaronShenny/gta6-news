# GTA 6's Wanted Level System: Escape the Law with Dynamic 'Hit-and-Run' Tactics

Rockstar Games has confirmed that Grand Theft Auto 6 will introduce a significantly more realistic and dynamic wanted level system, moving beyond the simple red zone evasion of past titles. In an interview with IGN, head of development Rob Nelson detailed how players, controlling Jason and Lucia, will have numerous options to shake off the law, embracing a 'hit-and-run' style of gameplay. This means escaping a crime scene before police arrive or escalating the situation will be crucial. The game features deeply evolved NPC interactions, where actions like eavesdropping or following can organically lead to confrontations and trigger a wanted level. To evade the authorities, players can now change outfits, get haircuts, utilize personal vehicles with stored clothing, or even split up if playing as both characters. The new system is designed to support the narrative of a couple 'on the run' and aims to prevent players from feeling 'all-powerful' as was often the case in GTA 5. Interactive safehouses will also serve as personal spaces for character switching and customization. GTA 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with pre-orders already reportedly reaching high numbers.

## FAQ
**How will the wanted level system in GTA 6 differ from previous titles?**
GTA 6's wanted level system emphasizes 'hit-and-run' tactics, offering more dynamic ways to escape the law beyond simply leaving a red zone. Players can change their appearance, split up, or flee before authorities arrive, making evasion more realistic.

**What new ways can players lose their wanted level?**
Players can lose their wanted level by changing outfits, getting a haircut, using a personal vehicle with stored clothes, splitting up with a partner (Jason or Lucia), or effectively fleeing the scene before police presence escalates.

**Will NPC interactions impact the wanted level system?**
Yes, NPC interactions are significantly deeper and can organically lead to wanted levels. Eavesdropping, following, or general interactions can escalate into confrontations, drawing the attention of the authorities.

**Does GTA 6 aim for more realism compared to GTA 5?**
Rockstar has stated that GTA 6 aims for more realism, particularly by not making players feel 'all-powerful' as often happened in GTA 5. The wanted system and NPC interactions are designed to reflect this.

**When is GTA 6 releasing and on what platforms?**
GTA 6 is set to release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
