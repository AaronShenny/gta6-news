# Cliff Richard Unaware of GTA 6 Inclusion, Mistook It for a TV Show

Cliff Richard's classic song "Devil Woman" is confirmed to be part of the *GTA 6* soundtrack, appearing in the "Netflix Extended Look." Despite his music's inclusion, the 86-year-old artist was initially unaware of the partnership and even thought *GTA 6* was a "game show" or "one of the biggest on TV," not a video game. Richard learned about his song's feature from his nephew's sons, who were surprised by his singing. Released in 1976, "Devil Woman" is now 50 years old, and Richard expressed excitement about a new generation discovering his music. Following its appearance in the "Netflix Extended Look," Spotify streams for "Devil Woman" saw a significant surge. *GTA 6* is set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S as a digital exclusive, with a massive file size. The official score by Justice will be released on December 11.
