# Devolver Digital Challenges GTA 6 with Satirical 2D Platformer 'Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed'

In a bold, humorous move, Devolver Digital is launching **Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed** on November 19, directly positioning it against the highly anticipated release of Rockstar's *Grand Theft Auto 6*. Despite its marketing as a 'remaster of the second remake of the original monochrome action-platformer starring the most famous video game mascot of all time,' this is a meta-joke, as the original 'Volvy's Adventure' never existed. The game is, in fact, an entirely new and original Game Boy Color-style 2D platformer.

Developed by Doinksoft, known for *Gato Roboto* and *Gunbrella*, the game stars Volvy, Devolver Digital's rat-esque mascot. Players will guide Volvy through vibrant levels, collecting onions, bouncing on enemies, and battling bosses. Volvy can dash, jump, burrow, hover with his tail, and even transform into a hulked-out alter-ego named Swolvy to smash through obstacles. The kinetic movement and popping soundtrack are reminiscent of its retro inspirations.

While *GTA 6* is expected to dominate headlines, *Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed* offers a distinct alternative, priced at an accessible $5 on Steam, unlike *GTA 6*'s projected $80-$100 cost and its initial absence on PC. This counter-programming strategy from Devolver Digital caters to those looking for a different kind of gaming experience this November.
