# Rockstar Confirms No Hair & Beard Growth System in GTA 6

In a definitive statement, Rockstar Games has confirmed that the highly anticipated natural hair and beard growth system, a fan-favorite feature from Red Dead Redemption 2, will *not* be present in Grand Theft Auto 6. This confirmation comes directly from transcripts of conversations with Rob Nelson, a key figure at Rockstar, shared with both content creator Davy Jones and TGG.

According to the transcript sent from Rockstar to Davy Jones, when asked if character hair grows naturally, Rob Nelson explicitly stated: "No. We thought about it, but that goes back to realism versus customizability, and we just thought that, with the pace of this world, it's better to just let you come in and do whatever you want to do."

Further clarifying the decision, Nelson told TGG that while players can perform basic actions like cutting hair shorter or shaving in safe houses, "if you go the barber or hair salon you can access lots of different styles, it's not that your hair has to grow and you cut it like in RDR2, you can go to a shop and do whatever you want— again part of the difference between GTA immediacy vs. RDR2 slightly more realistic take on things."

This decision highlights Rockstar's commitment to GTA's signature immediate customization and fast-paced gameplay, differentiating it from the more deliberate, realistic approach seen in RDR2. While some fans might be disappointed by the absence of this immersive detail, the emphasis is placed on giving players instant control over their character's appearance.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 will not feature a natural hair and beard growth system.
- This was confirmed by Rockstar Games' Rob Nelson in official transcripts shared with Davy Jones and TGG.
- Rockstar prioritized immediate player customizability and the fast-paced nature of GTA over RDR2-style realism.
- Players will still have extensive customization options at barbers/salons and basic options in safe houses.
- The decision aims to provide instant control over character appearance rather than a growth mechanic.
