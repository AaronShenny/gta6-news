# GTA 6 Stars Celebrate Launch with Heartfelt Statement as Game Earns Perfect Scores

Rockstar's highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6* has officially launched, and its lead actors, Dylan Rourke (Jason) and Manni L. Perez (Lucia), have released a joint heartfelt statement to mark the occasion. They expressed immense honor in collaborating on what they called a "worldwide testament to human creativity, ingenuity, and sheer willpower," acknowledging the hard work of the game-making artisans and signing off with "See y’all in Leonida.

While Rockstar initially kept the actors' identities under wraps, fans had long speculated their involvement. With the game's release, Rockstar has now published the full credits, confirming Rourke and Perez as the main characters. The extensive cast also includes Brad Carter as Donnie, Bobby Moynihan as Cal, and Stephen Root as Brian, alongside notable celebrity cameos such as "Weird Al" Yankovic, Doug Stanhope, Stavros Halkias, and JB Smoove.

The critical reception for *GTA 6* has been overwhelmingly positive. GameSpot awarded the game a rare perfect 10/10, with reviewer Tamoor Hussain calling it an "astonishing open-world action game." Furthermore, *GTA 6* has unseated the *Ocarina of Time* remake for Switch 2 to become the highest-rated game of 2026 on Metacritic, tying with the original *Ocarina of Time* for the highest aggregate review score ever. Following the stellar reviews, shares of Rockstar owner Take-Two saw a significant surge, despite the company's overall stock performance being down over the past year.
