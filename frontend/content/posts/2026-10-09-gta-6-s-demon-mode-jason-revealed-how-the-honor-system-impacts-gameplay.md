# GTA 6's 'Demon Mode Jason' Revealed: How the Honor System Impacts Gameplay

A fascinating new gameplay mechanic in Grand Theft Auto VI, dubbed "Demon Mode Jason," has been revealed, shedding light on the game's intricate honor system. According to Rockstar, GTA VI will feature an honor system with a distinct "point of no return." Once a character, such as Jason, accrues enough negative honor, they can enter this "demon mode," subtly indicated by a 😈 emoji appearing on screen. This mode drastically alters the character's behavior, prompting aggressive actions from the outset in situations where they might otherwise perform non-violent alternatives. An observed instance from a scene shows Jason immediately resorting to pistol-whipping an individual, directly showcasing the impact of this system on character interactions and the overall world-building. This subtle yet significant detail highlights Rockstar's commitment to dynamic character development influenced by player choices and moral standing within the game world.

'Demon Mode Jason' refers to a state Jason can enter within GTA 6's honor system, where his behavior becomes immediately aggressive after reaching a 'point of no return' in negative honor.

When in 'Demon Mode,' characters like Jason will bypass non-violent actions and immediately resort to aggressive behavior, such as pistol-whipping, in relevant situations.
