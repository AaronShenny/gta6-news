# Unpopular Opinion: A Gamer Prefers 30 FPS for GTA 6's Cinematic Experience

In an 'unpopular opinion' post on Reddit, user u/Vergleich_org expresses a strong preference for 30 frames per second (FPS) in story-driven games like the upcoming *Grand Theft Auto 6*. The user argues that a lower frame rate, coupled with higher graphical fidelity, creates a more 'cinematic' and 'movie-like' experience, which they find more immersive. They cite examples such as *The Last of Us* games and the *Spider-Man* series, where they consistently chose fidelity modes over higher frame rate options. For u/Vergleich_org, the potential of GTA 6 running at 30 FPS is not a technical issue to be resolved but rather a desirable 'feature'. They indicate that even if a 60 FPS option becomes available in the future, they would still opt for 30 FPS with enhanced visual quality. The user clarifies that their preference for higher frame rates (60 or 120 FPS) is reserved primarily for online shooters or sports games, and they are open to understanding why other players prioritize 60 FPS.
