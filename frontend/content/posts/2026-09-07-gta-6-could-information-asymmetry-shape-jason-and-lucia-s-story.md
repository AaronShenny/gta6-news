# GTA 6: Could 'Information Asymmetry' Shape Jason and Lucia's Story?

A recent fan theory dives deep into the potential narrative structure of Grand Theft Auto VI, specifically focusing on how information could be distributed among its dual protagonists, Jason and Lucia, and the player. Inspired by a scene from the Extended Look where Brian offers a job for only one of them (Lucia volunteers), the theory posits a concept called 'information asymmetry'.

This idea suggests that one protagonist, like Lucia, might learn crucial information during a solo mission and choose to withhold it from Jason. In such a scenario, the player would be aware of both sides of the story, creating a unique tension where the player knows more than one of the characters they control. The same dynamic could apply in reverse, with Jason keeping information from Lucia.

The article highlights that this narrative technique could significantly amplify the game's central theme of 'Trust' between the protagonists, leading to dramatic consequences as hidden information eventually surfaces. While Rockstar hasn't extensively explored this specific narrative device in their main line games (Max Payne 3 is cited as a tangential example), its implementation in GTA 6 could offer a fresh and compelling storytelling experience.

## FAQ
**What is 'information asymmetry' in the context of GTA 6's story?**
It's a narrative concept where the player is privy to information that one protagonist (Jason or Lucia) might have, while the other protagonist remains unaware, creating dramatic tension and conflict.

**Which specific scene from the GTA 6 trailer inspired this theory?**
The theory was sparked by the rooftop bar scene shown in the Extended Look, where Brian offers a job that only one of them needs to do, and Lucia volunteers to take it alone.

**How could this concept impact the relationship between Jason and Lucia?**
By having one character withhold crucial information from the other, it could create significant tension and conflict, directly challenging and exploring the game's central theme of 'Trust' between the protagonists.
