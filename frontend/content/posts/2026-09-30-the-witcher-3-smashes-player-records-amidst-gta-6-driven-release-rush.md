# The Witcher 3 Smashes Player Records Amidst GTA 6-Driven Release Rush

More than a decade after its initial release, *The Witcher 3: The Wild Hunt* is experiencing an unprecedented resurgence on Steam, driven by the launch of its free Remastered Edition. The game recently achieved a new all-time peak of 120,975 concurrent players, significantly surpassing its previous record of 103,329 set in January 2020 following the Netflix series' debut. This impressive milestone is particularly notable as it occurs during a fiercely competitive period in the gaming calendar, with numerous high-profile titles releasing in an effort to avoid the highly anticipated November launch of *GTA 6*.

The Remastered Edition, a complimentary upgrade for existing owners, introduces substantial technical and graphical enhancements to the acclaimed 2015 RPG. With lifetime sales exceeding 65 million units globally as of May 2026, many long-time fans are clearly returning to experience the improvements. Beyond PC platforms, *The Witcher 3* is also available on PS5, Switch 2, and Xbox Series X|S, indicating the total player count is likely much higher.

Looking ahead, *Witcher* enthusiasts have much to anticipate. A new expansion for *The Witcher 3*, 'Songs of the Past', is slated for 2027, and *The Witcher 4* could arrive as early as 2028. Furthermore, CD Projekt Red is reportedly working on a multiplayer *Witcher* game (Project Sirius) and recently confirmed a partnership with Scopely for a new mobile title based on one of its franchises, potentially *The Witcher* or *Cyberpunk*.
