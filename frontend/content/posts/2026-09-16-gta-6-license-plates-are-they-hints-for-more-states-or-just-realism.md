# GTA 6 License Plates: Are They Hints for More States or Just Realism?

A recent discussion on Reddit by user u/Next_Association1229 suggests that fans might be overthinking the presence of various license plates in the Grand Theft Auto 6 trailers. While some interpret these different plates as hints towards the ability to travel to multiple states or cities within the game, the user offers an alternative perspective rooted in realism.

Drawing from their experience as a Florida resident, the user points out that seeing vehicles with out-of-state license plates is a completely normal and frequent occurrence due to the influx of tourists, 'snowbirds,' and people relocating or driving through. They argue that if Rockstar Games aims to make Leonida, the game's Florida-inspired setting, feel authentic, then incorporating cars with diverse state plates would be a logical step to enhance realism.

While acknowledging that these plates *could* potentially be subtle hints, the user emphasizes that their presence does not automatically confirm that those represented locations will be playable areas within the game.
