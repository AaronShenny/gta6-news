# How to Set Stunning GTA VI Art as Your PS5 Welcome Background

Gaming enthusiasts eager to personalize their PlayStation 5 experience with Grand Theft Auto VI artwork can follow a simple, ingenious method. The trick involves navigating to the GTA VI store page on the PS5, then locating and interacting with the 'Extended Look' section. Specifically, users need to click 'Extended Look' and then highlight 'View Game'. During the brief moment the artwork appears in full brightness before transitioning, a quick screenshot can capture the desired image. Once captured, the image can be found in the Media Gallery and subsequently set as the PS5's Welcome Hub Background, allowing fans to immerse themselves in the GTA VI aesthetic even before the game's release.
