# Dedicated Fan Spends 19 Hours on Intricate GTA VI Pumpkin Carving

Reddit user u/HeySethhh recently unveiled an extraordinary Grand Theft Auto VI themed pumpkin carving, a project that demanded an impressive 19 hours of meticulous work over two days. Despite the belated post-Halloween reveal, the artist explained that the delay was due to significantly underestimating the complexity and time required for the detailed design. This marks the third consecutive year the user has crafted a GTA-themed pumpkin, highlighting a deep-seated passion for both the art form and the Grand Theft Auto franchise. The intricate carving stands as a testament to their immense anticipation for the highly awaited GTA VI.
