# GTA 6 Fan Hopes for "Road Trip" Style Traversal on a Massive Map

A passionate Grand Theft Auto fan has voiced their hopes for GTA 6's traversal mechanics, advocating for a truly expansive map that evokes the feeling of a "huge road trip." The user, u/StopPresent277, shared their vision on the r/GTA6 subreddit, pushing back against the common criticism that large maps often lead to "too much empty space." Instead, they argue that a vast open world would significantly enhance realism, particularly for activities like flying planes out of airports. They draw a parallel to a specific mission in GTA V where Trevor travels from Blaine County to Los Santos, noting how that experience, despite the map's size, still felt like "travelling miles" and was "so exciting." The user expresses immense excitement at the prospect of a GTA 6 map potentially larger than Red Dead Redemption 2's, believing it could deliver the immersive, long-distance travel experience they desire.

## Key Takeaways
- A fan desires GTA 6's traversal to simulate a "huge road trip" experience.
- The user advocates for a massive map, dismissing concerns about "too much empty space."
- A large map is believed to enhance realism for activities like flying and make travel more interesting.
- A GTA V mission (Trevor's drive from Blaine County to Los Santos) is cited as an example of this desired feeling.
- The fan expresses excitement at the possibility of GTA 6's map being larger than Red Dead Redemption 2's.
