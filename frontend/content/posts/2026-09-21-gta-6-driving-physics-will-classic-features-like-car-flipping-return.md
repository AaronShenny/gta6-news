# GTA 6 Driving Physics: Will Classic Features Like Car Flipping Return?

The upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI is anticipated to feature an entirely new, highly immersive, and individualized driving system for each vehicle. A recent Reddit discussion on r/GTA6, initiated by user /u/mkake_, delves into the potential return of specific driving mechanics that have been staples in previous GTA titles. The primary concern revolves around the ability to 'flip cars back over' after they've landed on their roof or side, a feature present in games like GTA San Andreas and GTA V. While acknowledging its lack of realism, the community ponders whether its absence in GTA VI would lead to significant player frustration, forcing players to abandon their vehicles frequently. Additionally, the discussion extends to the 'in-air control' feature, which allowed players in GTA V to steer their vehicles while airborne to ensure proper landings. The community is eager to know if Rockstar will prioritize player convenience and familiar gameplay elements over strict realism in GTA 6's highly anticipated driving mechanics.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA VI is expected to feature an all-new, highly immersive, and individualized driving system.
- The community is discussing whether the ability to manually flip over a car (a non-realistic feature from past GTA games) will return in GTA VI.
- Concerns are raised that without the car-flipping feature, players might frequently lose their vehicles due to minor accidents.
- Another point of discussion is the return of mid-air vehicle control, which allowed steering while airborne in GTA V to aid landing.
