# GTA 6 Hype Inspires Weekly Fan Art Series: Goylianos Kicks Off Acrylic Countdown

The immense anticipation for Grand Theft Auto 6 has inspired a unique fan project, as Reddit user Goylianos has embarked on a weekly art series. Committing to create a new acrylic painting inspired by GTA 6 every week until the game's official release, Goylianos has already unveiled the first piece. The artist is actively engaging with the vibrant GTA community, inviting suggestions for specific screenshots or moments from trailers to immortalize on canvas for upcoming installments in the series. This creative endeavor provides a refreshing way for fans to channel their excitement while counting down to one of the most anticipated game launches.
