# GTA 6 Leaker: Why They're Likely Just Rationing Pre-Recorded Footage

A recent analysis from Reddit user u/Ratingface debunks the popular belief that the current Grand Theft Auto 6 leaker possesses live access to the game. The article posits that the leaker likely had a one-time, brief window of live access, evidenced by a clip where they shot their name onto a wall, suggesting a hijacked game tester's remote login. Rockstar Games security is believed to have swiftly revoked this access, making it impossible for the leaker to log back in.

The author argues that the leaker, anticipating the imminent shutdown of their access, recorded as much random sandbox footage as possible. The current slow drip-feed of short, 30-second clips, coupled with paid polls and countdown timers, is presented as proof that the leaker is merely stretching a fixed, limited collection of video files. This strategy is reportedly aimed at prolonging hype and promoting a cryptocurrency, with the expectation that the leaks will cease once the recorded footage runs out.

## Key Takeaways
- The GTA 6 leaker likely had live access only once, for a very short period.

- The leaker is believed to be milking hype and promoting a crypto coin with their finite collection of footage.
