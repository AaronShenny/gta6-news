# GTA 6: Jason and Lucia's Relationship Is Player-Driven, Rockstar Confirms

Rockstar North has officially confirmed that the dynamic between *Grand Theft Auto 6*'s protagonists, Jason and Lucia, will be largely shaped by player choices, moving away from a predetermined romantic narrative. Rob Nelson from Rockstar North revealed to The New York Times that players will have the agency to define whether the duo completes their journey as a loving couple or strictly as partners in crime. This innovative approach means that player actions will have tangible consequences on the relationship. Nelson highlighted that managing their bond will involve responding to in-game texts and making conscious efforts, with even the level of violence impacting their connection. Subtle visual cues, such as Jason and Lucia holding hands after leaving a store, will dynamically reflect the current state of their relationship. Nelson emphasized that the game's narrative draws significant inspiration from the iconic 'Bonnie and Clyde' concept, stating it was the 'only way' for this installment. This aligns with Rockstar's previous statements that the story delves into 'whether love can survive through this storm,' a theme powerfully introduced with Tom Petty's 'Love Is A Long Road' in the first trailer. Beyond the relationship mechanics, the article also reiterated *Grand Theft Auto 6*'s launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, alongside other confirmed details like a map three times larger than *Red Dead Redemption 2*'s, an approximate 80-hour playthrough, the return of six-star wanted levels, and the ability to pet dogs.
