# GTA 6: Fans Demand Major Improvements Over GTA 5, Citing Story and Character Flaws

A recent Reddit thread on r/GTA6 sparked a lively discussion among fans regarding crucial improvements needed for Grand Theft Auto 6, building on the foundation of GTA 5. The original poster specifically called out several aspects that fell short in GTA 5, hoping for a significant upgrade in the upcoming sequel. 

One of the most prominent criticisms was directed at GTA 5's **antagonists**, described as 'disappointing and very forgettable,' with characters like Wei Cheng and Stretch cited as examples. The hope is for more compelling villains in GTA 6, especially for the potential main antagonist. 

Character writing also came under scrutiny, with fans wishing for **protagonists like Jason and Lucia** to be as well-developed as Arthur Morgan (RDR2), Niko Bellic (GTA 4), and CJ (GTA San Andreas). Franklin, in particular, was singled out as 'pretty boring,' especially in interactions with the otherwise entertaining Lamar. 

Beyond narrative elements, players expressed a desire for enhanced **world interactivity**, specifically the ability to enter fast-food restaurants like Clucking Bell, a feature surprisingly absent in GTA 5. The **mission count** in GTA 5 was also deemed too short, with 69 missions feeling insufficient to some players. 

Finally, the overarching **story of GTA 5** was criticized for feeling 'all over the place,' with fans yearning for a more cohesive and well-written narrative akin to GTA San Andreas, GTA 4, or the 'peak story mode' of Red Dead Redemption 2. The community is eager for Rockstar to 'lock in' with GTA 6's story, delivering a truly memorable experience.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 5's antagonists were forgettable and need significant improvement in GTA 6.
- Protagonist writing for Jason and Lucia should aim for the depth of characters like Arthur Morgan, Niko Bellic, and CJ.
- The open world needs more interactive elements, such as accessible fast-food restaurants.
- GTA 6 should feature a higher mission count than GTA 5's 69 missions.
- The overall story of GTA 6 needs to be more cohesive and better written, drawing inspiration from GTA 4, San Andreas, and RDR2.

## FAQ
