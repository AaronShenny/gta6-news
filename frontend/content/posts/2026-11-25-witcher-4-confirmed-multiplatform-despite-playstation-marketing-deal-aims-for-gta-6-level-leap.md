# Witcher 4 Confirmed Multiplatform Despite PlayStation Marketing Deal, Aims for GTA 6-Level Leap

CD Projekt Red has officially confirmed that **The Witcher 4** will be a multiplatform title, despite recently announcing a marketing deal with Sony Interactive Entertainment for the game. Michał Nowakowski of CD Projekt Red explicitly stated that "The Witcher 4 and our other upcoming titles will remain multiplatform," dispelling any rumors of PlayStation exclusivity. This marketing arrangement is noted to be similar to the one between Rockstar and Sony for **GTA 6**, where Rockstar's highly anticipated title received preferential treatment on PS5 during its marketing campaign.Development on The Witcher 4 is progressing with over 500 people currently working on the project, and a release is anticipated in 2028. Given Sony's projected shift away from supporting game discs by January 2028, The Witcher 4 is unlikely to see a physical disc release. However, CD Projekt Red has teased that fans can still expect some form of physical 'goodies' or 'feelies.'In a broader comparison, Nowakowski also addressed The Witcher 4's ambition in relation to **GTA 6**, stating, "We believe there’s going to be a comparable leap between The Witcher 3 and The Witcher 4." He emphasized the goal to make Witcher 4 "significantly better looking and improved in terms of pretty much every possible facet of the game" after 11 years since the previous installment, aiming for a "much bigger jump" in quality.Beyond The Witcher 4, CD Projekt Red is also working on several other projects, including a sequel to Cyberpunk (dubbed Cyberpunk 2), a new intellectual property codenamed Hadar, a rumored multiplayer Witcher title, and a remake of the original Witcher game. Additionally, a new mobile game is in development with Scopely.
