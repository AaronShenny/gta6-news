# The End of an Era: GTA 6 Memes Face Extinction as Release Nears

The gaming community is bracing for the bittersweet end of a beloved, long-running meme: 'We got _ before GTA 6'. For years, this humorous phrase has encapsulated the seemingly endless wait for the next installment in Rockstar Games' iconic Grand Theft Auto series. From new consoles and major world events to personal milestones, gamers have playfully noted countless occurrences that have taken place before the arrival of GTA 6. However, with official confirmations and trailers pointing to an imminent release, the meme's relevance is rapidly fading. This Reddit post from /u/CaraMiaChell on r/GTA6 captures the sentiment perfectly, highlighting the mixed emotions among fans – excitement for the game, yet a touch of nostalgia for the era of anticipation and the unique humor it spawned.

## FAQ

It's a popular internet meme used by gamers to humorously point out how long they've been waiting for Grand Theft Auto 6 by listing various events, products, or personal milestones that have occurred before the game's release.

**Why is the meme ending?**
The meme is ending because Grand Theft Auto 6's release is now officially confirmed to be on the horizon, with trailers and announcements signaling its impending arrival, making the 'long wait' premise obsolete.

**How do fans feel about the meme ending?**
Fans have mixed emotions; while excited for the game's release, there's also a sense of nostalgia and a touch of sadness about the retirement of a meme that became a significant part of the waiting experience and gaming culture.
