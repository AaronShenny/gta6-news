# Virgin Media Offers Irish Gamers €800 To Play GTA 6

Virgin Media has launched an exciting promotional campaign exclusively for residents of Ireland, offering one lucky individual the chance to get paid to play **Grand Theft Auto 6**. The chosen winner will receive **€800** (approximately $891 USD) for playing the highly anticipated game for eight hours, at a rate of €100 per hour. Virgin Media will also provide a copy of the **GTA 6 Ultimate Edition**.

To enter, applicants must submit a 100-200 word explanation on the contest site detailing why they deserve to be paid to play GTA 6. The winner's responsibilities include playing the game for eight hours (which can be split across a week) and posting a review on either Instagram or TikTok, tagging Virgin Media and clearly disclosing it as a paid advertisement.

Applications are open until **November 15**, with the winner being selected randomly and contacted within seven days of the closing date. Potential participants should note that a **PlayStation 5** or **Xbox Series X|S** console is required to play the game. GTA 6 is set to release on November 19 for these platforms.
