# GTA 6: Will Rockstar's Next Magnum Opus Shatter All Expectations?

A fervent post on Reddit's r/GTA6 community suggests that Grand Theft Auto 6 is poised to shatter all current expectations, arguing that the public is simply 'not prepared for what's to come'. The user, u/EternaIAtake1600, posits that despite the immense hype surrounding the title, its true scope and quality will be far greater than anticipated.

The core argument hinges on several points: Rockstar Games' unparalleled track record, the game's colossal development budget, and the exclusive design for current-generation consoles. The author highlights GTA 5's longevity and quality, despite being developed for PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 hardware from 2005. They suggest that if Rockstar could achieve that with older tech, their capabilities with modern hardware for GTA 6 are incomprehensible.

Further bolstering this claim, the post references Red Dead Redemption 2's enduring visual fidelity, released in 2018, as a benchmark for Rockstar's prowess. Crucially, the author points to an estimated development budget of $1.5 billion for GTA 6, which dwarfs RDR2's $350 million, implying an unprecedented scale and ambition.

The article also touches upon recent reports of an executive meeting regarding past leaks. Allegedly, executives stated that the public has not yet seen the game properly and that the eventual reveal will elicit 'great surprise'. It's also noted that leaked footage, reportedly from around 2024, is already 'mind-blowing', despite representing an earlier build of the game, with two more years of development having passed since then.
