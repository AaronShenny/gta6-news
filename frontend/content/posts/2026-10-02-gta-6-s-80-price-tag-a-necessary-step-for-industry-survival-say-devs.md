# GTA 6's $80 Price Tag: A Necessary Step for Industry Survival, Say Devs

Rockstar's highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6* is set to launch with a standard edition price of $80, a move that some industry veterans hope will inspire other developers to follow suit. Martin Klima, co-founder of Warhorse Studios (known for *Kingdom Come: Deliverance*), expressed to PC Gamer that increasing unit prices would "help a lot" and is "long overdue and also necessary for this business to survive." He noted that only major players like Rockstar and Take-Two can "afford, or who can dare" to initiate such price hikes, as others are "scared to death" of losing market share.

This isn't Take-Two's first foray into leading price increases; they were instrumental in shifting the industry standard from $60 to $70 with *NBA 2K21* in 2020, citing a 300% increase in production costs. Former PlayStation executive Shawn Layden echoed these sentiments in 2025, suggesting that game prices should have incrementally risen with each console generation over the past two decades. He attributed the stagnation to a collective fear of being the first to raise prices, which ultimately erodes operating income and profit margins. To compensate for stagnant prices and escalating development costs, the industry has increasingly relied on microtransactions, DLC, and battle passes, trends that are likely to continue even with higher base prices.

Prior to GTA 6's price reveal, a Bank of America analyst had already advocated for an $80 price point to encourage industry-wide adoption. While $80 games are not entirely new (Nintendo has priced *Mario Kart World* and the physical edition of *Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave* at this level, justifying it with ambition), the article notes that pricing can vary, with Take-Two's *Mafia: The Old Country* successfully launching at $50. Despite the $80 standard price, GTA 6 will also offer a $100 Ultimate Edition and a $400 collector's edition (which notably does not include the game). Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick previously stated that Rockstar could have charged even more for GTA 6 given its monumental demand, opting not to. Beyond initial sales, the inevitable new iteration of *GTA Online* is expected to be a significant revenue generator for Rockstar.
