# GTA 6 Image Debunked: AI-Filtered Convenience Store Used in MW4 Comparison

A recent discussion on the r/GTA6 subreddit has shed light on a misleading comparison circulating online, which pitted a purported Grand Theft Auto VI convenience store against one from Modern Warfare 4. User SickPleasuresM8 meticulously pointed out that the GTA 6 image in question was not authentic, but rather an 'AI slop' filtered version. This AI manipulation was evident through distorted text on various in-game elements, including garbled text on chip packaging, the word 'withdrawals' on an ATM screen, illegible beer banners hanging from the ceiling, and a mangled 'comida caliente' sign above a staff door. The author emphasized that their motive was purely to correct the record and counter the use of AI-generated content to propagate false narratives, rather than to participate in the comparison itself. This clarification is vital for the GTA community, ensuring that discussions and expectations for the highly anticipated title are grounded in genuine information rather than artificial representations.
