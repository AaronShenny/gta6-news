# Nintendo Deflects GTA 6 Switch 2 Inquiry, Points to Rockstar

Speculation is mounting regarding the potential release of **Grand Theft Auto 6** on Nintendo's anticipated Switch 2 console. During a recent interview, Nintendo of America's new president, Devon Pritchard, addressed the question directly. Rather than confirming or denying, Pritchard stated that any decision would be a 'conversation for Take-Two and Rockstar,' adding that she had 'nothing to announce at this time.'This non-committal response follows a May 2025 report from industry insider NateTheHate, which suggested Rockstar was conducting technical tests for GTA 6 on the Switch 2. However, the report also cautioned that such tests are standard practice for developers exploring platform feasibility and do not indicate a definitive release plan.

Currently, GTA 6 is officially confirmed only for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a launch date set for November 19. A PC version is widely anticipated to follow later, though it remains unconfirmed. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has expressed optimism about the Switch 2, calling himself a 'big believer' in the platform. While he noted opportunities for bringing older titles to the console, he also clarified that Take-Two 'would not necessarily bring every title' to the Switch 2, leaving the door open but not specifically for GTA 6. Historically, Rockstar has supported Nintendo platforms, releasing titles like *GTA: The Trilogy - The Definitive Edition* on the original Switch and *GTA: Chinatown Wars* on the DS, demonstrating a precedent for the franchise on Nintendo hardware.
