# GTA 6 Leak Highlights Digital-Exclusive Irony at 'Game Horde' and Sparks Preservation Debate

A new alleged Grand Theft Auto 6 leak has surfaced, featuring one of the game's main characters, Jason, inside a video game store named 'Game Horde' that sells physical discs. This scene has drawn attention to the irony of GTA 6 being a digital-exclusive title, despite its 'physical' editions only containing a download code. A watermark on the leaked video provocatively states, 'This is not about actual discs. It's about ownership. Resale rights still matter, even in a digital-only era,' fueling ongoing discussions about game preservation and consumer rights in a progressively digital market. While digital game sales continue to rise, a significant portion of the gaming community still values physical media and expresses concerns about losing access to games in a digital-only future. Previous 'commandments' from a leaker advocated against digital preorders and for the preservation of single-player content. GTA 6 is slated for launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Despite extensive gameplay leaks, a former Rockstar director suggests they are not as detrimental as some believe. The perpetrators behind the leaks, including the individual responsible for the major 2022 breach, Arion Kurtaj (who is now awaiting a new trial), have been subject to legal action. Gamers can look forward to official footage from Rockstar releasing on Netflix on August 27.
