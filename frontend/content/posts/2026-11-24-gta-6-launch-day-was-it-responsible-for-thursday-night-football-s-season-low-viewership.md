# GTA 6 Launch Day: Was it Responsible for Thursday Night Football's Season-Low Viewership?

On November 19, the highly anticipated launch day of *Grand Theft Auto 6*, Thursday Night Football recorded its lowest viewership of the season. The matchup between the Indianapolis Colts and Houston Texans on Prime Video garnered an average of 12.23 million viewers, marking a 9% decline from the Week 11 TNF game in 2025 and significantly less than the 16.27 million viewers for the Week 10 game this season. This dip caused the full-season average for TNF to fall to 16.62 million per game. While the timing immediately raised questions about *GTA 6*'s impact, experts suggest that live sports, which thrive on real-time viewing, are unlikely to be heavily affected by game releases. A more probable explanation for the low ratings is the game itself: a lopsided 26-3 victory by the Texans over the Colts, described as a 'boring game' with little suspense after the first half. Interestingly, other media, such as *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, released the following day, managed to exceed box office expectations despite the *GTA 6* phenomenon. Meanwhile, *GTA 6* itself achieved colossal success, selling 32.5 million copies via pre-purchases and generating nearly $4 billion on its first day. The game is expected to continue its strong sales, with a rumored PC release in 2027 set to provide another boost.

## Key Takeaways
- Thursday Night Football on GTA 6 launch day (Nov 19) had the season's lowest viewership (12.23 million).
- This viewership was 9% lower than the comparable 2025 TNF game and significantly less than the prior week's TNF.
- Experts believe a boring, one-sided game (Texans 26, Colts 3) was a more significant factor than GTA 6 competition for the low viewership.
- Live sports are generally less impacted by game releases due to their real-time viewing nature.
- GTA 6 achieved massive sales, with 32.5 million pre-purchases and nearly $4 billion generated on day one.
- Other major releases, like 'The Hunger Games' film, still performed well despite GTA 6's launch.
- A PC edition of GTA 6 is rumored to arrive in 2027.
