# Digital Car Addict Highlights Realistic Daytime Running Lights in GTA 6 Vehicles

In a recent video, automotive gaming enthusiast 'Digital Car Addict' brought attention to a notable visual enhancement in the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6: the inclusion of detailed Daytime Running Lights (DRLs) on modern vehicles. This feature, which was largely absent or barely noticeable in GTA 5, is set to add a new layer of realism and visual fidelity to the game's vehicle fleet. As a specific example, Digital Car Addict highlighted that the in-game Vapid Caracara, a vehicle that mirrors the real-world Ford Raptor, will sport amber DRLs, further emphasizing Rockstar Games' commitment to intricate details and immersive gameplay.
