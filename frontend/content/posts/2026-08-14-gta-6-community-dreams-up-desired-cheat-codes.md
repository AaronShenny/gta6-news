# GTA 6 Community Dreams Up Desired Cheat Codes

A Reddit thread on r/GTA6 has ignited a vibrant discussion among fans, speculating on which beloved cheat codes from previous Grand Theft Auto titles they hope to see make a return in the upcoming GTA 6. The conversation highlights the nostalgic value of cheats as a core part of the single-player sandbox experience. Users specifically mentioned a longing for classic cheats such as "mad NPCs" from Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas and the "SKYFALL" cheat from Grand Theft Auto V. The possibility of flying cars, a perennial fan favorite, was also brought up, though some users acknowledged its potential unlikelihood. Another key point of discussion revolved around the return of player ragdolling, a feature present in GTA V's single-player but notably absent from GTA Online, with many expressing their enjoyment of the mechanic for humorous, physics-defying stunts.
