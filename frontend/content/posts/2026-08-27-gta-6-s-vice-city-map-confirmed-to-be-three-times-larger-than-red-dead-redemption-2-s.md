# GTA 6's Vice City Map Confirmed to Be Three Times Larger Than Red Dead Redemption 2's

Rockstar Games has dropped significant new details regarding the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6*, confirming that its expansive map will be an astonishing three times larger than that of *Red Dead Redemption 2*. This monumental scale was revealed in a New York Times report, which included interviews with Rockstar developers. Players will revisit the iconic Vice City, a vibrant stand-in for Miami, alongside its extensive surrounding regions.

Beyond the sheer size, the level of environmental detail in *GTA 6* appears unprecedented. The game boasts "well over 600,000 animations," a staggering increase compared to *GTA 5*'s 55,000 and even *Red Dead Redemption 2*'s 300,000. Aaron Garbut, boss of Rockstar North, highlighted the meticulous design, stating that "every room in the game, somebody thought about this," detailing everything from drink marks on tables to personalized pictures on walls.

Further confirmed details for *GTA 6* include an estimated 80-hour main story playthrough, the return of the fan-favorite six-star wanted level system, and the charming ability to pet in-game dogs. The game is set to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, promising an immersive and vast open-world experience.
