# GTA 6 Story Focuses on Love's Survival Amidst Crime, Rockstar Confirms

Rockstar North has shed more light on the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI's narrative, confirming a strong focus on the relationship between its two main characters, Jason and Lucia. Rob Nelson, from Rockstar North, explained to the New York Times that the story aims to reflect a 'crazy, warped version' of an American state and city, while exploring profound themes. The core question posed by the narrative is 'whether love can survive through this storm' of a criminal lifestyle.

The gameplay will allow players to seamlessly switch between Jason and Lucia. Nelson indicated that the nature of their relationship—whether they become lovers or remain strictly partners in crime—will be influenced by player choices. This introduces a new layer of depth to the GTA series, where player actions, including how they treat their partner and their level of violence, will have meaningful consequences on the relationship's progression. Maintaining their bond will involve in-game activities such as hanging out and responding to texts. The story is also set to delve into themes of hopefulness, sacrifice versus selfishness, adding emotional weight to the criminal underworld.
