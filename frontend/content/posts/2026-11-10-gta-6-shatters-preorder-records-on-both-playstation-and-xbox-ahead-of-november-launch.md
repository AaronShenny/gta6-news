# GTA 6 Shatters Preorder Records on Both PlayStation and Xbox Ahead of November Launch

Grand Theft Auto 6 is poised to be a monumental release, already setting unprecedented records well before its official launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Sony has declared GTA 6 the most preordered game in PlayStation Store history, a record echoed by Microsoft, which confirmed it's also the most preordered game in Microsoft Store history for Xbox. Beyond platform-specific records, Circana reported that nearly one in three US console and PC gamers intend to purchase the game, setting a new purchase intent record. According to Sensor Tower, GTA 6 has already sold approximately 10 million copies, generating close to $1 billion in revenue, with projections suggesting sales could reach 25 million units and $2 billion before launch. The game is expected to surpass GTA 5's impressive launch day and three-day revenue records. Xbox CEO Asha Sharma also hinted at a new 10-year strategic partnership with Take-Two, though specifics remain undisclosed. While GTA 5 holds a staggering 235 million units sold to date, GTA 6 is launching into a different, more expensive console market. A PC version is anticipated as early as 2027, along with a new GTA Online experience, both expected to significantly boost future sales.
