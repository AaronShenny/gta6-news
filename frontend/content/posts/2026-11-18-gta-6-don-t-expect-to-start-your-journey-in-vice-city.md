# GTA 6: Don't Expect to Start Your Journey in Vice City

Contrary to some expectations, players diving into Grand Theft Auto 6 will not immediately find themselves cruising the familiar streets of Vice City. Instead, the opening hours of the highly anticipated title will unfold in Key Lento, a more secluded corner of the sprawling state of Leonida.

This design decision is a deliberate one, intended to align with the narrative progression of co-stars Lucia and Jason. Reviewers have noted that it could take a significant amount of playtime—potentially 15 to 20 hours, depending on individual playstyle—before players finally set foot in Vice City, which is described as the 'crown jewel' of the game's expansive world.

While Key Lento may not boast the immediate glamour of Vice City, it is presented as an equally detailed environment. It serves as an essential introductory area, allowing players to thoroughly grasp the game's mechanics and intricate simulation before transitioning to the truly massive open world that awaits. The glitz and glamor of Vice City are indeed part of the GTA 6 experience, but players are encouraged to embrace the initial journey through Key Lento to fully appreciate the game's ambitious scope.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 does not start in Vice City, despite sharing a setting with the classic game.
- Players will begin their journey in Key Lento, a smaller region within the state of Leonida.
- It could take anywhere from 15 to 20 hours of gameplay to reach Vice City.
- This design choice is deliberate, aligning with the narrative progression of protagonists Lucia and Jason.
- Key Lento is no less detailed than other areas and serves as an important introduction to the game's mechanics and simulation.
