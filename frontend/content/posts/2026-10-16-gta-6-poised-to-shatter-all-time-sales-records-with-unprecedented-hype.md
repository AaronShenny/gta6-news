# GTA 6 Poised to Shatter All-Time Sales Records with Unprecedented Hype

Launching November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, Grand Theft Auto 6 is widely anticipated to be a colossal commercial success, poised to break numerous industry sales records. The article highlights how GTA 6 is expected to surpass the monumental launch figures of its predecessor, GTA 5 ($800 million in 24 hours, $1 billion in three days), as well as Red Dead Redemption II ($725 million opening weekend) and Fallout 4 ($750 million on launch day). A contributing factor to its potential record-breaking revenue is its higher base price of $80, compared to the $60 launch price of previous Rockstar titles.

Industry research firm Sensor Tower projects an astounding 25 million preorders by launch, which could translate to approximately $2 billion in revenue, effectively covering the game's development and most marketing costs. As of October 13, Sensor Tower estimated 6.08 million preorders, generating $594 million, with a significant 74% skew towards PlayStation 5.

Despite the immense anticipation, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has chosen not to disclose official preorder numbers, citing concerns that raw figures could be misleading to investors as preorders can be canceled. However, Zelnick did state that preorders have "vastly" surpassed international expectations and that "No one’s ever seen anything like this before at Take-Two, or in the industry." He emphasized the company's cautious approach, adhering to his mantra of not declaring success prematurely.

The article also looks beyond the initial launch, noting the strong likelihood of an "evolution" of GTA Online for GTA 6, which is expected to create another significant revenue stream through microtransactions and subscriptions. Furthermore, an eventual PC release is anticipated to drive a second major sales cycle. Investment firm Jeffries is bullish on Take-Two stock, predicting a substantial jump, fueled by GTA 6's multi-faceted revenue potential.
