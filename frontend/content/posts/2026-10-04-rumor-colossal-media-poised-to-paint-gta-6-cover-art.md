# Rumor: Colossal Media Poised to Paint GTA 6 Cover Art

A recent Reddit post has ignited excitement within the Grand Theft Auto community, suggesting that **Colossal Media** is set to return as the artist for the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6* cover.
