# GTA 6's In-Game Console War: Xbox Loses to PlayStation's "JoySystem"

Grand Theft Auto 6 continues the series' tradition of sharp satire, turning its gaze on the gaming world itself. The article highlights an early game interaction where protagonist Jason and his friend Cal play on a "Booby's JoyStation" console, a thinly veiled parody of the PlayStation 5, complete with a user interface that's a near 1:1 copy of the PS5 UI. Notably, there's no in-game equivalent to an Xbox console, humorously dubbed "Xbox Egg-Cube Stations X" in the article, suggesting PlayStation's in-game dominance.

The "JoySystem" isn't merely decorative; players can return to Jason's bachelor pad to play a selection of impressive in-game titles, ranging from avant-garde experiences to shooter games, showcasing Rockstar's attention to detail. However, the article points out the comically terrible design of the JoySystem's controllers.

Beyond the in-game satire, the article draws a parallel to real-world sales figures. Video game research company Sensor Tower estimates that GTA 6 has sold approximately 24.58 million units, generating $2.36 billion in revenue. Of these sales, a staggering 18.44 million units are reportedly for the PS5, giving Sony a significant 30% cut. This real-world dominance by PlayStation mirrors the fictional console landscape depicted within GTA 6, where the "console wars" are seemingly already over, with Xbox conspicuously absent.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 features an in-game console called "Booby's JoyStation" or "JoySystem," a satirical take on the PlayStation 5.
- The game includes no equivalent to an Xbox console, humorously implying PlayStation's in-game victory in the console wars.
- Players can interact with the JoySystem and play three distinct, well-developed in-game video games.
- The JoySystem's user interface is a direct parody of the PS5's UI, though its controllers are depicted as poorly designed.
- Real-world sales data for GTA 6 shows a significant lead for PS5, with an estimated 18.44 million units sold on the platform out of 24.58 million total.
- Sony is projected to earn a substantial 30% cut from the PS5 sales of GTA 6.
