# Sony Is Updating The PS5 Again, And It Might Have GTA 6 Pirates In Mind

Sony has rolled out a new, mandatory PlayStation 5 firmware update, version 26.07-14.23.00, a substantial 1.2GB download. The official notes are brief, citing only "security fixes." This marks the fourth such update since October 1, an unusually rapid release schedule that suggests a focused effort by Sony. While not officially confirmed, industry speculation points to Sony strengthening its console defenses against potential piracy of the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6.

The update is crucial as it will be mandatory for all upcoming game releases, including GTA 6. This presents a significant challenge for the PS5 hacking community, which recently achieved breakthroughs in jailbreaking systems running firmware versions 14 and below, enabling the play of pirated games. Given that the PS5 currently accounts for the lion's share of GTA 6 preorders, Sony has a substantial financial incentive to secure the platform against illicit copying.

The article highlights an ongoing "arms race" between Sony and hackers, with researchers increasingly using generative AI and automated decompilation to quickly identify system vulnerabilities. Sony's strategy involves rapidly deploying mandatory patches whenever potential exploits, such as those recently targeting firmware 13.60 and 14.22, emerge. Beyond security, the article also notes the continued rollout of Quick Spectral Super Resolution (QSSR), an AI-based upscaling technology derived from the PS5 Pro's PSSR, to more base PS5 consoles and games.
