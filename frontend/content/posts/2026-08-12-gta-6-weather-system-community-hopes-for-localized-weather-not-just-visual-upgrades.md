# GTA 6 Weather System: Community Hopes for Localized Weather, Not Just Visual Upgrades

A recent Reddit post in the r/GTA6 community has ignited a discussion regarding the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6's weather system. The core sentiment expressed by user /u/WeGotThisBeforeGTA7 is a desire for a more dynamic and realistic weather experience than simply enhanced volumetric clouds and improved sound effects. The suggestion advocates for a revolutionary localized weather system where different areas of the expansive map could experience varying weather conditions concurrently – for example, rain in one district while another enjoys bright sunshine. This approach would significantly enhance immersion and environmental realism, moving beyond static, global weather patterns and offering a truly next-gen open-world experience.

## Key Takeaways
- The community desires more than just improved volumetric clouds and sound effects for GTA 6's weather system.
- A key suggestion is for a localized weather system where different map areas can have different weather simultaneously.
- This would allow for scenarios like rain in one part of the city while another part is sunny.
- The goal is to significantly enhance immersion and environmental realism in the game.

The main suggestion is for a localized weather system where different parts of the map can experience varying weather conditions (e.g., rain and sun) at the same time, rather than uniform weather across the entire map.
