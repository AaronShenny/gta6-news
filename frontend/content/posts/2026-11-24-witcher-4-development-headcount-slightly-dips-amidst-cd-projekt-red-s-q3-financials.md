# Witcher 4 Development Headcount Slightly Dips Amidst CD Projekt Red's Q3 Financials

CD Projekt Red has released its latest earnings report, offering a detailed look into the development progress and financial health of the renowned Polish studio. The report, covering the quarter ending October 31, highlights shifts in team sizes across its major projects.

**Key Project Headcounts:**
*   **The Witcher 4 (Project Polaris):** Remains CD Projekt Red's largest project by headcount, with 513 developers. This represents a slight decrease from 519 developers reported three months prior.
*   **Cyberpunk 2 (Project Orion):** Saw an increase in its development team, growing from 184 to 199 staffers.
*   **New IP (Project Hadar):** The team working on the mysterious new intellectual property expanded from 33 to 34 individuals.
*   **Project Sirius (Multiplayer Witcher):** Experienced a more significant reduction, with its team shrinking from 84 to 60. This project has reportedly undergone several reworks.

Overall, CD Projekt Red's total development headcount slightly increased from 1,045 to 1,051.

**Q3 Financial Performance:**
The company reported robust Q3 revenue of 208 million PLN ($51 million USD) and net earnings of 109 million PLN ($26.8 million). Despite substantial development costs exceeding 177 million PLN ($43.5 million) during the quarter, CD Projekt Red successfully grew its total cash reserves by 31 million PLN ($7.6 million USD).

**Upcoming Releases & Collaborations:**
CD Projekt Red continues to work on several highly anticipated titles, including The Witcher 3: Songs of the Past (slated for 2027) and The Witcher 4 (expected in 2028). Cyberpunk 2 is also in development, though a release window has not yet been announced. Furthermore, the studio is collaborating with mobile gaming giant Scopely on a new game.

In a recent lighthearted moment, Cyberpunk 2's creative director humorously suggested the game might be canceled given the perceived high bar set by GTA 6.
