# Fan Unveils Impressive GTA 6 Trading Card Concept Project

Reddit user u/Immediate-Elk-3652 has shared an exciting fan project: a concept for Grand Theft Auto VI-themed trading cards. Drawing heavy inspiration from classic Topps cards and other older trading card sets, the creator has meticulously designed a series of cards using official Rockstar graphics. The project, which started as a simple idea, grew in scope as the creator envisioned new categories to complete a full set, aligning with potential future promotional campaigns for the game. Designed primarily with Adobe Illustrator for the cards and a combination of Photoshop and Illustrator for the graphics, this fan-made initiative has garnered attention within the GTA community, with the creator expressing interest in potentially finishing a complete fan-made card set in the future.

## Key Takeaways
- A fan has designed a concept for Grand Theft Auto VI trading cards.
