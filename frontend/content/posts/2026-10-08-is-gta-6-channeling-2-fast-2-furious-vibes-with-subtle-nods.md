This connection is further amplified by the fact that both the movie and GTA 6 (set in the fictional Vice City, a stand-in for Miami) share a vibrant Miami backdrop.

While the user acknowledges these could be mere coincidences, they suggest the possibility of a "subtle nod" from Rockstar Games to the popular street racing film. This observation has ignited excitement among fans eager to uncover all of GTA 6's potential influences and hidden details.
