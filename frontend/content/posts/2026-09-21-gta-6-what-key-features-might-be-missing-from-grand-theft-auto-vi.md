# GTA 6: What Key Features Might Be Missing from Grand Theft Auto VI?

While much attention focuses on new features for Grand Theft Auto VI, this article, originally a Reddit discussion, explores the possibility of certain elements from past GTA games being absent. Drawing comparisons to GTA IV's limitations relative to San Andreas (e.g., no underwater world, non-enterable planes, limited car mods, removed fitness system), the discussion highlights how Rockstar has historically rotated or removed features. For GTA VI, the author speculates on a few potential omissions from GTA V: the absence of a third playable character, the specific 'LS Customs' brand name (though customization functionality is expected to be enhanced), the replacement of distinct character-specific special abilities with a universal 'focus mode', and potentially the in-game stock market. The stock market is identified as the most likely 'legitimate cut' due to a lack of clear indication for its return and its absence in GTA Online. Conversely, many core functionalities like flying, swimming, diving, workouts, and car customization are noted as already confirmed for GTA VI.

## Key Takeaways
- Previous GTA titles, like GTA IV, have shown a pattern of limiting or removing features present in their predecessors (e.g., no underwater world, limited car mods compared to SA).
- Key features from GTA V that might be absent in GTA VI include a third playable character, the specific 'LS Customs' brand name, and unique character special abilities (replaced by 'focus mode').
- The in-game stock market is considered the most probable feature to be genuinely cut from GTA VI, lacking clear confirmation and not being present in GTA Online.
- Many expected functionalities such as flying, swimming, diving, workouts, and extensive car customization are already confirmed for GTA VI.
- The article is based on player speculation rather than official announcements or leaks.

## FAQ
**Will GTA 6 feature a third playable character like GTA V?**
The article speculates that GTA 6 will likely not include a third playable character, moving away from GTA V's design.

**Is the stock market confirmed for GTA 6?**
No, the in-game stock market is the only feature the author genuinely suspects might be cut, as it hasn't been clearly indicated for GTA 6 and wasn't available in GTA Online.

**Will car customization be available in GTA 6?**
Yes, car customization functionality is expected to be diverse and even more extensive, though the specific 'LS Customs' brand name might not return.

**Are core activities like flying and swimming confirmed for GTA 6?**
Yes, many core activities and functionalities, including flying, swimming, diving, and workouts, have already been confirmed for GTA 6.
