# A new promotional message from PlayStation, for GTA6

An image circulating on Reddit, presented as a 'new promotional message' from PlayStation, has garnered attention for its mention of Grand Theft Auto VI. The message, which appears to be a reminder for PlayStation Plus Extra and Premium members to play Grand Theft Auto V before it leaves the Game Catalog on November 17, also explicitly states, 'If you’re counting down the days until Grand Theft Auto VI arrives on PS5 on November 19...' and references 'Jason and Lucia’s Vice City story.' While the message *suggests* a specific release date for GTA VI, it is crucial to note that the Reddit post featuring this image is approximately 10 years old. This significantly impacts the credibility of the implied release date, making it highly questionable and likely outdated or speculative. Rockstar Games has not officially announced a precise release date for Grand Theft Auto VI beyond a general 2025 window, further indicating that the 'November 19' date from this old message is not current information.
