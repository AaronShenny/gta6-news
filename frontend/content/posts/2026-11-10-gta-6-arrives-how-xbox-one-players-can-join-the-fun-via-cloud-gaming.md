# GTA 6 Arrives: How Xbox One Players Can Join the Fun via Cloud Gaming

Grand Theft Auto 6, one of the most anticipated titles, is set to launch next week, on November 19, initially for Xbox Series X|S and PlayStation 5. While it previously seemed that owners of last-generation consoles like the Xbox One would be left out, Rockstar has officially confirmed a solution.

On November 10, Rockstar announced that GTA 6 will support Xbox Cloud Gaming. This feature enables players to stream the game to an Xbox One, effectively allowing last-gen console owners to play the game. However, a caveat is that the cloud-streamed version will utilize Xbox Series S graphics settings, meaning it won't look as sharp as running it natively on an Xbox Series X.

Rockstar also noted that, like all cloud streaming, game performance can be influenced by connection speeds and other technical factors. Furthermore, there's a slight delay for cloud players, as the game will unlock an hour later than the native versions.

A significant factor to consider for Xbox Cloud Gaming users is Microsoft's recently implemented time limits. Game Pass Ultimate subscribers are limited to 15 hours of cloud gaming per month. Non-subscribers can purchase play time, with an example given of 50 hours costing $12, which is in addition to the cost of GTA 6 itself.

Beyond Xbox Cloud Gaming, GTA 6 will also support Remote Play across both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The game is confirmed to include bonus performance features for PS5 Pro. While launching as a single-player experience, game files hint at future multiplayer functionality.
