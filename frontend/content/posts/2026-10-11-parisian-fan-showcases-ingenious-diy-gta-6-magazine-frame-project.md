# Parisian Fan Showcases Ingenious DIY GTA 6 Magazine Frame Project

A passionate Grand Theft Auto fan, known as /u/GenAzur on Reddit, has shared their unique DIY project: a framed GTA 6 magazine cover. The journey began during a walk in Paris, where they discovered an MK2 cinema giving away free magazines featuring the highly anticipated GTA 6 cover. Seizing the opportunity, the fan secured two copies – one to preserve as a collector's item and another to meticulously transform into a display piece. A gold frame, acquired for a mere ",5 from the store Action, now beautifully encases the cover. Adding a personal and thematic touch, the creator included a $5 USD bill they had serendipitously found on the streets of Paris. This unique addition, symbolizing GTA's American setting, currently rests alongside the frame, prompting the creator to seek community input on whether to integrate it directly into the frame or display it separately.
