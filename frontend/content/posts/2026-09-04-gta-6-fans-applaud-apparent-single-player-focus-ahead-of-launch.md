# GTA 6 Fans Applaud Apparent Single-Player Focus Ahead of Launch

A recent discussion on the r/GTA6 subreddit highlights a growing sentiment among fans regarding the development focus of Grand Theft Auto VI. User 'RollingthunderBch' initiated a thread titled 'Anyone else happy that theres so much Focus on single player?', expressing contentment that current conversations and anticipation revolve primarily around the core single-player experience. The user acknowledges that an online component for GTA VI is inevitable and will likely launch months after the initial game release, but emphasizes that the present excitement is purely for 'simply Gta VI' rather than future updates or heists for an online mode. This reflects a desire within the community for a robust and engaging story-driven experience at launch, reminiscent of classic Grand Theft Auto titles, before the eventual introduction of its multiplayer counterpart.

## Key Takeaways
- Reddit user 'RollingthunderBch' expresses happiness over GTA 6's perceived single-player focus.
- The community is currently excited for the core game experience, not immediate online content.
- It is acknowledged that a GTA 6 Online component will likely launch months after the initial game.
- The sentiment suggests a desire for a strong story-driven experience at launch.
