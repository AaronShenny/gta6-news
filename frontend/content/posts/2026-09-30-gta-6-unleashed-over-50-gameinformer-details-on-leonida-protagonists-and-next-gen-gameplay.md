# GTA 6 Unleashed: Over 50 GameInformer Details on Leonida, Protagonists, and Next-Gen Gameplay

Rockstar Games has pulled back the curtain on an unprecedented wealth of details for Grand Theft Auto VI, as revealed in GameInformer's exclusive interview. The upcoming title is set in the sprawling state of Leonida, a vibrant, exaggerated take on Florida, described as "America dialed up to 11." This massive, dense map is double the size of GTAV, featuring iconic locations like Vice City, the treacherous Grassrivers (Everglades), the serene Leonida Keys, the struggling tourist hub Port Gellhorn, industrial Ambrosia, and the unique Mount Kalaga National Park, which blends Florida-Georgia border culture with private jet communities.

Players will step into the shoes of two new protagonists: Jason, an army veteran working for drug runners in the Keys, and Lucia, a combat-trained Liberty City native with a mysterious past that landed her in prison. Their relationship forms a core narrative element, allowing players to influence whether they remain friends or become a romantic couple, though Rockstar emphasizes this system is optional and offers no direct gameplay benefits, only affecting certain story elements.

The world of Leonida is brought to life with groundbreaking technological advancements. The weather system has been completely re-engineered, offering localized and persistent conditions, from dynamic cloudscapes and realistic rainbows after rain to intense hurricanes and tropical storms, all influenced by an intricate wind system. The ecosystem is equally impressive, boasting over 170 animal species – including many never-before-seen in a Rockstar title – from native alligators and flamingos to invasive iguanas and macaques. Animals are rendered with incredible detail, featuring simulated fur, iridescence, and distinct behaviors, with some categorized as rare or legendary for tracking.

NPCs receive a significant overhaul, becoming the most diverse and well-realized in the series' history. A dedicated team crafts their ambient dialogue, while their physical appearances vary greatly in height, body type, skin details, and culturally accurate tattoos and hairstyles, reflecting the diverse communities of Leonida.

Gameplay is enriched with a plethora of activities, from base jumping and scuba diving to mini-golf and air boating. The criminal underworld is deeper, with missions tied to financial progress and robberies allowing for solo or duo execution, with 'loud' or 'quiet' approaches dictating police response. The notorious 6-star wanted level returns, complemented by a new 'Heat' system where police gather intelligence based on player appearance and whereabouts. Evading the law will involve changing hairstyles, clothing, and vehicles, with the option to split up as a duo to divide police attention. Vehicle theft is also more nuanced, requiring specialized tools for high-end cars with sophisticated security, which can be scanned using the new WAiNK phone app to check registration costs or resale value. Rockstar also hints at a story designed to encourage multiple playthroughs, suggesting branching narratives or significant choices.

## Key Takeaways

## FAQ
**What is the main setting for GTA 6?**
The game is set in Leonida, an exaggerated, larger-than-life version of Florida, featuring Vice City and diverse regions.

**Who are the protagonists in GTA 6?**
The game features two protagonists, Jason, an army vet working for drug runners, and Lucia, a combat-trained Liberty City native with a mysterious past.

**How large is the GTA 6 map compared to previous games?**
Leonida is described as Rockstar's biggest, densest, and most detailed map ever, double the size of Grand Theft Auto V.

**What new features does the weather system offer?**
The re-engineered weather system includes localized and persistent conditions, dynamic cloudscapes, hurricanes, tropical storms, realistic rain effects with puddles and rainbows, and a detailed wind system.

**Can players influence the relationship between Jason and Lucia?**
Yes, players can influence whether Jason and Lucia remain friends or become a romantic couple, which affects certain story elements, though it offers no direct gameplay benefits.

**What kind of wildlife can be found in Leonida?**
Leonida boasts over 170 animal species, including native (alligators, flamingos) and invasive (green iguanas, rhesus macaques), with incredibly detailed rendering and distinct behaviors.

**How has the NPC system been improved?**
NPCs are the most diverse and well-realized in the series, featuring varied physical appearances, culturally accurate tattoos and hairstyles, and extensive ambient dialogue crafted by a dedicated team.

**How does the wanted system work in GTA 6?**
The 6-star wanted level returns, complemented by a new "Heat" system where police gather information based on player appearance and whereabouts. Players can evade capture by changing their look, ditching vehicles, or splitting up as a duo.

**What is the WAiNK app?**
WAiNK is a new in-game phone app used to scan expensive stolen vehicles to check their security systems, estimate resale value, or register them as personal vehicles.
