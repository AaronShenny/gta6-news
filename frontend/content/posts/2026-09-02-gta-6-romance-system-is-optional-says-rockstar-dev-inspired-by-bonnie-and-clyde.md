# GTA 6 Romance System is Optional, Says Rockstar Dev, Inspired by Bonnie and Clyde

Rockstar Games has confirmed that the much-discussed romance system between GTA 6 protagonists Jason and Lucia will be entirely optional. In a recent interview, Rockstar's Rob Nelson stated that players who aren't interested in the romantic aspect can simply choose not to engage with it, emphasizing that the system will not be 'foisted upon players'.

For those who wish to explore the romantic relationship, activities such as holding hands, visiting a zoo, or enjoying a romantic stroll on the beach will be available. Nelson hinted that opting into the romance could add a 'compelling emotional element' and lead players to 'feel protective' of the duo, suggesting that disengagement might mean missing out on certain narrative layers. He also noted that the developers didn't initially envision this emotional depth but 'felt their way through' its creation.

The relationship management will involve player actions, including responding to texts, with Nelson mentioning that 'your actions having meaning in terms of how you treat your partner' is a new concept for GTA, akin to an honor system. The core dynamic between Jason and Lucia is heavily inspired by the legendary outlaw couple, Bonnie and Clyde.

GTA 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Recent revelations also include a map 3X larger than Red Dead Redemption 2's, an estimated 80-hour playthrough, the return of six-star wanted levels, and the ability to pet dogs.
