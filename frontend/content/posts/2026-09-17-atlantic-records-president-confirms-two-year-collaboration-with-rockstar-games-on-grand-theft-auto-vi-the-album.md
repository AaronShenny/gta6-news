# Atlantic Records President Confirms Two-Year Collaboration with Rockstar Games on 'Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album'

In a significant revelation for Grand Theft Auto fans, Kevin Weaver, President of Atlantic Music Group, has confirmed that Atlantic Records has been actively collaborating with Rockstar Games for the past two years. This extensive partnership is focused on the development of 'Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album'. The announcement hints at a deeply integrated and potentially expansive musical experience for the highly anticipated title, suggesting that the game's soundtrack will be a major highlight, crafted by one of the music industry's leading labels.
