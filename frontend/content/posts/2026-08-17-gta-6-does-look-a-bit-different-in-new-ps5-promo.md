# GTA 6 Does Look A Bit Different In New PS5 Promo

Sony's latest "It Happens On PS5" ad campaign includes brief snippets of **Grand Theft Auto VI** footage, drawing keen attention from fans. While largely re-used, vigilant observers, like the X account DriftRespawn, have identified subtle visual alterations compared to the game's second trailer from May 2025. Notably, a scene depicting Jason Duval on a convertible driven by Lucia now appears **full screen**, with the previously seen cinematic letterboxing removed. Furthermore, the lighting in this particular scene seems **lighter**, and Lucia's character model exhibits minor tweaks, such as less pronounced eyebrows and potentially browner hair, though this could be attributed to the altered lighting. These changes are likely indicative of the ongoing development process for GTA 6. Fans can anticipate further revelations with an "Extended Look" trailer slated for release on Netflix on **August 27**. GTA 6 is scheduled to launch on **November 19**, with preorders already strong despite discussions around the game's digital-only release and Sony's future plans for disc production. The PS5 promo also highlights other anticipated titles like Marvel's Wolverine and God of War Laufey.
