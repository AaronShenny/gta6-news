# KFC Australia Staging Fake Funeral for GTA 6 Launch Day 'Absences'

KFC Australia is launching an absurd yet ingenious promotion for the release of Grand Theft Auto 6, staging a fake funeral for a fictional character named 'Ann Alibi'. The event, set for Thursday, November 19, at 11 AM at KFC Robina in Queensland, is designed to provide Australian fans with a 'legitimate' excuse to take the day off work and immerse themselves in GTA 6. The Gold Coast region, where Robina is located, is humorously likened by KFC to GTA 6's Vice City.

The elaborate 'funeral' will feature all the traditional elements, including a hearse, coffin, floral tributes, a book of condolences, an order of service, and a heartfelt eulogy delivered by a surprise guest. In addition to the theatrical send-off, in-person attendees will receive free fried chicken, while those unable to attend can still benefit from free delivery across Australia via the KFC app on November 19.

A KFC spokesperson emphasized the promotion's goal: "We appreciate that Australians have responsibilities, but some occasions are simply too important to miss." They added that the funeral was intentionally scheduled during working hours to align with 'Ann Alibi's' final wishes. This unique marketing stunt ties into GTA 6's global launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with Australia being among the first countries to access the game due to its midnight local time unlock.
