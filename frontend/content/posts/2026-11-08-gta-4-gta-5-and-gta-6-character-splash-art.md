# GTA 4, GTA 5 and GTA 6 Character Splash Art

A recent Reddit post on r/GTA6 has become a focal point for fans eager for any glimpse of Grand Theft Auto 6, compiling character splash art from across the series. The post features established art from *Grand Theft Auto IV* (attributed to Felipe Saenger) and *Grand Theft Auto V*. The most intriguing aspect for many is the inclusion of character art for *Grand Theft Auto 6*. This section highlights Lucia, whose art is explicitly credited to fan artist @harrydidthegraphic (u/Focus_Conscious). Alongside Lucia, art for characters Jason and Raul is also presented, with the post claiming these are 'by Rockstar' but 'submitted by u/meechCS'. While the authenticity of the Jason and Raul art as official Rockstar assets remains unconfirmed and should be treated as a rumor, its appearance alongside known fan art and previous game entries has ignited further speculation within the GTA community regarding the visual style and potential character roster for the highly anticipated title.

## Key Takeaways
