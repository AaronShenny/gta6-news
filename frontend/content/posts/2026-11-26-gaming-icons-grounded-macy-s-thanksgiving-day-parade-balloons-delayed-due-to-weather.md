# Gaming Icons Grounded: Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade Balloons Delayed Due to Weather

New York City's notorious weather has forced an unprecedented delay of the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade balloons. For the first time in its century-long history, the beloved tradition, which sees giant pop culture totems float through the city streets, has been postponed due to a forecast of heavy rain and strong windstorms.

Gaming characters have become a staple of the parade since Sonic the Hedgehog made his debut in 1993, marking the first video game character balloon. Since then, iconic figures such as Mario, Pac-Man, Pikachu, and even Red from Angry Birds have joined the aerial procession.

However, the combination of adverse weather and massive balloons poses significant safety risks. Past incidents include Sonic's 1993 appearance, where the balloon snagged on a lamppost, burst, and injured two bystanders. More severely, a series of mishaps in 1997 involving the Cat in the Hat balloon led to injuries, with one woman left comatose for days. These events have rightly led to a stronger emphasis on safety protocols.

While families might miss seeing their favorite characters float by on Thanksgiving, the good news is that the balloons are rescheduled to fly on Saturday at 8:30 AM ET, when weather conditions are expected to be much more favorable.
