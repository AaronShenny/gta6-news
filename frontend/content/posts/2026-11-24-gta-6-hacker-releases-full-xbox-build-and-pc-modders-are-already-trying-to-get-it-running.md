# GTA 6 Hacker Releases Full Xbox Build, And PC Modders Are Already Trying To Get It Running

Infamous hacker 'Cyberleek' has made good on their threats, publicly releasing a crucial file for the Xbox build of *Grand Theft Auto 6*. This leak is significant as it could enable the creation of an unofficial PC port of the highly anticipated game. Cyberleek, who previously resurfaced threatening this release based on a memecoin's market cap, claims to be 'fighting anti-consumerism' and funding a new project, despite having already cashed out hundreds of thousands of dollars from their memecoin in recent months.

Following the release, PC modders and homebrew enthusiasts are actively working to get the Xbox build running on PC. The 'Project Leonida' Discord server has already shared footage of the game booting up, though it currently only reaches a black screen. Reports indicate modders are utilizing AI, specifically Claude AI, to reverse-engineer the game, expressing confidence that a playable PC build could be ready within a week.

This development comes amidst rumors of an official *GTA 6* PC port potentially arriving in early 2027, a much shorter gap than typical for Rockstar titles. The emergence of a homebrewed PC port could significantly pressure Rockstar Games to officially release *GTA 6* on PC sooner than initially planned.

Despite these unauthorized leaks, *GTA 6* has achieved unprecedented success on its official console launch for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The game reportedly sold an astounding 32.5 million copies on its first day, rapidly increasing to 44.9 million within days. Sony has confirmed that *GTA 6* marks the biggest launch in PlayStation history.
