# GTA 6 Confirms Return of Six-Star Wanted Levels, Revamping Crime System

Rockstar Games has officially confirmed that Grand Theft Auto 6 will reintroduce the highly anticipated six-star wanted level, a staple from earlier GTA titles that was notably absent in GTA 5. This exciting return was unveiled during the recent GTA 6 Netflix gameplay video, signaling a deeper and more challenging experience for players.While the exact criteria for reaching a six-star wanted level remain under wraps, it's expected to involve committing the most egregious crimes as protagonists Jason and Lucia. The wanted system in GTA 6 is also undergoing a significant evolution beyond just the star count.A logo featured in the latest GTA 6 trailer hinted at the introduction of specific icons representing different crimes players commit. For instance, a car icon might appear after a vehicle theft and reportedly disappears once Jason or Lucia successfully hide the stolen car in a garage. Furthermore, players will have new tools for evasion, including the ability to destroy security camera footage to throw off law enforcement. The police response itself will be more dynamic and reactive; engaging in aggressive actions, such as going "guns blazing" into an area, will provoke a more aggressive and intense police pursuit.Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, promising a refined and immersive crime experience.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 will bring back the six-star wanted level, a feature absent since GTA 5.
- The return of six-star wanted levels was confirmed in the GTA 6 Netflix gameplay video.
- The new wanted system will feature crime-specific icons, which can disappear based on player actions (e.g., hiding a car).
- Players can destroy security camera footage to evade police detection.
- Police response and aggression will dynamically scale with the player's level of aggression.
- GTA 6 is scheduled to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
