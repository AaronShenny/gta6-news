# GTA 6 Preorders Skyrocket, Outpacing Major AAA Titles by 148%

Rockstar Games' highly anticipated title, Grand Theft Auto 6, is poised for an unprecedented launch, with new data from Sensor Tower highlighting its massive preorder success. The analytics firm's report, as covered by GI.biz, indicates that GTA 6 digital preorders in the US are pacing an astounding 148% ahead of Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 (2022) over a comparable period before release. This makes MW2 the biggest game in Sensor Tower's recent comparison of major AAA titles, including Diablo 4, Hogwarts Legacy, and Marvel's Spider-Man (2023).

Sensor Tower's live tracker shows GTA 6 has already sold over 6.45 million copies, generating more than $630 million in revenue from preorders alone. The firm projects that total prepurchases could soar to 25 million, potentially bringing in over $2 billion before the game even hits shelves. The PlayStation 5 currently dominates preorder sales, accounting for 74% of the total, while Xbox Series X|S holds 26%—a split largely attributed to the PS5's larger install base. Despite this, Xbox executives have expressed satisfaction with their platform's preorder performance.

While GTA 6 will initially be a single-player experience, a new iteration of GTA Online is expected to follow, promising to be another significant moneymaker for Rockstar. The game is officially set to launch on November 19, 2026, for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a PC version rumored to arrive potentially much earlier than expected in early 2027.
