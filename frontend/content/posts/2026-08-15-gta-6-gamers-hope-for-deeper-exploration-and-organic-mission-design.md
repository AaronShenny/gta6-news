# GTA 6: Gamers Hope for Deeper Exploration and Organic Mission Design

A dedicated GTA fan expresses their biggest hopes for the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6, advocating for a significant evolution in its open-world design. Drawing inspiration from Red Dead Redemption 2, the author wishes for GTA 6 to lean heavily into hidden encounters and mission chains that organically reward player exploration. The core idea is to move away from simple map markers, instead allowing side missions to be discovered naturally with minimal guidance.

Key to this vision is the integration of missions deeper within existing world activities, such as those tied to properties, races, or businesses. This approach would make mission discovery feel more earned and unique, potentially even influencing the main storyline. The author also suggests extending this creative reward system beyond missions to include hidden weapons and unique items that meaningfully alter gameplay, encouraging players to investigate unusual locations and activities.

Ultimately, the hope is for GTA 6 to blur the lines between traditional missions, side activities, and open-world exploration, creating a more alive and rewarding experience for players who venture off the beaten path. While acknowledging this might be wishful thinking, the author remains optimistic, especially with the game being Rockstar's biggest and most expensive title to date, eagerly awaiting potential gameplay reveals.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 should feature more hidden encounters and organic mission chains rewarding exploration.
- Side missions should be discovered organically with minimal guidance, moving beyond simple map markers.
- Missions should be deeply integrated into world activities like properties, races, and businesses, feeling more earned.
- Meaningful hidden rewards, such as unique weapons and items that change gameplay, should encourage exploration.
- The game should blur the lines between missions, activities, and exploration for a more immersive open-world experience.
