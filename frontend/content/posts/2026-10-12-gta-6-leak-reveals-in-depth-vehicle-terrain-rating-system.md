# GTA 6 Leak Reveals In-Depth Vehicle Terrain Rating System

According to a recent Reddit post by u/deepankrrr, an analysis of the 'Extended Leek' footage from Grand Theft Auto VI has uncovered what appears to be a sophisticated new vehicle rating system. While browsing dirt bikes for rent, and later customizing an Elegy, players observed a user interface displaying two distinct symbols: a mountain (📈) and a highway (🛣️), each accompanied by a numerical rating.This system is hypothesized to indicate a vehicle's performance capabilities across different terrains. For instance, dirt bikes showed a higher 'mountain' rating (e.g., 📈 600) and a lower 'highway' rating (e.g., 🛣️ 400), consistent with their off-road nature. Conversely, the Elegy, a presumably street-focused vehicle, displayed ratings like 📈 400 and 🛣️ 700, favoring highway performance.This discovery suggests that GTA 6 could feature a more in-depth vehicle customization and selection process than previously seen, potentially influencing gameplay strategies based on the environment. The user noted that the system is 'way more in-depth than I expected,' hinting at a richer driving experience.

## Key Takeaways
- A new vehicle rating system has been observed in the GTA 6 'Extended Leek' footage.

- This suggests a more detailed and impactful vehicle selection and customization experience in Grand Theft Auto VI.
