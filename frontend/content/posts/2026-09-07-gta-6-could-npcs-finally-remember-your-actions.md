# GTA 6: Could NPCs Finally Remember Your Actions?

A recent discussion on the r/GTA6 subreddit, initiated by user u/Content-Cheetah-6958, has sparked interest in a revolutionary feature for Grand Theft Auto 6: NPCs with a persistent memory. The core idea moves beyond the typical 'crime forgotten in 30 seconds' mechanic, suggesting that NPCs could remember past interactions, such as player-instigated fights, stolen vehicles, or chaotic incidents in their vicinity. The user envisions a world where returning to a neighborhood or encountering a specific NPC hours later could trigger unique reactions based on previous encounters. Even subtle recognition of the player's vehicle or attire could significantly enhance the realism and immersion of the game world. The post highlights Rockstar's established technological prowess and attention to detail, implying that such a feature is within their capabilities. The discussion ultimately poses a question to the community: would persistent NPC memory be a welcome addition, or would it prove to be 'too much' for the player experience?
