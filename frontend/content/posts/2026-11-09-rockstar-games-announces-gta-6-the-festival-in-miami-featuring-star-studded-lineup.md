# Rockstar Games Announces "GTA 6: The Festival" in Miami Featuring Star-Studded Lineup

Rockstar Games has officially announced "Grand Theft Auto 6: The Festival," a major concert event set to take place at Mana Wynwood in Miami on November 21. This "one-of-a-kind launch event" is designed to celebrate the highly anticipated release of GTA 6, which launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

The festival boasts an impressive lineup of artists, including Cardi B, Travis Scott, Kodak Black, and Justice, all of whom are either featured in the game or on its official soundtrack. The event, sponsored by PlayStation and produced by the team behind Miami's III Points festival, promises two stages of non-stop music.

Securing tickets for "GTA 6: The Festival" is exclusive and requires participation in a lottery system. Tickets cannot be purchased directly. The lottery is currently open and will close on November 10 at 4 PM ET. Winners will be drawn on the same day and will then have the opportunity to purchase tickets on November 11. There is a strict two-ticket maximum per winner, tickets are non-transferable, and attendees must be 17 years or older.

Notably, Justice, the French electronic music duo performing at the festival, also composed the original score for GTA 6. The game itself will feature a rich audio experience, including in-game radio stations packed with hits from famous artists and even podcasts players can listen to on foot across Vice City.
