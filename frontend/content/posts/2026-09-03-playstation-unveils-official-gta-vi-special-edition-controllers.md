# PlayStation Unveils Official GTA VI Special Edition Controllers

PlayStation has officially revealed a new line of special edition controllers themed around the highly anticipated game, Grand Theft Auto VI. The announcement, which appears to be focused solely on controllers, does not include any accompanying console bundles or faceplates. Gamers eager to get their hands on these exclusive accessories can mark their calendars for September 10th, as pre-orders will go live on that date. The official launch of the controllers is set for November 19th. Each controller will retail for USD$84.99.

## Key Takeaways
- PlayStation has announced special edition GTA VI controllers.
- The announcement is for controllers only, with no console or faceplate bundles.
- Pre-orders for the controllers begin on September 10th.
- The controllers will officially launch on November 19th.
- The retail price for each controller is USD$84.99.
