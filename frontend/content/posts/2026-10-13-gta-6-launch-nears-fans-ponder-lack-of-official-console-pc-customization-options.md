# GTA 6 Launch Nears: Fans Ponder Lack of Official Console & PC Customization Options

As the anticipated launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 reportedly draws near, a Reddit user has voiced surprise regarding the apparent absence of official console and PC customization options. The user, known as u/Into-The-Shadows1978, noted that with GTA 6 supposedly 'about a month out from launch,' there has been no release of official 'console flair,' such as profile pictures or dynamic backgrounds.

The user expressed a desire for official and interactive designs or graphics from Rockstar Games to personalize consoles and PCs. While acknowledging that players can use unofficial images, the appeal lies in official, branded content directly from the developer. The post speculates that such customization features might be added post-release but also queries the community if anyone has seen or heard any information regarding this.
