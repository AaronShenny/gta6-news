# GTA 6 Story Length: Fans Debate Ideal Playtime, Comparing to RDR2 and GTA V

A recent discussion initiated by Reddit user u/Junior-Structure-439 on the r/GTA6 subreddit has sparked a debate among fans regarding the ideal story length for the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI. The user posed the question, "How long do y'all want the GTA 6 story length to be? (hours)", providing comparative data from previous Rockstar titles.

Red Dead Redemption 2, known for its sprawling narrative, was cited as having a main objective completion time of approximately **50 to 60 hours**. In contrast, Grand Theft Auto V's main missions could be completed in roughly **31 to 32 hours**. These figures set a clear context for the community's expectations, with many likely weighing the desire for a deep, extensive story against the more focused experience of past GTA titles. The discussion highlights the community's keen interest in the scope and longevity of GTA 6's single-player campaign.
