# GTA 6 Deep Dive: Massive Map, Evolved Police System, and Realistic Vehicle Theft Revealed

This extensive preview of Grand Theft Auto 6 uncovers a wealth of new details, painting a picture of a vastly expanded and more immersive world than its predecessors. The game's map is confirmed to be significantly larger, with Vice City alone being twice the size of Los Santos, and the entire state of Leonida (pronounced Lee-Oh-Nigh-Da) dwarfing Red Dead Redemption 2's playable area. Players can expect hundreds of interactive interiors, extensive safehouse activities (like shaving and hair cutting), and new social features including fully implemented social media where NPC posts can lead to real-time events.

Transportation is also enhanced with multiple fast travel options (bus stops, trains, taxis, RydeMe) and the ability to call your personal vehicle via 'Scooter Bros'. Everyday interactions are more dynamic, with the return of 'greet and antagonise' for NPCs, and a deeper TV system featuring professionally animated in-game shows. A 'Streamer mode' is included to disable copyrighted music, and while there's no radio wheel, stations can be scrolled, and podcasts are available on demand.

The police and 'Heat' system have undergone a significant overhaul. Cops no longer appear on the minimap; instead, players must rely on 'Focus' to reveal their positions. Crimes require a witness or report to trigger police response, which can happen via phone, alarm, or vehicle trackers. A new 'Heat' system, layered under wanted stars, tracks specific information police know about the player, such as CCTV footage, outfits, and weapons. Players can mitigate 'Heat' by destroying footage, changing clothes, masking up, stashing weapons, or splitting up as a couple (Jason and Lucia). Wanted stars are more nuanced, indicating direct line of sight, active evasion, or a general search. At the highest wanted level, police conduct map-wide searches, and failed robberies can lead to perimeters being set around locations.

Vehicle mechanics are also more complex. Hijacking cars now involves quick-time events, and parked cars offer 'loud' (smash window, alarm) or 'quiet' (slim jim) theft options. Vehicles are stratified by security, with higher-tier cars requiring tools like a 'key cloner'. Theft tools and abilities like tracker removal unlock through progression with car fences. A phone app called 'Waink' can scan cars to reveal security levels, required tools, tracker tiers, and resale value. Higher-tier trackers have a speed mechanic, pinging police if the player drives too slowly. Mod shops now specialize, and Pay'n'Sprays are simplified for quickly shedding vehicle 'Heat'. Players can buy garages for storing multiple personal vehicles, but safehouses are not purchasable.

## Key Takeaways
- The GTA 6 map is immense, with Vice City alone being twice the size of Los Santos, and the total playable area three times that of RDR2's.
- Hundreds of interactive interiors and extensive safehouse interactivity, including character grooming.
- Social media is fully integrated, with NPC posts potentially leading to real-time in-game events.
- The police system is completely revamped: no minimap icons for cops, a 'Focus' ability to see them, and a 'Heat' system that tracks specific crime details.
- Players can mitigate police 'Heat' by destroying evidence, changing appearance, or splitting up as a couple.
- Vehicle theft is more nuanced, with security levels, required tools (slim jim, key cloner), and a 'Waink' app for scanning car details.
- Mod shops specialize in services, and Pay'n'Sprays are for quick 'Heat' removal rather than deep customization.
- Fast travel options include RydeMe (Uber equivalent) and 'Scooter Bros' for calling personal vehicles.

## FAQ
**How large is the GTA 6 map compared to previous titles?**
The map is twice the size of GTA 5's and three times Red Dead Redemption 2's playable area. Vice City alone is twice the size of Los Santos.

**How does the new police system work in GTA 6?**
Police no longer appear on the minimap. Players must use 'Focus' to reveal them. Crimes require a witness or report to trigger a response. A 'Heat' system tracks specific details (CCTV, outfits, weapons), which players can mitigate by addressing each indicator.

**Can I customize my vehicles extensively in GTA 6?**
Mod shops specialize, meaning no single shop does everything. Pay'n'Sprays are back but are simplified for repairing and respraying basic colors, primarily to shed vehicle 'Heat' rather than for deep customization.

**What are the new vehicle theft mechanics?**
Hijacking drivers involves quick-time events. Parked cars can be stolen loudly (smash window, alarm) or quietly (slim jim). Vehicles have security levels, with some requiring tools like a 'key cloner'. A phone app, 'Waink', can scan cars for security details and tracker information.

**Are there new social features or apps in GTA 6?**
Yes, social media is fully implemented, with NPCs posting and some posts leading to real-time events. New phone apps include BuckMe, RydeMe, Scooter Bros (to call your vehicle), WhatUp, Waink (car scanner), a fitness app, and Snapmatic.
