# GTA 6 Exploration Hype: Players Anticipate Spending 90% of Time Discovering Vice City

This highlights a community belief in Rockstar's ability to craft a living, breathing environment that encourages organic discovery and emergent gameplay, promising continuous engagement through world interaction beyond structured objectives.
