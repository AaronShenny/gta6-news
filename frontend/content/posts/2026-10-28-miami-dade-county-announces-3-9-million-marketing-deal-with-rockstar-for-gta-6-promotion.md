# Miami-Dade County Announces $3.9 Million Marketing Deal with Rockstar for GTA 6 Promotion

In a significant development for Grand Theft Auto fans, Miami-Dade County has officially announced a substantial $3.9 million marketing partnership with Rockstar Games. This collaboration aims to promote the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6* across various county facilities.

The announcement, which quickly spread across gaming communities, suggests a strong connection between the game's setting and the real-world Miami-Dade area, fueling speculation that the game will indeed return to the iconic Vice City. While Rockstar Games has yet to officially confirm the game's setting or release details, this multi-million dollar agreement with a real-world government entity lends significant weight to the long-standing rumors of a Vice City-inspired open world.

This marketing package indicates a proactive strategy from both the county and Rockstar, potentially leveraging local landmarks and cultural elements for in-game promotions or real-world tie-ins as the game's launch approaches. The deal not only confirms an official partnership but also indirectly reinforces the belief that GTA 6 will be set in a fictionalized version of Miami, much like previous titles have drawn inspiration from real-world locations.

## Key Takeaways
- Miami-Dade County announced a $3.9 million marketing package with Rockstar Games.
