# Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Nintendo Direct Announced, Pre-orders Live Ahead of GTA 6 Buzz

Nintendo has officially announced a dedicated Nintendo Direct broadcast for **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave**, scheduled for **August 4 at 7 AM PT / 10 AM ET**. This 20-minute presentation promises new information about the highly anticipated tactical-RPG. Notably, Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave marks a significant milestone as the first game in the long-running series developed exclusively for **Switch 2 consoles**.

The game introduces a fresh take on combat, focusing on four champions competing in the high-stakes **Dagosian Games** within an arena setting, a departure from traditional battlefields. Beyond the tournament, players will still have opportunities to explore new locations and engage with bandits. Each champion possesses a unique crest granting special abilities, and social events will complement the combat.

Set to launch on **September 17**, Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave enters a crowded release month, with many titles aiming to establish themselves before major heavyweights like **Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4** and **GTA 6** arrive. Pre-orders are currently open, with the standard edition priced between $70-$80 and a Special Edition available for $120, which includes the physical game and collectibles.

## Key Takeaways
- A dedicated Nintendo Direct for Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave will air on August 4 at 7 AM PT / 10 AM ET.
- The broadcast will last approximately 20 minutes, revealing new game details.
- Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave is the first game in the series developed exclusively for Switch 2 consoles.
- Gameplay features arena combat in the Dagosian Games, a new direction for the series, alongside exploration and social elements.
- The game is set to release on September 17, navigating a busy launch window ahead of major titles like GTA 6.
- Pre-orders are now available for both standard ($70-$80) and Special Editions ($120).
