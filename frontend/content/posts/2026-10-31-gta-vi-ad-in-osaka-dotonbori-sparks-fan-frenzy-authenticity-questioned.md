# GTA VI 'Ad' in Osaka Dotonbori Sparks Fan Frenzy, Authenticity Questioned

A Reddit user, u/bakatomoya, recently shared an image claiming to show a new Grand Theft Auto VI advertisement prominently displayed on a digital billboard in Osaka's bustling Dotonbori district. The image, which has rapidly circulated among GTA fans, depicts the 'GTA VI' logo on a large outdoor screen.

However, the authenticity of this supposed advertisement is highly questionable. There has been no official announcement or confirmation from Rockstar Games or its parent company, Take-Two Interactive, regarding any marketing campaign for GTA VI in Japan or elsewhere. Given the immense anticipation and global scale of a title like Grand Theft Auto VI, any official advertising rollout would undoubtedly be part of a meticulously planned, high-profile campaign, not a solitary, unconfirmed image shared on social media. Gaming journalists and news outlets worldwide would be reporting on such a significant development immediately.

While the image has certainly fueled fan speculation and excitement, it is widely believed to be either a fan-made mock-up, a prank, or a misinterpretation of other content on the screen. Until Rockstar Games provides official details, this 'ad' remains firmly in the realm of unconfirmed rumors and likely hoaxes.
