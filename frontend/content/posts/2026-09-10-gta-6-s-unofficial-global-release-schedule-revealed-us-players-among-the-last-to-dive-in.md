# GTA 6's Unofficial Global Release Schedule Revealed: US Players Among the Last to Dive In

GTA 6, one of the most highly anticipated games ever, is slated for release on November 19. While the official launch date is set, new unofficial information has emerged regarding its worldwide release times, indicating a staggered rollout rather than a simultaneous global launch.

According to findings by GTA Vice, which analyzed PlayStation Store listings across 63 countries, GTA 6 is expected to unlock at midnight local time in each region. This means players in New Zealand and Australia will be the first to access the game, effectively on November 18 at 5 AM PT / 8 AM ET. Conversely, the United States is projected to be among the last major markets to receive access, potentially waiting an additional 16 hours after New Zealand. This staggered release could create a challenge for US players, as they might face a flood of spoilers from regions that get the game earlier.

It is crucial to note that this information is based on PlayStation Store data and has not been officially confirmed by Rockstar Games. GameSpot has reached out to the developer for comment, and details remain subject to change. 

In related news, preloading for the massive game is scheduled to begin on November 12. Physical editions, which will contain a code in a box, will also be available for purchase in stores from November 12 but will not unlock until the official November 19 release. The game has already seen impressive preorder numbers, with over 5 million copies sold and projections suggesting 25 million by launch, potentially generating over $2 billion in revenue, which could cover its development budget.
