# GTA 6 Fan Community Ponders Top Questions About Upcoming Title

The dedicated Grand Theft Auto 6 community on Reddit, specifically a post by user u/niffum1, has compiled a list of significant questions that fans are eager to have answered regarding the highly anticipated title. These questions span a wide range of topics, showcasing the community's deep interest in every aspect of the game.Key areas of curiosity include the geographical expanse of the map, particularly what lies north, and the potential for hidden mysteries and easter eggs that are a hallmark of Rockstar Games. Story elements are also a major point of discussion, with fans wondering about character fates and the potential relationship mechanics between protagonists Lucia and Jason.Gameplay features are another hot topic, with questions about the interactivity of strip clubs, the variety and range of available weapons, and the return of popular police vehicle missions. Technical specifications are not overlooked, as players are keen to know if GTA 6 will target 60 frames per second or be limited to 30fps.Finally, environmental details such as the locations of Air Force stations and the accessibility of airports as enterable locations are also on the minds of the community, highlighting their desire for an immersive and detailed open world experience.

## Key Takeaways
- Fans are highly curious about the northern expansion of the GTA 6 map.
- Anticipation is high for new mysteries and easter eggs within the game world.
- The community is speculating on story outcomes, including character deaths and the relationship between Lucia and Jason.
- Questions about gameplay mechanics like strip club interactivity, weapon variety, and police missions are prominent.
- Performance targets (60fps vs 30fps) are a significant concern for players.
- Interest exists in environmental details such as Air Force stations and enterable airports.
