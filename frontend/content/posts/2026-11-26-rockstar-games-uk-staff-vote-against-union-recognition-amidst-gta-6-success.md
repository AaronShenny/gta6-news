# Rockstar Games UK Staff Vote Against Union Recognition Amidst GTA 6 Success

Approximately 75% of Rockstar Games' UK staff, with a massive 93% turnout of roughly 1,544 employees, have voted against a formal recognition agreement with the Independent Workers' Union of Great Britain (IWGB). This significant decision comes in the wake of *Grand Theft Auto 6*'s record-breaking launch, which is poised to achieve absurd new sales milestones.

Had the IWGB been formally recognized, Rockstar Games management would have been legally compelled to negotiate collective bargaining terms concerning crucial aspects like pay, working hours, and conditions. Despite the loss, the IWGB remains resolute, stating they are 'undeterred' and will continue their efforts to build support among Rockstar employees. They emphasized that this result does not prevent workers from organizing, joining the union, or exercising individual trade union rights.

This vote also closely follows an ongoing employment tribunal where the IWGB has accused Rockstar of unlawful dismissal and union busting. This allegation stems from the firing of over 30 employees last year, ostensibly for gross misconduct related to alleged confidential information leaks. A ruling on this tribunal is still pending.

The outcome represents a notable 'speed bump' for the broader game development unionization movement, which has gained considerable momentum recently. The IWGB acknowledges that more work is needed to gain the understanding and trust of a wider segment of their colleagues, particularly as Rockstar rides the wave of GTA 6's success, potentially focusing employees on bonuses and post-launch momentum.
