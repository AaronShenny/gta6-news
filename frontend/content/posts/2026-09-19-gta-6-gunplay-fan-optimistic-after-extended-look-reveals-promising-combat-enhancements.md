# GTA 6 Gunplay: Fan Optimistic After 'Extended Look' Reveals Promising Combat Enhancements

A long-time Grand Theft Auto and Red Dead Redemption player has expressed significant optimism regarding the potential gunplay mechanics in the upcoming GTA 6. Initially, the user harbored concerns, drawing comparisons to the less satisfying combat experience in GTA 5 and the intrusive screen shake in Red Dead Redemption 2. However, after reviewing an 'extended look video' and additional gameplay information, their anxiety has reportedly transformed into excitement.

Several key details have fueled this positive outlook. The user is particularly enthusiastic about the information suggesting no limit on handgun firing speed and the absence of RDR2's screen shake, which they found annoying. The ability to customize the default movement speed from walking to jogging is also highlighted as a welcome improvement. Furthermore, the 'extended play video' showcased diverse enemy reactions based on shot body parts, a feature reminiscent of RDR1 and Mafia 3, which the user found fluid and enjoyable. The presence of various environmental changes, such as broken glass and destroyed walls, upon shooting is also noted as crucial for immersion. While acknowledging that combat is just one aspect of the game, the user emphasizes that gunplay is their most anticipated feature for GTA 6.

## Key Takeaways
- User initially concerned about GTA 6 gunplay, citing GTA 5's combat as underwhelming and RDR2's screen shake as annoying.
- Excitement grew after viewing an 'extended look video' and reviewing 'additional gameplay information'.
- Anticipated gunplay improvements include unlimited handgun firing speed and the removal of RDR2-style screen shake.
- The ability to change default movement speed from walking to jogging is a welcome customizable option.
- Expectations for diverse enemy reactions based on shot body parts and detailed environmental destruction (e.g., broken glass, destroyed walls) are high.
