# Is GTA 6 Too Easy? Rockstar Weighs In on Difficulty Balance

Grand Theft Auto 6 has launched to massive success, but player discussion has quickly turned to its difficulty. Some players are finding the game too easy, prompting Rockstar boss Rob Nelson to weigh in on the design philosophy. In an interview with Destructoid, Nelson revealed that Rockstar's internal data suggested GTA 4 was "a little too punishing in places" while GTA 5 was "perhaps too easy." For GTA 6, the development team aimed to "land somewhere in the middle" to strike a better balance.

Consistent with franchise tradition, GTA 6 does not feature defined difficulty levels. However, players have options to customize their experience, such as adjusting aiming controls. Additionally, the game includes a suite of cheat codes that can either simplify or intensify the gameplay.

Design producer Scott Mitchell elaborated on the game's fail states, stating they were crafted to feel "appropriate." He emphasized the goal of maintaining player freedom and the use of mechanics like stealth, without sacrificing the "cinematic, high production value, explosive sequences" that are a hallmark of Rockstar titles. Mitchell believes they have achieved a good balance in this regard.

Nelson also acknowledged the inherent challenge in designing difficulty, especially for developers who are intimately familiar with every aspect of the game. He noted the process of "unpicking some of that to create an experience most players will be able to enjoy, rather than endure." To cater to players seeking a greater challenge, Rockstar has incorporated more difficult open-world content, such as compounds and fronts.

GTA 6's launch on November 19 was record-breaking, selling 32.5 million copies and generating an estimated $4 billion on its first day.
