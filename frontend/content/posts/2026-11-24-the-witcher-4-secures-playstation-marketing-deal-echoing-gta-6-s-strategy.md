# The Witcher 4 Secures PlayStation Marketing Deal, Echoing GTA 6's Strategy

CD Projekt Red has officially announced a new strategic partnership with Sony Interactive Entertainment, primarily focusing on the upcoming marketing campaign for **The Witcher 4**. The deal, confirmed by CFO Piotr Nielubowicz, encompasses CD Projekt Red's games on PlayStation platforms and within the PlayStation Plus catalog, aiming to bolster the studio's business and financial outlook for current and future years.

This collaboration has immediately drawn comparisons to Rockstar Games' co-marketing agreement with Sony for **Grand Theft Auto 6**. Under that previous deal, GTA 6 was heavily promoted with a "plays best on PS5" tagline, alongside exclusive PlayStation-branded merchandise like special DualSense controllers and custom consoles for high-profile individuals. The strategy aimed to give consumers the impression that PlayStation was the preferred platform for the highly anticipated title.

While specific details of The Witcher 4's marketing campaign are yet to be revealed, the gaming community is theorizing a similar PlayStation-centric approach. The Witcher 4 is currently in development and is **targeting a 2028 release** for both console and PC. This game will mark the beginning of a brand-new Witcher trilogy, with The Witcher 5 and The Witcher 6 expected to follow within a six-year window, potentially completing the entire new saga by 2034. It's noted that the 2028 release target, like previous CD Projekt Red titles such as The Witcher 3 and Cyberpunk 2077, could be subject to delays.
