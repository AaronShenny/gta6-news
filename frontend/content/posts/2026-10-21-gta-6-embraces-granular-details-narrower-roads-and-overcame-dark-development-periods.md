# GTA 6 Embraces Granular Details, Narrower Roads, and Overcame 'Dark' Development Periods

Veteran gaming reporter Jason Schreier recently unveiled a trove of new details about Rockstar's highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6*, gleaned from his interviews with the development team. The upcoming crime epic is set to feature an unprecedented level of granular detail, mirroring the dynamic environmental interactions seen in *Red Dead Redemption II*, such as the infamous horse testicle physics. This commitment to detail extends to the game's world design.According to Rockstar's Aaron Garbut, *GTA 6* will feature 'significantly' narrower roads than its predecessor, *GTA 5*. This design choice was intentional from the outset, aiming to create denser traffic scenarios and a more impactful urban environment, which in turn necessitated the creation of more buildings and elements.Schreier also shed light on a challenging period for Rockstar between 2019 and 2022, dubbed 'dark' years. This era was marked by the dual pressures of the COVID-19 pandemic and intense internal debates regarding the game's balance between realism and the series' signature arcade-style fun. During this time, discussions revolved around evolving the weapon system, with Rockstar's Rob Nelson revealing that the studio spent 'months if not years' exploring more realistic weapon limitations and alternatives to the traditional weapon wheel, ultimately deciding to retain the familiar setup.A fascinating insight into Rockstar's development philosophy is the emphasis on individual developer autonomy. Garbut noted that 'no one person has this whole thing in their head,' implying that *GTA 6* will be replete with small, unlisted details added independently by various team members, much like the unmandated horse testicle physics in *RDR2*.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 will feature a high level of granular, dynamic details akin to Red Dead Redemption II's environmental realism.
- Roads in GTA 6 are intentionally narrower than GTA 5, designed to create denser traffic and more immersive urban environments.
- Rockstar faced 'dark' development years (2019-2022) marked by challenges balancing realism with arcade gameplay and the impact of COVID-19.
- The studio experimented extensively with a more realistic weapon system, including limiting weapon slots, but ultimately decided to keep the traditional weapon wheel.
- Individual developers have significant autonomy to add small, unscripted details to the game world.
