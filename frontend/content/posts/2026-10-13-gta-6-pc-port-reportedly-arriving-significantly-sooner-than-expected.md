# GTA 6 PC Port Reportedly Arriving Significantly Sooner Than Expected

A new report suggests that PC gamers might not have to wait as long for Grand Theft Auto 6 as they did for previous Rockstar titles. According to industry sources speaking to GamesBeat, GTA 6 is slated to launch on PC "within the first three months" of 2027, following its console debut in November (presumably 2026).This timeline marks a significant shift from Rockstar's historical release patterns, where PC ports like GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 arrived 18 and 11 months, respectively, after their initial console releases.Take-Two Interactive boss Strauss Zelnick previously highlighted the growing importance of PC as a platform for the company, citing dramatic increases in PC game sales.Former Rockstar producer John Ricchio offered insight into the console-first strategy, explaining that developing for limited console hardware first allows for easier expansion to more powerful PC systems, rather than scaling down.It's important to note that Rockstar Games has not officially confirmed a PC version or release date for GTA 6, with only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S currently listed as platforms. Additionally, Xbox has clarified that GTA 6 will not be available for streaming to PC via Xbox Cloud Gaming, despite earlier reports.
