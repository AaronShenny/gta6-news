# Unlock Exclusive GTA 6 Twitch Rewards: Your Guide to Getting All the Drops!

Rockstar Games and Twitch are teaming up for an exciting Grand Theft Auto 6 promotion, offering exclusive Twitch-centric cosmetic rewards for viewers ahead of the game's release. This event, running from **November 18-30**, allows fans to accumulate unique items by simply engaging with GTA 6 content on the streaming platform.

To participate, viewers must tune into any Twitch Drop-enabled livestream during the promotional period. By watching for a minimum of 60 minutes on a given day, you can earn a chain badge from the GTA DVD Chest. Watching on six separate days will unlock all six unique chain badges: Purple, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, and the coveted Miami Chain.

Beyond just watching, supporting participating streamers also grants rewards. Each new or gifted subscription to an eligible GTA 6 streamer will unlock a special GTA-themed sub badge and an exclusive emote. To collect all three sub badges ("Welcome 2 Miami," "Scored Big," "Bleed Miami") and three emotes ("BustedPlz," "DinoBusted," "SabaBusted"), you'll need to make three such subscriptions. It's important to note that Prime subscriptions do not count towards these particular rewards.

For the most dedicated fans, collecting all 12 preceding rewards (six chain badges and six subscription items) will unlock the ultimate prize: the **Golden Iguana chat badge**.

While these drops are exclusive to Twitch and are not in-game items for GTA 6, there's another major incentive: Twitch will also be giving away free codes for the **GTA 6: Ultimate Edition**. This giveaway starts on November 19 and runs through November 30, with specific details on how to receive a code to be announced soon.

Finding eligible streamers is straightforward: simply use the "Find Streams" option on Twitch or search for "GTA 6" and filter by the "Drops Enabled" tag. Participating channels will clearly display a "Drop enabled" message in their chat.

## FAQ
**What are GTA 6 Twitch Drops?**
GTA 6 Twitch Drops are a promotional event offering exclusive cosmetic rewards for Twitch users, such as chat badges and emotes, by watching or subscribing to participating streamers.

**When do the GTA 6 Twitch Drops take place?**
The main Twitch Drops promotion runs from November 18 to November 30.

**How do I earn the daily chain badges?**
You can earn daily chain badges by watching any "Drops Enabled" GTA 6 livestream for a minimum of 60 minutes on a given day. There are six unique badges, earned over six days.

**Do Prime subscriptions count towards earning the subscription drops?**
No, only new or gifted subscriptions to participating GTA 6 streamers count towards unlocking the exclusive sub badges and emotes.

**How do I find streamers participating in the Twitch Drops?**
You can find eligible streamers by using the "Find Streams" option on Twitch or by searching for "GTA 6" and applying the "Drops Enabled" search tag.
