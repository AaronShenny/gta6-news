# GTA 6's Launch Fails to Dent Hunger Games Box Office Success

The highly anticipated new Hunger Games movie, "Sunrise on the Reaping," has delivered a colossal opening weekend, far surpassing initial expectations. The film, which premiered just one day after the monumental release of Grand Theft Auto 6, defied theories that the gaming giant would significantly limit its box office reach.

"Sunrise on the Reaping" raked in an impressive $120 million domestically and an additional $105 million from international markets, totaling a staggering $225 million globally in its first three days. This performance makes it Lionsgate's biggest post-COVID movie and its first film to open to over $100 million since "The Hunger Games: Mockingjay: Part 2" in 2015. It also stands as only the second prequel film ever to achieve a domestic opening of $100 million or more, following 2016's "Rogue One: A Star Wars Story."

Director Francis Lawrence's optimistic sentiment, "Hopefully, people find time for both," proved accurate, as audiences clearly managed to engage with both the new blockbuster game and the cinematic event. This success marks a significant improvement over the previous prequel, "The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes," which debuted with $44.6 million in North America.

## Key Takeaways
- "Sunrise on the Reaping" exceeded box office expectations, grossing $225 million globally in its opening weekend.
