# Community Pleads: No Unrealistic Vehicles in GTA 6 Online

A passionate plea from Reddit user 'rottenapple213' has resonated within the GTA 6 community, urging Rockstar Games to refrain from incorporating unrealistic vehicles such as flying bikes and cars into the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 and its online counterpart. The user argues that such additions, which have been a point of contention in previous GTA Online iterations, would be 'stupid' and could significantly deter a large portion of the player base, including themselves, from engaging with GTA 6 Online. This sentiment reflects a broader desire among some fans for GTA 6 to maintain a more grounded and realistic approach to its vehicle mechanics, contrasting with some of the more outlandish additions seen in the current GTA Online.

## Key Takeaways
- A Reddit user advocates against the inclusion of flying bikes, cars, and other unrealistic vehicles in GTA 6.
- The user believes such additions would be 'stupid' and detrimental to the game's experience.
- Concerns are raised that many players would stop playing GTA 6 Online if unrealistic vehicles are present.
