# GTA 6 Should Start in Snowy Liberty City, Fans Suggest

A compelling fan theory circulating on Reddit proposes that Grand Theft Auto 6 should kick off with a snowy prologue set in Liberty City. This suggestion aims to mirror the opening of GTA 5, which also featured a wintery introduction. The theory gains traction from the understanding that one of GTA 6's confirmed protagonists, Lucia, originates from Liberty City. Such a prologue would offer players a glimpse into Lucia's past, detailing the events that led to her eventual relocation to Leonida. Even a small, modern slice of a Liberty City borough, potentially Bohan or Broker, is envisioned as a way to provide a nostalgic yet fresh experience before the main narrative unfolds in the new setting.

## Key Takeaways
- The article suggests a snowy Liberty City prologue for GTA 6.
- This would continue a tradition established by GTA 5's snowy opening.
- The prologue could explain protagonist Lucia's origins and her journey to Leonida.
- It offers a chance to see a modern take on Liberty City, even if a small section.
- Specific boroughs like Bohan (Bronx) or Broker (Brooklyn) are mentioned as possibilities.
