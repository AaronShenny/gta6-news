# "The Witcher 4" Aims for "GTA 6" Level Leap in Quality, Says CD Projekt Red

CD Projekt Red (CDPR) has ambitious plans for `The Witcher 4`, aiming to achieve a quality leap comparable to the anticipated generational jump between `GTA 5` and `GTA 6`. During a recent Q3 earnings call, CDPR joint-CEO Michał Nowakowski stated that the studio is keenly inspired by Rockstar Games' technological and gameplay advancements seen in `GTA 6`. He expressed confidence that `The Witcher 4` will be "significantly better looking and improved in terms of pretty much every possible facet of the game" compared to its highly acclaimed predecessor, `The Witcher 3`. 

`The Witcher 3: The Wild Hunt`, released in 2015, was a groundbreaking title that set new standards for open-world adventure games, influencing numerous subsequent titles such as the `Assassin's Creed` trilogy (`Origins`, `Odyssey`, `Valhalla`), `Horizon Zero Dawn`, and `Ghost of Tsushima`. Now, with `GTA 6` poised to redefine the open-world genre once again, CDPR is committed to building upon `The Witcher 3`'s strengths while introducing more nuanced gameplay mechanics.

Beyond technological improvements, CDPR also aims to make `The Witcher 4` more appealing to a mainstream audience, although specific strategies for this remain under wraps. Nowakowski emphasized that achieving "mass market awareness" is a crucial objective for the game's future marketing campaigns.

Development on `The Witcher 4` is progressing, with over 500 people currently working on the project. CDPR has also secured a marketing agreement with PlayStation for the game, which is currently targeting a 2028 release. The high bar set by `GTA 6` has even led to lighthearted remarks from developers at CDPR, with the creative director of `Cyberpunk 2` jokingly suggesting a cancellation in response to Rockstar's impressive showing.
