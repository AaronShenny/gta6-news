# GTA 6 Sky Speculation: Will Rockstar Elevate Night Skies Beyond Red Dead Redemption 2?

A Reddit user, inspired by Red Dead Redemption 2's visually stunning and dynamic sky, has opened a discussion on what Grand Theft Auto 6's nocturnal and daytime skies might offer. They praised RDR2's sky for its brightness, beautiful clouds, and gorgeous nighttime transitions. The user then posed questions about GTA 6, specifically wondering if it might feature enhanced celestial events like meteorite showers. They also highlighted the importance of environmental details such as light pollution, which necessitates driving to mountainous regions to fully appreciate the night sky – a feature already present in RDR2 and hoped for in GTA 6. The core of the discussion revolves around how Rockstar will push the boundaries of skybox aesthetics and atmospheric phenomena in their upcoming title.

## Key Takeaways
- A Reddit user expressed deep admiration for Red Dead Redemption 2's visually impressive sky, noting its brightness, beautiful clouds, and stunning nighttime appearance.
- Speculation has begun regarding how Grand Theft Auto 6's sky might surpass RDR2's, particularly concerning nighttime visuals.
- Ideas for GTA 6's sky include the possibility of celestial events like meteorite showers.
- The importance of realistic environmental details such as light pollution, which requires players to venture to rural areas for optimal night sky viewing (a feature present in RDR2), is also a point of discussion for GTA 6.

## FAQ
**What aspects of Red Dead Redemption 2's sky were praised?**
The sky in Red Dead Redemption 2 was praised for its brightness, beautiful clouds, and gorgeous appearance as it transitions into night.

**What specific features are users hoping for in GTA 6's sky?**
Users are hoping for an even more amazing sky in GTA 6, especially during nighttime, with suggestions including meteorite showers and realistic light pollution effects that necessitate driving to mountains to experience the full grandeur of the night sky.

**Was light pollution simulated in Red Dead Redemption 2?**
Yes, Red Dead Redemption 2 already implemented light pollution, requiring players to drive to mountainous areas to fully appreciate the night sky, a detail users hope to see continued and enhanced in GTA 6.
