# Take-Two CEO’s Response To Second GTA 6 Delay: “Please Tell Me You’re Kidding”

Rockstar's highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6* has experienced two internal delays, pushing its release from an original 2025 target to its current November 19 launch date. Jennifer Kolbe, head of publishing, revealed to Bloomberg that when she informed Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick of the second delay to November 2026 (from an earlier May 2026 adjustment), his initial response was, "You’re kidding. Please tell me you’re kidding." However, after discussing the necessity to achieve Rockstar's full ambition for the game, Zelnick understood and approved the delay.

Kolbe expressed gratitude for Zelnick's reasonableness, noting that other executives might force a release, which often leads to subpar games. Zelnick himself stated that delaying a game is typically a "wise investment" if it leads to "the level of perfection it has the ability to reach." While development head Phil Hooker admitted that GTA 6 is "never completely settled" and changes have occurred even recently, no further delays are currently anticipated. Take-Two is expected to reiterate the November 19 release date during its upcoming earnings report on November 10. Physical copies, which contain a code rather than a disc, will be available on November 12 but will not unlock until the official release date.
