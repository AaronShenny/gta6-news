# GTA 6 Collector's Edition Rumored to be Rockstar's Most Expensive Yet at $400

A recent comparison circulating on Reddit has ignited discussions regarding the potential pricing and contents of the Grand Theft Auto VI Collector's Edition. Based on a speculative leak, the GTA 6 Collector's Edition is rumored to be the most expensive Rockstar has ever offered, with an estimated price tag of $400. This hefty price is notably higher than its predecessors; the Grand Theft Auto V Collector's Edition retailed for $150 (which included the game and 6 items), and the Red Dead Redemption 2 Collector's Edition was $100 (excluding the game, with 9 items). The rumored GTA 6 bundle is said to include 11 items, but, significantly, the game itself is *not* included. A primary contributor to the rumored $400 price is a pair of Oakley sunglasses, which alone are speculated to account for over half the bundle's total cost.

## Key Takeaways
- The rumored GTA 6 Collector's Edition could cost $400, making it Rockstar's most expensive to date.
- Unlike GTA 5's edition, the rumored GTA 6 bundle does not include the game itself.
- The high price is largely attributed to a pair of Oakley sunglasses, reportedly costing over half the bundle's value.
- For comparison, the GTA 5 Collector's Edition was $150 (game included, 6 items).
- The Red Dead Redemption 2 Collector's Edition was $100 (game not included, 9 items).
