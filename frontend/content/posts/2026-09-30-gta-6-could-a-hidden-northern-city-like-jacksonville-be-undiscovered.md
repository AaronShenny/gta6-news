# GTA 6: Could a Hidden Northern City Like Jacksonville Be Undiscovered?

Recent discussions among the Grand Theft Auto 6 community suggest that Rockstar Games may be keeping a significant portion of the game's northern map under wraps. The speculation centers on the possibility of a hidden city or substantial unrevealed area, akin to how New Austin was handled in Red Dead Redemption 2.

The primary catalyst for this theory stems from a quoted article (likely from Game Informer or a similar publication) which explicitly mentions **Jacksonville** as an inspiration for Mount Kalaga and the surrounding 'Florida-Georgia line' culture in Leonida. Jacksonville stands out as Florida's most populous city and one of the largest by landmass, located in the northeastern part of the state. This area of Leonida is notably one about which the mapping community has the least amount of leaked or revealed information.

The northern region, inspired by locations like Panama City, Jacksonville, and Ocala, is described as featuring diverse environments:
*   Mount Kalaga National Park on Leonida's northern border.
*   Communities centered around private jet ownership with personal landing strips.
*   ATV trails, mud parks, mountain bike trails.
*   Livestock and agriculture farms, high-end ranches, and luxury golf courses.
*   Blackrock and boneyard-inspired beaches, and Spanish moss-draped forests.

While there's no concrete confirmation, the specific mention of Jacksonville combined with the historical precedent of Rockstar holding back map sections (like New Austin's initial inaccessibility in RDR2) fuels the intriguing possibility that players might discover more to Leonida's north than initially revealed.
