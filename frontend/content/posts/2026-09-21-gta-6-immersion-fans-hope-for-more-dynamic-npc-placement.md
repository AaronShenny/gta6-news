# GTA 6 Immersion: Fans Hope for More Dynamic NPC Placement

A passionate Grand Theft Auto 6 fan has sparked discussion regarding potential enhancements to game immersion, specifically advocating for a more three-dimensional distribution of Non-Player Characters (NPCs). The user expresses a strong desire to see NPCs occupying elevated positions such as balconies, rooftops, and even looking out of windows, arguing that such placement makes the game world feel significantly more alive and immersive. They highlight how Rockstar's own *Red Dead Redemption 2* successfully implemented this in areas like Saint Denis and Valentine. The fan further notes that they observed NPCs on a balcony in the extended GTA 6 trailer, though these were relatively close to the ground, and encourages others to share any similar observations or screenshots.

## Key Takeaways
- Fans desire enhanced immersion in GTA 6 through dynamic NPC placement.
- Specifically, a call for NPCs to occupy elevated positions like balconies and rooftops.
- This "three-dimensional" distribution is believed to make the game world more immersive.
- *Red Dead Redemption 2* is cited as an example where this was partially implemented.
- A fan claims to have spotted NPCs on a low-level balcony in the GTA 6 extended trailer.
