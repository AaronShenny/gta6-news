# Buying GTA 6 Could Save You $5,000, Or At Least This Guy Thinks So

Self-made millionaire Mark Tilbury has offered a humorous take on how purchasing *Grand Theft Auto 6* could potentially 'save' players $5,000. While clearly a cheeky comparison intended for virality, his argument centers on the game's entertainment value versus the cost of a typical night out.Tilbury's calculation begins with a hypothetical $100 Ultimate Edition of *GTA 6* and an estimated 80 hours to complete the main story, equating to $1.25 per hour of fun. Factoring in side missions and open-world exploration, this cost per hour drops to an even more attractive 50 cents.He postulates that playing *GTA 6* for four hours every evening could provide 50 nights of entertainment. Comparing this to easily spending $100 on a single night out, Tilbury concludes that buying the game effectively 'saves' players $5,000 (50 nights x $100/night).The article acknowledges that while staying home to play a game *can* save money compared to other activities, Tilbury's math is largely for fun and justifying extensive playtime. It also points out that players are likely to spend far more than 50 days immersed in *GTA 6*, especially with *GTA Online 2.0* anticipated for 2027.*GTA 6* is set to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a PC release expected in 2027.

## Key Takeaways
