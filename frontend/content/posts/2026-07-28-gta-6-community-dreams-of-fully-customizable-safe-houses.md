# GTA 6 Community Dreams of Fully Customizable Safe Houses

A recent discussion on the r/GTA6 subreddit, initiated by user u/ajoota98, highlights a popular community desire for a comprehensive housing customization system in the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6. The user suggests that GTA 6 safe houses could benefit from a similar level of player agency seen in modern titles like Hogwarts Legacy and World of Warcraft, where players can build, craft, and extensively customize their living spaces, even altering structural elements.The core argument is that if players can already purchase, customize, and utilize vehicles within the GTA universe, a similar system could be seamlessly integrated for safe houses. This would allow players to buy or even 'acquire' furniture, arrange items freely, and potentially modify the interior layout of their properties, enhancing immersion and player expression. The sentiment reflects a broader trend in gaming where players appreciate deep personalization options, extending the gameplay beyond missions into personal spaces.
