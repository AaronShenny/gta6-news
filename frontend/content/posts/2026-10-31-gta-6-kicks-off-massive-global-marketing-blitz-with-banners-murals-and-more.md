# GTA 6 Kicks Off Massive Global Marketing Blitz with Banners, Murals, and More

Rockstar Games has initiated a significant global marketing campaign for Grand Theft Auto 6, with various promotional materials appearing across major international cities. This expansive rollout suggests the game is moving closer to its release or a major information drop. Key locations where marketing has been spotted include:

*   **Cologne, Germany**: A giant banner prominently displayed.
*   **Times Square, New York**: A new full display has taken over a prominent spot.
*   **Tokyo, Japan**: Multiple banners have been seen throughout the city.
*   **Stade de France, Paris**: Another giant banner has been erected at the iconic stadium.
*   **Melbourne, Australia**: A large mural is currently being painted, indicating a significant artistic promotion.
*   **Hong Kong**: Trams in the city are featuring artwork of Jason, one of the game's protagonists, adding a unique mobile advertising element.

This widespread marketing push, covering Europe, North America, Asia, and Oceania, highlights the global anticipation for GTA 6 and Rockstar's commitment to a massive launch.
