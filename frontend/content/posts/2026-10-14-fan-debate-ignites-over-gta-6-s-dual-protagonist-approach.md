# Fan Debate Ignites Over GTA 6's Dual Protagonist Approach

A recent post on the r/GTA6 subreddit has ignited discussion among fans regarding the optimal number of protagonists for the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6. User 'RobloxSpiderGoku' expressed a strong preference for the rumored dual protagonist setup featuring Jason and Lucia, suggesting it would be a significant improvement over Grand Theft Auto V's three-character system. The user criticized GTA V's approach, specifically highlighting Franklin's character as 'undeveloped' and 'boring,' coupled with 'forgettable antagonists.' In contrast, there's a belief that Rockstar Games will invest considerable development time into Jason and Lucia, aiming to create deeply engaging and beloved characters, drawing parallels to iconic figures like Niko Bellic, Carl 'CJ' Johnson, and Arthur Morgan. The sentiment concludes that a two-protagonist model is 'perfectly fine' and potentially superior for fostering a more focused and impactful narrative experience.
