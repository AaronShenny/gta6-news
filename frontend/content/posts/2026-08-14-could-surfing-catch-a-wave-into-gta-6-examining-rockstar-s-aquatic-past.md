# Could Surfing Catch a Wave into GTA 6? Examining Rockstar's Aquatic Past

A recent fan theory posted on Reddit's r/GTA6 subreddit speculates on the potential inclusion of surfing mechanics in the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6. The theory, titled "Skitzo or i might be cooking? hear me out tho," draws parallels from Rockstar's development history, specifically mentioning two past surfing-related projects.

Firstly, the post highlights an obscure surfing game developed in Japan using the Opus game engine, which was reportedly unsuccessful. More significantly, the theory points to Angel Studios (which later became Rockstar San Diego) and their critically acclaimed title, *TransWorld Surf*. This game was developed on the AGE (Angel Game Engine), which the theorist notes as the earliest version of the now-ubiquitous RAGE (Rockstar Advanced Game Engine), the foundation for GTA 6 (reportedly RAGE 9).

The core argument is that since the fundamental mechanics for surfing were developed and praised within a precursor to the RAGE engine, and with the engine's continuous evolution, it's plausible that these mechanics could be refined and integrated into GTA 6. While acknowledging it's not a guarantee, the theory suggests a strong possibility, inviting community discussion on whether this is a stroke of genius or mere 'copium' for eager fans.
