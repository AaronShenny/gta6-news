# GTA 6 Pre-Purchases Soar to 7.7 Million, PS5 Dominates Sales Split

A recent report from video game research firm Sensor Tower reveals that *Grand Theft Auto 6* has already amassed an impressive **7.71 million pre-purchase units**, generating approximately **$751 million** in gross revenue. The data, while unofficial, highlights a significant lead for the PlayStation 5, which accounts for **5.74 million units (74%)** of sales, compared to **1.97 million units (26%)** on Xbox Series X|S. This platform split aligns with the current console market dominance of the PS5 over the Xbox Series X|S.

Both Sony and Microsoft benefit from these sales through their 30% platform fees. Sensor Tower further projects that *GTA 6* could reach **25 million total pre-purchases by launch**, potentially translating to 18.5 million on PS5 and 6.5 million on Xbox if the current split holds.

Beyond sales, market research firm Circana indicates an unprecedented **29% purchase intent** among US console and PC gamers, the highest ever measured for any game. While *GTA 6* is poised to be a colossal commercial success, potentially boosting hardware and accessory sales, experts caution it won't resolve the industry's deeper systemic issues.

The highly anticipated title is set to launch on **November 19** for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, featuring a massive **212GB file size** and preloading available more than a week prior to release. New Zealand will be among the first regions to play at midnight local time.
