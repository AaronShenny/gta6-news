# GTA 6 Looks Pretty Realistic, Until It Comes To Gym Etiquette

Grand Theft Auto 6 is poised to be a remarkably realistic experience, introducing elements like consequential actions, relationship depth, and the need for gym visits to build muscle. However, a recent batch of screenshots has led one gaming journalist to a rather specific, albeit humorous, critique: protagonist Jason's appalling gym etiquette.The article highlights a particular image of Jason lifting weights shirtless and seemingly without a sweat towel. The author, drawing on the inspiration of Vice City from hot Miami, expresses exaggerated disgust at the thought of the unhygienic state of the gym equipment after Jason's workout. They lament the lack of proper gym attire and the inevitable "rank puddle of perspiration," even speculating if it's a home gym, which still wouldn't excuse the behavior.While acknowledging the absurdity of obsessing over such a minor detail when Rockstar Games is known for its meticulous attention to grander details in GTA 6, the journalist playfully asserts their right to scrutinize, especially when considering a potential $80 purchase. The piece concludes with a humorous declaration that while 'weaponized dog poop' was acceptable, 'bareback bench presses are where I draw the line,' emphasizing the unexpected boundary for escapism in the highly anticipated game.
