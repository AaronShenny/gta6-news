# GTA 6 Developers on the Brink of Historic Union Vote as Release Nears

Rockstar North, the UK-based lead developer behind the highly anticipated GTA 6, is at the center of a groundbreaking unionization effort. Workers will vote between November 12-26 on recognizing the IWGB Union at Rockstar, which, if passed, would become the largest video game union in the UK. This vote coincides with the planned release of GTA 6 on November 19, adding a unique layer of significance to the process.

The union aims to provide developers with a stronger collective voice on critical issues such as pay, bonuses, overtime policies, the integration of generative AI, work-from-home arrangements, and job security, particularly regarding layoffs. The unionization push is seen as a pivotal moment for the wider gaming industry, potentially inspiring similar movements given Rockstar North's high profile as the developer of what's expected to be one of the biggest games ever.

Concurrently, an employment tribunal is concluding regarding claims that Rockstar fired over 30 IWGB members in October 2025 for unionizing, a charge Rockstar denies, stating the dismissals were due to game leaks. Final arguments for this tribunal are set for November 17-18. Rockstar publishing boss Jennifer Kolbe has stated, "We go where the team goes. So if they want a union, they will have a union," indicating management's official stance.

Union representatives emphasize the historic nature of the vote, viewing it as an opportunity for developers' voices to be heard regarding their working conditions and future. This move reflects a broader, albeit slow, trend of increasing unionization within the global video game industry.
