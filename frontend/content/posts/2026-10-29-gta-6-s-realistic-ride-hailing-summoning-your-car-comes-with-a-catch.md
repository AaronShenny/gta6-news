# GTA 6's Realistic Ride-Hailing: Summoning Your Car Comes with a Catch

Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to revolutionize open-world car summoning, moving beyond instant vehicle spawns to a more grounded, immersive experience. Players can utilize the 'Scooter Bros.' smartphone app to have their personal vehicle delivered to their location. However, this convenience comes with a fee, and the car isn't teleported; a company employee drives it to you, then rides off on a compact scooter stored in the trunk. This system emphasizes strategic planning, as players won't be able to call for a quick getaway in the middle of a heist. Instead, foresight is key to ensuring a vehicle is available. While stealing cars remains an option, it introduces its own set of challenges, including potential confrontations with drivers, hidden trackers, and the possibility of low fuel. Speaking of fuel, Rockstar Games has confirmed that while fuel management is a feature, it's designed as a 'quick diversion' rather than a constant chore, with cars capable of running for extended periods before needing a top-up. These details underscore Rockstar's commitment to creating a deeply immersive entry in the series, integrating multiple realistic systems. Additional confirmed details ahead of the game's November 19 release for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S include an expansive landmass comparable to GTA 4, GTA 5, and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined, and a massive download size. The game also features a new system for interacting with NPCs, as highlighted in a recent South Park episode.

## FAQ
**How does car summoning work in GTA 6?**
Players can use the 'Scooter Bros.' app on their in-game smartphone to request their car. An NPC employee will deliver the vehicle for a fee, then leave on a scooter stored in the car's trunk.

**Can I call for a car during a robbery?**
No, the car-summoning feature requires strategic planning and cannot be used in the middle of active criminal activities or emergencies.

**What are the downsides to stealing cars in GTA 6?**
Stolen cars might have aggressive drivers who fight back, hidden trackers, or low fuel levels, adding complications.

**Will I constantly need to refuel my car in GTA 6?**
No, Rockstar Games describes fuel management as a 'quick diversion.' Cars can run for a long time before needing to be refueled.

**When is GTA 6 expected to be released?**
The article states a release date of November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
