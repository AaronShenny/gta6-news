# GTA 6 Features a Hilarious Literal Take on Easter Eggs, Plus Bigfoot's Return

Rockstar's highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 is now available (through early access methods), and players are already unearthing its many secrets within the massive open world. One particularly amusing discovery is the game's literal interpretation of an "Easter egg." As documented by Twitter user xGarbett, players can climb a cell tower to find a bird's nest containing eggs painted like traditional Easter eggs. Protagonist Jason can then impressively throw these eggs, sending them hurtling towards unsuspecting NPCs below, such as a "scummy-sounding real estate agent or lawyer." The climb to this specific Easter egg reportedly takes about seven minutes, requiring some dedication.

Given the immense scale of GTA 6's world, it's expected that countless more secrets will be uncovered by players exploring Leonida. Another notable early discovery confirms the return of Bigfoot, who, according to reports, is quite malodorous.

GTA 6 is officially set to launch for all players on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Players looking for more information can consult the official GTA 6 guides hub for tips on navigating Vice City.
