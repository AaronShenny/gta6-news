# NBA 2K27 Trailer Strongly Hints at GTA 6 Crossover Event for Season 3

NBA 2K27's Season 1 trailer, released today, has sent the gaming community into a frenzy with clear allusions to the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6*. The trailer, which outlines content for upcoming seasons, prominently features styling for Season 3 that directly references *GTA 6*. This includes iconic palm trees, distinct fonts, familiar color schemes, and even the sound of police sirens, all hallmarks of the *GTA* franchise.

While Season 3's exact start date isn't specified, Season 2 is confirmed for October 16. Based on historical season lengths, Season 3 could commence around late November. This timeline remarkably coincides with *GTA 6*'s rumored release date of November 19, suggesting a deliberate synchronization by publisher Take-Two, which owns both Rockstar Games and 2K Sports.

This isn't the first time *GTA 6* and basketball have been linked; earlier leaks revealed *GTA 6* protagonist Jason dribbling a basketball, hinting at a potential basketball minigame within *GTA 6*. The potential crossover is seen as a strategic move by Take-Two to leverage the immense hype surrounding *GTA 6* to boost *NBA 2K27*. *NBA 2K27* launches into early access on August 26, with a general release on September 4.

## Key Takeaways
- NBA 2K27's Season 1 trailer prominently features GTA 6-themed imagery and sounds for its upcoming Season 3.
- Season 3's projected start in late November aligns with GTA 6's rumored November 19 release date.
