# GTA 6 Launch Trailer Unleashes Vice City, Reveals Lucia & Jason's Dynamic Duo

The highly anticipated launch trailer for Grand Theft Auto 6 has finally arrived, offering a spectacular glimpse into the game's world. After over a decade in development, Rockstar Games has confirmed a **November 19 release date** for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a PC version anticipated in early 2027.The trailer, captured on a base PS5, prominently features the iconic **Vice City**, showcasing its diverse populace, bustling locales, and pervasive criminal underworld. A key highlight is the intimate and "steamy scene" involving protagonists **Lucia and Jason** in a shower, underscoring their central partnership. Players will navigate their relationship, which can evolve from lovers to crime-committing friends.Musically, the trailer introduces "Flower in a Hurricane," a new track from Justice featuring Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. Justice is also responsible for the game's entire original score. The trailer's PlayStation branding and inclusion of a DualSense-like controller hint at a significant promotional collaboration between Sony and Rockstar.
