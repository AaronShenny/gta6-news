# A Gamer's Hope: GTA 6 as a Beacon for Returning to Gaming Amidst Mental Health Journey

The Reddit user u/Speed3Fan shared a deeply personal reflection on their year-long struggle with depression, which significantly impacted their ability to engage with video games. Despite purchasing new titles, they found themselves unable to sustain interest, often stopping after just a few minutes of gameplay. However, with the assistance of their doctor and ongoing medication adjustments, the user reports a gradual improvement in their mental health. A significant source of their renewed optimism is the upcoming release of Grand Theft Auto 6. They express immense excitement, viewing GTA 6 not just as another game, but as a potentially "life-changing experience" that could reignite their passion for gaming. The user also boasts an impressive setup, including a 77-inch Samsung S90F TV and a PS5 Pro, ready for the game's eventual launch, and extends wishes of good physical and mental health to the entire gaming community.
