# GTA Online made billions, but GTA VI proves Rockstar still puts single-player first

For years, the gaming community speculated that Rockstar Games might shift its focus away from single-player experiences, given the unprecedented financial success of *GTA Online* and the absence of story DLC for *Grand Theft Auto V*. Many feared that *Grand Theft Auto VI*'s campaign could merely serve as an onboarding experience for its online component.However, recent observations and details emerging about *GTA VI* strongly indicate a renewed dedication to its single-player narrative. The article highlights the significant development effort being poured into the protagonists, Jason and Lucia, as well as the intricate missions, expansive world, character interactions, and meticulous small details. This extensive investment in the core single-player elements suggests that *GTA VI* is being crafted primarily as a monumental story-driven experience, rather than an online game with an attached campaign. This commitment to single-player, even with the inevitable success of *GTA Online 2.0*, is being hailed as one of the most reassuring revelations about the upcoming title.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA Online generated billions in revenue, leading to concerns about Rockstar's single-player commitment.
- GTA V notably lacked story DLC, fueling fears for future single-player content.
- Initial speculation suggested GTA VI's campaign might only serve as an introduction to its online mode.
- Current observations indicate significant development focus on GTA VI's single-player elements, including protagonists Jason and Lucia, missions, world details, and interactions.
- This suggests Rockstar still highly values and prioritizes the single-player experience.
- GTA VI is perceived as a "massive Rockstar story first," not an online game with a campaign.
