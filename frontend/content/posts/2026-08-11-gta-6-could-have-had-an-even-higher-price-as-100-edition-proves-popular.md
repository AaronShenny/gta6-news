# GTA 6 Could Have Had An Even Higher Price, As $100 Edition Proves Popular

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has stated that despite having significant "pricing power" due to monumental demand for Grand Theft Auto 6, the company chose not to charge a higher price. Their strategy was to deliver "way more value to the consumer than what we charged" and ensure the game's price point remained accessible.

The standard edition of GTA 6 is priced at $80, while a premium version featuring numerous digital extras costs $100. Interestingly, Zelnick noted that early preorders for GTA 6 are "skewing more toward" the $100 edition, attributing this to avid, less price-sensitive consumers who typically preorder games immediately.

Comparatively, GTA 5 launched at $60 in 2013, which would equate to over $85 in 2026 dollars due to inflation, making GTA 6's $80 base price seem reasonable. The article also touches on speculation that the accessible price point might encourage adoption, potentially funneling players into an anticipated GTA Online evolution.

Preorder bonuses for both editions include the '80s-inspired Vintage Vice City Pack, offering a classic car, special outfits and hairstyles, an exclusive weapon pattern, and a free month of GTA+. Zelnick also indicated that there would be no official price cuts for GTA 6 for some time, drawing a parallel to GTA 5's long-held pricing strategy. GTA 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a detailed reveal scheduled for August 27 via Netflix.
