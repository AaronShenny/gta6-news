# GTA 6 'Three Birds Theory' Suggests a Deeper, More Personal Narrative

A compelling new fan theory, dubbed the 'Three Birds Theory,' has emerged from the Grand Theft Auto 6 community, meticulously analyzing a seemingly innocuous detail in the official artwork. Reddit user /u/Idontfuckwityou1122 points out the presence of exactly three birds in the artwork's background, perched on power lines: two sitting closely together, and a third isolated further down the line. The theory posits that this isn't mere atmospheric dressing for Vice City but rather intentional symbolism representing the game's protagonists, Jason and Lucia, alongside a potential child.

This interpretation suggests a significant shift from the presumed Bonnie and Clyde-style criminal empire narrative. Instead, the theory speculates that if Jason and Lucia have a child who has been taken or is being used as leverage by a cartel or corrupt federal agents, the entire game's campaign could transform into a desperate rescue mission. This would reframe every high-stakes robbery and chaotic escape not as an act of ambition, but as a race against time by two parents striving to reunite their family.
