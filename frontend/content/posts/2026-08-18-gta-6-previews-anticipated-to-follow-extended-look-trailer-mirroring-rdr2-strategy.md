Gaming enthusiasts are speculating that Rockstar Games will soon release official previews for Grand Theft Auto 6, following a pattern observed during the marketing campaign for Red Dead Redemption 2. The theory suggests that much like RDR2's third trailer was accompanied by media previews, GTA 6's upcoming 'Extended Look' (which is technically its third major trailer) will trigger the release of detailed press and influencer previews. This would provide the first in-depth look at gameplay, story elements, and new features beyond what has been shown in cinematic trailers.
