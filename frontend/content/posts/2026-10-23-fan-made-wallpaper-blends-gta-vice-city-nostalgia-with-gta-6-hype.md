# Fan-Made Wallpaper Blends GTA Vice City Nostalgia with GTA 6 Hype

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto fan, known on Reddit as /u/OracleOverall-4264, has crafted a unique wallpaper that serves as a bridge between the classic era of Vice City and the highly anticipated GTA 6. Created in 2024 using GIMP, the artwork skillfully combines an early snapshot from the official GTA 6 trailer with a stylized rendition of Candy Suxxx, a memorable character from Vice City, whose art was inspired by DeadInside97. The creator expressed a hopeful sentiment, wishing to see fan art of Candy Suxxx reimagined in the distinct style of the 'GTA 6 trailer girl,' further fueling community discussions about potential character returns or stylistic homages in the upcoming game.
