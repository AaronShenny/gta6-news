# GTA 6 Pre-Release Iceberg Chart Surfaces, Challenging Fan Knowledge

A dedicated fan from the r/GTA6 subreddit, u/Individual_Type8874, has unveiled an extensive "GTA VI Pre-Release Iceberg" chart, capturing the vast ocean of speculation surrounding the highly anticipated title. Spanning entries from 2020 to 2026, the iceberg aims to categorize the myriad of rumors, leaks, and fan theories that have circulated prior to the game's official release. The creator challenges true enthusiasts to explain every entry, suggesting that only those deeply immersed in the game's pre-release lore can claim not to be a "larper." The ranking of items within the iceberg is intentionally structured to reflect how a casual observer might perceive them, with well-known concepts at the top and more obscure or controversial theories sinking to the depths. While the iceberg itself is a fan creation, it serves as a fascinating snapshot of the community's collective knowledge and the intense anticipation building around GTA 6.
