# GTA 6 Player Hopes for Removal of Rushed Gold Mission Ratings

A dedicated fan on the r/GTA6 subreddit has voiced a common sentiment among players regarding mission rating systems in Rockstar titles. User /u/tentandonaoserbanido specifically highlighted their dislike for the 'gold rating' mechanic, which they argue incentivizes players to rush through missions rather than experience them organically. The user pointed to Red Dead Redemption 2 as a prime example where this system clashed with the game's slower, more immersive pace. Their hope for Grand Theft Auto 6 is that it will move away from such performance-driven objectives, allowing players the freedom to engage with missions at their own pace and 'the right way,' without the pressure of achieving arbitrary speed or efficiency goals.
