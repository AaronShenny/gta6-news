# Take-Two CEO: "Let GTA 6 Cook First" Before Discussing GTA 7

Rockstar Games recently launched Grand Theft Auto 6 on November 19 to widespread critical acclaim and massive sales. Amidst the excitement, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick was pressed on the future of the franchise, specifically about GTA 7, during an appearance on OpenAI's TBPN show.

Zelnick humorously rebuffed the premature inquiries, stating, "Let me check my watch. Oh, it's still November 19. And you're asking a question about GTA 7." He underscored the importance of focusing on GTA 6's immediate success.

Despite the current focus on GTA 6, Take-Two has previously indicated that future GTA titles might see a quicker release cycle. Zelnick mentioned that the "nature and velocity" of content releases for major franchises, including GTA, will look "dramatically different over the next decade." This comes after a 13-year gap between GTA 5 and GTA 6, with GTA 6's serious development only commencing after Red Dead Redemption II's release in 2018 due to Rockstar's collaborative, one-project-at-a-time studio model.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 launched on November 19 to critical and commercial success.
- Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick stated it's too early to discuss GTA 7, given GTA 6's recent release.
- Zelnick hinted that future GTA game releases could happen more frequently.
- The long gap between GTA 5 and GTA 6 was partly due to Rockstar's development model, focusing on one major project at a time.
- Development for GTA 6 began in earnest after Red Dead Redemption II's release in 2018.
