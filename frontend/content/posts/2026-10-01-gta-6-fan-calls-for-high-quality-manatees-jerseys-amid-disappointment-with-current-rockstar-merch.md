# GTA 6 Fan Calls for High-Quality Manatees Jerseys Amid Disappointment with Current Rockstar Merch

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto fan has voiced strong opinions regarding the current lineup of Rockstar Games merchandise, expressing considerable disappointment with the perceived quality and design of several items. The user, posting on Reddit, specifically critiqued bags for looking "cheaper looking than expected," a "boring" money clip, and a Zippo lighter featuring only a basic R* logo and "Vice City" without unique engraving, unlike previous RDR2 merchandise. Hats also fell short of expectations. However, the user did praise a football item and passionately called for Rockstar to release official NFL-quality Manatees jerseys, suggesting both home, away, and special editions, stating they would be an instant purchase. This feedback highlights a desire among fans for premium, well-designed merchandise that reflects the quality of Rockstar's games.

## Key Takeaways
- A fan expresses significant disappointment with the perceived quality and design of current Rockstar Games merchandise.
- Specific items criticized include bags, money clips, a Zippo lighter, and hats, citing a lack of uniqueness and cheap appearance.
- The fan contrasts the current Zippo with the RDR2 Zippo, which had a "cool unique graving."
- A football item was the only piece of merchandise the fan liked.
- The fan strongly advocates for Rockstar to release high-quality, NFL-quality Manatees jerseys, stating they would purchase them instantly.
- This feedback suggests a demand for more premium and thoughtfully designed merchandise from Rockstar Games.
