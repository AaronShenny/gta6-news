# Cyberleek's GTA VI Leaks: Unpacking the Man, the Motives, and the Massive Build Details

The article provides an in-depth look into "Cyberleek," the individual behind significant Grand Theft Auto VI leaks, detailing both his activities and the revealed game build. Cyberleek reportedly invested $29,000 in setting up websites and a cryptocurrency, from which he has generated $50,000 in transaction fees and holds an estimated $390,000 in unsold coins. He frames his coin as a "donation" mechanism, not an investment, and uses Telegram and his website to disseminate information and host leaked materials.

Operating under the pseudonym "we," Cyberleek claims to be raising funds to "build infrastructure" for future leaks against other companies. His stated demands from Rockstar include a physical disk release or the inclusion of Ultimate Edition content in the base game, threatening further leaks if his conditions aren't met. To date, he has released five gameplay videos showcasing various aspects like basketball, taser use, and plane travel, alongside three images, including an overview of the entire map and detailed shots of islands south of Vice City.

Concerns about Cyberleek's identity and legal vulnerability are highlighted, as he reportedly used a crypto vendor requiring government IDs, making him potentially traceable by authorities. The article speculates that he may have been apprehended, is taking a break, or is progressing through the game for more content, noting that his inability to liquidate his substantial crypto holdings without crashing the market makes a quick financial escape unlikely.

Regarding the leaked GTA VI build, a wealth of information has been observed:
*   **Locations:** Gameplay footage covers Leonida Keys, mainland Vice City, and Ambrosia, with the entire map appearing complete in a plane clip.
*   **Vehicles:** Access to planes, cars, motorcycles, and likely bicycles, helicopters, and potentially boats.
*   **Missions/Activities:** One cutscene for a "Cal" mission has been seen, along with side activities like basketball.
*   **Player & World Details:** Jason, one of the protagonists, has multiple outfits. The leaker has access to a full menu map and an NPC interaction menu. An in-game restaurant icon suggests broader open-world store access. Underwater swimming and diving mechanics are present.
*   **Timeline:** The build is dated after January 2025 due to a specific song ("Sports Car" by Tate McCrae) being featured.
*   **Authenticity:** A "leek" spray confirms the leaker's control over the footage, indicating it's not stolen.

However, several aspects have *not* been seen, leading to questions about the build's completeness and the leaker's full access:
*   No story missions beyond the single Cal one.
*   No gameplay featuring Lucia, the other protagonist.
*   Absence of robbery mechanics, special items (beyond basic weapons/heals), store purchase menus, or car customization.
*   Crucially, there's no definitive evidence that Cyberleek *currently* has access to this build, or that the build was ever external to a Rockstar office.

The article concludes that while extensive, the leaks don't confirm access to full story progression or all open-world mechanics. It cautions against definitive statements regarding the leaker's current access or the build's origin, suggesting that more clips would be needed for a clearer picture.
