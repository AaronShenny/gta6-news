## FAQ
**What sales figure does EA founder Bing Gordon consider a success for GTA 6?**
Bing Gordon believes it would be "mind-blowing" if GTA 6 reaches 75 million copies sold to be considered a success.

**How did Bing Gordon arrive at the 75 million sales target?**
He based this number on an estimated 750 million collective views for GTA 6 preview videos across the internet, suggesting that selling 10% of that figure would signify success.

**What unconventional success metric did Gordon propose for GTA 6?**
Gordon suggested that if people play GTA 6 instead of watching football on Thanksgiving Day, just after the game's release, that would be a sign of its success.

**What are the current sales and preorder projections for GTA 6?**
Sensor Tower data claims GTA 6 has already sold about 7 million copies, generating close to $700 million. They project 25 million preorders by launch, which could amount to about $2 billion in revenue.

**What does Circana's data indicate about player interest in GTA 6?**
Circana's latest data shows that nearly 1 in 3 console and PC gamers in the US plan to buy GTA 6, which is the highest purchase intent ever recorded.
