# GTA 6 Shatters Sales Records with 44.9 Million Copies Sold in Opening Weekend, Analyst Reports

A new report from Alinea Analytics indicates that Grand Theft Auto 6 has achieved monumental success, selling an estimated **44.9 million copies** through its first weekend. This figure significantly surpasses Rockstar owner Take-Two's previously announced **32.5 million pre-purchases** on day one.

The sales breakdown, according to Rhys Elliott of Alinea Analytics, shows **33.7 million copies on PS5** (75.1%) and **11.2 million on Xbox Series X|S** (24.9%). Financially, Take-Two initially reported **$3.79 billion in sales on day one**, which Alinea Analytics estimates grew to **$4 billion within the first four days**.

To put this into perspective, GTA 6's initial $4 billion revenue is roughly equivalent to the combined box office results of this year's top two movies, *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* ($2.5 billion) and *The Odyssey* ($1.8 billion). Both Sony and Microsoft benefit significantly, retaining 30% of digital revenue through platform fees.

Player engagement is also exceptionally high, with approximately **40.8 million people logging in to play on Sunday, November 22**, 76% of whom were on PS5. Furthermore, an estimated **9 million players (20% of the total)** have already dedicated more than 20 hours to the game.

The game's commercial trajectory is expected to continue its upward trend, with a **PC release anticipated in 2027** and the introduction of an **online mode, complete with microtransactions, also slated for 2027**, promising further revenue streams for Rockstar.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 reportedly sold 44.9 million copies in its first weekend, according to Alinea Analytics.
- The game's revenue reached an estimated $4 billion within its first four days, surpassing major movie blockbusters.
