# GTA 6 Story Won't Sprawl Across Entire Map, Fuels Speculation on Hidden Regions

Paul MacPherson, Rockstar's Senior VP of Environmental Art, recently stated, "We don’t have time to take you everywhere," regarding the main story of Grand Theft Auto VI. This indicates that the core narrative will not guide players through every single corner of the expansive map.

The original author of the article views this as a positive design choice, suggesting it will **encourage players to explore the vast open world independently**, rather than being solely directed by story missions. This approach also addresses past concerns about potentially 'empty' sections of the map, similar to parts of Blaine County in GTA V, with the author confident that Rockstar has learned from previous titles and will ensure **every area of GTA 6 is rich with content**.

Furthermore, this selective story progression has led to speculation that Rockstar is **intentionally withholding reveals of significant portions of the map**, particularly the western and northern regions beyond currently known locations like Port Gellhorn and Mount Kalaga, to create a sense of surprise for players at launch. Confirmed story locations mentioned include the Keys, Vice City, The Grassrivers Ambrosia, Port Gellhorn, and Mount Kalaga, with the community eagerly anticipating the game's release.
