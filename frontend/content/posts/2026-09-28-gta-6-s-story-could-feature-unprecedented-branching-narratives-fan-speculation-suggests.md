# GTA 6's Story Could Feature Unprecedented Branching Narratives, Fan Speculation Suggests

A Reddit user, u/PrinceOfTotalQuiet, has put forth a compelling theory regarding the potential for massive story variations in the upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI. Based on interpretations of unconfirmed prior information, the theory suggests that Rockstar Games is implementing an incredibly deep narrative branching system.

The core of the theory revolves around the two protagonists, Jason and Lucia. Each character is expected to have their own 'honor' system, allowing players to guide them towards 'good' or 'evil' paths. This alone would offer double the narrative alteration compared to titles like Red Dead Redemption 2, which featured a single protagonist's honor system.

Adding another layer of complexity, the theory posits that Jason and Lucia's relationship can evolve in four distinct ways: 'romantic cool,' 'romantic chaos,' 'professional cool,' and 'professional chaos.' The user believes these relationship dynamics, combined with each protagonist's individual honor choices, will significantly alter the main storyline.

Through a mathematical breakdown, the user calculates that if these various modifiers are indeed present, GTA VI's main story could boast an astonishing sixteen possible variants. This level of narrative depth, especially for a game speculated to have a ninety-hour main story, would be unprecedented for a Rockstar title, offering immense replayability and unique experiences for each playthrough. It's important to note that this remains fan speculation, built upon unconfirmed details.
