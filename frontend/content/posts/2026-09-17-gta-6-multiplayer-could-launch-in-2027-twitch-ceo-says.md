# GTA 6 Multiplayer Could Launch In 2027, Twitch CEO Says

Twitch CEO Dan Clancy has speculated that the multiplayer component for the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 could launch in 2027. In an interview with Bloomberg, Clancy stated that Twitch has spent "a lot of time talking with Rockstar about their plans" and anticipates an "even bigger" bump to Twitch viewership in 2027 when multiplayer is released. While Rockstar Games has officially described GTA 6 as a "single-player experience" and has not announced any multiplayer details, Clancy's comments are presented as more than mere speculation due to his company's engagement with Rockstar. This potential timeline suggests a longer gap between the single-player and multiplayer releases compared to GTA 5, which saw GTA Online debut just weeks after the main game's launch in 2013. GTA 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a PC edition expected to follow.
