# Rainbow Six Siege Dev Addresses GTA 6 Impact, Confirms No Plans for Siege 2

Ubisoft's Rainbow Six Siege Creative Director, Joshua Mills, has weighed in on the looming release of Rockstar's highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 in November 2026. Mills candidly admitted that all games, including Siege, are expected to "take a hit" in player numbers when GTA 6 drops, acknowledging that developers anticipate a temporary dip. However, he emphasized that this effect is generally short-lived and that Ubisoft's "golden rule" is to fiercely serve and reward the dedicated players who continue to engage with Siege.

To counter the impact, Ubisoft plans to "double down" on content, with Siege's Year 11, Season 4, scheduled to release around the same time as GTA 6, promising "unannounced surprises" to make a splash.

Mills also addressed the long-standing question of a Rainbow Six Siege 2, confirming that there are no plans for a full sequel. He explained that a complete rebuild of the game's entire system would be a "mountain of work," an immense undertaking in terms of cost and development effort. The complexity of running both the original Siege and a new sequel simultaneously, requiring the same expert team, makes it an unfeasible endeavor. Instead, Ubisoft opted for 'Siege X,' described as a new and improved version of the existing game, rather than a full-scale sequel. The Rainbow Six universe is, however, set to expand with 'Rainbow Six Siege Tactics,' a turn-based game, launching in 2027.

## Key Takeaways
- Rainbow Six Siege's Creative Director, Joshua Mills, expects a temporary dip in player numbers for all games, including Siege, when GTA 6 releases in November 2026.
- Ubisoft's strategy is to "double down" on content and reward loyal players who continue to play Siege during this period.
- Siege's Year 11, Season 4, will launch around GTA 6's release and include "unannounced surprises" to engage players.
- There are currently no plans for a full Rainbow Six Siege 2 due to the immense development work, cost, and logistical challenges of rebuilding the entire game and running two versions concurrently.
- Ubisoft developed 'Siege X' as an improved version of the current game, not a full sequel.
- The Rainbow Six franchise will expand further with 'Rainbow Six Siege Tactics,' a turn-based game, set to release in 2027.
