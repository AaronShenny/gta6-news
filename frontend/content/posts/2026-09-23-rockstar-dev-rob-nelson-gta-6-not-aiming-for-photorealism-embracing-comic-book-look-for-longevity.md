# Rockstar Dev Rob Nelson: GTA 6 Not Aiming for Photorealism, Embracing 'Comic-Book Look' for Longevity

According to a 2013 report from the French media outlet Trois Couleurs, Rockstar Games made a conscious decision not to pursue a purely photorealistic visual style for Grand Theft Auto 6. Rob Nelson, a key figure at Rockstar, stated that the game's visual style would instead feature a 'strong comic-book look.' This artistic choice was reportedly made to help the game world 'age well' over time and to prevent it from falling into the 'uncanny valley,' a phenomenon where overly realistic but imperfect human representations can evoke feelings of eeriness or revulsion. This historical insight offers a glimpse into the early artistic direction considerations for the highly anticipated title, emphasizing stylistic longevity over raw graphical fidelity.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6's visual style is reportedly not focused on photorealism.
- The game is intended to have a 'strong comic-book look'.
- This artistic decision aims to help the game world age gracefully.
- Avoiding the 'uncanny valley' was a key motivation for the visual direction.
- The statement was attributed to Rockstar's Rob Nelson in 2013.
