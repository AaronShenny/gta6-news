# GTA 6 Revamps Car Theft: Deeper Mechanics, Story Progression, and Stealth Opportunities Revealed

Grand Theft Auto VI is set to revolutionize its iconic car theft mechanics, introducing a layered system that goes beyond simple hotwiring. According to details from an 'Extended Look' video and IGN's preview, featuring insights from Rockstar's Rob Nelson, players will experience a more involved and strategic approach to acquiring vehicles.Older cars can be stolen by smashing a window or using a 'slim jim' tool, which involves a timing-based mini-game. However, not all parked cars will be immediately accessible. Newer, electronically locked vehicles will require advanced tools like a 'key cloner,' which players will acquire as they progress through the story. This means certain high-end vehicles will be gated until later in the game. Carjacking vehicles in traffic, however, remains an option from the outset.A new in-game phone app called 'WAINK' will provide crucial intelligence, allowing players to scan cars before theft. This app reveals if a car has a tracker, its potential worth at a fence, and the cost to register it as personal property. A notable challenge comes from certain trackers that can alert police if a stolen car drops below a specific speed during a chase.The article also highlights a potentially significant change to police response: cops are reportedly only alerted if a theft is witnessed or an alarm is triggered. This suggests that stealth will play a much more crucial role in successful car theft operations, rewarding players for careful planning and execution. While these changes promise deeper gameplay, the community is divided on whether the added complexity will be a fun challenge or an inconvenient chore.

## Key Takeaways
- Car theft in GTA 6 is significantly more involved, featuring varied methods.
- Older cars can be stolen with a slim jim (mini-game) or by smashing windows.
- Newer, electronically secured cars require a 'key cloner,' unlocked via story progression.
- Some parked cars are inaccessible early in the game, requiring story advancement for tools.
- The 'WAINK' phone app scans cars for trackers, value, and registration costs.
- Certain car trackers can alert police if speed drops during a chase.
- Police are only called if a theft is witnessed or an alarm sounds, encouraging stealth.

## FAQ
**How has car theft changed in GTA 6?**
Car theft is now more involved, requiring different methods and tools depending on the vehicle type and its age.

**Can I steal any car from the beginning of the game?**
No, some parked cars, particularly newer models with electronic keys, require specific tools like a 'key cloner' that are acquired later in the story.

**What is the 'WAINK' app?**
WAINK is an in-game phone app that allows players to scan cars to check for trackers, determine their worth at a fence, and see the cost to register them.

**How do police get involved in car theft now?**
Police are reportedly only alerted if a car theft is witnessed by an NPC or if a car's alarm goes off, making stealth a more viable approach.

**Are there new challenges with stolen cars?**
Yes, some stolen cars may have trackers that ping the police if the vehicle's speed drops below a certain threshold during a chase.
