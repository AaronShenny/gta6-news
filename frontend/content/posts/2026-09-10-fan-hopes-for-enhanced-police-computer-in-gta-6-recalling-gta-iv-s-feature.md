# Fan Hopes for Enhanced Police Computer in GTA 6, Recalling GTA IV's Feature

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto fan has voiced their desire for an enhanced police computer system in the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6. Drawing inspiration from Grand Theft Auto IV, where players could access a police computer in stolen cruisers to track ongoing crimes and suspects, the user proposes a more expansive version for GTA 6. They envision a system that would allow players, upon acquiring a police vehicle, to respond dynamically to a wider array of criminal activities, including robberies, high-speed pursuits, and gang-related incidents. This suggestion comes with the anticipation of GTA 6's rumored new police system, hinting at the potential for deeper immersion and gameplay opportunities within the law enforcement aspect of the game.

## Key Takeaways
- A Reddit user proposes an expanded police computer feature for GTA 6.
- The idea is inspired by GTA IV's police computer, which allowed tracking of crimes.
- The suggested enhancement would let players respond to robberies, pursuits, and gang activity.
- The feature could integrate well with GTA 6's anticipated new police system.

In GTA IV, players could steal a police cruiser and use its in-car computer to view ongoing crimes and locate suspects to pursue.
