# GTA 6 Unpacked: The Guardian's Exclusive Report Details Vice City, Dual Leads, and 2025 Outlook

The Guardian's exclusive report offers a detailed look at what's known and anticipated for Grand Theft Auto 6, Rockstar Games' highly awaited next installment. While Rockstar has yet to confirm an official release date, industry analysts and insiders widely project a 2025 launch. The game is confirmed to return to the iconic, modern-day Vice City, a fictionalized version of Miami, as revealed through official trailers and earlier leaks.

A significant departure for the series is the introduction of two playable protagonists, Lucia and Jason, depicted as a Bonnie and Clyde-esque criminal duo. The game's map is expected to be substantially larger than Grand Theft Auto V's Los Santos, featuring increased verticality and more explorable interior spaces.

Gameplay innovations are anticipated to include an enhanced physics engine, leading to more realistic vehicle damage, alongside improved AI for characters and dynamic weather systems. Players can also expect more interactive environmental elements and an expanded array of side activities and mini-games. The narrative will delve into Vice City's criminal underworld, exploring themes of ambition, betrayal, and a satirical take on modern American culture.

Rockstar Games has confirmed that development is "well underway," with significant leaks in September 2022 providing early glimpses, followed by the official trailer release in December 2023. GTA 6 is slated to launch initially on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, with a PC version expected to follow at a later date.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 is widely expected to launch in 2025, though Rockstar Games has not provided an official release date.
- The game is set in a modern-day Vice City, a fictionalized Miami, confirmed by official trailers and leaks.
- It will feature two playable protagonists, Lucia and Jason, marking a first for the series with a female lead.
- The map is anticipated to be significantly larger than GTA 5's Los Santos, with more detailed interiors and verticality.
- Expected gameplay enhancements include an advanced physics engine, realistic vehicle damage, improved AI, and dynamic weather.
- GTA 6 will launch on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, with a PC version likely to be released at a later time.
