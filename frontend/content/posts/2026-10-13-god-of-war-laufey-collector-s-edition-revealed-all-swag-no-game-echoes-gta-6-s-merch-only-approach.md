# God of War Laufey Collector's Edition Revealed: All Swag, No Game (Echoes GTA 6's Merch-Only Approach)

The highly anticipated God of War Laufey Collector's Edition has been officially unveiled on the UK PlayStation Direct store, but with a significant catch: the game itself is not included. Priced at £130 (expected to be similar in USD), this premium set is purely a box of merchandise, designed for the most dedicated fans. The centerpiece of this collector's item is an impressive light-up statue depicting the protagonist, Faye, engaged in combat, with Phranque the Cube observing. Additionally, the edition includes a deck of 51 oracle cards and a small art book, all presented in exclusive collector's packaging. Purchasers will need to buy the 'God of War Laufey' game separately, likely for an additional $70. This strategy of offering merchandise-only collector's editions is not unprecedented. The article highlights a recent precedent set by Rockstar Games, which revealed a $400 GTA 6 collector's edition that similarly focused on physical 'stuff' without bundling the game. Publishers adopt this approach to target high-spending superfans, generating high-margin merchandise while simultaneously streamlining supply chains and reducing production overhead. In related God of War news, the next installment in the series will see the return of Kratos, picking up directly after the events of 'God of War Laufey'. Furthermore, a live-action God of War TV series is currently in development for Prime Video, with Dave Bautista confirmed to play Kratos, undergoing significant physical transformation for the role after original actor Ryan Hurst's departure due to injury.
