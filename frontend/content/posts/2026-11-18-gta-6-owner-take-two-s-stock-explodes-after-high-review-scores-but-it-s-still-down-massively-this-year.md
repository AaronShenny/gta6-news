# GTA 6 Owner Take-Two’s Stock Explodes After High Review Scores, But It’s Still Down Massively This Year

Rockstar's highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6* has finally launched (initially in New Zealand and Australia) after a decade of waiting, garnering overwhelmingly positive reviews. GameSpot awarded the game a rare perfect 10/10, with reviewer Tamoor Hussain calling it an "astonishing open-world action game." On Metacritic, GTA 6 has unseated the *Ocarina of Time* remake for Switch 2 as the highest-rated game of 2026 and is tied for the highest aggregate review score ever with the original *Ocarina of Time*.

This critical success has translated into a significant boost for Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar's owner. Ahead of the US stock market open, Take-Two's shares jumped nearly 7% in pre-market trading, potentially adding billions to its market capitalization. Analysts anticipate further growth with the expected 2027 launches of *GTA Online* and a PC edition. Despite today's surge, Take-Two's stock remains down close to 20% year-to-date in 2026 and about 15% over the past 12 months. This decline is partly attributed to investor spooking earlier in the year when Google announced new AI software for game development. The stock closed at $203.93 on November 17 but rose to $216 in pre-market trading.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 has launched in select regions and received overwhelmingly positive reviews, including a 10/10 from GameSpot.
- The game is currently the highest-rated of 2026 on Metacritic and tied for the highest aggregate score ever.
- Take-Two Interactive's stock saw a nearly 7% surge in pre-market trading following the strong reviews.
- Despite the current uplift, Take-Two's stock is still down approximately 20% year-to-date in 2026.
- Future growth for Take-Two is anticipated with the planned 2027 releases of GTA Online and a PC version of GTA 6.
