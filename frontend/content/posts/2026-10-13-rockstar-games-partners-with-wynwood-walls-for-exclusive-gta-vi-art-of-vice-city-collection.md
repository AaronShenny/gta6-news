# Rockstar Games Partners with Wynwood Walls for Exclusive GTA VI 'Art of Vice City' Collection

Rockstar Games has officially announced an exciting partnership with the renowned Wynwood Walls Museum in Miami. This collaboration introduces 'The Wynwood Walls Art of Vice City Collection,' an exclusive line of merchandise featuring original artwork from real-world artists whose designs will be prominently displayed on the streets of the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI's Vice City. The collection allows fans to wear a piece of the upcoming game's vibrant aesthetic, with designs by celebrated artists such as 1010, AIKO, Ahol, DENIAL, FAILE, Flmingo, Gons x Audet, Leon Keer, Queen Andrea, Ron English, Sonni, AES, D*Face, Tristan Eaton, and Typoe. The pre-sale for this unique collection is now live on the official Wynwood Walls Shop, offering fans a tangible connection to the artistic vision of GTA VI.
