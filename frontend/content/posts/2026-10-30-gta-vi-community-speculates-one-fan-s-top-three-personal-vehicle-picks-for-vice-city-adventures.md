# GTA VI Community Speculates: One Fan's Top Three Personal Vehicle Picks for Vice City Adventures

In the highly anticipated world of Grand Theft Auto VI, players are already dreaming up their ideal garages. This choice stems from deep personal nostalgia, recalling childhood fishing and camping trips with their father. The user envisions the Rebel as the perfect companion for exploring GTA VI's more remote areas, serving as both a practical off-roader and a heartfelt tribute.Next on the list is the **Declasse Tornado**, chosen with a distinct Stephen King influence. Inspired by the unassuming Suzuki Carry, this compact vehicle is envisioned for late-night fence runs, allowing players to load up stolen goods while maintaining a low profile.
