# PS5 QSSR and GTA 6: Could Sony's Tech Boost Next-Gen Graphics?

The gaming community is buzzing with speculation regarding the integration of Sony's Quick Successive Sub-Resolution Rendering (QSSR) technology into Grand Theft Auto 6. A recent discussion highlights the impressive visuals of GTA 6's extended look, powered by Rockstar's RAGE engine, yet points out current limitations, particularly in ray tracing. The article notes that while the game appears stunning, the low sample count for ray tracing results in somewhat blurry reflections due to denoiser challenges.

QSSR, which has reportedly shown significant image quality improvements in titles like "Wolverine" and "Ghost of Yotei" without a noticeable performance hit, could be a game-changer. The central question revolves around whether Rockstar had prior knowledge of QSSR during development or if Sony is now encouraging its implementation closer to release. Integrating QSSR could potentially resolve some of the identified graphical limitations, offering a substantial visual upgrade for GTA 6 on the PlayStation 5 and potentially giving it a significant graphical edge over the Xbox Series X.

## FAQ
**What is QSSR?**
QSSR (Quick Successive Sub-Resolution Rendering) is a PlayStation 5 technology that reportedly improves image quality without significant performance costs, as seen in titles like 'Wolverine' and 'Ghost of Yotei'.

**How might QSSR benefit GTA 6?**
QSSR could potentially enhance GTA 6's visuals on PS5, specifically by improving ray tracing quality and addressing current limitations like blurry reflections caused by low sample counts.

**Does GTA 6 currently have graphical limitations?**
While GTA 6 looks impressive, some areas in its extended look show limitations, particularly low ray tracing samples leading to somewhat blurry reflections due to denoiser challenges.

**Could QSSR give PS5 an advantage over XSX for GTA 6?**
If implemented effectively, QSSR could provide a significant visual edge for GTA 6 on the PlayStation 5, potentially making it look noticeably better than on the Xbox Series X.
