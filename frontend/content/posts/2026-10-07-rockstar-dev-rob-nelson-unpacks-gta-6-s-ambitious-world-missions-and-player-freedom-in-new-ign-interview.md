# Rockstar Dev Rob Nelson Unpacks GTA 6's Ambitious World, Missions, and Player Freedom in New IGN Interview

Rockstar Games' Rob Nelson recently sat down with IGN, revealing a treasure trove of exciting new details about Grand Theft Auto VI. A major focus for the upcoming title is blurring the lines between tightly directed cinematic missions and the expansive free-roam experience. Players can expect less restrictive story missions, offering multiple escape routes and allowing police chases and robberies to transition seamlessly into systemic free-roam gameplay. The world of GTA 6 is described as Rockstar's largest, deepest, most densely populated, and complex to date. Exploration is highly encouraged, with many discoveries not marked on the map, prompting players to wander and find events naturally. The game is designed around 'living in the world' rather than just mission completion, allowing players to interact with NPCs through an advanced RDR2-like system, discover activities, and make money through emergent events. Character switching between protagonists Jason and Lucia is a prominent feature, available during much of free roam, even at their RDR2-style safehouses where they maintain daily schedules. The unplayed character continues their routine, and their relationship can be affected by ignored texts or calls. When together, Jason and Lucia actively protect each other, with one able to drive during escapes while the other shoots. Other notable revelations include the return of hair and beard growth mechanics similar to Red Dead Redemption 2, location-specific clothing and hairstyles encouraging exploration, and the ability to level up both characters at the gym simultaneously. The Wanted system has been redesigned to favor a 'hit-and-run' playstyle, and combat encounters offer more escalation options, from threatening to incapacitating. Despite these realistic and immersive systems, Rockstar assures players that the classic chaotic GTA experience of massive shootouts and police chases remains readily available.

## Key Takeaways
- Story missions are less restrictive, offering multiple escape routes and seamless transitions into free-roam gameplay during chases and robberies.
- Safehouses function similarly to Red Dead Redemption 2's camps, with Jason and Lucia having daily schedules and interacting dynamically.
- Rockstar aims to blur the line between scripted missions and emergent free-roam gameplay, making spontaneous events feel cinematic.
- GTA 6's map is described as Rockstar's largest, deepest, most densely populated, and complex world to date.
- Players can freely switch between Jason and Lucia in free roam; the unplayed character continues their own routine.
- An advanced NPC interaction system, similar to RDR2's, allows for natural conversations and social dynamics.
- The Wanted system is redesigned to encourage a 'hit-and-run' criminal playstyle, focusing on quick escapes.
- The game is designed for players to 'live in the world,' encouraging exploration for unmarked discoveries and spontaneous activities.
- Features like hair/beard growth (similar to RDR2), location-specific customization, and joint gym activities are returning or new.
- Despite deeper realism and relationship mechanics, players can still create the classic chaotic GTA experience with massive shootouts and police chases.

## FAQ
**Are missions in GTA 6 less restrictive?**
Yes, some missions offer multiple escape routes, and police chases/robberies can transition seamlessly into free-roam gameplay.

**How do safehouses work in GTA 6?**
Safehouses are more like RDR2's camps, with Jason and Lucia having daily schedules and interacting like real people.

**Can I switch between Jason and Lucia freely?**
Yes, you can freely switch between them during much of free roam, including at safehouses, though story moments may temporarily restrict this. The unplayed character continues their routine.

**Does hair and beard growth return from RDR2?**
It's heavily implied. Jason can cut his hair/beard shorter at home, but barbers are needed for longer styles or full changes.

**How has the Wanted system changed?**
The new Wanted system encourages a 'hit-and-run' playstyle, focusing on quick escapes rather than escalating confrontations.

**Will GTA 6 encourage exploration?**
Absolutely. Many discoveries won't be marked on the map, and the game is designed for players to wander, interact, and find emergent events naturally.

**Can players still create classic GTA chaos?**
Yes, despite the more realistic systems and relationship mechanics, players can still engage in huge shootouts and police chases whenever they want.
