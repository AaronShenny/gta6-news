# GTA 6: Could Environmental Damage Open Up New Routes?

A recent discussion sparked by a Reddit user explores the intriguing possibility of environmental destructibility in Grand Theft Auto VI, suggesting that players might be able to create new routes by damaging the environment. The speculation stems from an observation in the 'Extended Look' footage, specifically during a 'trap-house raid' scene where an S4S member tackles a cop through a wall, leaving a new opening. While this could be a scripted NPC interaction, the article posits it might hint at a broader gameplay mechanic.

The discussion further connects this idea to the 'Focus' system seen in the Megamundo section of the footage. In this mode, Jason activates Focus to slow time and highlight enemy weak points. Given that 'Focus' is also known to mark interactable objects, the theory suggests that it could have an additional layer: revealing potential weak points in thin walls. This would align with the system's broader functionality and introduce innovative ways for players to navigate mission locations and engage with the world.
