# Do we think that it will be possible to fly airliners in GTA VI

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto fan on Reddit, u/SFDM99, has sparked a discussion regarding the potential for players to pilot mid-sized airliners, akin to Boeing 737s or Airbus A320s, within the upcoming GTA VI. The user expresses a strong desire for this feature, noting its absence in previous titles where larger aircraft often served as non-flyable placeholders, as seen in GTA V. A key concern raised is whether the game's commitment to realism might restrict players to smaller aircraft. However, u/SFDM99 counters this by questioning the logic of including massive airports and runways if they are not intended for larger, flyable planes. The user's ultimate hope is for a game where every visible aircraft is interactive and pilotable, a first for the franchise that would significantly enhance the immersive experience.

## Key Takeaways
- A Reddit user hopes to fly mid-sized airliners (B737/A320 equivalents) in GTA VI.

- A desire for all visible aircraft to be flyable, a feature not fully realized in previous GTA titles.
