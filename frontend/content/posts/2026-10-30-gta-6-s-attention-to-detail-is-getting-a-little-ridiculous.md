# GTA 6’s Attention To Detail Is Getting A Little Ridiculous

As the highly anticipated launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S approaches in 'a few more weeks,' Gamespot's senior video producer Jake Dekker has undertaken a meticulous investigation into the game's revealed screenshots. Analyzing a trove of 99 high-resolution images, Dekker uncovered a plethora of hidden details that underscore Rockstar's commitment to realism and immersion.

The investigation revealed impressive elements such as:
*   **Hyper-realistic Character Models:** Frazzled beards, realistic scars, and NPCs exhibiting the subtle effects of gravity, age, and Leonida's tropical climate.
*   **Intricate World Building:** Callbacks to previous GTA titles and advanced technical wizardry contributing to a deeply immersive environment.
*   **Unique In-Game Currency:** A standout detail is the hard currency, with dollar bills explicitly printed in the "United States of Paranoia," adding a layer of satirical realism.

Beyond these visual revelations, the article also touches upon other confirmed GTA 6 features, including an immersive fuel economy system, a robust consequence system for player actions, and the introduction of police dogs to track down culprits. The game's map is touted as exceptionally vast, reportedly rivaling the combined landmass of GTA 4, GTA 5, and Red Dead Redemption 2. Adding a touch of real-world crossover, legendary musician Cliff Richard's 1976 hit "Devil Woman" is confirmed to be featured in the game, much to his surprise and delight.
