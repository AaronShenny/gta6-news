# GTA 6 Speculation: Could RDR2-Style County Lockdowns Redefine Post-Heist Pursuits?

A compelling discussion has emerged from the GTA 6 subreddit, initiated by user /u/SoftDickJones, proposing a significant enhancement to the game's police response system. The core idea revolves around the potential introduction of county-wide lockdowns, akin to those seen in Red Dead Redemption 2, following major heists or significant shootouts within Grand Theft Auto VI.

This speculated feature suggests that after a major in-game incident involving protagonists Jason and Lucia, the surrounding area, and potentially neighboring counties, could experience a dramatically heightened police presence. This would go beyond the standard wanted level system, manifesting as roadblocks, increased patrols, and aerial surveillance via helicopters, making navigation and escape considerably more challenging for a sustained period.

The user emphasizes that this would be a 'post-wanted level' mechanic, distinct from immediate pursuit, and questions how Rockstar Games might leverage GTA VI's modern setting to depict sophisticated, coordinated regional responses among various law enforcement agencies. The discussion points to a desire for a more immersive and challenging consequence system, expanding on RDR2's lockdown mechanics to a larger, contemporary scale.
