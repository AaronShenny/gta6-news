# Fan Unveils Stunning Classic-Styled GTA VI Cover Art

A dedicated fan has unveiled a meticulously crafted, classic-styled cover art concept for the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI. The creator, who worked on the piece for an extended period, utilized a classic-styled logo originally designed by Takiva2006 on Deviantart, making several key modifications. These adjustments include more angular and thicker vertical lines, and the creation of both a white variant (reminiscent of VCS style) and an 'Ultimate Gradient Variant'. The fan also carefully adjusted the aspect ratio to match Rockstar Games' standardized website format. Furthermore, elements on the cover were repositioned to align with classic GTA stylization. A custom Rockstar logo, inspired by a Sony App icon submitted by Reddit user /u/ellie12134, was also incorporated, showcasing a blend of community creativity and traditional design.

## Key Takeaways
