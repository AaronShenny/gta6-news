# GTA 6's Connected Waterways Hint at Revolutionary Map Navigation

A recent discussion among the Grand Theft Auto 6 community has sparked intriguing speculation regarding the game's expansive map and its potential for water-based navigation. According to a Reddit user, a close examination of the rumored GTA 6 map reveals a highly interconnected network of waterways that appear to span across nearly all landmasses. This observation suggests a revolutionary approach to traversing Vice City and its surrounding areas, potentially allowing players to reach most locations using various watercraft. The implication is that players might find themselves less reliant on traditional fast travel options or commandeering helicopters for quick transit. The original poster also expressed a desire for a robust GPS auto-pathfinding system specifically designed for water travel, which would undoubtedly enhance the fluidity and enjoyment of navigating the game's aquatic routes.
