# Rockstar Explains GTA 6's Lengthy Development: Why 'Big Swings' Trumped a 'GTA 5.5'

Rockstar Games is finally set to release Grand Theft Auto 6 on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The long development cycle, according to Rockstar publishing executive Jennifer Kolbe and boss Rob Nelson, was a deliberate choice. Kolbe stated they 'could have made GTA 5.5, and done it faster,' but that wasn't their goal. Nelson added that the studio felt it was crucial to 'take big swings' and avoid 'more of the same,' aiming to go above and beyond with the new installment. Take-Two CEO further emphasized Rockstar's pursuit of 'perfection' and doing 'something that’s never been done before,' acknowledging the time and effort required.

GTA 6 promises significant advancements, including major visual and technical improvements. The narrative will feature a Bonnie-and-Clyde-style story centered around a crime-loving couple, Jason and Lucia, marking the series' first playable female protagonist. While the current development took years, Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick hinted that the 'nature and velocity' of future GTA releases might look 'dramatically different' over the next decade, though no specific timelines were provided.

For launch, GTA 6 will unlock at midnight local time globally, meaning players in the US will be among the last to experience the game. In a unique promotional stunt, Australians can even attend a fake funeral at KFC to get the day off work to play.
