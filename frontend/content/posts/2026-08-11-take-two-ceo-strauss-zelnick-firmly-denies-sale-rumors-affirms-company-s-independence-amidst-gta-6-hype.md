# Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick Firmly Denies Sale Rumors, Affirms Company's Independence Amidst GTA 6 Hype

Take-Two Interactive, the powerhouse behind Rockstar Games and the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6, has no interest in selling, according to CEO Strauss Zelnick. Appearing on CNBC, Zelnick directly addressed speculation that Take-Two's recent partnership with Netflix for the upcoming GTA 6 trailer might signal a potential buyout. He firmly stated that Take-Two is not looking to sell to Netflix or any other entity at this time.

Zelnick clarified that Take-Two has an established relationship with Netflix, distributing several of its games through Netflix Games. He views Netflix's foray into gaming as a natural progression for a company focused on subscriber growth and entertainment. While Netflix has made smaller gaming studio acquisitions, Zelnick noted their historical preference for organic growth over large-scale buyouts, leaving any major acquisition questions for Netflix's own leadership.

Underscoring Take-Two's strong position, Zelnick expressed immense pride in the company's independent growth, highlighting its status as the largest public pure-play in the gaming sector worldwide. With projected revenues exceeding $8 billion this year and consistent profitability, he asserted that Take-Two is well-deserving of its independent status, contrasting its path with other industry giants like Activision Blizzard and Electronic Arts, which have recently gone private through major acquisitions.

The company is gearing up for a monumental year, with GTA 6 set to launch this November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and more details expected to be revealed on August 27 via Netflix.
