# GTA 6 Might Give America A Very Expensive Sick Day

Anticipation for Rockstar Games' upcoming title, Grand Theft Auto 6, is so immense that an economics professor, José García Montalvo, projects approximately 1.5 million Americans may call in sick on its projected launch date, November 19. This mass absence could lead to an estimated $1 billion loss in productivity for the U.S. economy, based on an average annual salary of $70,000 per worker. However, the article clarifies that this isn't a net loss for the economy, but rather a transfer of funds, largely benefiting GTA 6 publisher Take-Two Interactive, which is also estimated to earn $1 billion on the same day.

It's important to note that Montalvo's projection is an academic estimation and not an official forecast. This phenomenon, dubbed 'GTA flu,' has historical precedent; an IGN poll before Grand Theft Auto 5's 2013 launch indicated that nearly half of its respondents planned to take time off. The article highlights the cultural impact of Rockstar's games, citing examples of extreme dedication, including a streamer planning a five-day vacation, social media users drafting 'contracts' with partners, and even the military at Fort Stewart, Georgia, offering soldiers days off to play the game.
