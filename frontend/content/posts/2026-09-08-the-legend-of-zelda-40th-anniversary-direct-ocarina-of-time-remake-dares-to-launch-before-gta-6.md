# The Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Direct: Ocarina of Time Remake Dares to Launch Before GTA 6

The Legend of Zelda's 40th anniversary received a grand celebration via a dedicated Nintendo Direct, with the spotlight firmly on a gorgeous remake of *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time*. This enhanced classic, boasting stylized realism, new features like jumping and underwater diving, and snappier combat, is set to launch on **November 5**. In a move demonstrating Nintendo's confidence, this release date places it just weeks before the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6*.

The remake will leverage the Switch 2's capabilities, including motion controls for weapons like the Fairy Slingshot and a unique mechanic allowing players to hum into the console's microphone to replicate sounds with the in-game Ocarina. Complementing this, official playable and electric Hori Ocarina replicas will be available, recognized by the game. The expanded "Zelda Notes" app will offer progress tracking, hint images, a virtual ocarina, and daily quizzes. Two new Amiibo will unlock special in-game masks, and a Zelda-themed Switch 2 console and accessories were also unveiled.

Beyond the game, the Direct also showcased the official logo for *The Legend of Zelda* live-action movie, set for an April 30, 2027 release, with Shigeru Miyamoto confirming it as an original work. Fans can also look forward to a wave of new merchandise, including collectibles and Lego, a 40th-anniversary exhibition at the Nintendo Museum in Japan, and a global musical anniversary concert tour in 2027. Nintendo also teased another 45-minute Direct on September 9, followed by a Treehouse livestream.
