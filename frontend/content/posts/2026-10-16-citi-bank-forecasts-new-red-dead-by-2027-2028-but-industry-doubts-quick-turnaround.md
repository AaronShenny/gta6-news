# Citi Bank Forecasts New Red Dead by 2027-2028, But Industry Doubts Quick Turnaround

Investment bank Citi has sparked discussion within the gaming community by suggesting a new *Red Dead* title could launch as early as 2027 or 2028. This ambitious forecast, highlighted in a memo, posits that if *Grand Theft Auto 6* didn't incur the rumored $1.5 billion development cost, Rockstar Games might be in a position to accelerate its future release schedule.

However, the article strongly refutes Citi's optimistic timeline, pointing to Rockstar's established development patterns. Historically, the studio maintains significant gaps between its major releases—for instance, *Red Dead Redemption* in 2010, *Grand Theft Auto V* in 2013, *Red Dead Redemption II* in 2018, and *Grand Theft Auto 6* slated for 2026. This demonstrates a consistent multi-year cycle between flagship titles, and even longer between entries in the same franchise.

Furthermore, Rockstar's internal development philosophy has evolved. The company previously attempted to develop *GTA 6* and *Red Dead Redemption II* concurrently but found this approach too ambitious, ultimately reassigning staff to prioritize *RDR2*. This experience led to a shift, with Rockstar's global network of teams now focusing collaboratively on one major project before moving to the next. With *GTA 6*'s impending launch, substantial resources are expected to be dedicated to its post-launch support and the next evolution of *GTA Online* for years to come.

While Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick and Rockstar's Phil Hooker have made statements hinting at faster development cycles in the future—attributing this to new technologies (potentially AI) and an expanded workforce—the article suggests these comments might be primarily aimed at reassuring analysts and investors. The industry remains skeptical that such initiatives would result in a *Red Dead* sequel just 1-2 years after *GTA 6*, emphasizing that only time will tell if Rockstar genuinely shortens its renowned development timelines.

## Key Takeaways
- Investment bank Citi suggests a new Red Dead game could launch by 2027 or 2028.
- Citi's prediction is based on the assumption that GTA 6's development cost wasn't $1.5 billion and Rockstar might be able to accelerate future projects.
- Historically, Rockstar has significant gaps between major titles and even longer between entries in the same series (e.g., RDR 2010, GTA V 2013, RDR2 2018, GTA VI 2026).
- Rockstar previously tried and failed to develop GTA 6 and Red Dead Redemption II concurrently, leading to a shift to single-project focus across its studios.
- Extensive post-launch support for GTA 6 and its online component is expected, which would tie up significant resources.
- Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick and Rockstar's Phil Hooker have hinted at new technologies (possibly AI) and hiring strategies to potentially shorten future development cycles.
- Despite these statements, the article remains skeptical, suggesting such comments might be aimed at investors rather than indicating a genuine, rapid turnaround.
