# GTA 6 Protagonists Lucia and Jason Share Intriguing Parallels with Vice City Legends

A recent fan observation from Reddit user /u/Ze_peixoto highlights intriguing parallels between the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6 protagonists, Lucia and Jason, and the iconic characters from previous Vice City titles, Tommy Vercetti and Vic Vance. The theory posits that these similarities, especially with GTA 6 returning to Vice City, could be more than mere coincidence, potentially serving as clever Easter eggs or nods to the franchise's history.

Lucia's background, emerging from prison with ties to Liberty City, strongly echoes Tommy Vercetti's initial storyline in GTA: Vice City. Similarly, Jason's past, involving military service before becoming entangled with drug dealers, draws a direct parallel to Vic Vance's narrative arc in GTA: Vice City Stories. The author emphasizes that while these resemblances are notable, they do not imply that Lucia or Jason will have identical personalities or that the game's story will mirror the older titles. Instead, the presence of both sets of parallels within the same game, set in the familiar Vice City, is seen as a curious and potentially intentional design choice by Rockstar Games.
