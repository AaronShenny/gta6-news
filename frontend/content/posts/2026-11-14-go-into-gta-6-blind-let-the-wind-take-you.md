# Go into GTA 6 blind. Let the wind take you

A recent post by Reddit user u/xanderico7 on the r/GTA6 subreddit advocates for a 'blind' approach to playing Grand Theft Auto 6. The user expresses concern over a trend of players creating and sharing 'checklists' for what they plan to do in the game, suggesting this practice could detract from the overall experience.

The core argument is that programming one's mind with a checklist transforms the game into a series of tasks rather than a source of spontaneous fun. Instead, u/xanderico7 encourages players to 'go in free' and 'let the wind take you,' emphasizing that the joy of discovery and engaging in unplanned activities is a crucial part of the gaming experience.

## Key Takeaways
- Avoid creating or following GTA 6 checklists.
- Playing with a checklist can turn the game into a task-oriented experience, reducing fun.
- Embrace organic discovery and spontaneity for a richer gaming experience.
- Unplanned activities and discoveries are a significant part of the enjoyment in games like GTA.
