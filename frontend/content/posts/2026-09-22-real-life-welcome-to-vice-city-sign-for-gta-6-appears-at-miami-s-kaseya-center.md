# Real-Life 'Welcome to Vice City' Sign for GTA 6 Appears at Miami's Kaseya Center

The highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI is officially kicking off its real-world marketing campaign, with a prominent 'Welcome to Vice City' sign recently installed on the roof of the Kaseya Center in Miami. This marks one of the very first instances of tangible, real-life promotion for the upcoming title, directly linking the real-world location to the game's iconic setting. The Kaseya Center is widely recognized as the real-life counterpart to the in-game Sahara Arena, a location familiar to fans who have been closely following leaks and official announcements related to the game's Vice City setting. This bold move by Rockstar Games signals the beginning of a larger marketing push, building excitement for what promises to be one of the biggest game releases in history.
