# Rockstar Confirms No Disney World Parody in GTA 6: Resource & Narrative Reasons Cited

Grand Theft Auto 6, set in the fictional Florida state of Leonida, will notably not include a parody of Walt Disney World. This revelation, coming directly from Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson, addresses a common fan question, especially given that GTA: Vice City featured its own theme park spoof, "Fun World," years ago.Nelson clarified that the decision was primarily due to two factors: **resource allocation** and **narrative necessity**. He explained that creating a theme park to Rockstar's exacting standards would be an immense undertaking, requiring significant development resources that they simply didn't have for such a feature. Rockstar is known for its attention to detail, and a GTA 6 theme park would need to be a "fully functioning" experience, a task deemed too large to execute properly.The sheer scale of the real Walt Disney World, encompassing over 25,000 acres with multiple parks, shopping districts, water parks, and hotels, underscores the challenge of replication. Nelson pondered, "Where do you stop? When do you feel like it's enough?" when considering the player expectation of fully functional rides and attractions.Furthermore, Nelson highlighted the **lack of a strong narrative reason** to send protagonists Jason and Lucia hundreds of miles north from Vice City to the Orlando area, where the real Disney World is located. While GTA 6 boasts a vast map, its scope is contained to Vice City and its immediate surroundings, not extending far enough north to naturally incorporate such a location into the storyline. Nelson stressed that Rockstar, despite perceptions, does not have unlimited time and money, necessitating pragmatic decisions about what can be included to meet their high quality benchmarks.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6, set in fictional Leonida (Florida), will not feature a parody of Walt Disney World.
- Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson confirmed the exclusion, citing resource limitations and a lack of narrative necessity.
- Creating a "fully functioning" theme park to Rockstar's high standards would demand excessive development time and resources.
- The real Walt Disney World's immense scale (over 25,000 acres) makes even a partial, high-quality replication a monumental task.
- There is no strong story reason to send protagonists Jason and Lucia hundreds of miles north from Vice City to the Orlando area, as the game's map is contained to Vice City and its immediate surroundings.
