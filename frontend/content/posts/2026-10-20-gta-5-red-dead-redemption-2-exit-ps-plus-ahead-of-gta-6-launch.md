# GTA 5 & Red Dead Redemption 2 Exit PS Plus Ahead of GTA 6 Launch

PlayStation Plus subscribers will soon lose access to several major titles from the Game Catalog, including two of Rockstar Games' biggest hits: Grand Theft Auto 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2. While Sony is adding new games, the removal of these popular open-world adventures is seen by some as a strategic move by Rockstar and Take-Two Interactive to eliminate 'distractions' ahead of the highly anticipated November 19 launch of *Grand Theft Auto 6*.

Both GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 have historically rotated in and out of the PS Plus catalog, suggesting a possible return in the future. In addition to these blockbusters, nine other titles are also exiting the service, including the critically acclaimed puzzle game *The Talos Principle 2* and the unique tower-defense game *Rock of Ages 3: Make & Break*.

Subscribers are advised to play these games soon if they wish to experience them before their removal. Meanwhile, PS Plus Essential members can still claim October's free games—*F1 25*, *Hunt: Showdown 1896*, and *Earth Defense Force: World Brothers 2*—until November 2.
