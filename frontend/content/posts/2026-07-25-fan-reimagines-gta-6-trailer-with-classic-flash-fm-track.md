# Fan Reimagines GTA 6 Trailer with Classic Flash FM Track

A creative Grand Theft Auto fan has taken to Reddit to share their unique re-edit of the highly anticipated GTA 6 trailer. The user, known as /u/shanu5erattayill, replaced the trailer's original soundtrack with the iconic 80s hit 'Life's What You Make It' by Talk Talk. This particular track holds a special place in the hearts of long-time GTA players, as it was famously featured on Flash FM, one of the popular radio stations in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City. The fan-made video offers a fresh, nostalgic perspective on the trailer, blending the new visuals of Leonida with a classic Vice City soundscape, creating a distinct vibe that resonates with the series' rich musical history.
