# Fan Crafts Impressive 3D Printed GTA VI Themed Wallet

A passionate Grand Theft Auto VI enthusiast, known as u/simtom on Reddit, has unveiled a remarkable fan-made accessory: a 3D printed wallet adorned with GTA VI themes. The creator utilized a 3D printer for the main structure of the wallet, crediting MakerVerse Designs for the base model. To bring the iconic GTA VI aesthetics to life, a UV printer was employed to add various images and graphics, with a special shout-out to GFXGTA on X for their contributions to the visual elements. The project showcases a high level of craftsmanship and dedication, combining modern manufacturing techniques with fan creativity to produce a unique piece of merchandise that has garnered positive attention within the GTA 6 community.

## Key Takeaways
- A fan created a custom GTA VI themed wallet.
