# GTA 6 Trailer Gets a Nostalgic Makeover in Fan-Made GTA 4 Reimagining

It stands as a testament to the community's passion and anticipation for GTA 6, showcasing the innovative ways fans are engaging with the upcoming title while celebrating the series' rich history.
