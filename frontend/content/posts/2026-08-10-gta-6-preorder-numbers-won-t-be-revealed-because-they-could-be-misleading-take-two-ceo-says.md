# GTA 6 Preorder Numbers Won’t Be Revealed Because They Could Be Misleading, Take-Two CEO Says

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has confirmed that the company will not be revealing the actual preorder numbers for Grand Theft Auto 6, despite calling them "unprecedented in the gaming industry" and stating they "vastly" surpassed internal expectations. Zelnick explained to Bloomberg that the decision stems from uncertainty about what these massive early figures truly signify. He suggested that the high demand could reflect a "pull forward of sales" that would otherwise materialize over time, and sharing raw data might mislead investors.

Zelnick emphasized that "No one’s ever seen anything like this before at Take-Two, or in the industry," but also cautioned that preorders are cancellable, adding to the ambiguity of early figures. Alongside this news, Take-Two reiterated that GTA 6 has not been delayed and is still on track for its November 19 release on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Management also defended the game's $80 price point, assuring it offers "phenomenal value." GTA 6 is widely anticipated to be 2026's biggest game, with a future evolution of GTA Online also expected.
