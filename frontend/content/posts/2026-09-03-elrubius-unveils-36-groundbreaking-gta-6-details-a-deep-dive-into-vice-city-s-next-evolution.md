# ElRubius Unveils 36 Groundbreaking GTA 6 Details: A Deep Dive into Vice City's Next Evolution

Spanish YouTube sensation ElRubius recently had an exclusive preview of *Grand Theft Auto 6* gameplay at Rockstar North, sharing an extensive list of 36 new details. He confirmed that everything seen in the trailers is real, with the current build looking even better, running smoothly on a base PS5 at 30 FPS. The lighting, reminiscent of *Cyberpunk 2077*'s path tracing, demonstrates incredible realism in how light interacts with the environment, characters, and structures.

The game world is reportedly three times larger than *Red Dead Redemption 2* and is an island. A significant focus on **roleplay-like gameplay** is evident, with highly interactive safehouses and real-time interiors. Players can look out windows to see the live world and physically navigate buildings without loading screens. NPC behavior is remarkably dynamic; a park visit revealed diverse activities, and interactions like asking to pet a dog can have varied outcomes.

New mechanics include the ability to kick from e-scooters and open car doors to hit pedestrians while driving. Refueling cars is present but balanced, making it less of a chore than in *RDR2*. Players can loot car trunks, disable vehicles by shooting engines, and use a 'Focus Mode' (similar to Dead Eye) to identify weak points. Carrying weapons openly provokes realistic NPC reactions.

Character switching between Jason and Lucia is seamless, akin to *GTA V*. Emergent events are a core part of the experience, constantly distracting players with dynamic occurrences like crashed cars with loot or a naked man needing a ride (which ElRubius strongly recommends experiencing). **Full nudity** is confirmed, with realistic physics. Relationship dynamics between Jason and Lucia are also nuanced, affected by choices like abandoning a partner during a chase, though their relationship is described as "chill" enough for solo strip club visits.

Heists are a primary focus, available anytime, and are aided by a fully 3D in-game map that displays building entrances and potential earnings. During a jewelry store heist, characters operate independently, showcasing advanced AI. The level of violence and detail, including dismemberment and realistic ragdoll physics, was particularly impressive, though ElRubius noted uncertainty about whether all gore levels would remain in the final release. The game also features a compendium and highly reactive environments, such as sand reacting to footsteps on a packed beach.

## FAQ
**Is the GTA 6 footage seen in trailers representative of the final game?**
Yes, ElRubius confirmed that everything in the trailers is real, and the current gameplay build looks even better. However, Rockstar mentioned that content is still subject to change.

**What are the graphics and performance like on the PS5?**
ElRubius was highly impressed, stating it runs smoothly at 30 FPS on a base PS5, initially mistaking it for a PC build. The lighting is particularly advanced, resembling Cyberpunk 2077 with path tracing.

**How large is the GTA 6 map?**
The map is reportedly three times larger than Red Dead Redemption 2 and is an island.

**Are there loading screens when entering buildings or switching characters?**
No, interiors like safehouses and even elevator rides are in real-time without loading screens. Switching between Jason and Lucia is also seamless, similar to GTA V.

**What new gameplay mechanics were revealed?**
New mechanics include kicking from e-scooters, opening car doors to hit people while driving, looting car trunks, shooting car engines to disable vehicles, and a 'Focus Mode' for identifying vehicle weak points. Car refueling is present but balanced to avoid being tedious.

**How do NPCs and the environment react to player actions?**
NPCs react realistically to players carrying weapons, moving away. The environment, like sand on a beach, reacts to character weight and movement. There are also dynamic emergent events constantly occurring.

**What is the level of violence and nudity in the game?**
Full nudity is confirmed, and the game features highly detailed violence, including dismemberment and realistic ragdoll physics. ElRubius noted he wasn't sure if all gore levels would make it into the final release.

**How do heists work in GTA 6?**
Heists are a primary focus and can be initiated anytime. The 3D in-game map helps plan heists by showing building entrances and potential earnings. During heists, both Jason and Lucia can operate independently.
