# GTA 6 Coffee Is Here To Keep You Warm Until The Game Releases

Rockstar Games has partnered with Miami-based Vice City Bean to launch an officially licensed GTA 6 coffee blend, named 'Leonida'. This limited-release coffee aims to capture the vibrant energy of Miami, which serves as the inspiration for GTA 6's Vice City, with 'Leonida' being the game's version of Florida. The blend, a combination of Colombian and Ethiopian coffees, is described as a medium roast with a bold and tropical tasting profile, suitable for filter coffee and espresso.

A 10oz bag of Leonida beans is priced at $23, excluding shipping, and can be purchased from Vice City Bean's website or their three physical locations in Miami. Customers can choose between whole beans or various grind sizes. This coffee release is part of Rockstar's extensive marketing campaign for GTA 6, which also includes plans for officially branded GTA 6 beer. The game itself is set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
