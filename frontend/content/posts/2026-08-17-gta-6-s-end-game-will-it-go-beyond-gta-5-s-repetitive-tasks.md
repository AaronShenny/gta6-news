# GTA 6's End-Game: Will It Go Beyond GTA 5's Repetitive Tasks?

Amidst the widespread excitement for GTA 6's anticipated immersion, a Reddit user, u/WeirdAssBeings, raises a pertinent question: what will the end-game gameplay loop truly entail? The user highlights that Grand Theft Auto V's post-story content largely revolved around a few repetitive tasks, such as towing missions, cab driving, or hunting in the wilderness with Trevor. This prompts a discussion on how GTA 6 could evolve this experience, especially given its expected emphasis on 'big-crimes'. Speculations include the possibility of players being able to rob random people's houses or engage in repeatable missions to steal specific cars from various small villages, suggesting a desire for more dynamic, crime-oriented, and engaging activities to keep players invested long after the main narrative concludes.
