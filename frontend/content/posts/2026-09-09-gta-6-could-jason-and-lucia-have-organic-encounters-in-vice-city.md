# GTA 6: Could Jason and Lucia Have Organic Encounters in Vice City?

Recent discussions among the GTA 6 community are buzzing around the potential for a more dynamic open-world experience, specifically concerning the game's dual protagonists, Jason and Lucia. The speculation stems from insights shared by Mike Shosha, who reportedly gathered information directly from Rob Nelson at Rockstar North during a hands-on session. This information suggests that both Jason and Lucia will maintain independent routines, meaning the character not currently being controlled won't simply be static, awaiting a player switch.

This detail has sparked a fascinating question: will players be able to organically encounter the other protagonist in the open world while playing as one? Unlike GTA V, where switching between protagonists often involved a cutscene-like teleportation rather than a true physical crossing of paths, fans are hoping GTA 6 will push the boundaries. The idea is that while playing as Jason, for instance, you might turn a corner and spot Lucia at the gym, grabbing a bite to eat, or simply driving through Vice City, living out her own in-game life. This level of dynamic interaction would significantly enhance immersion and make the world feel even more alive.
