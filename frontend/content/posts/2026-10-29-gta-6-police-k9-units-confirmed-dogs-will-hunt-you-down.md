# GTA 6 Police K9 Units Confirmed: Dogs Will Hunt You Down

GTA 6 is set to introduce a more sophisticated police system than its predecessor, with a newly confirmed detail that law enforcement will deploy K9 units to hunt down players. This revelation comes from an Edge Magazine preview where Rockstar's Rob Nelson demonstrated the game. In one sequence, protagonists Jason and Lucia, attempting to evade police after a motorcycle crash, were pursued on foot by a K9 unit. A German Shepherd swiftly engaged Jason, pinning him to the ground, with developers hinting that shooting the dog is an option, similar to GTA 5.

The game's K9 integration mirrors real-life police tactics, particularly in Miami (parodied as Vice City), where specially trained dogs are used for crimefighting. Beyond K9 units, GTA 6 will feature an expanded six-star Wanted system and a more intricate criminal profile system allowing cops to cast a wider net. Players will also be notified if spotted by CCTV cameras, providing an opportunity to destroy evidence and evade capture. Additionally, police will remember the specific clothing worn by Jason and Lucia, necessitating costume changes for effective evasion.

In other dog-related news, a recent South Park episode humorously depicted Jason throwing and reusing dog poop as a weapon. GTA 6 is scheduled for launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a PC edition and GTA 6 Online potentially arriving in early 2027.
