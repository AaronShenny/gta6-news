# GTA 6 leaks aren’t funny or exciting anymore

A recent post on the r/GTA6 subreddit, titled 'GTA 6 leaks aren’t funny or exciting anymore,' highlights a growing sentiment of frustration among some fans as the game's release date approaches. The original poster, u/Puzzleheaded_Disk599, recalls an initial period where early leaks fostered excitement and community speculation, providing a thrilling first glimpse at the highly anticipated title. However, with the official launch now only 40 days away, the continuous stream of leaks is increasingly viewed as detrimental, spoiling key story beats and mission details. The author argues that these late-stage leaks are no longer generating hype but are instead ruining the carefully built anticipation for dedicated fans. Furthermore, the post suggests that those responsible for the leaks are indifferent to the gaming community's experience, implying motives beyond mere exposure, such as financial gains like 'crypto rug-pulls.' The overarching message is a plea to preserve the launch experience, emphasizing that after such a long wait, spoilers are unwelcome.

## Key Takeaways
- Early GTA 6 leaks were initially fun, exciting, and fostered community speculation.

- The author believes hackers are motivated by financial gain (e.g., 'crypto rug-pulls') rather than gamer interest.
