# Miami Heat to Sport GTA 6 Jerseys and Vice City Court Ahead of Game Launch

In an unprecedented cross-promotional event, the NBA's Miami Heat have officially announced they will don Grand Theft Auto 6-inspired jerseys and play on a specially branded court on November 18. This highly anticipated game against the Milwaukee Bucks, airing on ESPN, will take place at Miami's Kaseya Center, which has already been transformed with 'Vice City' signage.The event, occurring just one day before GTA 6's November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, promises 'special activities' and 'unique game night activations,' with further details on the jersey and court designs to be revealed soon. Fans can already purchase tickets and explore the new 'Vice City Collection' merchandise, including themed t-shirts.This massive advertising push by Rockstar Games in Miami has garnered attention, even sparking concerns from some local officials. Meanwhile, anticipation for GTA 6 continues to build, with reports indicating nearly 6 million pre-sales generating over $570 million. Recent revelations about the game include a diverse ecosystem with over 170 animal species and an advanced dynamic weather system.
