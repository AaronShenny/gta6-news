# GTA 6 Soars to Unprecedented Acclaim, Rivals Ocarina of Time as Highest-Rated Game

Grand Theft Auto 6 has officially launched, immediately garnering **overwhelmingly positive critical reception** worldwide. The highly anticipated title is currently making history, tying with the legendary 1998 classic, *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time*, as the **highest-rated game of all time on Metacritic**, a remarkable feat 28 years in the making.

Reviewers across the board are showering praise on Rockstar Games for crafting a **genre-defining open world** that is both exquisitely detailed and incredibly reactive, setting a new benchmark for future titles. Many major outlets, including GameSpot, IGN, The Guardian, Destructoid, Eurogamer, Bloomberg, and Game Informer, have awarded the game perfect scores, highlighting its obsession with minutia, uncompromising engineering, and the compelling narrative of its co-protagonists, Jason and Lucia.

Critics describe GTA 6 as a 'generational game' that pushes the boundaries of technical and artistic detail, offering an impossibly deep and detailed sandbox ripe for endless experimentation. The game is available on **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S**, with a Standard Edition priced at $80 and an Ultimate Edition at $100. Its release date was November 19, marking a monumental moment in gaming history.
