# Gamer Uncovers Potential GTA+ Pre-order Loophole for GTA 6

A Reddit user, u/MalikDMoneyy, has shared what they believe to be a 'loophole' in the GTA 6 pre-order system that could grant players multiple free months of the GTA+ subscription service. The user explained that the Grand Theft Auto 6 Ultimate Edition includes one complimentary month of GTA+. They discovered that upon canceling their pre-order, this GTA+ license is wiped. However, re-pre-ordering the Ultimate Edition triggers a fresh license, effectively granting another free month of GTA+.

The user suggests that by repeatedly canceling and re-pre-ordering the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition every month until the game's projected launch on November 19, 2026, players could accumulate several free months of GTA+. Based on the German GTA+ cost of €7.99 per month, the user calculated that performing this action for '4 months' would result in a saving of exactly **€31.96** in subscription fees, effectively 'stealing' value from Rockstar's pockets. This claim was submitted on Reddit and has not been officially confirmed or addressed by Rockstar Games.
