# Gamers Turn Film Critics: GTA 6 Extended Look Gets Humorous 'Movie Reviews' on Letterboxd

The recent Grand Theft Auto VI extended-look trailer, which premiered on Netflix, has become an unexpected sensation on Letterboxd, a social media platform dedicated to film journaling. Users are humorously treating the 25-minute advertisement as a movie, logging it, rating it, and offering up mock reviews.

Notable critiques include user eddyburback's satirical take, describing the 'film's' structure as a mess and its narrative vague, while also praising the 'incredible gameplay.' Another top review humorously references the ongoing GTA 6 leaks by noting the trailer starts with 'Cyberleek's execution.' Memes are also prevalent, with users like Albert quipping 'GTA in Letterboxd before GTA VI.' Some users, however, question the premise, asking 'So we are logging ads now?'

While most reviews are lighthearted, some, like Stephanie Gillespie's one-star critique, offer serious commentary, lamenting the trailer as 'limply edited-together footage' rather than a deep dive into game mechanics. The trend extends beyond Letterboxd, with comparisons made between GTA 6 character Jason Duval's voice and Tom Hardy's Bane from The Dark Knight Rises due to their similar mumbling.

Beyond the viral reviews, the article also confirms key details about GTA 6, including an estimated 80-hour playthrough, the absence of microtransactions, and a launch date of November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
