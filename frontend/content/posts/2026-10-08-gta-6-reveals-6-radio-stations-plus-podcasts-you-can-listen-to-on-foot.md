# GTA 6 Reveals 6 Radio Stations, Plus Podcasts You Can Listen To On Foot

Rockstar Games has officially revealed details about the in-game audio experience for Grand Theft Auto 6, confirming six new radio stations and, for the first time in the series, the ability to listen to on-demand podcasts and radio outside of vehicles. Protagonists Jason and Lucia will be able to access these audio features through their in-game smartphones, enhancing the immersion while exploring Leonida.

The diverse selection of confirmed radio stations caters to various musical tastes:
*   **Cocoteo FM:** Features modern Latin music, with celebrity DJs Bad Bunny and Lana del Rey lending their voices.
*   **Back Country Radio:** Offers renegade country music, including contributions from Lana del Rey's husband, Jeremy Dufrene, as Mud Dog Mike.
*   **AfroBank FM:** Showcases African music, from 20th-century classics to undiscovered gems.
*   **The Chamber 106.6:** Delivers a heavy metal mix of classic and modern tracks.
*   **Flash FM:** Provides easy listening with a selection of pop hits.
*   **Dirty South Classics:** Focuses on regional rap and hip-hop anthems.

This announcement builds on Rockstar's previous hints about the 'next evolution of in-game radio.' Furthermore, a collaboration with Spotify is underway to promote GTA 6, and a dedicated GTA 6 album featuring 34 original songs, including new music from Keith Richards and Travis Scott, has been announced. More specifics regarding the in-game podcasts are expected to be unveiled soon. In related news, a known leaker recently shared 25 minutes of new gameplay footage.
