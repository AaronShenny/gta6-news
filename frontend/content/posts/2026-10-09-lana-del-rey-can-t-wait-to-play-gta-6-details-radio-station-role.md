# Lana Del Rey 'Can't Wait' to Play GTA 6, Details Radio Station Role

Eleven-time Grammy-nominated artist Lana Del Rey has shared her immense excitement for the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6, where she will feature prominently as a co-host on one of the game's in-game radio stations. Del Rey, known for her song 'Video Games,' will portray the character Delta Dawne on the 'Back Country' radio station, co-hosting with country star Morgan Wallen (as MW). Adding to the family affair, her husband, Jeremy Dufrene, will also lend his voice to the station as Mud Dog Mike. Speaking to Rolling Stone, Del Rey described her involvement as 'super special and a privilege,' eagerly anticipating the game's release: 'Can’t wait to get into the game and start playing.' This continues the GTA franchise's tradition of integrating real-world music talent, with other notable musicians like Bad Bunny, and Slayer's Kerry King and Tom Arya also confirmed for various stations. Players will be able to enjoy a rich audio experience, listening to music in vehicles and podcasts on foot throughout Vice City. GTA 6 is slated for release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, marking the culmination of a development period stretching back to 2015.
