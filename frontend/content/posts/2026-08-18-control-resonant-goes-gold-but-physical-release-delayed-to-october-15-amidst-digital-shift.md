## FAQ
**When is the digital release date for Control Resonant?**
The digital version of Control Resonant will be released on September 24, 2024.

**When will the physical edition of Control Resonant be released?**
The physical disc edition of Control Resonant has been delayed and will be available starting October 15, 2024.

**Can I play Control Resonant early?**
Yes, players who pre-order the digital deluxe edition of Control Resonant on PlayStation 5 will receive two days of early access.

**Will Control Resonant have a parry mechanic?**
No, Control Resonant will not feature a parry mechanic. Remedy intends for players to focus on offensive abilities and powers.

**What platforms will Control Resonant be available on?**
Control Resonant is set to release on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.
