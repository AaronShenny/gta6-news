# GTA 6 & RDR2: Fans Debate What Should (and Shouldn't) Be Copied

A recent discussion on Reddit's r/GTA6 subreddit, initiated by user u/Toney520, has sparked a lively debate among fans regarding the influence of Red Dead Redemption 2 (RDR2) on the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6. While acknowledging RDR2's exceptional open-world detail, the original poster emphasized that not all its mechanics would translate well to the faster-paced nature expected of a GTA title. The core of the discussion revolves around what elements from RDR2—such as detailed worlds, NPC behavior, random encounters, and exploration—should be embraced, and conversely, which aspects, particularly those that slow down routine actions for realism, should be left behind. The community is invited to choose one RDR2 feature to bring into GTA 6 and one to discard.

## Key Takeaways
- Red Dead Redemption 2 is praised for its detailed open world and immersive elements.
- The core debate is whether RDR2's slower, realistic mechanics suit GTA 6's expected faster pace.
- Fans are asked to identify one RDR2 feature they want in GTA 6 and one they want to leave out.
- The discussion highlights a desire for detailed worlds and NPC behavior but not necessarily slow, realistic routine actions.
