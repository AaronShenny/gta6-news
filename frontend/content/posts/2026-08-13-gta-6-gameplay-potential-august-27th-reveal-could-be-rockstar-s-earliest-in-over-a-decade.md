# GTA 6 Gameplay: Potential August 27th Reveal Could Be Rockstar's Earliest in Over a Decade

A detailed comparison of Rockstar Games' previous gameplay reveal timelines indicates that a *potential* Grand Theft Auto VI gameplay showcase on August 27th would be an unusually early unveiling. Historically, Rockstar has released first gameplay footage closer to the game's official launch. For instance, Grand Theft Auto V's gameplay arrived 70 days before its September 17th release, and Red Dead Redemption 2's footage was shown 78 days prior to its October 26th launch. With GTA VI slated for a November 19th release, an August 27th reveal would mean gameplay footage is available 84 days in advance. This timing would make it the earliest pre-release gameplay reveal for a major Rockstar title since the original Red Dead Redemption in 2010, challenging the common perception among fans that gameplay reveals for Rockstar titles are always delayed.

- A rumored GTA 6 gameplay reveal on August 27th would be 84 days before its November 19th release.
