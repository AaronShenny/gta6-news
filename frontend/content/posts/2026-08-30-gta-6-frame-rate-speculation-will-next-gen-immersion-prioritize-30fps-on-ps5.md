# GTA 6 Frame Rate Speculation: Will Next-Gen Immersion Prioritize 30FPS on PS5?

A recent Reddit post by user u/Impressive-Power8187 on the r/GTA6 subreddit has sparked discussion regarding the anticipated frame rate for Grand Theft Auto 6. The user speculates that GTA 6, despite being a 'next-gen' title developed for platforms like the PlayStation 5, will likely launch targeting a performance of 30 frames per second (fps). The core argument presented is that developers are currently focused on delivering deeply 'immersive features' and high environmental density, rather than optimizing for higher frame rates.

The post further suggests that achieving higher frame rates, such as 60fps, is considered a technical 'switch' that can be 'turned on once next gen systems come out.' This implies a potential strategy where groundbreaking visual fidelity and expansive world detail take precedence during the initial release on current-gen hardware, with the possibility of performance enhancements arriving with future console iterations or highly optimized PC versions. This perspective highlights a development philosophy that prioritizes a rich, detailed experience over raw performance numbers at launch.
