# GTA 6 Day/Night Cycle: Players Debate Ideal In-Game Time Progression

A recent Reddit discussion on the r/GTA6 subreddit has sparked a conversation among fans regarding the ideal speed for the in-game day/night cycle in the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6. User u/Desperate_Report_695 initiated the thread, pointing out that previous GTA titles have featured relatively fast day/night transitions.

The core of the debate centers on finding a balance between enhancing immersion and maintaining gameplay convenience. With the highly detailed world of 'Leonida' expected in GTA 6, a slower day cycle could allow players more time to appreciate the environment, enjoy extended road trips, and fully experience dynamic events like sunsets. This would contribute to a deeper sense of realism and presence within the game world.

However, the counter-argument highlights potential drawbacks of overly slow time progression. Players might find themselves waiting tedious amounts of time for specific missions that require certain times of day or for nighttime-exclusive events to trigger. The original poster suggested an in-game day duration of approximately one real-world hour as a potential sweet spot, offering enough time to soak in the world without making gameplay feel sluggish or inconvenient. The discussion invites other players to share their preferences on this often-overlooked aspect of open-world game design.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA games traditionally feature fast day/night cycles.
- The detailed world of GTA 6 (Leonida) prompts a discussion on slowing down the cycle for better immersion.
- Longer in-game days could enhance appreciation for the world, road trips, and sunsets.
- Overly long days might lead to tedium when waiting for time-specific missions or events.
- The original poster suggests an in-game day length of approximately one real-world hour as a balanced option.
