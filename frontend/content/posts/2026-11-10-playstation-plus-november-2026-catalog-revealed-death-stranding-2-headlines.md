# PlayStation Plus November 2026 Catalog Revealed: Death Stranding 2 Headlines

The PlayStation Plus Game Catalog for November 2026 has been officially unveiled, bringing a total of 10 new titles for Essential and Premium subscribers. These games will begin rolling out starting November 11, with some regional variations for US and UK subscribers later in the month.

Headlining this month's additions is **Death Stranding 2: On the Beach**, Hideo Kojima's unique post-apocalyptic open-world adventure. For those seeking a different kind of sandbox experience, **Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii** offers its signature blend of strange, melodramatic, and action-packed gameplay starring Majima.

Other notable titles joining the catalog include the **Dragon Quest 3 HD-2D Remake**, the survival-action game **Atomfall**, and the intense first-person shooter **Trepang 2**. Fans of multiplayer FPS can dive into the massive battles of **Hell Let Loose**, while sports enthusiasts can enjoy **Cricket 2026** and **MotoGP 26**.

PlayStation Plus Premium subscribers receive two additional classics: **Soul Reaver 2** and **Freedom Wars Remastered**.

In other news, Sony has announced the discontinuation of the PlayStation Plus PC app, effective December 10, 2026, citing its use by fewer than 0.4% of subscribers.

Separately, all PlayStation Plus subscribers can still claim the trio of Essential free games until November 30: **Dead Island 2**, **Tomb Raider 4-6 Remastered**, and **Monster Train 2**.

## Key Takeaways
- The PlayStation Plus Game Catalog for November 2026 adds 10 new games for subscribers.
- Death Stranding 2: On the Beach is the headline title for this month's catalog additions.
- Other significant games include Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii, Dragon Quest 3 HD-2D Remake, and Trepang 2.
- PS Plus Premium members gain access to classic titles Soul Reaver 2 and Freedom Wars Remastered.
- The PlayStation Plus cloud-streaming app on PC will be retired on December 10, 2026.
- Essential free games for November, including Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider 4-6 Remastered, and Monster Train 2, are available until November 30.
