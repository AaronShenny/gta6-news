# Community Debates Rockstar's Post-GTA 6 Future: Sequels vs. New IP

A recent post on the r/GTA6 subreddit has ignited a discussion among fans regarding Rockstar Games' development strategy post-Grand Theft Auto 6. The core question posed to the community is whether they prefer Rockstar to continue with more sequels to established franchises or venture into entirely new intellectual properties. One user's comment highlighted a common sentiment, suggesting that non-GTA titles, like a potential 'Midnight Club 5,' might not be considered 'GTA Big' in terms of commercial scale or development priority. This comment underscores the broader conversation about Rockstar's potential to innovate with new games versus leveraging the proven success of its existing, highly popular titles.

## Key Takeaways
- Fans are actively discussing Rockstar Games' future plans after Grand Theft Auto 6.
- The primary debate centers on whether Rockstar should prioritize developing more sequels or entirely new intellectual properties.
- Concerns exist that non-GTA sequels might not achieve the same commercial scale or receive the same level of attention as the Grand Theft Auto series.
