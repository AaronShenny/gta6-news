# Rockstar Lays Down the Law: Comprehensive Modding Guidelines Revealed for GTA 6 and Beyond

Rockstar Games has published a comprehensive set of modding guidelines that will apply to upcoming titles like Grand Theft Auto VI, as well as their existing game catalog. These guidelines aim to protect Rockstar's intellectual property, maintain the integrity of their online services, and regulate how mods are created and distributed.

Key restrictions include prohibiting the alteration or expansion of official storylines, plots, missions, and characters, or combining assets from different Rockstar titles. Multiplayer modding is strictly limited to Rockstar's official licensed Creator Platform or direct licensing. Furthermore, modders are forbidden from porting games to unreleased platforms or creating cheat software that interferes with official online services. The guidelines also explicitly state that modders cannot use Rockstar's IP or logos to imply endorsement, nor can they use protected third-party intellectual property without permission. Modifying or reusing in-game voiceovers, or using real-world likenesses without consent, is also banned. Finally, monetization of mods is exclusively permitted through Rockstar Games' official Creator Platform; all other modding must remain strictly non-commercial.

## Key Takeaways
- Do not alter or expand Rockstar's official storylines, characters, or established continuity.
- Do not combine IP (maps, characters, assets) from one Rockstar title into another.
- Multiplayer modding is only permitted via Rockstar's official Creator Platform or direct licensing.
- Do not modify games to operate on unreleased consoles or platforms.
- Do not create or use cheat menus or software that interferes with official online services.
- Do not use Rockstar IP or logos to suggest endorsement or affiliation.
- Do not use protected third-party intellectual property (art, music, real-world locations) without permission.
- Do not modify or reuse in-game voiceovers, or use real-world voices/likenesses without written permission.
- Monetization of mods is exclusive to Rockstar's Creator Platform; all other modding must be non-commercial.

## FAQ
**Can I create a mod that adds a new storyline to GTA 6?**
No, the guidelines explicitly state that you cannot expand or modify Rockstar Games' official storylines, plots, missions, characters, or other elements of their established continuity.

**Am I allowed to port GTA 6 to a console where it hasn't been officially released?**
No, the guidelines prohibit modifying Rockstar games to operate on consoles, hardware devices, or other platforms where they have not been officially released by Rockstar Games.

**Can I monetize my GTA 6 mod?**
Monetization is only allowed through Rockstar Games’ official licensed Creator Platform. Unless permitted via this platform, all modding must be strictly non-commercial.

**Can I use characters or maps from Red Dead Redemption in a GTA 6 mod?**
No, the guidelines state that you cannot make mods that combine maps, locations, characters, storylines, in-game assets, or other IP from one Rockstar Games title into another title.

**Are cheat menus for GTA Online allowed?**
No, you are not allowed to create, share, or use cheat menus or other software that interferes with Rockstar Games’ official multiplayer, online services, or licensed Creator Platform.
