# GTA 6's Industry-Defining Launch Has Cyberpunk 2 Director Joking About Cancellation

Grand Theft Auto 6 has undoubtedly set a new benchmark for open-world gaming, deeply impressing developers across the industry. Igor Sarzyński, creative director for Cyberpunk 2, jokingly declared the cancellation of his own project, stating that "GTA 6-induced-open-world-game-dev-depression is real." He described GTA 6 as "probably the single greatest artistic achievement in history," a "technological and creative miracle" due to its scale, density, coherence, and thoughtfulness.However, Sarzyński quickly clarified that Cyberpunk 2 is not actually canceled, emphasizing that their game will offer a "different, unique flavor" compared to Rockstar's latest masterpiece. He acknowledged GTA 6's role in expanding what's possible in gaming, calling it a "captivating vision flawlessly executed."Other notable industry figures have echoed this sentiment. Michael Barclay, Naughty Dog's world design director, likened GTA 6 to a "Bugatti Veyron" for its technological advancement. David Goldfarb, director of Payday 2, expressed astonishment, and Del Walker, character artist for The Last of Us: Part 2, even joked about "black magic" being involved in its creation.The article also briefly touches upon Cyberpunk 2077's strong performance in 2026, boosted by the Edgerunners anime and a significant update. Cyberpunk 2 game director Gabe Amatangelo noted the sequel is evolving beyond its initial vision. Meanwhile, GTA 6 itself has had an extraordinary launch, selling 44.9 million units in its opening weekend and generating $4 billion, with its first post-launch patch already live.
