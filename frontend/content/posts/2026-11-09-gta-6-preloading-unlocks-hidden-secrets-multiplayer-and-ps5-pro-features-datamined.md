# GTA 6 Preloading Unlocks Hidden Secrets: Multiplayer and PS5 Pro Features Datamined

The highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 has officially begun its preloading phase, initially rolling out in Australia and New Zealand, with global availability commencing at midnight local time. This early access has allowed eager dataminers to delve into the game's files, unearthing intriguing details not yet officially revealed by Rockstar Games.

Among the most significant discoveries are numerous references to multiplayer functionalities, including terms like "join Session," "launch Activity," and "launch Multiplayer Activity." While Rockstar has previously stated GTA 6 will be a single-player experience at launch, these findings strongly suggest an online component akin to a new GTA Online is in the works, though likely slated for a post-launch release, possibly in early 2027 according to industry experts. Further fueling speculation, the "Story" mode appears as an individual download within the game's structure, leading to theories that GTA 6's narrative and online experiences could be separate entities.

Beyond multiplayer, datamining efforts have also pointed towards potential enhancements for the PlayStation 5 Pro. Files reportedly contain references to 120Hz support and multi-frame super resolution, known as PSSR on the PS5 Pro. It's important to note that Rockstar has yet to announce any specific PS5 Pro features for GTA 6, and all previously released trailers were captured on a standard PS5.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Due to a non-simultaneous worldwide release, players in the US will be among the last to access the game.
