# Danish Casting Call Fuels GTA 6 Commercial Speculation

A recent Facebook post from a Danish casting agency has ignited fervent speculation within the Grand Theft Auto community. The agency is actively seeking extras for what it describes as a "GTA commercial," with filming slated for November 12 in Zealand, Denmark. The call specifically targets a diverse range of individuals, including women aged 60-80, dark-skinned men 50-70, white men 40-60, and men with tattoos and muscular physiques aged 30-50. While the casting call itself only mentions a "GTA commercial" without specifying a game title, the context of the post appearing in the r/GTA6 subreddit, coupled with the immense anticipation for the next installment, has led many to believe this is an early sign of a live-action marketing campaign for Grand Theft Auto 6. The Reddit user who discovered the post questioned if similar casting calls have surfaced in other countries, hinting at the potential for a broad international promotional effort by Rockstar Games. This development, though not a direct confirmation, adds to the growing buzz around the highly anticipated title.
