# GTA VI Cameos: Fan Theories on Which Past Characters Could Return

With Grand Theft Auto VI on the horizon, the gaming community is actively speculating about potential character cameos from previous titles, a tradition Rockstar Games has upheld throughout its HD Universe. Historically, GTA V brought back several familiar faces from GTA IV and its DLCs, such as Johnny, Packie, the ULP Guy, Karen Deaniels (Michelle), Lazlow, and Rocco. This precedent has sparked discussions about who from GTA IV, Episodes from Liberty City, Online, V, and Chinatown Wars might reappear in GTA VI.A Reddit user, /u/Bubbly_Outcome5016, put forward their top five picks for returning characters:*   **Martin Madrazo** (from GTA V)*   **Jimmy De Santa** (from GTA V)*   **Yusuf Amir** (from GTA IV: The Ballad of Gay Tony)*   **Luis Lopez** (from GTA IV: The Ballad of Gay Tony)*   **Phil Collins** (as himself, a bonus pick)The article concludes by inviting readers to ponder whether Rockstar will continue this trend of weaving in characters from Los Santos or Liberty City, or if the 13-year gap since GTA V and the introduction of new protagonists, Lucia and Jason, will lead to a completely fresh start.

## Key Takeaways
- Rockstar Games has a history of bringing back characters from previous GTA titles into new installments.
- GTA V featured cameos from characters originating in GTA IV and its DLCs, including Johnny, Packie, and Lazlow.
- Fan speculation suggests potential GTA VI cameos could include Martin Madrazo, Jimmy De Santa, Yusuf Amir, and Luis Lopez.
- One user even speculates about a bonus cameo from Phil Collins.
- The community is debating whether GTA VI will continue this tradition or opt for a completely fresh start with new protagonists Lucia and Jason.
