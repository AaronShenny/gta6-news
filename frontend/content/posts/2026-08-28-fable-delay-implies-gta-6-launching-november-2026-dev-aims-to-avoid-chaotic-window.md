# Fable Delay Implies GTA 6 Launching November 2026, Dev Aims to Avoid 'Chaotic Window'

Microsoft's highly anticipated *Fable* reboot has been delayed to February 23, 2027. While the news might initially seem disappointing, the game's director, Ralph Fulton, expressed significant relief over the new launch timing. In an interview with IGN, Fulton stated he feels 'great' about the shift, emphasizing that it helps *Fable* avoid a 'chaotic window' that was originally slated for late 2026.

Though Fulton did not explicitly name *Grand Theft Auto 6* in this particular interview, the article points out that he previously did so in June, confirming the delay was to avoid a 'really congested period' and 'obviously avoiding Grand Theft Auto.' The 'chaotic window' Fable is now sidestepping reportedly includes *Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4* (October 23, 2026) and, notably, *Grand Theft Auto 6* (November 19, 2026).

Fulton highlighted the strategic importance of the delay, stating that a 'much less contested window allows us the opportunity to go out and really make a moment for ourselves, and I think the attention this game deserves.' This move underscores the immense anticipated impact of *GTA 6* on the gaming landscape, with other major titles adjusting their schedules to avoid direct competition. Despite some rough-looking gameplay footage from Gamescom, GameSpot's hands-on impressions suggest the combat is not accurately represented by the leaked video.

*Fable* is set to launch on Xbox Series X|S, PS5, and PC in February 2027.
