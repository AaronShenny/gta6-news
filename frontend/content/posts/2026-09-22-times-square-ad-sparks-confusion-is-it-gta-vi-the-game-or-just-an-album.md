# Times Square Ad Sparks Confusion: Is It GTA VI The Game, Or Just An Album?

An intriguing advertisement featuring the prominent 'GTAVI' logo recently appeared in New York's iconic Times Square. Spotted on a Monday and shared by Reddit user /u/Imango_productions, the massive billboard initially caused a stir among eager fans hoping for news on the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI game. However, closer inspection and the accompanying text revealed that the advertisement was specifically promoting a 'newly released GTAVI album,' rather than Rockstar Games' next installment in the beloved open-world franchise. This clarification aims to manage expectations, as the ad is for a musical release, not a game announcement or tease.
