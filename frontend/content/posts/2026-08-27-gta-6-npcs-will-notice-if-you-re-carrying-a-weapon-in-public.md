# GTA 6 NPCs Will Notice If You’re Carrying A Weapon In Public

Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to revolutionize player interaction with its world, introducing significantly more perceptive non-player characters (NPCs). Unlike previous titles where players could openly brandish weaponry without consequence, GTA 6's NPCs will now actively notice if you're carrying a weapon in public, potentially leading them to call the police. This new mechanic, confirmed by a preview in The New York Times, adds a fresh layer of realism and strategic consideration for players navigating Vice City.

Further enhancing the game's challenge, the six-star Wanted level is making a return, as disclosed in Netflix's extended GTA 6 preview special. While simply holding a weapon will attract attention, firing one, especially a rocket launcher, will undoubtedly escalate the situation. Beyond these gameplay changes, GTA 6 is also bringing a massive open world, with its map reportedly three times larger than Red Dead Redemption 2's. It will also feature an honor system, similar to RDR2, which tracks player behavior and violence.

Despite some mixed reactions to the male antihero Jason's vocal stylings, other aspects of the game have been highly praised, including the ability to interact with dogs and a potentially complex love story between the co-leads, Jason and Lucia. Grand Theft Auto 6 is slated for release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
