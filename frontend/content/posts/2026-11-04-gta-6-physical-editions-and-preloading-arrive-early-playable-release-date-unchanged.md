# GTA 6 Physical Editions and Preloading Arrive Early, Playable Release Date Unchanged

Rockstar Games has announced an adjustment to the release schedule for *Grand Theft Auto 6*, allowing players to purchase physical 'code-in-a-box' editions and begin preloading digital versions two days earlier than initially planned. As of November 10, fans can get their hands on the game's physical packaging, a change from the previous November 12 date. Simultaneously, preloading for both digital and code-in-a-box copies has also been advanced to November 10.

Crucially, this accelerated availability does not impact the actual playable release date. Players will still need to wait until November 19 to dive into the world of GTA 6. Rockstar has not provided any explanation for these changes, nor did they respond to GameSpot's inquiries for comment.

The launch will not be simultaneous worldwide, meaning players in the US will be among the last to gain access. Furthermore, Rockstar confirmed that the $100 Ultimate Edition of GTA 6 will be an exclusively digital offering, not available in physical stores. This move, alongside the general shift away from disc-based releases, is seen as a strategy to maximize revenue.

*Grand Theft Auto 6* is slated for release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Despite significant player interest in a potential online component, the game is currently confirmed as a single-player experience, with no multiplayer aspect announced at this time.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 physical editions and digital preloading dates have been moved up to November 10.
- The actual playable release date for GTA 6 remains November 19, unaffected by the early availability.
- Rockstar Games has not provided an explanation for the changes to the release schedule.
- The $100 Ultimate Edition of GTA 6 will be sold exclusively as a digital download, not in physical stores.
- GTA 6 is confirmed as a single-player game for its November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with no multiplayer component announced yet.
