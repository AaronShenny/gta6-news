# GTA 6's Hyper-Realistic Sweating System Revealed: Characters Will Perspire Based on Effort

Rockstar Games is taking its commitment to realism to an unprecedented level in Grand Theft Auto 6 with the introduction of a sophisticated, context-specific sweating system. As reported by journalist Jason Schreier, protagonists Lucia and Jason will dynamically perspire based on their physical exertion and environmental conditions.This intricate detail means characters will sweat more when engaging in strenuous activities like dancing vigorously in a nightclub or working out at the gym. The system will also account for environmental factors, such as the intense heat of GTA 6's South Florida-inspired setting. This level of granular detail is cited as one reason for the game's extended development cycle.While groundbreaking for the Grand Theft Auto series, the concept of realistic in-game sweat isn't entirely new, with titles like EA Sports' Madden and 2K Sports' NBA 2K franchises having implemented similar visual enhancements in the past. Beyond just bodily fluids, Rockstar's pursuit of authenticity for GTA 6 has included real-world research, such as police ridealongs and visits to strip clubs, all while striving to strike a balance between immersive simulation and an enjoyable gameplay experience.Grand Theft Auto 6 is slated for release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
