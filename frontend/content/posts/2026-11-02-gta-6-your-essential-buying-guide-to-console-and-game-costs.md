# GTA 6: Your Essential Buying Guide to Console and Game Costs

Rockstar's highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6* is set to launch on November 19, exclusively for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Players looking to dive into Vice City will need to factor in potentially steep hardware costs, as current-gen console prices have seen significant increases since their initial release.A new Xbox Series S, the most affordable entry point, now costs $500, a $200 jump from its launch price. Xbox Series X models range from $750 (digital) to $800 (disc drive), while PlayStation 5 options span from $600 (digital) to $900 for the high-performance PS5 Pro with a disc drive. Savvy shoppers can find certified refurbished consoles directly from Sony and Microsoft, with prices starting at $500 for a PS5 and $400 for an Xbox Series S. It's important to note that *GTA 6* will not be available on PC until a rumored early 2027 release, and there are no plans for a Switch 2 version.The game itself will set players back $80 for the Standard Edition or $100 for the Ultimate Edition, which includes exclusive in-game cosmetics and shops. Both editions are digital-only, with even "physical" retail copies providing just a download code. This digital-exclusive strategy allows Rockstar to maximize profits by eliminating the secondhand market and avoiding manufacturing costs for what would likely be a multi-disc physical release, given the game's reported massive 201GB file size.Players will need a robust internet connection for the installation, with preloading scheduled to begin on November 12, a full week before launch. While *GTA 6* will initially be a strictly single-player experience, meaning PlayStation Plus or Xbox Game Pass subscriptions are unlikely to be required at launch, these services will likely become necessary for the anticipated *GTA Online* component, rumored to arrive in early 2027.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 launches November 19, exclusively for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- Console prices for new units range from $500 (Xbox Series S) to $900 (PS5 Pro), significantly higher than their launch prices. Certified refurbished options offer savings.
- The game is digital-only, costing $80 for the Standard Edition and $100 for the Ultimate Edition (which includes extras).
- A PC release is rumored for early 2027, with no plans announced for a Switch 2 version.
- GTA 6 has a reported file size of 201GB, requiring a strong internet connection for download; preloading begins November 12.
- Rockstar's digital-exclusive strategy aims to increase profits by eliminating the secondhand market and reducing manufacturing costs.
- PlayStation Plus or Xbox Game Pass subscriptions are likely not required for the single-player launch, but will probably be necessary for future GTA Online content.
