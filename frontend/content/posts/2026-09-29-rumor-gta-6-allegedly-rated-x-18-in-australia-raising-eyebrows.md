# Rumor: GTA 6 Allegedly Rated X 18+ in Australia, Raising Eyebrows

A recent Reddit post on the r/GTA6 subreddit has sparked discussion by claiming that Grand Theft Auto VI will receive an 'X 18+' rating in Australia, citing a PlayStation ad as the source. The post includes an image of its own title and states that an X 18+ rating is "strictly reserved for sexually explicit material involving actual sexual intercourse between consenting adults." This claim has raised significant questions and skepticism within the gaming community.

It is crucial to understand that Australia's classification system has distinct categories. While 'R18+' is a common rating for mature video games, allowing for high-impact themes like violence, drug use, and nudity, the 'X 18+' rating is specifically and exclusively applied to content depicting actual sexual intercourse. For a major video game title like Grand Theft Auto, which historically receives R18+ ratings for its mature content, an X 18+ classification would be unprecedented and highly improbable. Such a rating would also severely restrict its distribution and sale within Australia.

The Reddit post provides no direct evidence of the alleged 'PS ad' or any official confirmation from Rockstar Games or the Australian Classification Board. Without concrete evidence, this claim remains unsubstantiated and is likely a misunderstanding or misinterpretation of Australia's strict classification guidelines.
