# Gaming Fan Celebrates Themed Joy-Cons, Citing 'Vice City' Vibes and Good Stock Levels

A passionate gaming enthusiast, posting in the r/GTA6 subreddit, shared their profound excitement for a new set of themed Joy-Con controllers. They particularly lauded the controllers' sophisticated design, which includes not only molded palm trees but also a distinct molded logo, distinguishing them from standard printed designs. The user explicitly compared the aesthetic to a 'Vice City voucher design,' indicating a strong association with the Grand Theft Auto universe despite the product being a 'Direct exclusive' (likely referring to Nintendo Direct). A significant point of relief for the user was the apparent ample stock, which defied their expectations of scalpers quickly depleting inventory. The post concludes with anticipation for 'November 19th,' a date likely associated with the product's release or shipping.
