The reviewer likened their initial experience with GTA 6 to the awe-inspiring moment of watching *Avatar* in 2009, suggesting the game will deliver an unprecedented level of immersion and visual fidelity. This comparison emphasizes that GTA 6 is poised to be a landmark title, marking a "brand new beginning" for the series, rather than just another iteration.
