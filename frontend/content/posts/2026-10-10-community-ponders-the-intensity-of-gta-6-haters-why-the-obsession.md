# Community Ponders the Intensity of GTA 6 Haters: 'Why the Obsession?'

A recent Reddit post titled "I don't get why so many people are madly obsessed on hating on GTA 6" from u/imachoculatedonnut has sparked discussion within the community. The original poster expresses bewilderment at individuals who actively wish for GTA 6 to fail, even going so far as to declare happiness at the prospect of its flop. The author points out the irony of these 'haters' dedicating significant time and energy to discussing a game they claim not to like, often dismissing any counter-criticism as 'glazing' or 'bootlicking'. The core sentiment is one of confusion: if someone genuinely dislikes something, why do they continue to engage with it so intensely, to the point of making it a central part of their online persona?
