# PS5 And Xbox Series X|S Console Sales Surged Just Before GTA 6 Came Out

Rockstar's highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 finally launched, and new data from Circana's Mat Piscatella confirms a substantial surge in PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S console sales in the week preceding its release (ending November 14).

Demand for native gameplay, which requires a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S, drove PS5 sales up over 2.4 times compared to the same week last year, while Xbox Series X|S sales increased by over 2.3 times. Interestingly, the Switch 2 also saw a 40% sales surge compared to the same week in 2025, despite GTA 6 not being available on it.

Piscatella had previously predicted a boost but noted that the astronomical cost of new consoles might temper the dramatic increase. However, the data indicates a strong consumer response. For those looking for alternatives, streaming GTA 6 to an Xbox One or PlayStation Portal device has been identified as a workaround.

The article also highlights GTA 6's monumental debut on November 19, reporting 32.5 million copies sold and approximately $4 billion generated on day one.

## Key Takeaways
