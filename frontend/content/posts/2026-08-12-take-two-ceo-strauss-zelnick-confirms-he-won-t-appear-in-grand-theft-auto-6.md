# Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick Confirms He Won't Appear in Grand Theft Auto 6

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has officially confirmed he will not be making an appearance in the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6*. This clarification comes after his unexpected inclusion as a playable character in *WWE 2K26*, which sparked speculation among fans and media alike regarding a potential cameo in Rockstar's next blockbuster title.
Zelnick revealed in an interview with IGN that his *WWE 2K26* appearance was entirely his idea, stating he simply asked the development team to include him for fun. He participated in motion capture and described the experience as enjoyable, intending the cameo to be 'funny for insiders and maybe fun for some consumers.' Despite the game's commercial success, Zelnick acknowledged the online reaction to his *WWE 2K26* role was largely a puzzled 'Huh?', which he felt he deserved.
Responding directly to questions about a *GTA 6* role, Zelnick unequivocally stated, 'You will not.'
*Grand Theft Auto 6* is slated for release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Further details about the game, including a new trailer, are expected to be unveiled on August 27 exclusively via Netflix, a decision that has generated considerable debate among the gaming community due to its 'paywall' nature.
