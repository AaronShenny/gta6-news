# GTA 6 Leak Analysis Hints at a Potentially Epic 3-4 Hour Real-Time Day/Night Cycle

A recent analysis of the infamous 'hypercar part 2' **GTA 6 leak** has offered intriguing insights into the potential pacing of the game's in-game day/night cycle. The observation, shared by Reddit user u/Sufficient-Flan6944, focuses on a brief moment where the in-game time on the minimap updates from 7:29 PM to 7:30 PM.

According to the observer, this one-minute in-game progression occurred in approximately **7 real-time seconds**. While this initial finding suggests an in-game minute could be around 7 seconds, the analysis posits a more likely scenario where an in-game minute might actually be closer to **10 real-time seconds**. This discrepancy could be due to the time display not showing the full duration of 7:29 PM.

If an in-game minute truly translates to 10 real-time seconds, a full 24-hour cycle in GTA 6 would remarkably take around **4 real-time hours**. The article, however, proposes a more nuanced and plausible theory: the day and night cycles could operate at different speeds. For instance, a day cycle might last approximately 2 real-time hours, while a night cycle could be shorter, perhaps around 1 real-time hour (equating to roughly 5 real-time seconds per in-game minute during the night).

This would mean that a complete 24-hour cycle in GTA 6 could ultimately range between **3 to 4 real-time hours**, offering players extended periods within specific times of day, which could significantly impact gameplay, missions, and exploration.
