# GTA 6 will visibly give Jason & Lucia dark circles if they don’t sleep + NEW info

Fresh rumors regarding Grand Theft Auto VI have surfaced, hinting at an unprecedented level of detail and player agency, particularly concerning the protagonists Jason and Lucia. The leak, attributed to 'TGG,' outlines a robust system where **side activities directly influence character physical appearance, core stats, and even the bond between Jason and Lucia.**

Players can expect a highly immersive fitness system, featuring **full gym memberships, locker rooms, and pre-workout boosts** from vending machines. Weightlifting is an interactive mini-game using L2/R2 triggers, with daily caps on workouts to increase max health. Neglecting workouts will result in visible muscle loss over time, adding a layer of consequence to character maintenance. A **phone-based fitness app will track sleep quality**, with a lack of rest visibly manifesting as dark circles under the characters' eyes and slowing attribute regeneration.

Beyond personal upkeep, the world of Vice City offers a plethora of activities. **Fishing returns with the depth of Red Dead Redemption 2**, integrated with narrative, challenges, and collectibles. **Hunting is fully monetized**, allowing players to track animals for income. Combat sees an expansion with **distinct hand-to-hand brawling styles** (Lucia's kickboxing, Jason's wrestling) and **dynamic shooting ranges** that allow movement while firing.

Exploration extends to the depths with **scuba diving**, complete with an oxygen gauge, for discovering shipwrecks and marine life. Underwater swimming automatically converts stamina into a trainable lung capacity. **Kayaking** is fully playable and even serves as a casual date activity for Jason and Lucia.

Sports enthusiasts will find **basketball with manual aiming**, pool tables in various establishments, and returning **mini-golf and full island golf courses**, all potentially boosting the 'focus' stat. Adrenaline junkies can enjoy **skydiving and base jumping** with a streamlined HUD.

Vehicle customization and racing are also enhanced, with **Wyman's classic car collection** requiring players to find scattered parts, and **street racing** encompassing circuit tracks, off-road events, and nitrous-fueled night sprints. For downtime, players can hit nightclubs, strip clubs, visit a zoo, or watch new animated shows on safehouse TVs.
