# Miami Heat Brings Vice City's Cluckin' Bell and Burger Shot to Life on Concourse

The Miami Heat NBA team has announced a unique, limited-time event, bringing two iconic fictional fast-food establishments from the Grand Theft Auto universe to life. Fans attending games will have the opportunity to "Get a taste of Vice City’s Cluckin’ Bell and Burger Shot restaurants" directly on the concourse of their arena. This activation directly references "Vice City," strongly hinting at the setting of the upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI, which is widely rumored to be set in a modern-day Vice City (Miami equivalent). This initiative blurs the lines between virtual and real-world experiences, offering a nostalgic nod to long-time GTA fans and building excitement for the franchise's future.
