# EA Pleads with Players: Don't Delete Madden 27 for Massive GTA 6!

Rockstar Games' highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6* is set to launch on November 19, demanding a staggering 212GB of storage space. This colossal file size has prompted an unusual plea from Electronic Arts, who are actively trying to prevent players from deleting *Madden NFL 27* to accommodate the new title.

*Madden NFL 27* has reportedly begun displaying a "don't delete" pop-up message to players. This message, framed as an "[Encrypted Signal]," hints at a "high-stakes run" and an "insider" signal on November 17, urging players not to "leak this transmission." This cryptic message is understood to be a direct reference to *GTA 6*'s impending release.

Further cementing their efforts, EA also announced on *Madden NFL 27*'s official X (formerly Twitter) account on November 10 that a "throwback Madden 97 update" will arrive on November 18. This update promises classic playbooks, 16-bit gameplay, and the All '97 Team, clearly timed to coincide with the *GTA 6* launch window.

The article highlights the significant storage challenge posed by *GTA 6*. While *Madden NFL 27* requires approximately 60GB, *GTA 6*'s 212GB footprint means that a standard PS5 (with ~667GB usable space) could quickly run out of room, especially for players with other large recent titles like *The Blood of Dawnwalker* (50GB), *Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen* (81GB), *Phantom Blade Zero* (135GB), and *Valor Mortis* (60GB). Future releases like *Final Fantasy VII Revelation* (potentially 200GB) further exacerbate this issue.

This unusual marketing tactic comes amidst a period of significant restructuring for EA, including recent layoffs of 400 employees and the closure of BioWare. Despite these changes, EA has reiterated its commitment to the *Madden* franchise, with a new title codenamed Project Gridiron in development, though concerns exist about its potential direction.
