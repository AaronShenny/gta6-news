# GTA 6 Looks Like It Features This Popular TV Show Apartment

Grand Theft Auto 6, set in the familiar Vice City (a fictionalized Miami), seems to be taking inspiration from popular culture in a very direct way. Keen-eyed fans have reportedly identified an apartment building within the game that strongly resembles Dexter Morgan's residence from the hit TV show *Dexter*. The real-world location, the Bay Harbor Club condos in Miami, served as the exterior for Dexter's apartment in the series, and comparison images shared by fans highlight the striking similarities with the in-game rendition.
This discovery has sparked speculation among the community, with some theorizing about potential in-game references, character cameos, or satirical nods to *Dexter* itself. Interestingly, the second season of *Dexter Resurrection* is expected to air around GTA 6's November 19 release date for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
Rockstar, however, has stated its intention to be 'careful' with satire to prevent the game from feeling dated, specifically avoiding topics like former US President Donald Trump or the COVID-19 pandemic.
Beyond this intriguing architectural Easter egg, recent revelations about GTA 6 confirm a massive open world, with the map reportedly three times larger than Red Dead Redemption 2's, an estimated 80-hour main story playthrough, the return of six-star wanted levels, and even the ability to interact with dogs.
