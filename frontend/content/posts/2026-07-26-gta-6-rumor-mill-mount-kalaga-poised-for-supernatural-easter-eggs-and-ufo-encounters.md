The Brown Mountain Lights are mysterious glowing orbs that appear above certain mountains in North Carolina, with various explanations ranging from natural phenomena to UFOs and alien activity.

Mount Kalaga's real-world counterpart, Table Rock Mountain, is a known site for the Brown Mountain Lights phenomenon, making it a plausible source of inspiration for Rockstar Games to include similar mysteries in GTA 6.

Yes, Rockstar has a history of incorporating supernatural and extraterrestrial Easter eggs, including UFO encounters at Mount Chiliad in GTA V and Mount Shann in Red Dead Redemption 2.

Cal Hampton is speculated to be a conspiracy theorist character who could potentially connect players like Jason and Lucia to paranormal missions related to the mysteries around Mount Kalaga.
