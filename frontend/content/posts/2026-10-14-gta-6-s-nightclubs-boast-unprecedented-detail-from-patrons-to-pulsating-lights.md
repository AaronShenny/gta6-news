# GTA 6's Nightclubs Boast Unprecedented Detail, From Patrons to Pulsating Lights

Rockstar Games is setting a new benchmark for in-game realism with the nightclubs featured in Grand Theft Auto 6. The developer undertook extensive research, including multiple trips to Miami, to meticulously craft these social hubs within the fictional city of Leonida.

Artist Aaron Garbut revealed to Bloomberg that the development team went on police ride-alongs, visited trendy nightclubs, and interacted with dancers to gather authentic insights. This deep dive into real-world nightlife has informed an "absurd amount of detail" in the game's clubs. Players can expect locations filled with distinct animations and scripts for each patron, a wide array of unique drinks at the bar, and advanced lighting systems that dynamically sync with the music being played. Garbut described it as "layers and layers and layers of detail."

While other titles like *Hitman 3* have offered impressive nightclub environments, GTA 6 aims to elevate this specific segment of game design. This focus on immersion extends beyond nightclubs, as Rockstar has even established a dedicated studio solely for creating non-playable characters (NPCs) to populate the world. Players are encouraged to explore these detailed establishments to fully appreciate the atmosphere when the game releases. The article also briefly touches on other GTA 6 news, including Rockstar's confidence in quicker follow-up titles and debunking a $1.5 billion budget rumor.
