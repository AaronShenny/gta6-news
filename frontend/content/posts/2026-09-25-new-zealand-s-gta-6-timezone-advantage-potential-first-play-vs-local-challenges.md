# New Zealand's GTA 6 Timezone Advantage: Potential First Play vs. Local Challenges

A Reddit user from New Zealand has sparked discussion regarding the potential for Kiwi gamers to be among the first to play Grand Theft Auto 6 upon its release. The theory hinges on New Zealand's advantageous +12:00 GMT timezone, which could position it for early access during global midnight launches. However, the user quickly tempers this excitement by outlining several significant drawbacks faced by New Zealand consumers. These include a general lack of exclusive collection bundles or major marketing materials for games, the country's geographical isolation from major tech and software manufacturing hubs, and a notably high cost of living, which significantly impacts the affordability of goods like fresh produce and by extension, entertainment. The post suggests that while the timezone might offer a fleeting moment of early access, the overall gaming experience for New Zealanders is often hampered by these economic and logistical realities.
