# GTA 6 Shatters Records, Dominating Sales and Internet Traffic

Rockstar's highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 has made an explosive debut, setting numerous records across sales, internet traffic, and player engagement. On day one, the game sold over 30 million copies and generated nearly $4 billion, showcasing immense pent-up demand.

Internet service provider Comcast reported that November 18, the final day of preloading, marked its busiest day ever for network demand, with peak traffic 31% higher than an average day and 9.6% higher than its previous record. GTA 6 now accounts for five of Comcast's top 10 busiest days of all time. Remarkably, approximately 24% of all internet traffic on Comcast's network on November 18 was attributed to people downloading or playing GTA 6. Furthermore, Twitch traffic on Comcast's network surged by almost 50% and continued to climb. Comcast also noted that between November 10 and 18, PlayStation and Xbox traffic for GTA 6 pushed enough data to allow one million homes to stream The Odyssey in 4K for two days straight, leading the company to offer free speed boosts to subscribers.

Engagement data from Circana's Mat Piscatella further illustrates the game's colossal impact. On November 19, 61% of active PlayStation 5 accounts in the US engaged with GTA 6, while 56% of Xbox players globally did the same. PS5 players averaged 513 minutes of play, and Xbox players averaged 494 minutes on that day. These engagement and playtime figures were described as "absolutely bonkers," with GTA 6 dethroning Fortnite to become the No. 1 game based on these metrics.

## Key Takeaways
