# How to Play GTA 6 on PC and Mobile: Xbox and PlayStation Remote Play Confirmed

Rockstar's highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 can now be streamed to various devices, including PCs and smartphones, utilizing both Xbox and PlayStation's remote play functionalities.Microsoft has officially confirmed support for **Xbox Remote Play** for GTA 6. This service enables owners of an Xbox Series X|S console to stream the game directly to their PC, smartphone, or handheld devices like the Xbox Rog Ally. The article provides clear instructions on how to activate remote features on the Xbox console via settings and how to connect from a mobile device using the Xbox Remote Play website or from a PC/Xbox Rog Ally via the Xbox app.Similarly, the **PlayStation Store page for GTA 6** indicates support for Remote Play, allowing PlayStation 5 and PlayStation Portal owners to stream the game. Cloud streaming for PlayStation users necessitates a Premium membership.While current streaming options require ownership of a next-gen console, there's a broader vision for the future of gaming. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has stated that the increasing cost of gaming hardware is unsustainable and believes that commercial, low-latency game streaming could become a significant industry within three years, potentially expanding the effective installed base of games considerably.Despite the potential for wider reach through full cloud streaming without a console, GTA 6 has already achieved immense success, reportedly selling 32.5 million copies and generating nearly $4 billion in revenue on day one. The game is also expected to further broaden its audience with a **PC release in 2027**, alongside a new online mode that some analysts predict could rival the scale of Fortnite.

## FAQ
**Can I play GTA 6 on my PC or phone?**
Yes, you can stream GTA 6 to your PC or phone using Xbox Remote Play if you own an Xbox Series X|S, or via PlayStation Remote Play if you own a PS5.

**Do I need a console to stream GTA 6?**
Currently, yes. Xbox Remote Play requires an Xbox Series X|S, and PlayStation Remote Play requires a PS5.

**How do I enable Xbox Remote Play for GTA 6?**
Go to Settings > Devices & connections > Remote features on your Xbox console and check 'Enable remote features.'

**Will GTA 6 eventually be available for full cloud streaming without a console?**
Take-Two's CEO believes commercial low-latency streaming could be widespread within three years, potentially allowing this in the future.

**When is GTA 6 expected to release on PC?**
GTA 6 is reportedly expected to release on PC in 2027.
