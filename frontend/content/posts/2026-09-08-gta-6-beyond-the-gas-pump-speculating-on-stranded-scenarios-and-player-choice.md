# GTA 6: Beyond the Gas Pump - Speculating on Stranded Scenarios and Player Choice

A recent discussion sparked by a Reddit post delves into the potential gameplay mechanics surrounding being stranded in Grand Theft Auto 6, building on the assumption that vehicles will require fuel. The article, originally from r/GTA6, explores various 'what if' scenarios when players find themselves with an empty tank or a blown tire.

The core of the discussion revolves around the player's options. While simply abandoning a vehicle and stealing another is always a viable, classic GTA solution, the post imagines more immersive alternatives. This includes the possibility of carrying a gas can, calling for fuel delivery, or even summoning a tow truck to retrieve a cherished vehicle. Flat tires could introduce similar dilemmas, forcing players to decide whether to brave driving on a damaged wheel, attempt a roadside repair, or seek professional assistance.

Adding another layer of realism, the article speculates on dynamic NPC interactions. Stranded players might find themselves offered help by passing NPCs, or a tow truck could eventually arrive to clear the road. Conversely, players could encounter broken-down NPCs, presenting opportunities to assist, offer a ride, or, in true Grand Theft Auto fashion, exploit their vulnerability. The environment could also play a crucial role, with running out of gas in a bustling Vice City differing vastly from being stranded in a remote, desolate area at night.
