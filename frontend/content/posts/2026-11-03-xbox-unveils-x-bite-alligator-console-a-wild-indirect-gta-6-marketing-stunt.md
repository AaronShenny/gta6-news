# Xbox Unveils 'X-Bite' Alligator Console: A Wild, Indirect GTA 6 Marketing Stunt

Microsoft has unveiled a highly unusual and eye-catching marketing initiative for Grand Theft Auto 6: the **X-Bite**, a playable Xbox Series X console encased within a five-foot-long alligator sculpture. This unique creation is designed to evoke the 'pure Miami vibes' of GTA 6's Vice City setting, a clever workaround given Sony holds the primary marketing rights for the highly anticipated title.

Only one X-Bite console exists, crafted by creature animatronic specialist John Nolan Studio (known for 'The Witcher' and 'Jurassic World Dominion'). It comes complete with a custom Xbox controller featuring simulated bite marks. Microsoft explicitly states that no real alligators were used or harmed in its creation.

Gamers can enter a sweepstakes to win this one-of-a-kind console by following Xbox on social media (X, Instagram, or TikTok), reposting the sweepstakes announcement, and using the hashtag #XbiteSweepstakes. The contest runs until November 10.

Despite Sony's aggressive PS5-centric marketing for GTA 6, including claims of the game 'playing best' on PS5, Xbox remains confident. The company notes that its GTA 6 pre-order figures, while lower than PS5, align with Xbox's overall console market share. GTA 6 is set to launch on November 19 for both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and is expected to be a massive revenue driver for Microsoft.
