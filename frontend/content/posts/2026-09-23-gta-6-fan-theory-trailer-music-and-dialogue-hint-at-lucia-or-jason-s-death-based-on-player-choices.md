# GTA 6 Fan Theory: Trailer Music and Dialogue Hint at Lucia or Jason's Death Based on Player Choices

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto fan has put forth an intriguing theory regarding the potential fates of GTA 6 protagonists, Lucia and Jason. The theory, heavily reliant on interpretations of trailer dialogue and song lyrics, suggests that one of the main characters might die depending on the player's choices concerning their romantic relationship.

The core argument traces clues from both official trailers and the 'Extended Look' footage. In Trailer 1, Lucia's 'bad luck' comment while in jail is linked to the 'Devil Woman' song from the Extended Look, which features lyrics about 'bad luck' after encountering a 'cat' (metaphorically, Lucia). The first trailer's song, 'Love is a Long Road,' hints at a codependent romance, emphasizing 'Trust.'

Trailer 2's 'Hot Together' song is interpreted as the 'honeymoon phase' of their developing relationship. However, the 'Extended Look' introduces a critical metaphor through Brian's carousel story. Brian describes a friend who stayed on a carousel as it went 'too fast' and died, while Brian 'jumped off' at the right time. This is allegorized to Jason 'breaking trust' and potentially quitting, while Lucia, like the friend, might go 'too far' and get 'burned.'

The 'Devil Woman' song further solidifies the theory, with lyrics implying Lucia might be Jason's 'bad luck' and potentially 'backstabbing' him if their relationship turns romantic. The fan speculates that Rockstar might implement a relationship system similar to Red Dead Redemption 2's Honor system. If players pursue a deep romance, Jason might die for 'not letting go,' akin to staying on the carousel. Conversely, if the relationship remains professional, Lucia might meet her end for 'going too far' without Jason's tempering influence, while Jason survives by 'getting off at the right time.' This fascinating theory invites players to ponder the weight of their choices in the upcoming title.
