# GTA 6 Is Crashing On Some PS5s With CE-108255-1 Error Code

After a long wait, Grand Theft Auto 6 has launched, but a subset of PlayStation 5 players are encountering a frustrating **CE-108255-1 error code** that causes the game to crash or fail to load. This error can occur mid-game, which is particularly problematic given GTA 6's save mechanics, or prevent the game from starting entirely.

Sony's official support suggests updating the game as a potential fix, but many users report this solution is ineffective, leading to calls for an official patch from Rockstar. One Reddit user documented their experience, noting continuous crashes at a specific story point after initial normal gameplay, with Rockstar support directing them to Sony, yielding no resolution.

Online theories, including those cited by Gizmodo, speculate that the crashes might stem from GTA 6's high hardware demands, possibly affecting older PS5 consoles more significantly. Rockstar Games has not yet issued an official response to these reports, and their current GTA 6 error troubleshooting page offers generic advice, linking only to console support sites without specific guidance for this error. Players experiencing the issue are advised to submit a support ticket to Rockstar.

While these issues do not appear to be widespread, they are a significant concern for affected players. Despite these problems, GTA 6 has seen immense commercial success, reportedly selling 25 million copies and generating over $2 billion in revenue.
