# Massive GTA 6 Leak Surfaces: 25 Minutes of New Gameplay and Cutscenes Allegedly Released by Cyberleek

Just weeks before the anticipated release of Grand Theft Auto 6, notorious leaker Cyberleek has allegedly surfaced once again, releasing a substantial 25-minute video of new gameplay footage and cutscenes. This follows a period of relative silence from Cyberleek since a wave of shorter GTA 6 leaks in August.The new leak came to light after a user on the Cyberleek subreddit shared an image of an uploaded file, which other users are now attempting to download. Reports indicate that the video, titled "Extended Leek" (a clear nod to Rockstar's official "GTA 6: An Extended Look" trailer), showcases fresh gameplay and cutscenes. Notably, some commenters claim to have seen protagonist Jason running around naked, a detail that aligns with earlier confirmations of nudity in the game, including Jason in the shower and the existence of a nudist colony.Cyberleek's prior leaks caused significant issues for Rockstar, prompting a public response from the developer. With the official game release imminent, Rockstar continues to advise fans to stick to official footage to avoid spoilers.
