# GTA 6 Fan Hopes for Deeper Side Activities and Enhanced Character Hangouts

A dedicated Grand Theft Auto fan has sparked discussion on Reddit, expressing their strong desire for *Grand Theft Auto 6* to feature significantly improved side activities and character hangout systems. Drawing comparisons to *GTA 5*, the user, u/Far-Requirement121, noted that the previous installment's hangout options felt "wayyy too plain," often limited to a handful of locations before inevitably leading to chaotic shootouts. They envision a *GTA 6* where players can visit "a lot more places" and engage in "improvement in the dialogues," dreaming of scenarios like surprising friends with a commercial plane or simply "ballin with Lucia, Jason and Carl in the beach house." This sentiment highlights a community interest in more immersive and varied social interactions beyond the franchise's signature high-octane action.

## Key Takeaways
- A Reddit user is advocating for more robust side activities and character hangouts in GTA 6.
- Critiques of GTA 5's hangout system include its limited locations and repetitive nature, often ending in shootouts.
- Hopes for GTA 6 include a wider variety of places to visit and more engaging dialogue during character interactions.
- The user envisions unique interactions, such as surprising friends with a commercial plane or relaxing with rumored protagonists Lucia and Jason, and Carl.
