# GTA 6 Fuels Take-Two's Massive $8.2 Billion Revenue Forecast for FY2027

Take-Two Interactive is bracing for an exceptionally profitable financial year, running from April 1 to March 31, 2027, projecting a staggering $8.2 billion in revenue and 20% growth. This optimistic forecast is predominantly anchored by the highly anticipated launch of Grand Theft Auto 6, which remains on schedule for November. Rockstar Games, the powerhouse behind GTA 6, is expected to account for approximately 37% of this revenue, bolstered by the continued success of legacy titles like GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, alongside their lucrative microtransactions. Mobile gaming giant Zynga is projected to contribute 34% with its expansive portfolio, while the 2K label, featuring annual sports hits like NBA, PGA Tour, and WWE, is set to make up 29%. Take-Two highlighted NBA 2K26's "outstanding" year, crediting its robust live-service execution and strong microtransaction sales for sustained success. While other titles like Judas, Project Ethos, and the next BioShock are in development, their release dates are yet to be announced. Gamers can look forward to an extended, exclusive look at GTA 6 on Netflix on August 27, further building anticipation for its confirmed November debut.

## Key Takeaways
- Grand Theft Auto 6 is confirmed to launch in November.
- Take-Two Interactive forecasts $8.2 billion in revenue for its FY2027, representing 20% growth.
- Rockstar Games, led by GTA 6, is expected to generate 37% of Take-Two's total revenue.
- Zynga and 2K Games will also be significant contributors, with 34% and 29% respectively.
- An extended preview of GTA 6 will be exclusively available on Netflix on August 27.
