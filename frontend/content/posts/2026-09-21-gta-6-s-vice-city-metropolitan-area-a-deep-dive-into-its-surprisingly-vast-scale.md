# GTA 6's Vice City Metropolitan Area: A Deep Dive into its Surprisingly Vast Scale

A recent community analysis, primarily based on mapping project conclusions by Saukko505, indicates that the **Vice City Metropolitan Area (VCMA) in Grand Theft Auto VI is significantly larger than many players might initially expect.** Utilizing measurement tools, the mapping project estimates the region to be **9.28 kilometers in length and 6.11 kilometers in width**. To provide a tangible comparison, traversing the entire length of the VCMA is reportedly nearly equivalent to the vast distance from the heart of Los Santos to Paleto Bay in Grand Theft Auto V.

The article further highlights Rockstar's apparent effort to **better differentiate the various cities and towns within the VCMA**. This approach aims to avoid the 'Los Santos-Davis merge' issue perceived in GTA V, with areas like Vice Beach and Vice City potentially having their own distinct identities and even separate police forces. Additionally, the northern city within the VCMA, Waning Sands, briefly featured in Trailer 1, is described as a city in its own right with its own cluster of highrises, suggesting it will be a substantial and unique urban center.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6's Vice City Metropolitan Area (VCMA) is estimated to be 9.28 km long and 6.11 km wide, based on community mapping.

- GTA 6 is expected to feature more distinct and separate cities/towns within the VCMA, such as Vice Beach and Vice City having their own police forces.
- Waning Sands, a northern city in the VCMA, is depicted as a significant urban center with its own highrises, similar to Capri.
