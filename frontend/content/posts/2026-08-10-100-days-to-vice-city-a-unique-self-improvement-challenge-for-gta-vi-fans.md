# 100 Days to Vice City: A Unique Self-Improvement Challenge for GTA VI Fans

A unique self-improvement initiative dubbed '100 Days to Vice City' has emerged from the Grand Theft Auto VI community, proposing a structured challenge for players leading up to the game's anticipated release. Originating from Reddit user u/Unhappy-Desk-1572, the concept encourages participants to dedicate approximately 100 days before GTA VI's launch to personal growth across three core areas: 'Move,' 'Fuel,' and 'Grow.' The 'Move' pillar emphasizes physical activities such as gym workouts, running, walking, or cycling. 'Fuel' focuses on making healthier nutrition choices or learning dietary improvements. Lastly, 'Grow' encourages mental development through reading, skill acquisition, or self-reflection. The creator envisions building a dedicated app to track daily progress, facilitate check-ins, and manage weekly reviews, with checkpoints at 25, 50, 75, and 100 days. The overarching goal is not perfection, but rather to achieve noticeable personal betterment by the time players dive into Vice City. The proposal has sparked community interest, with the author actively seeking feedback on potential participation and additional elements for the challenge.
