# Stephen Root Officially Confirms Role in Grand Theft Auto VI

Veteran actor Stephen Root, known for his roles as Bill Dauterive in *King of the Hill* and Milton Waddams in *Office Space*, has officially confirmed his involvement in the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto VI*. Attending the BAFTA TV Tea Party, Root revealed to the Associated Press that he will appear in the game. He noted that his distinctive voice likely led to early speculation after the first GTA 6 rollout almost a year ago. Root is set to portray Brian Heder, a veteran drug smuggler operating in the Leonida Keys area. Heder also appears to be protagonist Jason Duval's landlord, though he doesn't charge rent. Far from a minor NPC, Brian Heder is highlighted as a key figure in *GTA 6*'s story, even featuring his own profile on the official Rockstar Games website. This profile details his continued drug-smuggling operations through his boatyard with his third wife, Lori, preferring to delegate the 'dirty work.' *Grand Theft Auto VI* is slated for release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar Games is reportedly working diligently to protect the game's secrets from drones and hackers as development continues.
