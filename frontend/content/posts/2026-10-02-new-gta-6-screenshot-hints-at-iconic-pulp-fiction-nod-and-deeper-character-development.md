# New GTA 6 Screenshot Hints at Iconic Pulp Fiction Nod and Deeper Character Development

A newly revealed screenshot from Grand Theft Auto 6 has sent fans into a frenzy, strongly suggesting a direct reference to the iconic opening diner scene from Quentin Tarantino's 1994 film, *Pulp Fiction*. Shared initially by Love magazine on Instagram and highlighted by the GTA 6 Countdown account, the image features protagonists Lucia and Jason, both gripping pistols, with an empty duffel bag nearby, mirroring the setup of Pumpkin and Honey Bunny's robbery. This reinforces the game's 'Bonnie and Clyde'-style narrative, where Lucia and Jason are a criminal duo. Rockstar Games has a history of incorporating overt movie references, such as *Scarface* in *Vice City* and *Heat* in *GTA 5*, making a *Pulp Fiction* homage a natural fit.Writers Elizabeth Gauvey-Kern and Alexa D’Agostino weighed in on the protagonists' development. Gauvey-Kern stated their goal was to make Lucia and Jason 'vulnerable, tough, flawed, right sometimes, wrong sometimes, able to learn from each other,' aiming for authenticity. D’Agostino added that focusing on 'a fully realized person with their own wants' results in 'something perfectly imperfect--with clichés, but also undeniably real.'GTA 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Ahead of its release, on November 18, the Miami Heat will sport GTA 6-inspired jerseys and play on a Vice City-themed court. The game will primarily feature Vice City (Rockstar's take on Miami) and other parts of Florida, though notably, it won't include a spoof of Walt Disney World.
