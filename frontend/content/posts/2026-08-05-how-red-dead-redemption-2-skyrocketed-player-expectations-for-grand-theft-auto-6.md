# How Red Dead Redemption 2 Skyrocketed Player Expectations for Grand Theft Auto 6

A Reddit user, u/Primary_Snow818, posits that the unparalleled success and quality of *Red Dead Redemption 2* (RDR2) are the primary drivers behind the 'sky-high expectations' currently surrounding *Grand Theft Auto 6* (GTA 6). The author suggests that, had RDR2 not been released, player expectations for GTA 6 would have been considerably lower, likely only anticipating an incremental improvement over *Grand Theft Auto V* – a task deemed 'not really a difficult task' given their assessment of GTA 5 and *Max Payne 3* as not Rockstar's strongest pre-RDR2 titles.RDR2 is highlighted as having 'raised the bar in every aspect,' establishing new benchmarks in areas such as open-world design, grounded storytelling, immersive realism, meticulous attention to detail, and rich lore. Consequently, the author now uses RDR2 as the definitive standard for GTA 6, expressing a strong desire for 'a similar or better open world, equally good and grounded storytelling, the same level of realism, the insane attention to detail, and rich lore.' This perspective underscores how RDR2's exceptional quality has cultivated increasingly 'demanding' player expectations for the upcoming Grand Theft Auto installment.

## Key Takeaways
- Red Dead Redemption 2 is identified as the main reason for the current elevated expectations for Grand Theft Auto 6.
- Without RDR2, expectations for GTA 6 would likely have been much lower, aiming only to surpass GTA 5.
- RDR2 set new industry benchmarks for open-world design, storytelling, realism, attention to detail, and lore.
- Players are now using RDR2 as the standard against which GTA 6's quality and features will be judged.
