# Rockstar Unveils New Justice Artwork, Confirms Duo's Role in GTA 6's Dynamic Score

Furthermore, Rockstar confirmed that Justice will be contributing as composers to GTA 6's original dynamic score, suggesting a unique and immersive auditory experience for players. This news highlights Rockstar's commitment to delivering a rich and diverse soundtrack, integrating renowned artists into the game's core musical identity.
