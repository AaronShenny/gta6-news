# GTA 6 to Mandate PS5 Firmware 14.22.00.00, Impacting Jailbreak Enthusiasts

Rockstar Games' highly anticipated title, Grand Theft Auto 6 (GTA 6), will reportedly require PlayStation 5 owners to update their console's firmware to at least version 14.22.00.00 to play the game. This news, initially shared via a Reddit post, has significant implications for a segment of the PS5 community.

Players who intentionally keep their consoles on older firmware versions, often for the purpose of maintaining potential jailbreak access, will be faced with a choice: update their system to meet GTA 6's requirements and consequently lose their current jailbreak capabilities, or forgo playing the game. This mandate is a common practice for new game releases, as developers often build their titles to leverage the latest system features, security patches, and performance optimizations provided by newer firmware.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 will require PlayStation 5 firmware version 14.22.00.00 or newer.
- Players on older PS5 firmware will need to update their console to play GTA 6.
- This requirement will impact users who maintain older firmware for potential jailbreak access, forcing them to update and lose access.
- The information originates from a Reddit post within the GTA 6 community.

## FAQ
**What PS5 firmware version will GTA 6 require?**
GTA 6 on PlayStation 5 will require players to have at least firmware version 14.22.00.00 installed on their console.

**Can I play GTA 6 if my PS5 is on an older firmware?**
No, players on older firmware versions will be prompted to update their PS5 to version 14.22.00.00 or newer before they can play GTA 6.

**How does this requirement affect PS5 jailbreak users?**
Players who have kept their PS5 on older firmware for jailbreak purposes will need to update their system to play GTA 6, which will result in the loss of their current jailbreak access.
