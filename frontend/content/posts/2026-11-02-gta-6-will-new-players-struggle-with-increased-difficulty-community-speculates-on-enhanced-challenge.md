# GTA 6: Will New Players Struggle with Increased Difficulty? Community Speculates on Enhanced Challenge

A recent Reddit discussion on the r/GTA6 subreddit, initiated by user RobloxSpiderGoku, raises the question of whether new players will find GTA 6 significantly more challenging than previous installments. The original poster specifically highlights concerns regarding the difficulty of evading police and executing store robberies, suggesting that these aspects will not be as easy as they were in GTA 5. The sentiment expressed is that GTA 6 appears to be adopting a more difficult and challenging overall approach, a direction welcomed by the user. The post invites community members to share their own thoughts on this anticipated increase in game difficulty.

## Key Takeaways
- Community members anticipate GTA 6 will present a higher difficulty curve for new players.
- Specific concerns revolve around increased challenge in police evasion and store robberies.
- The expected difficulty is seen as a departure from the perceived easier mechanics of GTA 5.
- Some players welcome the prospect of a more challenging gameplay experience in GTA 6.
