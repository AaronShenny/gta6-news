The post prompts a debate on whether players hope for harder enemies or a more limited weapon selection per mission in GTA 6 to increase the challenge. It's explicitly noted that the discussion does not pertain to stealth mechanics, as the user believes GTA V's stealth implementation was fundamentally flawed.
