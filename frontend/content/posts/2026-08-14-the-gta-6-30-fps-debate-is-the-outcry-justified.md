# The GTA 6 30 FPS Debate: Is the Outcry Justified?

A recent Reddit post from user /u/DieErdnuss565 addresses the ongoing debate surrounding the potential 30 frames per second (FPS) performance of Grand Theft Auto 6. The user suggests that much of the community's reaction to the rumored 30 FPS target is an overreaction. They argue that a stable 30 FPS is perfectly acceptable, drawing parallels to Rockstar's previous titles, Grand Theft Auto V and Red Dead Redemption 2, which also ran at this frame rate and were widely enjoyed. The core argument is that stability at 30 FPS is more crucial than a higher, potentially unstable, frame rate.

## Key Takeaways
- Concerns about GTA 6 potentially running at 30 FPS are considered an overreaction by some.
- Previous Rockstar titles, Grand Theft Auto V and Red Dead Redemption 2, successfully ran at 30 FPS.
- A stable 30 FPS is deemed perfectly acceptable for gameplay, according to the user.
- The focus should be on stable performance rather than just a higher frame rate.

## FAQ
**Is GTA 6 confirmed to run at 30 FPS?**
The article discusses a user's opinion on the *potential* or *rumored* 30 FPS target for GTA 6, not a confirmed specification from Rockstar Games.
