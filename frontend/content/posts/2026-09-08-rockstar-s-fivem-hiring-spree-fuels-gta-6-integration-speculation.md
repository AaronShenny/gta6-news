# Rockstar's FiveM Hiring Spree Fuels GTA 6 Integration Speculation

Rockstar Games has opened several new positions specifically for its recently acquired Cfx.re creator platforms, FiveM and RedM, fueling speculation about their potential integration into *Grand Theft Auto VI*. New job postings include "Senior Software Engineer, FiveM" roles at both Rockstar Leeds and Rockstar London, with descriptions highlighting work on the "Rockstar Creator Platform Team" to maintain low-level tech, code branches, release pipelines, performance, and stability. Additionally, a "Senior Manager, Product – Creator Platform" position in London suggests a broader, strategic initiative beyond simply maintaining existing services. Rockstar acquired Cfx.re in 2023, publicly stating its intent to support the role-playing and creator communities and enhance the services provided by Cfx.re. The ongoing recruitment drive has led many to ponder if the long-term strategy involves making Cfx.re/FiveM technology an official and integral component of Rockstar's multiplayer ecosystem for the highly anticipated *GTA VI*.

## Key Takeaways
- Rockstar Games is actively hiring for multiple FiveM-specific roles, including Senior Software Engineers.
