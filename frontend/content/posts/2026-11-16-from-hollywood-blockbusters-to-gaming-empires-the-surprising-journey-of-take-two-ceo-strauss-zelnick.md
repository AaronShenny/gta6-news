# From Hollywood Blockbusters to Gaming Empires: The Surprising Journey of Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick

Strauss Zelnick, the current CEO of Take-Two Interactive, the parent company of Rockstar Games and **GTA 6**, has a fascinating history rooted in Hollywood. At just 32, Zelnick served as president and COO of 20th Century Fox, where he played a pivotal role in bringing the beloved 1990 Christmas movie *Home Alone* to fruition. Warner Bros. had initially backed the film but pulled the plug due to budget concerns just four days into production. Under Zelnick's leadership, Fox swooped in, providing the necessary funding that led to *Home Alone* becoming a massive success, grossing over $476 million globally against a $13 million budget and earning two Oscar nominations.

Despite his success in film, Zelnick foresaw a brighter future in interactive entertainment. He voluntarily left Fox in 1993, taking a substantial pay cut to become the CEO of the nascent video game company Crystal Dynamics. His career path continued through the music industry at BMG Entertainment, where his gaming unit, BMG Interactive, notably published the original *Grand Theft Auto* in 1997. Following this, Zelnick and an investor group took over a financially struggling Take-Two, which was embroiled in accounting fraud lawsuits at the time.

Under Zelnick's guidance, Take-Two has experienced an extraordinary turnaround, with its share price soaring and its market capitalization exceeding $40 billion. While the GTA franchise remains a juggernaut, it accounts for only about 15% of the company's revenue in non-release quarters, as Take-Two boasts a diverse portfolio of major franchises including NBA 2K, Borderlands, BioShock, and Civilization. The article also notes that **GTA 6** is slated to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, reportedly having already sold close to 15 million copies and generated nearly $1.5 billion in revenue.
