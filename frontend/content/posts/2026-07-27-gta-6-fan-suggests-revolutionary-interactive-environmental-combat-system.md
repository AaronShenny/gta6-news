# GTA 6: Fan Suggests Revolutionary Interactive Environmental Combat System

A passionate fan from the GTA 6 subreddit, u/No-Speed7715, has ignited discussion with a detailed vision for an incredibly interactive combat system in the upcoming Grand Theft Auto title. Moving beyond traditional punching and shooting, the suggestion emphasizes making the entire world an active participant in every fight.

The core idea revolves around players being able to dynamically interact with their surroundings and opponents. This includes actions such as smashing non-player characters (NPCs) against car windows or bonnets, shoving them into walls, fences, or shop displays, and throwing them over railings or into dumpsters. The proposal also highlights the use of improvised weapons, suggesting players could pick up everyday objects like glass bottles, bricks, pool cues, chairs, or even traffic cones to use in brawls.

Further enhancing the immersive combat experience, the fan suggests contextual interactions where doors could be slammed into people, or enemies kicked through glass windows. The environment itself should dictate unique combat opportunities, meaning a fight in a bar would feel distinct from one in a construction site, restaurant, or beach.

Drawing parallels to Rockstar's previous success, the user points to *Red Dead Redemption 2*'s 'weighty, physical combat' as a foundation, arguing that GTA 6 could evolve this concept to make every environment feel unique during a fight, rather than encounters playing out in a repetitive manner.

## Key Takeaways
- Combat should be highly interactive with the environment, not just punching or shooting.
- Players could smash NPCs against cars, walls, fences, and shop displays.
- Ability to pick up and use improvised weapons like bottles, bricks, chairs, and traffic cones.
- Dynamic actions such as throwing enemies over railings or into dumpsters.
- Contextual environmental combat: slamming doors into people, kicking through glass windows.
- Different locations (bars, construction sites, beaches) should offer unique combat interactions.
- Builds upon *Red Dead Redemption 2*'s physical combat system for enhanced realism.
