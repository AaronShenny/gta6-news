# GTA 6 Pre-Load Details Confirmed: PS5 and Xbox Timings Revealed

Rockstar Games has officially outlined the pre-loading schedule for Grand Theft Auto VI, providing crucial information for players on both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. 

For PlayStation 5 users, pre-loading will commence at midnight in your respective timezone on November 10th, ensuring a staggered global rollout starting with New Zealand. Xbox users, however, will experience a unified pre-load start time across all regions, beginning at 12am GMT on November 10th, which translates to 7PM EST on November 9th. 

The article also highlights essential support resources for players who may encounter issues during the pre-load process. Dedicated support articles for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S are linked, alongside a comprehensive official Rockstar Pre-load FAQ article, which is expected to address most common questions and troubleshooting steps. This ensures players have direct access to assistance to prepare for the highly anticipated title.
