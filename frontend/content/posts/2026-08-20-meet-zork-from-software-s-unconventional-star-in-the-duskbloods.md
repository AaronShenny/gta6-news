# Meet Zork: From Software's Unconventional Star in The Duskbloods

While the gaming world buzzes about GTA 6 leaks, a new, decidedly peculiar character from From Software's upcoming Nintendo Switch 2 exclusive, *The Duskbloods*, is demanding attention: **Zork**. Revealed as one of the playable 'Bloodsworn' characters in the game's network test, Zork stands out with his exceedingly pear-like diving suit, an undersea cable for a weapon, and an array of bizarre abilities including howling, firing a back-mounted cannon, and launching his entire body at enemies to knock them off the map.
Hidetaka Miyazaki, From Software's president, explained Zork's inclusion, noting that while *The Duskbloods* features traditional Gothic characters, Zork represents the extreme 'wild card' designed to make the multiplayer experience feel like a 'festival' of diverse and chaotic characters. Miyazaki himself enjoys playing as Zork.
A key detail revealed is the function of the beak-like appendage on Zork's mask: it extends like a straw to suck up blood, a fitting, albeit grotesque, feature for a vampire-themed game. This unique design choice, possibly an Ultraman reference, has already garnered significant internet reaction, suggesting Zork is poised to become a fan favorite.
