# GTA 6 Unveils Colossal Map, Dynamic Weather, and Deep NPC Systems in New Game Informer Details

New insights from Game Informer's latest issue shed light on several key aspects of Grand Theft Auto 6, painting a picture of Rockstar's most ambitious open world to date.

The story is set to begin with players controlling Jason, while Lucia is still incarcerated. Upon her release, the duo embarks on a 'simple job' to achieve a wealthier life, which quickly escalates into a sprawling mystery across the entire state of Leonida. Lucia's past in Liberty City and family-related prison sentence are also hinted at.

The highly anticipated map of Leonida is now confirmed to be an unprecedented scale, **twice the size of GTA 5's map** and the largest world Rockstar has ever created. This colossal environment is divided into six distinct regions: the familiar **Vice City** (based on Miami), **Leonida Quays**, the Everglades-inspired **Grassrivers**, the tourist-trap laden **Port Gellhorn**, the industrial 'heart' of **Ambrosia**, and the sprawling **Mount Kalaga National Park**.

Environmental realism receives a significant upgrade with a **dynamic and localized weather system**. Thunderstorms can be seen in the distance while other areas remain sunny, and conditions like puddles, wet pavements, and even rainbows will dynamically appear. Wind physics have been overhauled from Red Dead Redemption 2, realistically affecting clothes, hair, items, and water surfaces. Crucially, **hurricanes** will also be present, potentially impacting gameplay through floods and impassable routes.

Interactions are expanded with the **RydeMe rideshare app**, allowing players to hail rides, chat with NPCs, and enjoy music on the go. The world will be teeming with over **170 species of wildlife**, including manatees, flamingos, alligators, and even non-native monkeys, all featuring reactive fur that can appear wet or matted. NPCs are more diverse than ever, with varied body types and appearances that are influenced by their specific location within Leonida, affecting their clothing, tattoos, and hairstyles. A wide array of activities, from base jumping and kayaking to mini-golf and airboating, ensures a rich and immersive experience.
