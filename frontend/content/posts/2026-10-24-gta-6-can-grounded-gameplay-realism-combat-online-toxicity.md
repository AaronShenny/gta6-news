# GTA 6: Can Grounded Gameplay & Realism Combat Online Toxicity?

A recent Reddit post by u/furiousgeorge54 ignited a community discussion on the potential for a significantly less toxic player base in Grand Theft Auto 6. The user expressed hope that GTA 6's rumored emphasis on realism and grounded gameplay could lead to a positive shift in player interactions, both in and out of PvP scenarios.
The primary concern stems from the 'absurd' level of toxicity observed in GTA 5 Online, where PvP often devolves into endless kill-counter battles rather than meaningful objective-based encounters. The user envisions GTA 6's PvP to be centered around logical objectives, such as securing drug or weapon shipments, where conflicts conclude once the objective is met—similar to the dynamics seen in looter shooters.
Several key suggestions were put forth to mitigate toxicity:
*   **Player Blips:** Either completely removed or highly localized, akin to Red Dead Redemption 2, to reduce persistent tracking.
*   **Free Aim Lobbies:** To encourage more skill-based combat.
*   **Hidden Stats:** No public K/D ratios or kill counts between players, removing a common source of competitive aggression.
These proposed changes, combined with already rumored or confirmed tweaks like a limit of two primary weapons and more realistic character movement, are seen as crucial steps towards cultivating a more engaging and less hostile online environment for GTA 6.
