# Mistaken Identity: 'GTA 6 Mural' on Sunset Blvd Actually Features Grand Theft Auto V Art

A recent post on Reddit's r/GTA6 subreddit caused a stir among fans, purporting to showcase a new mural for *Grand Theft Auto VI* on Sunset Blvd in Hollywood, California. The user, /u/Kar1zza, shared an image of the mural, noting its location. However, a closer inspection of the shared image reveals that the mural in question prominently features key art from *Grand Theft Auto V*, not the highly anticipated sequel. This instance underscores the fervent excitement surrounding *GTA 6* and the ease with which unverified sightings can spread, even when based on previous game titles. While the mural itself is a legitimate piece of public art, its connection to *GTA 6* is a clear misidentification.
