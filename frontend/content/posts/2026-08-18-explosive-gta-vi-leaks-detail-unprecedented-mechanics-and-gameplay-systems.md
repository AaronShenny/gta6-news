# Explosive GTA VI Leaks Detail Unprecedented Mechanics and Gameplay Systems

Recent extensive leaks for Grand Theft Auto VI have surfaced, detailing a plethora of groundbreaking mechanics and systems. Players can expect deep car management, including fuel mechanics, mechanical maintenance akin to RDR2's horses, object and weapon storage, and highly detailed damage with paint scratches and realistic tire smoke. Car door interactivity, such as opening/closing tailgates, is also hinted at. Character systems will feature an Honor system, Stamina, and a dual cash system separating 'cash on hand' from 'cash at home/vault.' Side activities like basketball will contribute to increasing character attributes. The world promises advanced physics, with Jason taking realistic damage from crashes, dynamic water and object interactions, and denser vegetation. Visually, ray-traced real-time reflections are expected, and leaked footage reportedly runs at 60 FPS. The wanted system is getting an overhaul with a 6-star level and three distinct detection icons indicating known car, clothes, and face, alongside realistic cop behavior like speed traps and NPC drivers dynamically dodging. NPC interactions are set to be more immersive, with improved vehicle exit animations, contextual reactions (e.g., looking at Jason during a crash), and the return of dialogue options like Greet, Antagonize, Defuse, and Threaten. Hints of an improved fight animation system, building on RDR2, are also present, and a full map leak has been mentioned.

## Key Takeaways
- Deep car mechanics: fuel, maintenance, storage, detailed damage, realistic tire smoke, and interactive doors/tailgates.
- Complex character systems: Honor, Stamina, segregated cash (on-hand/vault), and attribute-boosting side activities.
- Advanced physics and graphics: Realistic crash damage, dynamic water and object physics, dense vegetation, and ray-traced reflections.
- Revamped 6-star wanted system with detailed detection mechanics (car, clothes, face known) and dynamic cop behavior.
- Enhanced NPC interactions: realistic dodging, improved animations, and the return of dialogue options like Greet/Antagonize/Defuse/Threaten.
- Leaked footage reportedly runs at 60 FPS, with hints of a full map reveal.
