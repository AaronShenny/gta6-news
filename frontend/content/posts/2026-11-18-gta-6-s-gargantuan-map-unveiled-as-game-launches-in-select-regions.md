# GTA 6's Gargantuan Map Unveiled as Game Launches in Select Regions

Grand Theft Auto 6 has officially launched in New Zealand and Australia, giving early players their first look at the game's highly anticipated features. A major revelation from these initial playthroughs is the truly gargantuan scale of the GTA 6 map. As previously hinted, the map's playable space surpasses the combined areas of Grand Theft Auto 4, Grand Theft Auto 5, and Red Dead Redemption 2.

Streamer N3on showcased the expansive map, confirming that the game is set in the state of Leonida, a clear homage to Florida. Vice City, GTA's iconic take on Miami, serves as the central hub, but the game extends far beyond, encompassing numerous surrounding counties and the Leonida Keys, a stand-in for the Florida Keys. The traditional 'fog of war' mechanic returns, meaning map details will progressively reveal themselves as players explore new areas.

Beyond its immense world, GTA 6 is also receiving critical acclaim. GameSpot awarded the title a perfect 10/10, with reviewer Tamoor Hussain hailing it as an 'astonishing open-world action game that I’d wager won’t be outdone until Grand Theft Auto 7.' This stellar reception has propelled GTA 6 to become the highest-rated game of 2026 on Metacritic, tying with the original Ocarina of Time for the highest aggregate review score ever recorded. While the game's success caused a surge in shares for Rockstar owner Take-Two, the company's stock remains down over the past year.
