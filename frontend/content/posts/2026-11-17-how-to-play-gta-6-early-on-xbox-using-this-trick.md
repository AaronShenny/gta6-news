# How To Play GTA 6 Early On Xbox Using This Trick

Grand Theft Auto 6 is set for a non-simultaneous global release, meaning players in New Zealand will be the first to experience the highly anticipated title at midnight local time on November 19. For eager Xbox players, a known method exists to potentially access the game ahead of their local release time.

### The Xbox Region Trick

By changing your Xbox console's region settings to New Zealand, you might be able to trick the system into allowing early access. This trick has worked for previous game launches, though its effectiveness for GTA 6 is not guaranteed. It's important to note that Microsoft's policy advises against changing your region unless you have physically moved to a new country.

**Steps to change Xbox region:**
1.  Go to **Settings**.
2.  Click **System**, then **Language & Localization**.
3.  Switch your location to **New Zealand**.
4.  **Restart** your console.

### PlayStation 5 Considerations

The process for PlayStation 5 is more complex, involving numerous steps. Furthermore, there have been reports of PS5 players receiving account suspensions without warning for changing their region, making it a riskier endeavor for Sony console owners.

### Early Access Times

For players in the US, midnight in New Zealand on November 19 translates to 3 AM PT / 6 AM ET on November 18. This means US players could potentially gain access almost a full day earlier than their official local release time.
