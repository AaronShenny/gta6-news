# GTA 6 Doesn’t Compromise Scale For Detail, Rockstar Says

Rockstar Games is setting a new benchmark with Grand Theft Auto VI, as confirmed by Rockstar boss Aaron Garbut. In a recent interview, Garbut stated that unlike typical game development where compromises are made between scale and detail, GTA 6 goes "the other way." He emphasized that "everything is dialed up--detail, scope, breadth, scale, interaction, and density," asserting that Rockstar has "never created anything close to this before." This ambitious approach is a direct response to the immense pressure on the project, with the team aiming to "overdeliver.

The game is set in Leonida, a fictional state inspired by Florida, promising the "full Florida experience" despite not featuring a spoof of Walt Disney World. GTA 6 is slated for release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Pre-release figures are already impressive, with reports indicating close to 6 million copies sold and over $570 million generated in pre-sales.

Further details revealed this week include an expansive ecosystem with more than 170 animal species, such as cane toads and monkeys, and a sophisticated new dynamic weather system capable of simulating tropical storms and hurricanes.
