# GTA 6 Poised for Record-Breaking Launch with 55 Million Sales and $90 ASP Predicted

Rockstar Games' highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and analyst reports compiled by Investor's Business Daily suggest it will be a commercial juggernaut of unprecedented scale.

BMO Capital Markets' Brian Pitz predicts GTA 6 could sell an astounding 55 million copies by the end of Take-Two's fiscal year 2027 (March 31, 2027), with 45 million units alone in its launch quarter. This significantly surpasses IBD's compiled estimate of 34.2 million for the launch period. Circana's Mat Piscatella emphasized the game's unique position, stating, "It's in its own league" due to the massive investment and anticipation.

Raymond James analyst Andrew Marok forecasts 50 million unit sales for fiscal year 2027, estimating a blended average selling price (ASP) close to $90. This takes into account the $80 standard and $100 Ultimate editions, with early data suggesting preorders initially skewed towards the higher-priced version, potentially balancing to a 50/50 split by launch.

The game's immense success is also expected to pave the way for a new iteration of GTA Online. While Rockstar has yet to officially confirm an online mode for GTA 6, its inclusion is considered all but guaranteed given GTA 5 Online's historic success. Morgan Stanley's Matthew Cost sees this as a "large potential audience" and a "next major catalyst" for Take-Two's stock, which has been down 10% in 2026. D.A. Davidson analyst Wyatt Swanson anticipates a surge in Take-Two's share price to $300, partly due to the expected release of a PC edition and GTA Online 2.0, which would enable "structurally higher margins."

Remarkably, Sensor Tower data indicates GTA 6 has already sold 12 million copies and generated $1.2 billion in revenue before its official release, surpassing GTA 5's record of clearing $1 billion in 72 hours.

## Key Takeaways
- GTA 6 launches November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- Analysts predict sales of up to 55 million units by March 31, 2027, with 45 million in the launch quarter.
- The average selling price (ASP) is expected to be around $90, considering $80 standard and $100 Ultimate editions.
- A new GTA Online iteration is highly anticipated and expected to be a major revenue driver and stock catalyst for Take-Two.
- The game has already generated $1.2 billion in revenue from 12 million pre-release sales, breaking records set by GTA 5.
- Future PC release and GTA Online 2.0 are expected to further boost Take-Two's stock and profit margins.
