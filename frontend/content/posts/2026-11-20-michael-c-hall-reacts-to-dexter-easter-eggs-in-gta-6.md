# Michael C. Hall Reacts to Dexter Easter Eggs in GTA 6

Grand Theft Auto 6 players have discovered a plethora of Easter eggs referencing the popular TV show *Dexter*, and now, the actor behind the titular serial killer, Michael C. Hall, has shared his reaction. Hall, who famously portrayed Dexter Morgan, revealed in an interview on Barstool Movies that he was "informed" about the in-game nods but was not "consulted" during development. He found it "pretty cool" that his character has become such a significant part of the cultural zeitgeist.
The references in GTA 6 are quite detailed and numerous. Players have identified what appears to be Dexter's iconic Miami apartment building, initially spotted in the 'Extended Look' trailer and now confirmed in the full game. Beyond the apartment, players can also access a "kill house" that eerily resembles Dexter's infamous kill room, complete with plastic sheeting, hammers, and cleavers. Furthermore, underwater discoveries of bones are believed to be another dark homage to Dexter's method of disposing of his victims in the harbor.
GTA 6 launched on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, achieving staggering success with over 32 million copies sold and nearly $4 billion in revenue on day one. The game is brimming with various Easter eggs, encouraging players to explore Vice City thoroughly.
