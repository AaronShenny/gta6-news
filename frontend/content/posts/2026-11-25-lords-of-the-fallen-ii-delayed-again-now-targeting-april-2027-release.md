# Lords of the Fallen II Delayed Again, Now Targeting April 2027 Release

CI Games has officially announced that **Lords of the Fallen II** has been delayed yet again, with the new target release window set for **April 2027**. This marks the second significant postponement for the highly anticipated title.

The game was initially scheduled for release in 2026. However, CI Games previously pushed it to Q1 2027 (January-March), citing a desire to **avoid direct competition with GTA 6** and to allow for additional time to polish the experience. The latest delay now shifts the launch into Q2 2027.

According to CI Games, the decision for this new delay stems from "meaningful opportunities" identified through feedback and internal tests, which indicate that more time would allow them to "further refine and strengthen the overall experience." An exact release date for April 2027 is expected to be announced in December, with many theorizing this reveal could take place at The Game Awards on December 10. 

Despite its name, Lords of the Fallen II is actually the third game in the series, following the 2014 original and the 2023 reboot. CI Games, the Polish developer behind the title, is also known for their work on the Sniper: Ghost Warrior series.
