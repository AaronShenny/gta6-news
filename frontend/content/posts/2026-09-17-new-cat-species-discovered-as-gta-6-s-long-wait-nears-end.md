# New Cat Species Discovered as GTA 6's Long Wait Nears End

The highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6* has been in development since 2015, a period so extensive that it has now been outlasted by the discovery of a brand-new species of cat. Scientists recently unveiled the **Leopardus tilcayo**, or tilcayo tiger cat, a small, spotted feline with round ears found in Bolivia's Yungas forest. This marks the first new cat species discovered in over a century, a testament to the lengthy wait for Rockstar's next blockbuster title.
The discovery itself was serendipitous, made by co-author Paola Nogales-Ascarrunz at a wildlife rehab facility. Genetic testing confirmed the tiny creature, initially mistaken for a kitten, was indeed a previously undocumented species.
The article draws parallels between this scientific milestone and other significant events that have transpired since *GTA 5*'s release, including major shifts in the gaming industry like *Halo* coming to PlayStation and the tenure of Phil Spencer as Xbox CEO. The extended development has even spawned the popular 'We got ___ before GTA VI' meme, a phenomenon acknowledged by Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick.
However, the wait is almost over. *Grand Theft Auto 6* is officially scheduled for release on **November 19** for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. While the game will not be available on physical discs, fans can still get a disc with the *GTA 6* soundtrack.
